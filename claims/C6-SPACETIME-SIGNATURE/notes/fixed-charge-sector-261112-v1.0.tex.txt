\section{Stable minimum set is not an attractor}
EXCH001 already supplies complete finite-$K$ flow conserving $H$ and $Q$.
Consequently $\mathcal M$ is invariant. For any distance $\delta>0$, the
compact sector subset at distance at least $\delta$ from $\mathcal M$
has a positive energy gap above $m=\min_\Sigma H$ (if the subset is empty,
there is nothing to prove). Uniform continuity supplies a smaller initial
neighborhood with energy below that gap. Energy conservation prevents
escape. This proves two-sided set-Lyapunov stability, not stability of every
individual orbit, a unique condensate or cutoff-uniform bounds.

For any initial state outside $\mathcal M$, $H>m$ stays constant. If its
distance to $\mathcal M$ tended to zero, compactness and continuity would
force its energy to tend to $m$, a contradiction. Thus no outside
\emph{full-state} trajectory converges to the ground set. This does not
exclude coarse-observable relaxation or transient structures. The unstable
reservoir manifold has energy $E_R>m$, not the mixed minimum energy.
