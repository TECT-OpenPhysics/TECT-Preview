\section*{Precise conditional theorem}
Let $S(t)$ be any symmetric, strongly continuous contraction semigroup on
this same $H$, positive and $L^\infty$-contractive, with generator $G$
satisfying $D\subset\operatorname{Dom}(G)$ and $G|_D=A$. Then
\[
 S(t)=T_{\min}(t)\qquad(t\geq0),
\]
where $T_{\min}$ is the R-512 extension with its Markov proof repaired in
R-567. No minimal form domain, graph core, path realization or boundary
condition is imposed on the competitors. This is Markov-extension
uniqueness, not essential self-adjointness and not finite PAH convergence.

\section*{Every extension respects radial localization}
For radial $a\in D$, $Ga=Aa=0$. Generator closedness and density of radial
cylinders show that the full radial subspace $H_{\rm rad}$ lies in
$\ker G$. Thus $S(t)a=a$ there, including $a=1$. For any radial event $E$
and $0\leq f\leq M$, positivity gives
\[
 0\leq S(t)(1_Ef)\leq M S(t)1_E=M1_E.
\]
The complementary event gives a disjoint image. By additivity and bounded
approximation, $S(t)1_Ef=1_ES(t)f$ for all $f\in H$. Differentiation at
zero therefore gives $G(1_Ef)=1_EGf$ for $f\in\operatorname{Dom}(G)$.
This is an all-extension statement; it uses no infinite process.

\section*{Uniform state tail and the exact rate envelope}
Write $K$ for the nonsymmetric SPATIAL transfer of R-510, not the time
generator. Its source bounds are $K(x,y)\leq8u(x)u(y)$ and
$u^2\leq\exp[-(r_0^6+r_1^6)/6]$. With its normalized positive right
eigenfunction $\phi$, set $U=\|u\|_2$, $c=\langle u,\phi\rangle>0$ and
$B=\|P\|+C$, where $\|\lambda^{-i}K^i-P\|\leq Cq^i$.
The anchored column density, not a translation-invariant surrogate, is
\[
 \rho_i=\frac{(K^*)^iu\,\phi}{\lambda^i c}.
\]
The product envelope gives $\phi\leq8Uu/\lambda$ and, for $i\geq1$,
$(K^*)^iu\leq8B\lambda^{i-1}U^2u$. Treat $i=0$ separately. Hence
\[
 \rho_i\leq D_0u^2,\quad
 D_0=\max\left(\frac{8U}{\lambda c},
                  \frac{64BU^3}{\lambda^2c}\right).
\]
It follows that $\sup_{i,v}\mu(e^{r_{i,v}^6/12})\leq C_{\rm tail}<\infty$.
These are finite operator-defined constants, not numerical spectral-gap
certificates or estimates uniform in changed source parameters.

\section*{The all-extension domain bridge}
Let $A^{[N]}$ sum the exact roots centered in columns $0,\ldots,N$,
retaining their full exterior-dependent rates. Its finite label matrix
defines $P_N(s)$ at each fixed amplitude vector and exterior labels.
This is a backward test, not a substituted finite-volume Hamiltonian.

For $f\in D$ supported through $m\leq N$, $P_N(s)f$ reads only columns
through $N+R_c$. It is gauge invariant and Lipschitz on every compact
amplitude box by finite matrix-exponential regularity. A radial cutoff
equal to one on the $E_M$ prefix box makes it an element of the ORIGINAL
$D$. The cutoff has zero $A$ increment. Radial multiplier commutation
therefore yields, for every candidate generator,
\[
 v_s=1_{E_M}P_N(s)f\in\operatorname{Dom}(G),\qquad
 Gv_s=1_{E_M}AP_N(s)f,\qquad
 v_s'=1_{E_M}A^{[N]}P_N(s)f.
\]
These functions and derivatives are graph-norm continuous in $s$ on
compact intervals, using the finite number of bounded rates on that box.
Neither $A(D)\subset D$ nor a new common graph core is assumed.

\section*{Boundary influence and uniqueness}
For fixed $T$, put $d_N=\lceil(N+1-m)/R_c\rceil$, $x_N=B_cq_NT$ and
$k_f=b(m+1)$. A finite rate-$q_N$ proposal construction, preserving every
channel and its acceptance probability $c_r/q_N$, bounds exterior influence:
\[
 |\Delta_rP_N(s)f|\leq2\|f\|_\infty k_f
       \sum_{k\geq d_N}\frac{x_N^k}{k!}
 \leq2\|f\|_\infty k_f e^{x_N}\frac{x_N^{d_N}}{d_N!}.
\]
Ordered proposals contribute $s^k/k!$ even when paths share channels;
the proof uses a union bound, not independence between paths. At most
$pR_c$ exterior roots can change the test, so
\[
 \sup_{s\leq T}\|1_{E_M}(A-A^{[N]})P_N(s)f\|_H
 \leq 2\|f\|_\infty k_fpR_cq_N e^{x_N}\frac{x_N^{d_N}}{d_N!}
 =:\varepsilon_N.
\]
Using $q_N=O(\sqrt N)$ and $d!\geq(d/e)^d$ gives
\[
 \log\varepsilon_N\leq-\frac{N}{2R_c}\log N+O(N)+O(\sqrt N)
 \longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
The constants depend on $M,T,f$, not $N$ or the extension. The zero-vector
and zero-time cases are immediate. Differentiating $S(t-s)v_s$ now gives
\[
 \sup_{t\leq T}\|S(t)1_{E_M}f-1_{E_M}P_N(t)f\|_H\leq T\varepsilon_N.
\]
Two arbitrary extensions share the same comparison. First take
$N\to\infty$ at fixed $M$, then remove $E_M$ using the remaining norm
error $2\|f\|_\infty\sqrt{\mu(E_M^c)}$. Density of $D$ proves equality
on $H$. R-567 supplies one member, the minimal extension.

\section*{Independent formulation and verification coverage}

$L_N=\sum_jb_j(\tau_j-I)$, summing rather than deleting coincident channels.
Since the binary flips commute, $w_i=(\tau_i-I)P_N(t)f$ satisfies
\[
 w_i'=L_Nw_i+\sum_j(\tau_i b_j-b_j)\tau_i(\tau_j-I)P_N(t)f.

\section*{Devil's-advocate}

against the shortcut; the localization and boundary estimates are essential.
\item $P_N(s)f$ automatically belongs to $D$: UPHELD without the compact

\item A globally bounded rate or fixed $R_{\max}$ is needed: DISMISSED
here by the state-derived tempered events and subpolynomial rate envelope.
\item Linear rate growth is covered by the same decay argument: UPHELD
against that inference; the logarithmic coefficient requires $\delta<1$.
\item Uniqueness identifies original finite PAH dynamics: UPHELD against
promotion. No finite-to-common-space realization or convergence is supplied.
\end{enumerate}
