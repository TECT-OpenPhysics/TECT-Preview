second weighted-energy moment, the minimal three-half-energy moment,

rung-by-rung.

No common thermodynamic automorphism, common-alpha KMS identification,
algebraic ground state, GNS or physical mass gap, continuum removal, physical

\section{Exact model and first-moment input}

At every site $x\in\mathbb Z^3$, let $q_x,p_x\in\mathbb R^8$.  The
finite-volume Hamiltonian is

\[
 H_\Lambda(h)=\sum_{x\in\Lambda}
 \left\{{|p_x|^2\over2\chi}+{r\over2}|q_x|^2+W_4(q_x)
 -h u\mathbin\cdot q_x\right\}
 +{c\over2}\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}|q_x-q_y|^2,             \tag{2.1}
\]

where $\chi,c,g>0$, $\lambda\ge0$, and

\[
 W_4(q)={g\over4}\sum_eq_e^4
 +{\lambda\over4}\sum_{e\sim f\ {\rm in}\ Q_3}
 (q_e-q_f)^2(q_e^2+q_f^2).                                  \tag{2.2}
\]

For $|h|\le h_0$ and $0<\gamma<g/32$, a scalar shift makes the local
energies positive with

\[
 e_x\ge {|p_x|^2\over2\chi}+\gamma|q_x|^4
 +{c\over4}\sum_{y\sim x}|q_x-q_y|^2.                       \tag{2.3}
\]

Fix $f_x=e^{-\mu d(x,X)}$, $S_f=\sum_xf_x<\infty$, and set

\[
 A=1+E_f=1+\sum_xf_xe_x=T_f+U_f+1.                           \tag{2.4}
\]

The first-moment theorem from \texttt{EXP-000792} is

\[
 U_\Lambda(t)^*AU_\Lambda(t)\le e^{v_\mu|t|}A,
 \qquad
 v_\mu=6\sqrt{c\over2\chi}\,(e^\mu-1).                    \tag{2.5}
\]

This controls only one energy moment.  Positive operator order is not
preserved by taking powers, so (2.5) cannot simply be squared.

\section{Why automatic squaring fails}

Let

\[
 E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 U={1\over5}\begin{pmatrix}3&-4\\4&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A_1=U^*EU,\qquad c_0={5\over2}.                             \tag{3.1}
\]

Exact rational arithmetic gives

\[
 (c_0E-A_1)_{11}={11\over50},\qquad
 \det(c_0E-A_1)={7\over20}>0,                               \tag{3.2}
\]

but

\[
 \det(c_0^2E^2-A_1^2)=-{127\over16}<0.                      \tag{3.3}
\]

Thus the first form cone is not a higher-moment theorem.  The next section
uses the exact Q3LOCK current rather than this invalid order inference.

\section{Exact second weighted-energy moment}

On the common Schwartz/form core let

\[
 B_f=\delta(E_f)={i\over\hbar}[H_\Lambda(h),E_f].             \tag{4.1}
\]

The local continuity equation gives

\[
 B_f=-\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}(f_x-f_y){c\over2\chi}
 (p_x+p_y)\mathbin\cdot(q_x-q_y).                            \tag{4.2}
\]

Put $\theta=e^\mu-1$ and $z=6$.  Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz yields

\[
 \|B_f\psi\|^2\le {cz\theta^2\over\chi^2}
 \int U_f\sum_xf_x|p_x\psi|^2.                              \tag{4.3}
\]

The remaining term has the exact Schroedinger cross identity

\[
 \int U_f\sum_xf_x|p_x\psi|^2
 =2\chi\,\Re\langle T_f\psi,U_f\psi\rangle
 +{\hbar^2\over2}\int\left(\sum_xf_x\Delta_xU_f\right)|\psi|^2.
                                                                    \tag{4.4}
\]

For one Q3 edge with coordinates $a,b$,

\[
 (\partial_a^2+\partial_b^2)
 {\lambda\over4}(a-b)^2(a^2+b^2)
 =\lambda(4a^2-6ab+4b^2)\le7\lambda(a^2+b^2).               \tag{4.5}
\]

Since every Q3 vertex has degree three,

\[
 \Delta W_4(q)\le C_2|q|^2,\qquad C_2=3g+21\lambda.         \tag{4.6}
\]

For every $\epsilon>0$,

\[
 \sum_xf_x\Delta_xU_f\le C_2\epsilon U_f
 +S_f\left(8r_++8cz+{C_2\over4\epsilon\gamma}\right).     \tag{4.7}
\]

Equations (4.3)--(4.7) close both operator orientations:

\[
 \|B_fA^{-1}\|=\|A^{-1}B_f\|\le M_\mu,                    \tag{4.8}
\]

where

\[
 M_\mu=\theta\left\{{cz\over\chi}\left[1+{\hbar^2\over2\chi}
 \left(C_2\epsilon+S_f\left(8r_++8cz
 +{C_2\over4\epsilon\gamma}\right)\right)\right]\right\}^{1/2}.
                                                                    \tag{4.9}
\]

Because $\delta(A^2)=AB_f+B_fA$, form Gronwall gives

\[
 U_\Lambda(t)^*A^2U_\Lambda(t)\le e^{2M_\mu|t|}A^2,
 \qquad
 \|AU_\Lambda(t)A^{-1}\|\le e^{M_\mu|t|}.                 \tag{4.10}
\]

The constants are uniform in finite volume, sign phase, and the admitted
compact source interval.

\section{Three-quarter energy-domain propagation}

Interpolating (2.5) and (4.10) gives

\[
 \|A^{3/4}U_\Lambda(t)A^{-3/4}\|
 \le\exp\left[\left({v_\mu\over4}+{M_\mu\over2}\right)|t|\right],
                                                                    \tag{5.1}
\]

or equivalently

\[
 U_\Lambda(t)^*A^{3/2}U_\Lambda(t)
 \le e^{(v_\mu/2+M_\mu)|t|}A^{3/2}.                        \tag{5.2}
\]

Scalar power counting identifies a necessary target for the cubic force: in
the commuting model $A\sim1+\gamma q^4$, $q^3A^{-s}$ can be bounded only for
$s\ge3/4$.  A symmetric sandwich analog needs $s\ge3/8$.  This power count
does not prove $q^3A^{-3/4}$ bounded for the noncommuting anharmonic
Schroedinger energy, nor the embedding $D(A^{3/4})\subset D(q^3)$.  Equations
(5.1)--(5.2) prove energy-domain propagation only; the cubic multiplier and
domain embedding remain part of the next gate.

The boundary position multiplier also closes.  Balakrishnan calculus gives

\[
 \|[A^{1/2},q_x]A^{-3/4}\|
 \le\hbar\sqrt{f_x\over2\chi},                               \tag{5.3}
\]

and therefore

\[
 f_x^{1/4}\|A^{1/2}q_xA^{-3/4}\|
 \le\gamma^{-1/4}+{\hbar\over\sqrt{2\chi}},                \tag{5.4}
\]

with the adjoint orientation as well.

\section{Conditional thermodynamic-Cauchy theorem}

For $\Lambda\subset\Lambda'$, the exact boundary Duhamel identity is

\[
 \tau_t^{\Lambda'}(A_0)-\tau_t^\Lambda(A_0)
 ={i\over\hbar}\int_0^t\tau_{t-s}^{\Lambda'}
 [V_{\partial\Lambda},\tau_s^\Lambda(A_0)]\,ds.             \tag{6.1}
\]

For $R=q_x-q_y$, $C_1^a=[R_a,D]$, and
$C_2^a=[R_a,C_1^a]$,

\[
 [c|R|^2/2,D]={c\over2}\sum_a(C_2^a+2C_1^aR_a).             \tag{6.2}
\]

Assume both one-sided orientations satisfy, uniformly in finite volume and
compact time,

\[
 \|C_1^aA^{-1/2}\|+\|A^{-1/2}C_1^a\|
 \le C e^{-\rho(d(x,X)-v|t|)},                               \tag{6.3}
\]

\[
 \|C_2^aA^{-3/4}\|+\|A^{-3/4}C_2^a\|
 \le C e^{-\rho(d(x,X)-v|t|)},\qquad \rho>{\mu\over4}.      \tag{6.4}
\]

Then the cubic-lattice shell sum and (5.1)--(5.4) give exponential boundary
decay in both one-sided $A^{-3/4}$-damped norms.  Union comparison makes the
limit exhaustion independent.  The two orientations pass products,
adjoints, group law, inverse, and strong time continuity on the stable core.
An invariant Hamiltonian-derived C-star algebra or norm-tight energy tails
are still required for the C-star conclusion.

Symmetric damping alone is insufficient.  On $\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)$, let
$We_n=(n+1)e_n$ and $D_n=|e_n\rangle\langle e_0|$.  Then both symmetric
sandwich norms are $(n+1)^{-s}\to0$, including the adjoints, but
$D_ne_0=e_n$ is not strongly Cauchy.

\section{The infinite ladder and the rejected rung route}

The cubic force does not preserve a finite first/double commutator system:

\[
 [q^3,D]=3[q,D]q^2+3[q,[q,D]]q+[q,[q,[q,D]]].                \tag{7.1}
\]

A proposed repair was a polynomial separate-rung estimate for
$K^{j/2}V(t)K^{-(j+1)/2}$.  It is exactly false.  Let
$k\phi_m=\epsilon_m\phi_m$ be a positive confining onsite Hamiltonian,
$K=k\otimes1+1\otimes k$, and
$V=-c\sum_aq_a\otimes q_a$.  A nonzero upward transition gives

\[
 \|K^{j/2}VK^{-(j+1)/2}\|
 \ge {b_n\over\sqrt{2\epsilon_0}}
 \left({\epsilon_n\over\epsilon_0}\right)^{j/2},
 \qquad b_n=c\sum_a|\langle\phi_n,q_a\phi_0\rangle|^2>0.  \tag{7.2}
\]

The bound is unchanged in the onsite interaction picture.  In the exact
fixture

\[
 K=\operatorname{diag}(1,4),\qquad
 V=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},                    \tag{7.3}
\]

the norm is $2^j$.  No fixed polynomial in $j$ can dominate it.  A simple
exponential rung allowance is also unsuitable for sequential multiplication,
which produces an $\exp[O(n^2)]$ cost.

The base bond rung survives:

\[
 \|V_{xy}(t)K_{xy}^{-1/2}\|,
 \ \|K_{xy}^{-1/2}V_{xy}(t)\|
 \le {c\over\sqrt{2\gamma}}.                                \tag{7.4}
\]

The replacement must estimate the complete Volterra product without moving a
growing power through every bond.  Candidate architectures are a direct
linked-cluster estimate, a heat/strip-loss analytic ideal, or a KMS-specific
state-weighted theorem, followed by a noncollapsing finite-density algebra
bridge.

\section{Convex subregime: a true lemma and a false promotion}

For $0\le\lambda\le2g$, the quartic is genuinely convex and

\[
 D^2W_4(q)[\xi,\xi]
 \ge3(g-\lambda/2)\sum_aq_a^2\xi_a^2.                        \tag{8.1}
\]

This gives an unweighted Hilbert--Schmidt monotonicity sign, but not the
energy/KMS-weighted operator sign needed in (6.3)--(6.4).  Already for a
scalar convex quartic set $q=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,2)$, let
$D$ be the negative all-ones matrix, $C=[q,D]$, and $F=[q^3,D]$.  Then

\[
 X={C^*F+F^*C\over2}
 =\begin{pmatrix}17&11&-4\\11&8&5\\-4&5&23\end{pmatrix}.   \tag{8.2}
\]

Although ${\rm tr}\,X=48$, the vector $v=(-2,2,-1)$ satisfies
$v^*Xv=-1$.  Hence the faithful weight
$(vv^*+\epsilon I)/(9+3\epsilon)$ has negative expectation for
$0<\epsilon<1/48$.  Convexity alone cannot provide the missing weighted
commutator estimate.
