\section{Exact pure-bond coordinate-tail theorem}

Write the full cross-bond multiplication operator and its unitary layer as

\[
 V_\times=-c\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}q_x\mathbin\cdot q_y,
 \qquad B_\delta=\exp(-i\delta V_\times/\hbar).             \tag{3.1}
\]

Every bounded coordinate multiplier $F(q_S)$ commutes with $B_\delta$.
Hence, in both history orientations,

\[
 B_\delta^*F(q_S)B_\delta=F(q_S).                         \tag{3.2}
\]

This includes the local tail projection

\[
 E_{S,L}=1_{\{X_S>L\}},\qquad X_S=\max_{x\in S}|q_x|,     \tag{3.3}
\]

and, by strong commutation of affiliated multiplication operators, weighted
coordinate multipliers on their natural finite-moment forms.

Let $V_{\times,L}$ be a coordinate cutoff and put

\[
 W_L=V_\times-V_{\times,L},\qquad
 B_{\delta,L}=\exp(-i\delta V_{\times,L}/\hbar).           \tag{3.4}
\]

All three multiplication operators commute.  For every finite-volume type-I
normal state $\sigma$ satisfying $\sigma(W_L^2)<\infty$, functional calculus
gives the exact two-orientation Hilbert--Schmidt identity

\[
\begin{split}
 \|(B_\delta-B_{\delta,L})\sigma^{1/2}\|_2^2
 &=\|\sigma^{1/2}(B_\delta-B_{\delta,L})\|_2^2\\
 &=\sigma\!\left(4\sin^2{\delta W_L\over2\hbar}\right)\\
 &\le {\delta^2\over\hbar^2}\sigma(W_L^2).
\end{split}                                                \tag{3.5}
\]

Therefore a state-weighted all-bond telescope pays no artificial
$\exp(CL^2)$ operator-norm multiplier at a pure bond layer.  Equation (3.5)
does not estimate $\sigma(W_L^2)$ in the actual intermediate states.  The
missing input is precisely the two-orientation $W_L^2$ tail after onsite and
bond layers have been interspersed.

\[

\section{Local measured-Renyi sufficient condition}

Fix finite $S$, choose $\alpha>1$, and define

\[
 \vartheta={\alpha-1\over\alpha}.                         \tag{5.1}
\]

Let $\nu_{0,S}$ be the reference law of $X_S$, and let
$\nu_{P,S}^+$ and $\nu_{P,S}^-$ be its laws after a partial history and its
adjoint.  The minimal direct target is

\[
 \sup_{u\ge L_0}
 {\nu_{P,S}^{\pm}(X_S>u)\over
  \nu_{0,S}(X_S>u)^\vartheta}
 \le C_{\alpha,T,S},                                      \tag{5.2}
\]

uniformly in volume, source, mesh, partial history, and orientation.  A
stronger checkable condition confined to the commutative coordinate algebra
is

\[
 \int\left({d\nu_{P,S}^{\pm}\over d\nu_{0,S}}\right)^\alpha
 d\nu_{0,S}\le Q_{\alpha,T,S}.                            \tag{5.3}
\]

Condition (5.3) is weaker than a global quantum sandwiched-Renyi bound.  If

\[
 \nu_{0,S}(X_S>u)\le M_a|S|e^{-au^2},                     \tag{5.4}
\]

Holder's inequality yields the two-orientation probability estimate

\[
 \nu_{P,S}^+(X_S>L)+\nu_{P,S}^-(X_S>L)
 \le2Q_{\alpha,T,S}^{1/\alpha}(M_a|S|)^\vartheta
 e^{-\vartheta aL^2}.                                     \tag{5.5}
\]

Putting $b=\vartheta a$, fourth-moment layer cake gives

\[
 2Q_{\alpha,T,S}^{1/\alpha}(M_a|S|)^\vartheta e^{-bL^2}
 \left(L^4+{2L^2\over b}+{2\over b^2}\right).            \tag{5.6}
\]

This closes only the local-tail implication for each fixed finite $S$.  It
does not by itself supply the full growing weighted-tail corridor.  That
corridor still requires either a bound uniform over translated two-site
shapes, or an explicit growth envelope for $Q_{\alpha,T,S_R}$ that remains
absorbable when the history region $S_R$, range $R$, and cutoff $L$ grow.
No onsite-interspersed Q3LOCK estimate of the form (5.2) or (5.3) is proved
here.

\section{Q3 onsite semiclassical theorem}

\[
 R=-r>0,\qquad \mu={\lambda\over g}>0,\qquad

\[
 W_\mu(x)={1\over4}\sum_{e=1}^8(x_e^2-1)^2

 (x_e-x_f)^2(x_e^2+x_f^2).                                \tag{6.3}
\]

For fixed $\mu>0$, $W_\mu$ is coercive and has exactly the two global minima
$\pm\mathbf1$.  At either minimum,

\[
 \operatorname{Hess}W_\mu=2I+\mu L_{Q_3},                 \tag{6.4}
\]

with spectrum

\[
 2,\qquad(2+2\mu)^{\times3},\qquad
 (2+4\mu)^{\times3},\qquad2+6\mu.                        \tag{6.5}
\]

Both wells are nondegenerate, and the exact dimensionless Agmon action is

\[
 S_0={16\sqrt2\over3}.                                     \tag{6.6}
\]

The load-bearing primary sources are Simon's nondegenerate-minimum and
degenerate-cluster results, including Theorem 1.1, Theorem 4.1, Lemma 4.2,

\url{https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPA_1984__40_2_224_0/}; the multiple-well

For every fixed $\mu>0$, there is a non-explicit $h_0(\mu)>0$ such that

\[
 \delta_1=\epsilon_1-\epsilon_0,\qquad

\[
 \lim_{h\downarrow0}h\log{\delta_1\over E_*}=-S_0,         \tag{6.8}

\[
 {\Gamma\over E_*}=\sqrt2h+O(h^{3/2}),                    \tag{6.10}

and

\[

 \sqrt2+3\sqrt{2+2\mu}+3\sqrt{2+4\mu}+\sqrt{2+6\mu}

After fixing the phase of $\phi_1$, cube symmetry and weighted harmonic

\begin{split}
