the finite-dimensional hypotheses.  For every separately fixed order \(n\),

\begin{equation}
 H_{{\rm eff},n}=\sum_CK_{n,C},\qquad
 K_{n,C}=0\quad\hbox{unless }C\hbox{ is connected and }|C|\le n.
 \tag{4.1}
\end{equation}

The operator \(K_{n,C}\) acts only on the vertices of \(C\).  A nonzero

\subsection{A zero low--high tail does not control a moving insertion family}

Let

\begin{equation}
 {\cal H}=\mathbb Cp\oplus\ell^2\{e_j:j\ge1\},\qquad
 K=R=Q,\qquad T=|e_1\rangle\langle p|,                  \tag{9.1}
\end{equation}

and let \(\Pi_M\) project onto \(p,e_1,\ldots,e_M\).  For every \(M\ge1\),

\begin{equation}
 \tau_M=\|(1-\Pi_M)T\|=0.                              \tag{9.2}
\end{equation}

For \(j\ge2\), define the uniformly bounded positive family

\begin{equation}
 C_j=|e_1+e_j\rangle\langle e_1+e_j|,\qquad\|C_j\|=2.   \tag{9.3}
\end{equation}

Choosing \(j=M+1\) gives

\begin{align}
 \|(1-\Pi_M)C_j\Pi_MT\|&=1,                            \tag{9.4}\\
 \|(C_jT)^*(C_jT)-(\Pi_MC_j\Pi_MT)^*(\Pi_MC_j\Pi_MT)\|&=1.\tag{9.5}
\end{align}

Every fixed \(C_j\) becomes exact once \(M\ge j\), but the supremum over the
moving family fails.  Thus even an exactly zero low--high tail does not
automatically control a uniform high--high insertion cutoff.  The actual Q3
route instead uses (5.5) and finite-rank ranges.

\subsection{Orbit-smearing labels do not automatically define a spatial net}

On two qubits take \(H=X\otimes X\) and
\(\alpha_t=\operatorname{Ad}(e^{itH})\).  Formally disjoint seeds evolve as

\begin{align}
 \alpha_t(ZI)&=\cos(2t)ZI+\sin(2t)YX,\notag\\
 \alpha_t(IZ)&=\cos(2t)IZ+\sin(2t)XY.                  \tag{9.6}
\end{align}

Smear the first with \(f_+(t)=e^{-t}{\bf1}_{t\ge0}\) and the second with
\(f_-(t)=e^t{\bf1}_{t\le0}\).  Both have \(L^1\) norm one, and

\begin{equation}
 \int f_\pm(t)\cos(2t)dt={1\over5},\quad
 \int f_+(t)\sin(2t)dt={2\over5},\quad
 \int f_-(t)\sin(2t)dt=-{2\over5}.                    \tag{9.7}
\end{equation}

Thus

\begin{equation}
 A_+={1\over5}ZI+{2\over5}YX,\qquad
 B_-={1\over5}IZ-{2\over5}XY,                           \tag{9.8}
\end{equation}

but

\begin{equation}
 [A_+,B_-]=-{8i\over25}Y\otimes Y,
 \qquad\|[A_+,B_-]\|={8\over25}.                       \tag{9.9}
\end{equation}

The raw norm oracle is \texttt{8/25}.  Temporal orbit smearing does not
automatically turn formal seed support into commuting spatial local algebras.
This is not a no-go for every orbit-smear C-star carrier, for a construction
with propagation estimates, or for every possible local net.
