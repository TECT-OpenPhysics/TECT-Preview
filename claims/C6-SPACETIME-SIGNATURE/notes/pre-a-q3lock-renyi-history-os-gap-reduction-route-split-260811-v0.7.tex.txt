\section{Exact fixed-Trotter exhaustion compatibility}

\[
 \mathcal A_X=B\!\left(L^2(\mathbb R^{8|X|})\right)

 \mathcal A_X\longrightarrow\mathcal A_Y,\quad A\longmapsto A\otimes1,
                                                               \tag{3.1}
\]

cross-bond generator

\[
 v_{xy}=-c\,q_x\mathbin{\cdot}q_y                            \tag{3.2}
\]

is a multiplication polynomial.  All such generators commute exactly,
including generators on bonds with one common endpoint.  Factors on bonds
not incident on the current support commute with the observable and with
every retained bond factor.  Consequently one exact all-bond layer maps

\[
 \mathcal A_X\longrightarrow\mathcal A_{N_1(X)}              \tag{3.3}

and agrees in every finite ambient volume containing $N_1(X)$.

More generally, a fixed split-product word containing $b$ bond layers lies

\[
 \Theta_{t,n}=(\sigma_{t/n}\beta_{t/n})^n,\qquad
 \Theta_{t,n}:\mathcal A_X\longrightarrow\mathcal A_{N_n(X)}, \tag{3.4}

\section{Sandwiched-Renyi history domination}

Let $\rho$ be a faithful finite Gibbs density, let $P$ be a unitary partial
split history, let $\alpha>1$, and set

\[
 \vartheta={\alpha-1\over\alpha},\qquad
 \widetilde Q_\alpha(P\rho P^*\Vert\rho)
 =\left\|\rho^{-\vartheta/2}P\rho^{1/2}\right\|_{2\alpha}^{2\alpha}.
                                                               \tag{4.1}
\]

For every projection $E$, noncommutative Holder, or equivalently binary
measurement data processing, gives

\[
 \operatorname {Tr}(P\rho P^*E)
 \le
 \widetilde Q_\alpha(P\rho P^*\Vert\rho)^{1/\alpha}
 \operatorname {Tr}(\rho E)^\vartheta.                       \tag{4.2}
\]

Suppose that both orientations, uniformly over the finite volume, compact
source, mesh, partial history, and $|t|\le T$, satisfy

\[
\begin{split}
 \widetilde Q_\alpha(P\rho P^*\Vert\rho)&\le Q_{\alpha,T},\\
 \widetilde Q_\alpha(P^*\rho P\Vert\rho)&\le Q_{\alpha,T}.
\end{split}                                                   \tag{4.3}
\]

Then the required orientation sum has the exact factor two:

\[
 \rho(P^*EP)+\rho(PEP^*)
 \le2Q_{\alpha,T}^{1/\alpha}\rho(E)^\vartheta.                \tag{4.4}
\]

This is a sufficient reduction.  The smaller model-specific target is (4.4)
and its coordinate-polynomial weighted form on the actual cutoff events;
the full uniform Renyi hypothesis is not asserted to be necessary.

\section{Zero-temperature OS mass and GNS gap equivalence}

Let $(\pi_\omega,\mathcal H_\omega,\Omega_\omega)$ be one selected
zero-temperature phase representation with

\[
 H_\omega\ge0,\qquad H_\omega\Omega_\omega=0,\qquad
 \ker H_\omega=\mathbb C\Omega_\omega.                       \tag{7.1}
\]

For an invariant unital observable core, put

\[
 \xi_A=\pi_\omega(A-\omega(A)1)\Omega_\omega.                \tag{7.2}
\]

Assume that these centered vectors form a form core for
$H_\omega^{1/2}$ on $\Omega_\omega^\perp$.  Define physical imaginary time

\[
\begin{split}
 G_A(\tau)
 &=\langle\xi_A,e^{-\tau H_\omega/\hbar}\xi_A\rangle\\
 &=\int_{(0,\infty)}e^{-\tau E/\hbar}\,d\mu_A(E).
\end{split}                                                   \tag{7.3}
\]

For every $\Delta>0$, the following are equivalent:

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\sigma(H_\omega)\subset\{0\}\cup[\Delta,\infty)$;
\item for every core observable,
 \[
 -i\hbar\,\omega(A^*\delta(A))
 \ge\Delta\{\omega(A^*A)-|\omega(A)|^2\};                   \tag{7.4}
 \]
\item for every core observable and $\tau\ge0$,
 \[
 G_A(\tau)\le e^{-\Delta\tau/\hbar}G_A(0).                  \tag{7.5}
 \]
\end{enumerate}

Here $\delta(A)=(i/\hbar)[H_\omega,A]$ on the core, and

\[
 -\hbar G_A'(0+)
 =\langle\xi_A,H_\omega\xi_A\rangle
 =-i\hbar\,\omega(A^*\delta(A)).                             \tag{7.6}
\]

The spectral theorem proves the forward implications; the form-core
hypothesis promotes the converse coercivity to the whole vacuum complement.
A weaker large-time test is also exact: if every core observable has a
finite $C_A$ and there is one common $m>0$ such that, for all sufficiently
large $\tau$,

\[
 G_A(\tau)\le C_Ae^{-m\tau},                                \tag{7.7}
\]

then no core spectral measure has support below $\hbar m$, and the form-core
hypothesis gives

\[
 \Delta\ge\hbar m.                                          \tag{7.8}
\]

This theorem identifies the precise spectral target.  A fixed-beta periodic
OS/KMS reconstruction supplies a thermal Liouvillean, not automatically the
positive vacuum Hamiltonian in (7.1).  Q3LOCK still needs beta-infinity
phase selection and one phasewise, beta-uniform half-line rate.
