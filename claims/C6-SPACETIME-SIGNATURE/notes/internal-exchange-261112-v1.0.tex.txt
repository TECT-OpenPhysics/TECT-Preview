\section{Controlled shifted coexistence}
For $\mu\le\mu_t$, $a=1/2-\mu\ge a_0=1/6$.
Pointwise $|S|\le\rho$ and square completion give
\[
 a|\chi|^2-\varepsilon\Re(\bar\chi S)
 \ge-\frac{\varepsilon^2|S|^2}{4a}
 \ge-\frac{\varepsilon^2\rho^2}{4a_0}.
\]
Put $A=\gamma/6$, $b=\varepsilon^2/(4a_0)$ and
\[
 D=2b\rho_*+\frac{b^2}{2A}
   =\frac{2827}{1179648},\qquad \mu_-=\mu_t-2D>0.
\]
For $\delta=\mu_t-\mu$, the density bracket has the exact form
\[
 \frac\delta2+A(\rho-\rho_*)^2-b\rho
 =A\left(\rho-\rho_*-\frac b{2A}\right)^2+\frac{\delta-D}{2}.
\]
Hence at $\mu_-$, $\Omega\ge DQ_\Psi$, and if $\Psi=0$ the remaining
$a|\chi|^2+|\chi|^4/4$ is strictly positive unless $\chi=0$.
Zero is the unique minimizer at $\mu_-$ and below. The preceding legal trial
has negative energy at $\mu_t$.

\paragraph{Compactness, not a finite table.}
Minimizers exist uniformly over the compact $\mu$ interval at each fixed $K$.
Indeed $F_{\rm P1}\ge\lambda_0Q_\Psi+(\lambda/4)\int\rho^2
+(\gamma/6)\int\rho^3$, and
$-\varepsilon|\chi|\rho\ge-\varepsilon(|\chi|^2+\rho^2)/2$.
Since $a_0-\varepsilon/2>0$, the sextic $\Psi$ and quartic $\chi$ terms
dominate the remaining lower-degree polynomials. This is equicoercivity
in the finite Fourier coordinate space, so sublevel sets are compact.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusion of a continuous collapse to zero.}
Let $M_\Psi=(2K+1)^3$, $M_\chi=(4K+1)^3$ and
\[
 c_0=\min(g,a_0),\quad A_K=2\varepsilon\sqrt{M_\chi/V},
 \quad B_K=|\lambda|M_\Psi/V,
 \quad \eta_K=\min\left\{\left(\frac{c_0}{4A_K}\right)^2,
                         \frac{c_0}{4B_K}\right\}>0.
\]
The exact Fourier bounds $\|f\|_\infty\le\sqrt{M/V}\|f\|_2$,
$\|\Psi\|_2^2\le2Q$ and $\|\chi\|_2^2\le Q$ imply
\[
 \Omega_\mu\ge c_0Q-A_KQ^{3/2}-B_KQ^2
       \ge\tfrac12c_0Q>0\quad(0<Q\le\eta_K).
\]
Here nonnegative sextic, reservoir quartic and Class-II terms were dropped,
not replaced. Thus a sequence of negative-energy minimizers cannot collapse
to zero, uniformly in $\mu$ on this interval, at this fixed $K$.

\paragraph{Threshold and jump.}
Let $m_K(\mu)=\min\Omega_\mu\le0$. Equicoercivity and continuity imply
continuity of $m_K$; it is nonincreasing since $Q\ge0$.
The small-charge exclusion and compactness also show a neighborhood above
$\mu_-$ still has no nonzero minimizer of nonpositive energy: otherwise
compact minimizers would converge to a nonzero zero-energy state at $\mu_-$.
Define $\mu_c(K)=\inf\{\mu\in[\mu_-,\mu_t]:m_K(\mu)<0\}$.
It lies strictly between $\mu_-$ and $\mu_t$.
Approach it from above with minimizing states. A compact subsequence converges
to a minimizer $z_c\ne0$, with $Q(z_c)\ge\eta_K$, and $m_K(\mu_c)=0$.
Zero and $z_c$ coexist, and $\Psi_c\ne0$ because pure reservoir states have
strictly positive energy here. Below $\mu_c$ any nonzero zero-energy minimizer would
give negative energy at a slightly larger multiplier below $\mu_c$, a
contradiction. Above $\mu_c$, the trial $z_c$ gives
$m_K(\mu)\le-(\mu-\mu_c)Q(z_c)<0$.
This is a finite-regulator variational first-order onset, with
\[
 0<\mu_t-\mu_c(K)<2D=\frac{2827}{589824}<0.004793.
\]
The window is common to finite $K$, but $\eta_K$ depends on $K$.
No uniform jump, cutoff limit, thermodynamic transition or continuum dynamics
has been obtained.

\section{Devil's-advocate review and verification}

declared convention by independent real Hamilton equations and a nonzero

exact inheritance. The old shell fails under a legal reservoir variation;
no external coherent reservoir or fitted $\mu(\tau)$ was used.
\item \textbf{A sign change alone proves first order.} Upheld unless compactness
and exclusion of small-charge minimizers are supplied. Both are proved above.

that interpretation. Generic nonequilibrium states exchange; static phases
need not carry net exchange. No trajectory crossing, relaxation, seed
generation or Gibbs equilibration is proved. Exact $\Psi=0$ remains invariant.
\item \textbf{Finite statements supply physical time or a limit.} Upheld
against promotion. $K$-dependent $\eta_K$, fixed spatial carrier and new
canonical time remain explicit. The pairing need not have local gauge or
full internal unitary invariance.
\end{enumerate}
