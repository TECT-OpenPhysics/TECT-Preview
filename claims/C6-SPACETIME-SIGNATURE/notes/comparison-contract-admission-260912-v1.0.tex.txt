\section*{2. Setting and precise finite-definition statement}
Fix integers $1\le q_0\le m_0$, $R_0>0$ and the parent's real parameters.
For $\rho=(r,h,N)$, the graph has side $W=2^{h+N+1}$, vertices in
$\{0,\ldots,W-1\}^2$, every horizontal and vertical nearest-neighbor edge,
and every unit square face. Stored positive edge orientations determine signs.
The anchors are $O=(0,0)$ and $C=(2^h,0)$; no fields are clamped.
Degree is at most four. These are combinatorial, not physical, coordinates.

Let $K_r$ be the product of the first $r+1$ primes and set
\[
 M_{s,r}=2^r,\quad M_{\psi,r}=m_0\,4^r,\quad
 R_{\max,r}=R_0\,2^r,\quad Q_r=q_0\,2^r .
\]
All cutoffs are unbounded and $Q_r\le M_{\psi,r}$. Every integer tuple
$(j,\ell,n,u)$ in the parent's ranges with total occupation $Q_r$ remains a
state, including hidden labels at $\ell=0$ and $\epsilon=1$.
Set $A_\rho=\mathbb C^{\Omega_\rho}$ and let $A_\rho^{\rm inv}$ be its full
gauge/anchor-cell invariant subalgebra.

\begin{theorem}
On this exact family, the approved maps
$p_{\sigma,\rho}:\Omega_\sigma\to\Omega_\rho$ are total, surjective and compose
for every $\rho\le\sigma$ in product order. Their pullbacks are unital
$*$-algebra embeddings preserving the full invariant subalgebras and inherited
supremum norm, with the declared finite support bound.
Directed-root assignments retain unpaired channels and are inverse-coherent
where paired.
\end{theorem}
This is not $L_\sigma I=I L_\rho$, an infinite-dynamics domain theorem, or state
convergence. The original local-cutoff, lattice, exhaustion, then inherited
later-limit order is retained; finite composition does not interchange limits.

\section*{3. Independent arbitrary-pair formula and proof}
For $\rho=(r,h,N)\le\sigma=(s,k,M)$ put
\[
 D=2^{s-r},\quad H=2^{k-h},\quad b=H^2,\quad
 P=K_s/K_r,\quad A=P^{-1}\pmod{K_r}.
\]
The primes in $P$ do not divide $K_r$, so the inverse exists.
In Morton order the representative of coarse $z$ is $bz$ and its descendants
are the contiguous block $bz,\ldots,b(z+1)-1$. A retained refined box is a
prefix of the fine box. Put $v=|V_\rho|$, $t=v-1$, and use exclusive prefixes
$S_a=\sum_{i<a}\ell_i$ of the fine tuple. Then
\[
 \ell'_z=\lfloor S_{b(z+1)}/D\rfloor-\lfloor S_{bz}/D\rfloor\ (z<t),
 \qquad \ell'_t=Q_r-\lfloor S_{bt}/D\rfloor ,
\]
\[
 j'_z=\lfloor j_{bz}/D\rfloor,\qquad n'_z=A n_{bz}\pmod{K_r},
 \qquad u'_e=A\sum_{a\in\gamma_H(e)}u_a\pmod{K_r}.
\]
Here $\gamma_H(e)$ is the signed axial path of length $H$ between representatives.
These are expressions for the approved compositions, not new definitions.

\paragraph{Totality and surjectivity.}
Prefix monotonicity gives nonnegative differences. Including the terminal
complement they sum to $Q_r$, so each is at most $Q_r\le M_{\psi,r}$.
Since $S_{bt}\le Q_s=DQ_r$, the terminal value is valid. All other ranges hold.
For any coarse tuple put $D j_z,D\ell_z,Pn_z$ at $bz$, zero at other fine
vertices, and $P u_e$ on the first edge of $\gamma_H(e)$, zero on its other
edges. Distinct coarse paths share no fine edge. This is valid because
$D M_{s,r}=M_{s,s}$, $D M_{\psi,r}\le M_{\psi,s}$, and $DQ_r=Q_s$.
Substitution gives $pJ={\rm id}$ on every coarse tuple. This injection does
not condition the actual fine ensemble or replace its Gibbs normalization.

\paragraph{Composition for every comparable triple.}
Every nonterminal output prefix is the corresponding fine prefix divided
by $D$ and floored; the complete output prefix is $Q_r$.
Grouping contiguous blocks telescopes differences. The identity
$\lfloor\lfloor a/d\rfloor/e\rfloor=\lfloor a/(de)\rfloor$ handles successive
cutoffs and cutoff/block squares. Terminal complements use the same total
and the same nonterminal prefix on either route, handling cutoff/exhaustion
and lattice/exhaustion squares. Representatives multiply their $H$ factors;
paths concatenate; modular multipliers become the inverse of the product
prime ratio modulo the final $K_r$. They commute with signed sums. Aperture
floors commute with sampling. This proves the arbitrary-pair formula, identity
and composition for all comparable triples, not just the executed sample cube.

\paragraph{Boundary transport is not surjective on faces.}
Each coarse face maps to four fine faces at an adjacent lattice step, with
internal signed edges cancelling. The fine graph also contains an outer
right/top fringe:
\[
 (2W-1)^2-4(W-1)^2=4W-3 .
\]
All these additional fine faces remain in the source functional and carrier.
No requirement that the cellular transport be onto faces is imposed.
Literal exhaustion keeps all old faces. The approved contract does not
identify a fixed physical-volume target; it is not pure subdivision of an
identical physical boundary.

\section*{4. Symmetry, algebra, support and roots}
Gauge transformation adds $g_v$ to $n_v$ and $g_w-g_v$ to $u_{(v,w)}$.
The coarse label is $A g_{bz}$ modulo $K_r$. Gauge increments telescope along
each path, giving $p(gx)=g'p(x)$ for every pair. This modular projection is an
additive group map, not generally a unital ring map or small-angle rounding.

The full anchored graph group on this family is trivial. Four degree-two
corners are intrinsic. Distances from $O$ identify the opposite corner; the
two adjacent corners have distinct distances $W-1-2^h$ and $W-1+2^h$ from $C$.
The pair $(x+y,W-1-x+y)$ then uniquely determines every vertex.
Thus the actual full cell group is used, not a conveniently chosen subgroup.
No all-carrier symmetry theorem follows.

For $If=f\circ p$, linearity, unit, products and conjugation follow by evaluation.
Gauge equivariance preserves all invariant observables. Surjectivity gives
\[
 \sup_{x\in\Omega_\sigma}|f(p x)|
 =\sup_{y\in\Omega_\rho}|f(y)|.
\]
The algebraic direct limit of the full invariant algebras has this
representative-independent norm. Formulas equal on $\Omega_\rho$ cannot be
distinguished by pullback. No Gibbs norm or reduced observable set is used.

If $f$ is represented at $(r,h,N)$ and the target is $(s,k,M)$, all output
coordinates depend only on vertices and internal edges of $G_{k,N}$.
The terminal coordinate is total $Q_s$ minus a retained prefix. This whole
refined source box is a computable support for all $M\ge N$.
Use $N_{\rm support}(f)=N$ at fixed target $k$. This need not be minimal support,
a uniform metric-radius bound, or a generator stabilization index.

The lesser endpoint/root key chooses one orientation of each fine incidence
and its inverse. Select the first original valid coarse label joining projected
endpoints, or UNPAIRED. Reversal chooses the same key and reverses that coarse
label. All fine labels, duplicate $K=2$ inverse labels and unmatched coarse
labels remain. This is bookkeeping, not a gauge-equivariant root-Hilbert
intertwiner or rate equality. The measure is still $\pi(x)$ per directed root,
with $\sqrt{c/2}$ in $B$.
