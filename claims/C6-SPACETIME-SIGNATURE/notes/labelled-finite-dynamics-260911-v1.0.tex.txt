\section*{2. Statement and setting}

Fix any admissible finite anchored oriented two-cell complex and any declared
finite regulator. Faces carry nonempty closed oriented boundary words, as
required for the displayed boundary average $J_p$ to be defined. Automorphisms
preserve the full cell/attaching and anchor data, not just graph adjacency.
Set $K\geq2$, $M_s,M_\psi\geq1$ integer and
$0\leq Q\leq |V|M_\psi$ integer. All real parameters have the source domain,
in particular $0<\epsilon\leq1$, $\beta,\nu>0$; $m^2,\lambda_4$ may be negative.
The state space $X$ consists of the distinct integer tuples
\[
 j_v\in\{0,\ldots,M_s\},\quad
 \ell_v\in\{0,\ldots,M_\psi\},\quad n_v,u_e\in\mathbb Z_K,
 \qquad \sum_v\ell_v=Q.
\]
Keep phase labels at $\ell_v=0$ and aperture indices at $\epsilon=1$.
Displayed fields are
\[
 s_v=\epsilon+\frac{j_v(1-\epsilon)}{M_s},\qquad
 \psi_v=\frac{R_{\max}\ell_v}{M_\psi}\zeta_K^{n_v},\qquad
 U_e=\zeta_K^{u_e}.
\]
The displayed finite functional $F$ is incorporated verbatim by the revision's
parent hash and pointer. No quotient of counting states is taken.

For each primitive cell/type/sign root retain the incidence $(x,r)$ precisely
when $rx\in X$. PH and LK add the sign modulo $K$ to $n_v$ and $u_e$;
AP adds the sign to $j_v$ without wrapping. TR along $e=(v,w)$ adds
$\sigma(\delta_w-\delta_v)$ to occupations and keeps all other coordinates.
Different roots remain distinct, including coincident signs at $K=2$.
Invalid moves are omitted, and identity rows are allowed.

Let $\mathcal R$ be the finite set of valid labelled incidences and write
\begin{align*}
 Z&=\sum_{x\in X}e^{-\beta F(x)},&
 \pi(x)&=Z^{-1}e^{-\beta F(x)},\\
 c_r(x)&=m_r(x)e^{-\beta(F(rx)-F(x))/2},&
 Lf(x)&=\sum_{r:(x,r)\in\mathcal R}c_r(x)(f(rx)-f(x)),\\
 Bf(x,r)&=\sqrt{c_r(x)/2}\,(f(rx)-f(x)).
\end{align*}
Use $D=\mathbb C^X=L^2(\pi)$ and the full root space
$\mathbb C^{\mathcal R}$ with weight $\pi(x)$ on every directed incidence.
Inner products are conjugate-linear in their first entries.

\begin{theorem}
For every such finite instance, all retained roots have valid inverses,
$L1=0$, and $\pi(x)c_r(x)=\pi(rx)c_{r^{-1}}(rx)$.
The declared gauge/anchor-automorphism group average $P_{\rm cand}$ is
self-adjoint and idempotent and satisfies $P_{\rm cand}L=LP_{\rm cand}$.
For the actual adjoint under the displayed pairings, $B^*B=-L$ on all $D$.
The simultaneous invariant finite subspace is preserved by $L$ and the
corresponding root lift. No cross-regulator common space is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\section*{3. Proof: inverse maps and Gibbs balance}

$X$ is finite and nonempty. Positive $s_v$ and nonempty finite face words
make $F(x)$ finite and real, even with negative mass/quartic coefficients.
Thus $0<Z<\infty$ and every $\pi(x)>0$. All displayed operators have full
finite domains; no extension or closability inference is needed.

PH/LK inverses subtract the same residue. AP reverses its integer increment.
TR subtracts its incidence vector and preserves $Q$; for a loop the vector is
zero. Since the original tuple lies in $X$, the reverse of any valid move is
valid. Retained phases prevent information loss at zero occupation. Hence
$\iota(x,r)=(rx,r^{-1})$ is an involutive bijection of $\mathcal R$.

The mobilities satisfy inverse symmetry by their exact definitions: PH uses
$s_v^\nu$; TR/LK use $(s_vs_w)^{\nu/2}$ and leave aperture unchanged; AP
uses the symmetric product of its before/after aperture values. Therefore,
with $y=rx$ and $m=m_r(x)=m_{r^{-1}}(y)$,
\[
 \pi(x)c_r(x)=\frac{m}{Z}
       e^{-\beta(F(x)+F(y))/2}=\pi(y)c_{r^{-1}}(y).
\]
Summing over all labels gives state-level detailed balance, without dividing
by multiplicity. Also $L1=0$ term by term. Bare root measure $\pi(x)$ need
not be preserved by $\iota$; only the conductance $\pi(x)c_r(x)$ is paired.

\section*{4. Proof: symmetry and projection}

The gauge action sends $n_v$ to $n_v+g_v$ and $u_{vw}$ to
$u_{vw}+g_w-g_v$. It is a bijection on tuples, not merely displayed values.
The edge term transforms by
\[
 \psi'_w-U'_{vw}\psi'_v
   =\zeta_K^{g_w}(\psi_w-U_{vw}\psi_v).
\]
Its absolute square is unchanged; onsite and aperture terms are invariant.
Gauge factors telescope on every closed face word, proving exact $F$ and
$\pi$ invariance. All four partial moves commute with this action; their
validity and mobility are unchanged. Thus their rates are equivariant too.

An anchor-preserving cell automorphism permutes vertex coordinates. If it
sends stored edge $e$ to $e'$ with orientation sign $\tau_e$, it sends
$u_e$ to $\tau_eu_e$ and TR/LK$(e,\sigma)$ to
TR/LK$(e',\tau_e\sigma)$. PH/AP signs do not change. This exactly
intertwines the partial moves and preserves their mobilities. Reversed edge
energy is unchanged because
\[
 |\psi_v-U_e^{-1}\psi_w|^2
   =|-U_e^{-1}(\psi_w-U_e\psi_v)|^2
   =|\psi_w-U_e\psi_v|^2.
\]
Reversal conjugates a unit face holonomy, preserving its real part; boundary
averages and multiplicities are permuted. Hence $F,\pi$ and rates are invariant.

For either group, the pullbacks $U_hf(x)=f(h^{-1}x)$ are unitary. Root
reindexing gives $U_hL=LU_h$. The finite average $P_H$ is self-adjoint by
inversion and idempotent because each group product occurs $|H|$ times.
Automorphisms normalize the gauge group by permuting its vertex coordinates;
therefore $P_{\rm Aut}P_G=P_GP_{\rm Aut}$. Individual gauge and automorphism
actions need not commute. The product $P_{\rm cand}$ is consequently an
orthogonal projection commuting with $L$, whose range is invariant.

\section*{5. Proof: the weighted adjoint}

Expand $\langle Bf,h\rangle_{\mathcal R}$ and collect each
$\overline{f(x)}$. Because all sums are finite and $\pi(x)>0$, the adjoint is
\begin{align*}
 B^*h(x)={}&\frac1{\pi(x)}\sum_{(y,r):ry=x}
        \pi(y)\sqrt{c_r(y)/2}\,h(y,r)\\
 &-\sum_{r:(x,r)\in\mathcal R}\sqrt{c_r(x)/2}\,h(x,r).
\end{align*}
Substitute $h=Bf$. The incoming part equals
$\tfrac12\sum_r c_r(x)(f(x)-f(rx))$ by the incidence bijection and detailed
balance. The outgoing part is the same expression, giving $B^*B=-L$.
Identity rows contribute zero. No extra rate or half weight is put in the
root measure. Equivalently, pairing directed incidences proves
\[
 \frac12\sum_{(x,r)}\pi(x)c_r(x)
    \overline{f(rx)-f(x)}(g(rx)-g(x))=-\langle f,Lg\rangle_\pi.
\]
This is the full sesquilinear identity, not a real-observable sample test.
The root symmetry pullback is unitary and intertwines $B$ with $U_h$.
Thus the identity also restricts to the candidate invariant spaces.

The finite balance/Dirichlet formalism is standard background: Aldous--Fill,
\emph{Reversible Markov Chains and Random Walks on Graphs}, section 3.1,
equation (3.4), and section 3.6.1, equations (3.74)--(3.76), author-hosted at
\url{https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/RWG/book.html}.
The identities required here are rederived above; irreducibility-dependent
uniqueness/mixing statements are not imported. R-479's separate successor
PASS is not reused as proof of this revision.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Factor/measure objection: two directions might double the form.
DISMISSED by the explicit two halves in the actual adjoint calculation.
Bare-root reversal invariance is UPHELD as a false shortcut and not used.
\item Domain objection: zero-radius or unit-floor values might identify
states and erase inverses. DISMISSED by retained integer labels. Empty face
words are a VALID domain warning: an undefined $J_p$ is not certified.
\item Symmetry objection: static invariance need not imply dynamic symmetry.
UPHELD in general; exact root/mobility equivariance is separately established.
Elementwise Aut--gauge commutation is not assumed.
\item Limit objection: finite identities might imply refinement or a limiting
semigroup. UPHELD as overclaim. No comparison maps, uniform estimates or
limits are supplied here.
\item Independence objection: computer PASS might replace a general proof or
external review. UPHELD as a scope warning. Universal quantifiers are carried
by the proof, and same-author executable independence is explicitly limited.
\end{enumerate}
