\section{Purpose and scope}
On the original side-16 periodic three-torus use the unchanged full
EXCH-001 Hamiltonian, exact rectangular projections $P_K,P_{2K}$,
and its dimensionless canonical time $\tau$. Let the common initial
order and reservoir fields be $\Psi_0\in H^2(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb C^3)$
and $\chi_0\in L^\infty(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb C)$. For every integer
$K\ge1$, use exactly $(P_K\Psi_0,P_{2K}\chi_0)$, without a rescaling,
and denote the original trajectory by $(\Psi_K,\chi_K)$. Then
\[
 \lim_{R\to\infty}\sup_{K\ge1}\sup_{|\tau|\le T}
 \|(1-P_R)\chi_K(\tau)\|_2=0\qquad(0<T<\infty).
\]
In fact the reservoirs form a relatively compact family in
$C([-T,T];L^4)$, with normalized measure throughout. No tightness
assumption along the finite trajectories is used. The class includes
all common finite Fourier data, in particular the unmodified low-energy
R-579 plane. For general data the projected charges are bounded but
need not equal one another; no exact-sector renormalization is made.

Write $\rho=|\Psi|^2$, $S=\Psi^T\Psi$, and let
$b(\Psi)=\langle\Psi,(L-\lambda_0)\Psi\rangle$ denote the normalized
real spectral form in R-158; $\lambda_0$ is its spectral bottom
before subtraction. The R-158 identity has nonnegative Class-II
energy and sextic remainder $c\langle\rho(\rho-\rho_*)^2\rangle$
with $c=\gamma/6$. Since $|S|\le\rho$,
\[
 \epsilon|\chi|\rho\le \tfrac14|\chi|^2+\epsilon^2\rho^2,\qquad
 c\rho(\rho-\rho_*)^2-\epsilon^2\rho^2\ge-D\rho,\quad
 D=\epsilon^2\rho_*+\frac{\epsilon^4}{4c}.
\]
Thus $H/V\ge \frac12b(\Psi)+(\mu_t/2-D)\|\Psi\|_2^2
+\frac14\|\chi\|_2^2+\frac14\|\chi\|_4^4$ for the pinned
$\omega=u=1$. Completing the square in
$Y|k|^4+Z|k|^2+r$ gives
$b(\Psi)\ge(Y/2)\|\Delta\Psi\|_2^2-C\|\Psi\|_2^2$.
R-578 conserves $H$ and $Q/V=\|\Psi\|_2^2/2+\|\chi\|_2^2$
and supplies the complete finite flow.
The separate static $H^2$ continuity of P1 follows from the R-158
formula: the derivative terms of Class II are quadratic in first
derivatives with smooth coefficient matrices on bounded amplitudes
and strictly positive denominator floors. Thus convergence in $H^2$
implies convergence of these terms using $H^2\hookrightarrow L^\infty$.

\section{Uniform forcing and a bounded comparison field}
Here is an elementary projection justification at the only non-Hilbert
exponent needed. For a real trigonometric polynomial $a$ on the circle,
let $h$ be its conjugate polynomial (multiplier $-i\,\mathrm{sgn}(n)$).
Then $a+ih$ has no negative frequencies. Taking the real part of
the mean of its fourth power and using Jensen,
\[
 \langle a^4-6a^2 h^2+h^4\rangle=\langle a\rangle^4
 \le \langle a^4\rangle,\qquad
 \|h\|_4^4\le 6\|a\|_4^2\|h\|_4^2 .
\]
Split into real and imaginary parts for complex data. By modulation
and differences of half-line projections this bounds interval
projections uniformly on $L^4$; apply each one-dimensional projection
in turn by Fubini for boxes. Polynomial density then gives strong $L^4$
convergence of $P_N$. No $L^\infty$ sharp-projection bound is used.
Together with $H^2$ projection convergence for $\Psi_0$, this gives
uniform initial $H,Q$. The preceding coercivity argument gives
\[
 \sup_{K,\tau}\bigl(\|\Psi_K(\tau)\|_{H^2}+\|\chi_K(\tau)\|_4\bigr)<\infty.
\]
In particular $G_K=\epsilon S_K$ is uniformly bounded in $H^2$
and $L^\infty$ by the $H^2$ algebra on the fixed torus.

Put $Jz=-iz$ and $f(z)=\omega z+u|z|^2z$.
The exact first variation gives
$\dot\chi_K=JP_{2K}(f(\chi_K)-G_K)$.
Define a comparison field $w_K$ by the \emph{pointwise} equation
$\dot w_K=J(f(w_K)-G_K)$, $w_K(0)=\chi_0$, for the same $G_K$.
This is not substituted into the model. The radial terms rotate only,
so $|w_K(\tau,x)|\le|\chi_0(x)|+\int_{\min(0,\tau)}^{\max(0,\tau)}
 |G_K(v,x)|\,dv$. Thus $w_K,\dot w_K$ are uniformly bounded in
$L^\infty$ on each finite window; the pointwise solutions exist there.

For spatial translations $\theta_h$, the forcing satisfies
$\sup_{K,\tau}\|\theta_h G_K-G_K\|_4\to0$ by the uniform $L^\infty$
and $H^1$ bounds (interpolate $L^2$ and $L^\infty$).
On the bounded range of $w_K$, $f$ is pointwise Lipschitz.
Subtract the translated pointwise ODEs and use Gronwall;
the resulting $L^4$ modulus comes from the preceding forcing modulus
and $\|\theta_h\chi_0-\chi_0\|_4\to0$. It is uniform in $K$
and $|\tau|\le T$. The fields $f(w_K)-G_K$ have the same
uniform translation property.

\section{Projected consistency and relative-energy comparison}
Let $A_N$ be the tensor product of the one-dimensional Fejer averages
through degree $N$, and $W_K=A_{2K}w_K$.
Positive normalized kernels give $\|W_K\|_\infty\le\|w_K\|_\infty$
and the same inequality for their time derivatives. The range
of $A_N$ is in $P_N$, and the uniform translation moduli imply
uniform $L^4$ approximation by $A_N$ (integrate the translation
modulus against the approximate identity). In particular
\[
\begin{split}
 e_K&=\dot W_K-JP_{2K}(f(W_K)-G_K)\\
 &=JP_{2K}\big[(A_{2K}-I)(f(w_K)-G_K)+f(w_K)-f(W_K)\big],
 \qquad \sup_{|\tau|\le T}\|e_K\|_4\longrightarrow0 .
\end{split}
\]
The residual estimate uses uniform $L^4$ boundedness of $P_{2K}$
and local Lipschitz continuity on the bounded \emph{comparison} range,
not on a hypothesized bounded range for $\chi_K$.

Use real Euclidean pairing and potential $U(z)=\omega|z|^2/2+
u|z|^4/4$, gradient $f$. With $d=\chi_K-W_K$, let $\mathcal B_K$
be the spatial mean of $U(W_K+d)-U(W_K)-f(W_K)\cdot d$.
The quartic Taylor remainder is
$2|W_K|^2|d|^2+(2W_K\cdot d+|d|^2)^2\ge |d|^4/3$;
the inequality is a square completion using
$(W_K\cdot d)^2\le |W_K|^2|d|^2$. Consequently
\[
 \mathcal B_K\ge \tfrac{\omega}{2}\|d\|_2^2
                      +\tfrac{u}{12}\|d\|_4^4 .
\]
Differentiating in the original finite carrier and pairing there,
the term $\langle f(\chi_K)-f(W_K),JP_{2K}[f(\chi_K)-f(W_K)]\rangle$
is exactly zero by orthogonality and skewness. Therefore
\[
 \dot{\mathcal B}_K =
 \langle f(\chi_K)-f(W_K)-Df(W_K)d,\dot W_K\rangle
 -\langle f(\chi_K)-f(W_K),e_K\rangle .
\]
The first term is bounded in absolute value by
$C_T\langle |d|^2+|d|^3\rangle\le C'_T\mathcal B_K$.
The second is bounded by $C_T\|e_K\|_4$ since the original
$\chi_K$ are uniformly $L^4$ and the comparison field is bounded.
$P_{2K}\chi_0-A_{2K}\chi_0\to0$ in $L^4$, so
$\mathcal B_K(0)\to0$. Two-sided Gronwall on the finite window gives
$\sup_{|\tau|\le T}\mathcal B_K(\tau)\to0$, hence
$\sup_{|\tau|\le T}\|\chi_K-w_K\|_4\to0$.

\section{Devil's-advocate review and compactness}
The comparison states are uniformly approximable in $L^4$ by
fixed finite-degree Fejer polynomials by the translation estimate.
Their time derivatives are uniformly $L^\infty$ on the window.
Arzela--Ascoli gives relative compactness of $w_K$ in $C_\tau L^4$.
The vanishing $C_\tau L^4$ discrepancy transfers this to $\chi_K$:
bounded $K$ give finitely many trajectories, and unbounded $K$ can
be taken to infinity on a subsequence. Alternatively use the uniform
auxiliary translation modulus to bound $L^2$ tails, then bound the
high-$K$ difference and take $R$ above all the remaining finite carriers.

\paragraph{Adversarial review.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Pointwise amplitude estimates would be false as a derivation
  for the projected nonlinear flow; the pointwise equation is only a
  comparison. The skew cancellation is in the exact original carrier.
\item Charged Fourier projections need not conserve the charge of
  the common initial field at time zero. Only a uniform initial bound
  and the actual conservation in each carrier are used.
\item EXCH-008 has persistent high-frequency mass at time zero and
  is not such a common projected preparation. The failure of recovery
  on the whole R-583 sublevel is not reversed.
\item A general unbounded $L^4$ initial field is not covered:
  the comparison Lipschitz and $\dot W_K$ bounds depend on its
  essential supremum. Truncating amplitudes and sending the truncation
  to infinity is not justified by these constants.
\end{itemize}
Two independent routes check bounded data: the moving Fejer
comparison above and first smoothing the common datum at fixed
degree, projecting the resulting $H^2$ comparison, then removing
the smoothing with a relative-energy constant controlled by the
common essential supremum. Read-only hostile review also rejects
an $L^2$-Lipschitz shortcut using only the reservoir $L^4$ energy.
The runnable audit checks the core identities, signs and hashes;
it is not a formalization of the functional-analytic proof.
