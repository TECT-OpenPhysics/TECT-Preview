\section{Purpose and scope}

This checkpoint records \texttt{EXP-000803} and advances reusable result
\texttt{R-167} to v1.6 without allocating a new result number.  The stable
result identifier remains
\url{PA-CP1-ST8-Q3LOCK-SECOND-WEIGHTED-ENERGY-MOMENT-AND-COMMON-ALPHA-CAUCHY-GATE-SPLIT}.
The v1.0--v1.5 weighted-energy, graph, topology, canonical fixed-temperature
OS-mixture and selected-tangent results remain in force with their registered
boundaries.

There are two deliberately separate positive conclusions.  First, an exact
KMS modular right-context estimate and a recursive right-to-left Fejer
smoothing argument upgrade the selected \texttt{EXP-000781} tangent-net
theorem from one cyclic character to every fixed finite raw
configuration-orbit word moment and Gram block.  The conclusion is pointed
finite-core Fell/GNS convergence only.

Second, the complete family of exact zero-source finite periodic Hamiltonians
defines a beta- and state-independent universal $L^1$ orbit-smear C-star
carrier with one point-norm continuous time shift.  The approximate broken
doublets from \texttt{EXP-000789} have two distinct weak-star cluster states
on this carrier, and the negative-Arveson estimate proves that both are ground
states for its common shift.

The second construction is categorical and nonlocal.  It is not a
quasi-local thermodynamic limit: its finite-volume quotient maps are not an
inductive family, raw configuration characters need not belong to it, and
temporal smears need not be spatially local.  No all-exhaustion Cauchy,
canonical momentum/full CCR carrier, polynomial local generator, phase-KMS
quotient identification, broken-sector GNS gap, physical mass gap, continuum,
physical empty-space comparison, C6, CP1, Sector A or Pre-A closure is claimed.

\section{Selected-tangent finite raw-word completion}

Fix $\beta>0$ and the already registered plus/minus source tangent diagonal
net of \texttt{EXP-000781}.  The v1.5 theorem gives convergence of every fixed
finite bandlimited word Gram block and, for each rational finite-support
configuration label $\xi$, the cyclic Fejer error

\[
 \epsilon_\xi(R)
 = {\|\xi\|\over\sqrt{\beta\chi}}
   \left({2\over R^2}+{\beta\hbar\over R}\right)^{1/2}.       \tag{3.1}
\]

Use the support-reduced faithful KMS standard form, with the convention
$\sigma_s=\alpha_{-\beta\hbar s}$.  If $C$ is entire analytic and $Y$ is a
bounded left factor, Tomita's right-action identity gives

\[
 YC\Omega=J\sigma_{-i/2}(C^*)JY\Omega.                       \tag{3.2}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \|YC\Omega\|
 \le \|\sigma_{i/2}(C)\|\,\|Y\Omega\|.                     \tag{3.3}
\]

If the physical Arveson spectrum of $C$ lies in $[-S,S]$, the standard strip
estimate yields

\[
 \|\sigma_{i/2}(C)\|
 \le e^{\beta\hbar S/2}\|C\|.                              \tag{3.4}
\]

For a fixed word $A_1\cdots A_m$, let $C_j$ be a Fejer-smoothed version of
$A_j$ with bandwidth $R_j$, and put $Y_j=A_j-C_j$.  Replace factors from right
to left.  When the already smoothed right context has bandwidth

\[
 S_j=\sum_{k>j}R_k,
\]

the $j$-th telescoping error is bounded by

\[
 e^{\beta\hbar S_j/2}\epsilon_{\xi_j}(R_j).                 \tag{3.5}
\]

Choose $R_m$ first.  Once it is fixed, choose $R_{m-1}$ large enough for its
finite right-context cost; continue recursively through $R_1$.  Every finite
sum of errors in (3.5) can therefore be made arbitrarily small.  Combining
this with the fixed-band theorem proves convergence of every fixed finite raw
configuration-orbit word moment and Gram entry on the selected tangent net.
Independent limiting pivots give pointed finite-core Fell/GNS convergence on
the algebraic raw orbit-word span, equivalently an identification of its
cyclic subspace in a GNS ultraproduct.

The bandwidths may grow very rapidly toward the left.  This proof is not
uniform in word length and does not give an arbitrary bounded-context
multiplier theorem, common-Hilbert operator strong-star convergence or
all-exhaustion uniqueness.

\section{Universal zero-source orbit-smear carrier}

Let $\Xi_{\mathbb Q}$ be the finite-support rational configuration labels on
$\mathbb Z^3\times\{1,\ldots,8\}$.  For every finite periodic volume
$\Lambda$, periodize $\xi$ and set

\[
 W_{\Lambda,\xi}=\exp(i\xi\mathbin\cdot q),
 \qquad
 \alpha_t^\Lambda(A)=e^{itH_\Lambda(0)/\hbar}A
 e^{-itH_\Lambda(0)/\hbar}.                                \tag{4.1}
\]

Only the exact zero-source Hamiltonians $H_\Lambda(0)$ enter this
construction.  For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ introduce a symbol
$A_{\xi,f}$, linear in $f$, with

\[
 A_{\xi,f}^*=A_{-\xi,\bar f},
 \qquad
 \pi_\Lambda(A_{\xi,f})=
 \int_{\mathbb R}f(t)\alpha_t^\Lambda(W_{\Lambda,\xi})\,dt. \tag{4.2}
\]

The integral is a weak-operator integral and satisfies the volume-uniform
bound

\[
 \|\pi_\Lambda(A_{\xi,f})\|\le\|f\|_1.                    \tag{4.3}
\]

Take the supremum of all finite-volume representation norms on the unital
formal star algebra, quotient the common kernel, and complete.  Denote the
result by $\mathcal A_H^0$.  The product of all $\pi_\Lambda$ is faithful,
although no individual quotient representation need be faithful.

Define

\[
 \theta_s A_{\xi,f}=A_{\xi,\tau_s f},
 \qquad (\tau_sf)(t)=f(t-s).                                \tag{4.4}
\]

Finite-volume equivariance makes $\theta_s$ isometric.  The $L^1$
translation theorem gives point-norm continuity, so
$(\mathcal A_H^0,\theta)$ is a beta- and state-independent C-star dynamical
system.  On the smooth smear core its generator is exactly

\[
 \delta_H A_{\xi,f}=-A_{\xi,f'}.                            \tag{4.5}
\]

Every finite-volume Gibbs state pulled back through $\pi_\Lambda$ is a
$\beta$-KMS state of $\theta$, and fixed-$\beta$ weak-star accumulation
points remain KMS.  This is a common categorical carrier theorem, not an
identification of the finite Hamiltonian quotients with one spatial
thermodynamic limit or with the phase envelopes of \texttt{EXP-000800}.

\section{Two distinct ground-state clusters}

Use the zero-source periodic doublets $\Psi_L^\pm$ of \texttt{EXP-000789}.
Write

\[
 K_L=H_L(0)-E_{0,L}\ge0,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_L=\langle\Psi_L^\pm,K_L\Psi_L^\pm\rangle.     \tag{5.1}
\]

If $V=L^3$ and $m_L^2=\langle S_L^2\rangle/V^2$, then eventually
$m_L\ge m_0=\sqrt{\rho_*/2}$ and

\[
 \varepsilon_L
 \le {\hbar^2\over4\chi V m_L^2}
 \le {\hbar^2\over2\chi V\rho_*}.                         \tag{5.2}
\]

At one fixed site let

\[
 X=\sum_{e=1}^8q_e=\sqrt8\,Q,
 \qquad
 M_3=(64M_{4,Q})^{3/4},                                    \tag{5.3}
\]

where the local fourth moment is uniformly bounded on the doublets.  Choose
one fixed rational $r>0$ such that

\[
 r^2M_3\le3\sqrt8\,m_0,
 \qquad \xi=r(1,\ldots,1).                                 \tag{5.4}
\]

The rational label is essential; using the normalized diagonal unit vector
would introduce irrational entries.  From $|\sin z-z|\le|z|^3/6$ and parity,

\[
 \omega_L^+(\sin rX)\ge d,
 \qquad
 \omega_L^-(\sin rX)\le-d,
 \qquad d={r\sqrt8\,m_0\over2}>0                           \tag{5.5}
\]

for all sufficiently large $L$.

Fix $T>0$ and the triangular function

\[
 f_T(t)=T^{-1}(1-|t|/T)_+,
 \qquad
 \int f_T=1,
 \qquad
 \int |t|^{1/2}f_T(t)\,dt={8\sqrt T\over15}.               \tag{5.6}
\]

For every contraction $B$, the near-ground energy estimate gives

\[
 |\omega_L^\pm(\alpha_t^L(B))-\omega_L^\pm(B)|
 \le2\sqrt{{2|t|\varepsilon_L\over\hbar}}.                \tag{5.7}
\]

The single self-adjoint abstract element

\[
 b={A_{\xi,f_T}-A_{-\xi,f_T}\over2i}\in\mathcal A_H^0      \tag{5.8}
\]

therefore obeys the smear error bound

\[
 {16\over15}\sqrt{{2T\varepsilon_L\over\hbar}}
 \le {16\over15}\sqrt{{\hbar T\over\chi V\rho_*}}
 \longrightarrow0.                                         \tag{5.9}
\]

Thus every joint weak-star cluster pair satisfies
$\omega^+(b)\ge d/2$ and $\omega^-(b)\le-d/2$ and is distinct.

For the Fourier convention

\[
 \widehat f(\nu)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{+i\nu t}f(t)\,dt,       \tag{5.10}
\]

let $a\in\mathcal A_H^0$ have $\theta$-Arveson spectrum in
$(-\infty,-\nu]$, with $\nu>0$ in inverse-time units.  Finite-volume
equivariance gives

\[
 \pi_L(a)P_{[0,\hbar\nu)}(K_L)=0,
 \qquad
 \omega_L^\pm(a^*a)
 \le {\|a\|_H^2\varepsilon_L\over\hbar\nu}\longrightarrow0. \tag{5.11}
\]

Every cluster state is therefore a ground state of $(\mathcal A_H^0,\theta)$.
This proves algebraic ground states only on the universal orbit-smear carrier;
it proves neither a quasi-local oscillator ground representation nor a
positive GNS or physical mass gap.

\section{Why the carrier does not close common alpha}

The exact finite-dimensional pair

\[
 (\beta_1,H_1)=(1,-\sigma_x),
 \qquad
 (\beta_2,H_2)=(2,-2\sigma_x)                              \tag{6.1}
\]

with common configuration label $\sigma_z$ has orbit frequencies $2$ and
$4$.  Its $L^1$ orbit smears generate $M_2\oplus M_2$ and carry one abstract
C-zero shift with both KMS pullbacks, while the labelled Hamiltonian
generators differ by more than a scalar.  Hence a universal orbit-smear
carrier can package inequivalent quotient dynamics.  Carrier existence does
not imply a quasi-local thermodynamic Hamiltonian limit, exhaustion uniqueness
or a canonical raw local generator.  This preserves, rather than overturns,
the registered posthoc-direct-sum and automatic-cross-beta-gluing warnings.

\section{Projected-tail corridor audit}

For a degree-$z$ graph with bounded pair interactions
$\|\Phi_e^L\|\le J_L$, a conservative pairwise-union cutoff estimate is

\[
 8\sqrt2\,|X|\|A\|e^{\nu_LT}{(\nu_LT)^R\over R!},
 \qquad \nu_L={4zJ_L\over\hbar}.                            \tag{7.1}
\]

For the coordinate cutoff $J_L\le4cL^2$ and $z=6$, so
$\nu_L\le96cL^2/\hbar$.  Taking $L=R^\alpha$ makes (7.1) vanish on every
compact real-time interval for $0<\alpha<1/2$.  This is a theorem about the
bounded cutoff dynamics only.

Static tail estimates do not remove the cutoff.  An exact four-dimensional
fixture uses

\[
 r_n=(2n+1)\pi,
 \quad \epsilon_n=e^{-r_n^4},
 \quad Z_n=1+3\epsilon_n,
 \quad \rho_n=Z_n^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(1,\epsilon_n,\epsilon_n,\epsilon_n), \tag{7.2}
\]

and

\[
 q_x=r_n\operatorname{diag}(0,0,1,1),\quad
 q_y=r_n\operatorname{diag}(0,1,0,1),\quad
 W=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1,-1),\quad X=q_xq_y.            \tag{7.3}
\]

With $k=\pi\hbar/(4T)$, let $K_{03}=-ik$, $K_{30}=ik$ and all other entries
zero; put $H=K+X$.  Direct calculation gives

\[
 \|X\|_D^2={r_n^4\epsilon_n\over Z_n}\to0,
 \qquad [\log\rho_n,X]=0,                                  \tag{7.4}
\]

but, for $B=\alpha_T^K(W)$,

\[
 \|[X,B]\|_D^2={2(1-\epsilon_n)\over Z_n}\to2,             \tag{7.5}
\]

and the full-versus-cutoff orbit two-sided distance tends to $2$.  The
coordinate Gaussian moments are bounded for every fixed coefficient.  Thus
static Gaussian tails and a vanishing first modular derivative do not imply
projected real-time orbit locality.

This fixture is not a Q3LOCK counterexample: $\rho_n$ is not asserted to be
Gibbs invariant for $H$ or $K$.  It rejects only that proof inference.  A
connected two-orientation dynamic tail or quasi-invariance estimate is still
required.

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The modular sign in (3.2) was checked in a finite-density standard-form
fixture.  The right-context cost uses $\sigma_{i/2}(C)$ and the physical
bandwidth factor $e^{\beta\hbar S/2}$.
\item The Fejer replacements are ordered from right to left.  One common
bandwidth does not close the exponentially weighted estimate; the recursive
finite-word choice does.
\item The universal norm is the supremum of exact finite-volume representation
norms and is finite by (4.3).  Its existence is not confused with inductive
compatibility or spatial locality.
\item The zero-source restriction is explicit.  Including source-dependent
Hamiltonians in the same categorical family would merely package different
generators and would not prove source-independent dynamics.
\item The ground-state Fourier sign and energy denominator are fixed by
\texttt{(5.10)}: negative transfer frequency costs the positive energy
$\hbar\nu$ in (5.11).
\item The order witness uses the rational vector $r(1,\ldots,1)$, not the
irrational normalized diagonal vector.  The constants $8/15$ and $16/15$
are independently recomputed.
\item The four-dimensional static-tail fixture rejects one implication only.
It is not a Gibbs Q3LOCK non-Cauchy example and does not prove nonexistence of
the sought dynamics.
\item Neither pointed finite-core Fell/GNS convergence nor the categorical
carrier is promoted to common-Hilbert operator strong-star, all-exhaustion,
quasi-local, momentum, GNS-gap or physical conclusions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Route decision}

Three scoped subgates are closed: selected-tangent fixed finite raw-word
moments, the zero-source universal finite-Hamiltonian orbit-smear carrier, and
two distinct algebraic ground states on that carrier.  The projected corridor
still lacks a dynamic tilted-tail estimate.

The live successor is
\url{PA-CP1-ST8-Q3LOCK-QUASI-LOCAL-RAW-OSCILLATOR-ALL-EXHAUSTION-COMMON-ALPHA-AND-BROKEN-GNS-GAP}.
It must construct a noncollapsing raw or resolvent oscillator carrier with a
spatial local net, prove pairwise all-shape exhaustion Cauchy, identify the
finite Hamiltonian generator and phase-KMS quotient representations, and only
then determine the broken-sector GNS gap.  Until those obligations close, the
present ground states are ground states of the categorical carrier only.
