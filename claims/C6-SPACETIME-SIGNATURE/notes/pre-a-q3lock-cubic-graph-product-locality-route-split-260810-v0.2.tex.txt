\section{Purpose and scope}

This note records \texttt{EXP-000796} and strengthens reusable result
\texttt{R-167} to v1.1 without allocating a new result number.  It closes the
weighted cubic graph-domain obligation left open by \texttt{EXP-000795},
computes the exact heat-simplex coefficient for every prescribed interaction
word, and then tests whether this is enough for common real-time dynamics.

It is not enough.  Two termwise-absolute organizations fail exactly: raw
connected histories have factorial multiplicity, and absolute complex-time
continuation has a nonintegrable $e^{C/\varepsilon}$ boundary.  These are
proof-route no-go results, not nonexistence of the dynamics.  The surviving
phase-independent target is a first-passage backbone response theorem which
resums branches and repeated edges before taking norms.  A separate
fixed-temperature route requires a uniform fifth onsite-energy moment plus a
genuinely two-sided modular or dual-state cutoff estimate.

No common thermodynamic automorphism, common-alpha KMS identification,
algebraic ground states, GNS or physical mass gap, continuum removal, physical
empty-space comparison, C6, CP1, Sector A or Pre-A closure is claimed.  The
spatial commutator hierarchy also remains open.

\section{Exact weighted cubic graph theorem}

For the centered weight $f_x=e^{-\mu d(x,X)}$, write

\[
 A=1+E_f=1+T_f+U_f,
 \qquad U_f\ge\gamma f_x|q_x|^4,                              \tag{3.1}
\]

where $0<\gamma<g/32$ and

\[
 S_f\le |X|\left(\frac{1+e^{-\mu}}{1-e^{-\mu}}\right)^3.    \tag{3.2}
\]

The exact R-167 Laplacian input is

\[
 L_f\le C_2\varepsilon U_f+S_f
 \left(8r_++8cz+\frac{C_2}{4\varepsilon\gamma}\right),
 \qquad C_2=3g+21\lambda.                                    \tag{3.3}
\]

On the finite-volume Schwartz core, the exact Schrodinger cross identity
expands the full graph norm as

\[
\begin{split}
 \|A\psi\|^2={}&\|T_f\psi\|^2+\|U_f\psi\|^2+\|\psi\|^2
 +2\langle T_f\rangle+2\langle U_f\rangle\\
 &+\frac{D_f}{\chi}-\frac{\hbar^2}{2\chi}\langle L_f\rangle.
\end{split}\tag{3.4}
\]

Choose

\[
 \varepsilon_*=\frac{4\chi}{\hbar^2C_2},
 \quad
 b_*=S_f\left(8r_++8cz+\frac{\hbar^2C_2^2}{16\chi\gamma}\right),
 \quad
 \beta_*=\frac{\hbar^2b_*}{2\chi}.                          \tag{3.5}
\]

The negative $U_f$ coefficient in (3.4) then cancels exactly.  Since $A\ge1$,

\[
 \|U_f\psi\|^2\le\max(1,\beta_*)\|A\psi\|^2,
 \qquad
 \|U_fA^{-1}\|\le\kappa:=\sqrt{\max(1,\beta_*)}.           \tag{3.6}
\]

The finite polynomial Schrodinger operator has the stated Schwartz core as
an operator core.  Heinz--Kato graph interpolation therefore gives

\[
 \|U_f^\theta A^{-\theta}\|\le\kappa^\theta,
 \qquad0\le\theta\le1.                                      \tag{3.7}
\]

Because $U_f$ and $q_x$ commute as multiplication operators,

\[
 \boxed{
 f_x^{m/4}\bigl\||q_x|^mA^{-m/4}\bigr\|
 \le\gamma^{-m/4}\kappa^{m/4},\qquad0\le m\le4.}          \tag{3.8}
\]

The adjoint orientation has the same bound.  In particular,

\[
 f_x^{3/4}\bigl\||q_x|^3A^{-3/4}\bigr\|
 \le\gamma^{-3/4}\max(1,\beta_*)^{3/8}.                    \tag{3.9}
\]

For each Q3 component,

\[
 |\partial_eW_4(q)|\le(g+12\lambda)|q|^3,                   \tag{3.10}
\]

so (3.9) also closes the cubic onsite force.  The full eight-component
gradient costs an additional factor $\sqrt8$.

\section{Sharp spatial boundary and moving centers}

A compact product bump translated by amplitude $R$ at site $x$ satisfies

\[
 \|q_{x,e}^3\psi_{x,R}\|\ge(R-1)^3,
 \qquad
 \|A\psi_{x,R}\|\le C_0+C_1f_xR^4.                         \tag{4.1}
\]

Hence spectral interpolation forces every unweighted moving-site constant to
obey

\[
 C_x\ge C_1^{-3/4}f_x^{-3/4}.                               \tag{4.2}
\]

This rejects support-location-uniform unweighted bounds, not boundedness at a
fixed $x$.

The energy can instead be recentered.  For neighboring centers $x,y$, let
$\vartheta=e^\mu-1$.  The weighted kinetic terms commute, the potentials are
nonnegative, and (3.6) gives

\[
 \|(U_x-U_y)A_y^{-1}\|\le\vartheta\kappa,
 \qquad
 \|(T_x-T_y)A_y^{-1}\|\le\vartheta(2+\kappa).               \tag{4.3}
\]

Thus, in both orientations,

\[
 \|A_xA_y^{-1}\|\le C_\mu:=1+2(e^\mu-1)(1+\kappa),
 \qquad
 \|A_x^sA_y^{-s}\|\le C_\mu^s,\qquad0\le s\le1.           \tag{4.4}
\]

A fixed graph exponent therefore pays only $C_\mu^{sn}$ along an $n$-step
backbone.

\section{Prescribed-word heat-simplex theorem}

Let $K\ge1$ and suppose every bond obeys

\[
 \|V_eK^{-1/2}\|,\ \|K^{-1/2}V_e\|\le b.                  \tag{5.1}
\]

Using $\|K^{1/2}e^{-sK}\|\le(2es)^{-1/2}$ and the exact Dirichlet integral,

\[
 \int_{\sum s_j=\beta}\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}s_j^{-1/2}\,ds
 =\frac{\pi^{n/2}\beta^{n/2}}{\Gamma(1+n/2)},               \tag{5.2}
\]

one obtains for every prescribed edge word

\[
 \boxed{
 \|\mathcal I_{{\bf e},n}(\beta)\|
 \le\frac{[b\sqrt{\pi/(2e)}\sqrt\beta]^n}{\Gamma(1+n/2)}.}\tag{5.3}
\]

For $V_{xy}=-c q_x\mathbin\cdot q_y$, quartic coercivity gives

\[
 b=\frac{c}{\sqrt{2\gamma}},
 \qquad
 \kappa_0=c\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{e\gamma}}                     \tag{5.4}
\]

after the two commutator orientations are summed.

\section{Why raw absolute histories fail}

Take a length-$m$ backbone and attach four distinct transverse leaves at each
of $m$ selected vertices.  This $5m$-edge animal admits at least $(4m)!$
connected growth histories.  Multiplying the history count by the uniform
per-word bound (5.3) gives

\[
 M_m=\frac{(4m)!a^{5m}}{\Gamma(1+5m/2)},
 \qquad
 \log M_m=\frac32m\log m+O(m).                               \tag{6.1}
\]

The raw absolute majorant has zero radius.  This does not prove that the exact
series or dynamics diverges; it proves that branches and repeated edges must
be resummed before taking norms.

Even granting a reduction to edge chains, the absolute complex-time estimate
at $\zeta=\varepsilon+it/\hbar$ contains

\[
 \exp\left[-\rho d+
 P^2\kappa_0^2e^{2\rho}\frac{|\zeta|^2}{\varepsilon}\right],
 \qquad P=2z-1=11.                                           \tag{6.2}
\]

For $t\ne0$, every finite Balakrishnan power encounters

\[
 \int_0^1\varepsilon^{s-1}e^{C/\varepsilon}\,d\varepsilon
 =\infty.                                                     \tag{6.3}
\]

Thus absolute strip continuation also fails as a proof method.  Oscillatory
or unitary cancellation remains open.

\section{Exact surviving real-time target}

Let $\mathcal R_{\pi,n}^{(j)}$ sum every branch and repeated-edge history with
one first-passage backbone $\pi$, before taking a norm.  With
$s_1=1/2,s_2=3/4$, the load-bearing target is

\[
 \int_{\Delta_n(t)}\left(
 \|\mathcal R_{\pi,n}^{(j)}A^{-s_j}\|
 +\|A^{-s_j}\mathcal R_{\pi,n}^{(j)}\|\right)d{\bf t}
 \le C_j\frac{[G_j\sqrt{|t|/\hbar}]^n}{\Gamma(1+n/2)}.      \tag{7.1}
\]

It would imply

\[
 \mathcal C_j(d,t)\le\frac{2C_jz|X|}{P}
 \exp\left[-\rho d+\frac{P^2G_j^2e^{2\rho}}{\hbar}|t|\right],\tag{7.2}
\]

with velocity

\[
 v_{\rho,j}=\frac{P^2G_j^2e^{2\rho}}{\rho\hbar}.            \tag{7.3}
\]

The moving-center factor from (4.4) is absorbed into $G_j$.  If
$\rho>\mu/4$, the conditional R-167 Duhamel theorem then supplies two-sided
volume Cauchy and strong-star core dynamics.  Equation (7.1), the branch
resummation, and a noncollapsing Hamiltonian algebra remain open.

\section{Equilibrium cutoff alternative}

At fixed temperature, split

\[
 H_\Lambda(h)+C|\Lambda|=\sum_xk_{x,h}
 +\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}V_{xy},
 \qquad k_{x,h}\ge1+\gamma|q_x|^4.                           \tag{8.1}
\]

For $P_{x,R}=1_{[k_{x,h}\le R]}$, projected bonds are $O(R^{1/2})$.
If one separately proves both a local moment

\[
 \sup_{\Lambda,h,x}\varphi_{\Lambda,h}(k_{x,h}^{\,p})\le M_p,
 \qquad p>d+1,                                                \tag{8.2}
\]

and a genuinely two-sided nontracial cutoff estimate, then $R_m=m^b$ with

\[
 \frac{2d}{p-1}<b<2                                          \tag{8.3}
\]

balances the leakage and factorial tails.  In $d=3$, $p=5,b=7/4$ give
exponents $-1/2$ and $-1/8$ in the two leading asymptotic tests.

The moment alone is not enough.  Let

\[
 He_n=ne_n,
 \quad p_n=(1-e^{-\beta})e^{-\beta n},
 \quad X_n=|e_n\rangle\langle e_0|.                          \tag{8.4}
\]

Both squared Duhamel norms satisfy

\[
 (X_n,X_n)_D=(X_n^*,X_n^*)_D
 =\frac{p_0-p_n}{\beta n}\longrightarrow0,                  \tag{8.5}
\]

but $X_ne_0=e_n$ and the symmetric GNS square norm tends to $p_0/2>0$.
Hence Duhamel convergence does not imply strong-star dynamics.  Uniform
modular bandwidth $\Omega$ would repair the topology via

\[
 \|X\|_{\rho,\#}^2\le
 \left[\frac{\beta\Omega}{2}\coth\left(\frac{\beta\Omega}{2}\right)\right]
 (X,X)_D,                                                     \tag{8.6}
\]

while Q3LOCK currently requires a high-modular-energy tail theorem instead.
