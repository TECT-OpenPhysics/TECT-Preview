\section*{2. Full-state bounds and the primary proof}
The exact charge constraint, not a tail truncation, gives
\[
 \sum_V|\psi_v|=(R_{\max}/M_\psi)Q=A:=R_0q_0/m_0,
 \quad \epsilon\leq s_v\leq1,\quad |U_e|=1.                 \tag{5}
\]
Thus $|\psi_v|\leq A$ even though $R_{\max}\to\infty$.
For $J_e=2/(s_v+s_w)$ and the face average $J_b$, both are at most
$1/\epsilon$. Taking absolute values term by term in the pinned functional
gives $|\mathcal F_r|\leq B$, where
\begin{align*}
 b_0={}&\tfrac12\lambda_s(1-\epsilon)^2+\tfrac12|m^2|A^2
       +\tfrac14|\lambda_4|A^4+\tfrac16\eta_6 A^6+\tfrac12gA^2,\\
 B={}&v b_0+\tfrac12\kappa_s e(1-\epsilon)^2
             +2\kappa_D e A^2/\epsilon+2\kappa_g p/\epsilon.  \tag{6}
\end{align*}
Indeed, $|\psi_w-U_e\psi_v|\leq2A$ and
$0\leq1-\operatorname{Re}U_b\leq2$. All original mobilities lie in $(0,1]$;
even aperture moves use two admissible endpoints. Therefore
\[
 c_a(x)\leq C:=e^{\beta B}.                                 \tag{7}
\]
This bound includes negative allowed $m^2,\lambda_4$ and every full state.
It need not be uniform as geometry grows or $\epsilon\downarrow0$.

Set $\delta_r=(R_0/m_0)2^{-r}$, $a_r=(1-\epsilon)2^{-r}$ and
$k_r=2\sin(\pi/K_r)\leq2\pi/K_r$. The four signed families have the following
upper bounds in (3):
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 \text{family}&\text{number of labels}&d(z(ax),z(x))\\ \hline
 \mathrm{PH}&2v&A k_r\\
 \mathrm{TR}&2e&2\delta_r\\
 \mathrm{LK}&2e&k_r\\
 \mathrm{AP}&2v&a_r
\end{array}                                                \tag{8}
\]
For TR the occupation changes but both phase coordinates remain fixed;
the two complex increments each have magnitude $\delta_r$. For PH/LK use
chord distance across modular wrap. Invalid roots are absent, so counting
all labels is an upper bound, not an endpoint modification.
Equations (2), (7), (8) yield
\begin{align*}
 \|L_r f_r\|_\infty&\leq \ell_F C D_r,\\
 D_r&=[2v(1-\epsilon)+4eR_0/m_0]2^{-r}
                         +4\pi(vA+e)/K_r.                 \tag{9}
\end{align*}

For completeness the finite semigroup step requires no invariant smooth
subspace. Let $q\geq\max_x\sum_{y\ne x}L_r(x,y)$, taking $q>0$.
Then $P=I+L_r/q$ is stochastic and
$S_r(t)=e^{-qt}\sum_{n\geq0}(qt)^nP^n/n!$ is a sup-norm contraction on the
full finite space. Differentiate the matrix exponential to obtain
\[
 S_r(t)f_r-f_r=\int_0^t S_r(u)L_r f_r\,du,
 \qquad
 \sup_{t\leq T}\|S_r(t)f_r-f_r\|_{L^2(\pi_r)}
       \leq T\ell_F C D_r.                                \tag{10}
\]
Normalized $\pi_r$ gives the last norm comparison. Since
$K_r\geq2^{r+1}$, $D_r\to0$ geometrically. This proves (4) for every fixed
$F$, not uniformly over unbounded Lipschitz constants. It neither identifies
$S_r(t)f_r$ as a sampled Lipschitz function nor compares different $r$ spaces.

\section*{3. Independent reversible-state derivation}
An independent derivation avoids the pointwise rate cap. Each fixed signed
root is a partial bijection with inverse root. The original midpoint rate
and symmetric mobility give exactly
\[
 \pi_r(x)c_a(x)=m_a(x)\sqrt{\pi_r(x)\pi_r(ax)},\qquad
 \sum_{x:\,ax\text{ valid}}\pi_r(x)c_a(x)\leq1.              \tag{11}
\]
The inequality follows from Cauchy--Schwarz on the valid domain and its
injective image, each of probability mass at most one, and $m_a\leq1$.
It remains true for omitted endpoints and distinct labels with the same map.
Pairing inverse incidences gives the exact full Gibbs Dirichlet form
\[
 \mathcal E_r(f)=\langle f,-L_rf\rangle_{\pi_r}
       =\tfrac12\sum_{x,a\text{ valid}}\pi_r(x)c_a(x)|f(ax)-f(x)|^2.
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
Thus (8), (11) imply
\[
 \mathcal E_r(f_r)\leq\ell_F^2
 [vA^2k_r^2+4e\delta_r^2+ek_r^2+va_r^2]
 \leq\ell_F^2 H4^{-r},                                    \tag{13}
\]
where $H=\pi^2(vA^2+e)+4e(R_0/m_0)^2+v(1-\epsilon)^2$.
The finite matrix $-L_r$ is self-adjoint nonnegative in $L^2(\pi_r)$ by
(12). Diagonalize this finite matrix; for each eigenvalue $\lambda\geq0$,
$(1-e^{-t\lambda})^2\leq t\lambda$. For $x=t\lambda\leq1$ use
$1-e^{-x}\leq x$, and for $x\geq1$ use $1-e^{-x}\leq1$.
Summing the squared spectral coefficients proves independently
\[
 \sup_{t\leq T}\|S_r(t)f_r-f_r\|_{L^2(\pi_r)}
       \leq \ell_F\sqrt{TH}\,2^{-r}.                       \tag{14}
\]
This is finite linear algebra at each cutoff, not an infinite-dimensional
spectral theorem. Neither irreducibility nor a spectral gap is assumed.

\section*{4. Positive variance: not a constant-observable artifact}
The source face readout $W$ is bounded and real. Closed holonomies are gauge
invariant; real parts are unchanged by orientation reversal, and averaging
over all elementary faces is invariant under every actual source automorphism.
Telescoping products of unit links give
$|W(U)-W(U')|\leq(2/p)\sum_e|U_e-U'_e|$, since each edge lies in at most two
faces. In particular $W$ is one fixed admissible Lipschitz readout.

Let $\gamma_r$ be normalized uniform counting on the full tuple space,
used only as a comparison measure. The fixed-$Q$ constraint acts on
occupations only; link coordinates under $\gamma_r$ are independent uniform
$\mathbb Z_{K_r}$. For every elementary face its signed link character has
zero mean. Distinct elementary faces possess an edge in their symmetric
difference with coefficient $1$ or $-1$, so the character averages in both
$U_bU_c$ and $U_b\overline{U_c}$ vanish. This proves zero covariance; it
does not assert independence of adjacent face variables. Also
$\mathbb E_{\gamma_r}(\operatorname{Re}U_b)^2=1/2$ for $K_r\geq3$.
Consequently
\[
 \mathbb E_{\gamma_r}W=0,\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_{\gamma_r}(W)=1/(2p)\quad(r\geq1).       \tag{15}
\]
At $K_0=2$ the variance is $1/p$, so the lower bound below remains safe.
From (6), without assuming $\mathcal F_r\geq0$,
$d\pi_r/d\gamma_r=e^{-\beta\mathcal F_r}/\mathbb E_{\gamma_r}
e^{-\beta\mathcal F_r}\geq e^{-2\beta B}$. Minimization over the centering
constant, not a false claim that the Gibbs mean is zero, now gives
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{\pi_r}(W)
 =\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}\mathbb E_{\pi_r}(W-a)^2
 \geq e^{-2\beta B}\inf_a\mathbb E_{\gamma_r}(W-a)^2
 \geq\frac{e^{-2\beta B}}{2p}>0.                           \tag{16}
\]
Equations (14) and (16) distinguish vanishing dynamics from vanishing static
variance. The lower bound is not claimed uniform in $h,N,\epsilon,\beta$.
