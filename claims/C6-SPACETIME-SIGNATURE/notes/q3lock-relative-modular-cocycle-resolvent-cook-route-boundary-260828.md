# EXP-001227 relative-modular cocycle and resolvent Cook route boundary

EXP-001227 is a claim-nonbearing route design for the Q3LOCK common-alpha
problem.  It proposes a phase-local relative modular cocycle on a resolvent
core as the unit of comparison between nested finite volumes.

The route starts from `H_prime=H+B` and the exact finite cocycle
`U(t)=exp(i t H_prime) exp(-i t H)`.  It seeks a phase-local conditional
expectation and a two-sided fixed-beta GNS/BKM bound for the boundary
commutator `[B,R_z(A)]`, together with the same estimate for the modular
derivative and the adjoint orientation.  A uniformly summable shell coefficient
would feed a Cook integral and give direct `D,delta-D` strong-star Cauchy on the
resolvent core.  The resolvent identity would then provide the product and
inverse bookkeeping needed before common-alpha promotion.

The key difference from the R-384 spectral corridor is that all split histories
are resummed in the relative cocycle before estimating.  The key difference
from EXP-001203 is that no positive folded real-time replica is assumed.  The
route still needs an actual Q3LOCK boundary coefficient, cutoff removal,
all-shape embeddings and a noncollapsing phase-mixture representation.

Status: idea/route only.  No cocycle summability, direct Cauchy theorem,
common alpha, KMS/GNS identification, gap, continuum, C6, Sector-A or Pre-A
closure is asserted.
