\section{Fixed-order star obstruction and exact resummation}

For one component, let

\[
 V_j=-cq_0q_j,\qquad W_0(a)=e^{-iap_0/\hbar}.
\]

The Weyl relation and commutativity of all bond multipliers give

\[
 \operatorname{ad}_{V_m}\cdots\operatorname{ad}_{V_1}W_0(a)
 =(-ca)^m\left(\prod_{j=1}^m q_j\right)W_0(a),       \tag{3.1}
\]

with the same sign for every ordering.  One repeated edge gives

\[
 \operatorname{ad}_{V}^{,n}W_0(a)=(-ca)^nq_y^nW_0(a). \tag{3.2}
\]

On translated smooth product bumps,

\[
 \|A^s\psi_R\|=O(R^{4s}),\qquad
 \left\|\prod_{j=1}^m q_jW_0(a)\psi_R\right\|
 \gtrsim R^m.                                        \tag{3.3}
\]

Thus both one-sided graph operators fail whenever $m>4s$.  The first failures
are $m=3$ at $s=1/2$ and $m=4$ at $s=3/4$, both inside a degree-six lattice
star.  This rejects the literal coefficientwise target, not the dynamics.

Indeed the complete star subflow is

\[
 \operatorname{Ad}\!\left(e^{it\sum_jV_j/\hbar}\right)W_0(a)
 =\exp\!\left[-{icat\over\hbar}\sum_jq_j\right]W_0(a), \tag{3.4}
\]

an exact unitary.  Cancellation occurs across all perturbative orders.

\section{Tree theorem and loop boundary}

On a tree, let $e_1,\ldots,e_d$ be the unique path between the observable
supports.  Absorb every off-path component into $H_0$ and put

\[
 H_k=H_0+\sum_{r\le k}V_{e_r},\quad
 \mathcal U_k(t)=\operatorname{Ad}(e^{itH_k/\hbar}),\quad
 \mathcal D_r={i\over\hbar}\operatorname{ad}_{V_{e_r}}.
\]

Successive Duhamel identities give

\[
\begin{split}
 [\mathcal U_d(t)A,B]
 =\int_{0<t_1<\cdots<t_d<t}
 [&\mathcal U_d(t-t_d)\mathcal D_d
 \mathcal U_{d-1}(t_d-t_{d-1})\cdots\\
 &\cdots\mathcal D_1\mathcal U_0(t_1)A,B],d\mathbf t .
\end{split}                                           \tag{4.1}
\]

Each path edge appears once; all side branches and post-activation repeats are
inside exact unitaries.  On the square $0-1-2-3-0$, however, deleting the
chosen backbone $0-1-2$ leaves $0-3-2$.  The base propagator can already
connect the endpoints.  A cubic lattice therefore needs a cut/forest
organization or a global bond resummation.

\section{All-bond Trotter successor}

Write

\[
 H=\sum_xh_x-c\sum_{\langle xy\rangle}q_x\cdot q_y. \tag{5.1}
\]

Every cross bond commutes with every other one, including bonds sharing a
site.  The complete bond kick factorizes exactly and acts by

\[
 q_x\mapsto q_x,\qquad
 p_x\mapsto p_x+\delta c\sum_{y\sim x}q_y.          \tag{5.2}
\]

The onsite flow is tensor-local.  If a two-sided graph-Lipschitz core can be
found with the one-step recurrence

\[
 L_x'\le(1+C\delta)L_x+J\delta\sum_{y\sim x}L_y,    \tag{5.3}
\]

then the exactly $d$-transfer coefficient after $N$ steps is

\[
 {N\choose d}(Jt/N)^d(1+Ct/N)^{N-d}
 \longrightarrow e^{Ct}{(Jt)^d\over d!}.           \tag{5.4}
\]

The graph-Lipschitz core, uniform Trotter convergence, invariant Hamiltonian
algebra, and KMS identification are open.  Equation (5.4) is a conditional
reduction, not a completed locality theorem.

\section{Duhamel-to-GNS modular theorem}

For a faithful Gibbs density $\rho$ in one fixed finite-volume type-I
representation, with $X$ and $[\log\rho,X]$ in the Duhamel form domain, define

\[
 \|X\|_D^2=\int_0^1\operatorname{Tr}(\rho^{1-s}X^*\rho^sX),ds,
 \qquad
 \|X\|_\#^2={1\over2}\operatorname{Tr}\rho(X^*X+XX^*). \tag{6.1}
\]

The respective matrix weights are the logarithmic and arithmetic means.  If
$u=|\log p_m-\log p_n|$, then

\[
 {A(p_m,p_n)\over L(p_m,p_n)}
 ={u\over2}\coth{u\over2}\le1+{u\over2}.          \tag{6.2}
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz gives the exact inequality

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \|X\|_\#^2\le\|X\|_D^2
 +{1\over2}\|X\|_D\|[\log\rho,X]\|_D .\quad}    \tag{6.3}
\]

With $\delta=(i/\hbar)[H,\cdot]$,
$[\log\rho,X]=i\beta\hbar\delta X$.  Hence Duhamel convergence and a
uniform first modular-derivative bound imply two-sided GNS convergence.  On a
uniformly operator-bounded set this yields strong-star convergence in one
fixed faithful type-I representation.  Faithfulness (or support restriction)
is essential; this matrix proof is not a general W-star modular-operator
theorem, and it does not create a common representation across volumes.

For a modular-analytic multiplier $B$, put

\[
 M_0(B)=\sup_{|t|\le1/2}\|\rho^tB\rho^{-t}\|,\qquad
 M_1(B)=M_0([\log\rho,B]).                           \tag{6.4}
\]

Then

\[
 \|[X,B]\|_D\le2M_0\|X\|_D,                       \tag{6.5}
\]

\[
 \|[\log\rho,[X,B]]\|_D
 \le2M_0\|[\log\rho,X]\|_D+2M_1\|X\|_D.          \tag{6.6}
\]

These are exact sufficient multiplier conditions.  Uniform $M_0,M_1$ for the
structured truncated evolution remain open.

\section{Why an arbitrary bounded multiplier still fails}

Let

\[
 H_n=\operatorname{diag}(0,n),\quad
 \rho_n={\operatorname{diag}(1,e^{-\beta n})\over1+e^{-\beta n}},
\]

\[
 W_n=e^{\beta n/4}|1\rangle\langle1|,\qquad
 B_n=|1\rangle\langle0|.
\]

Then $[H_n,W_n]=0$ and

\[
 \varphi_n(W_n^2)={e^{-\beta n/2}\over1+e^{-\beta n}}\to0,
\]

but

\[
 \|[W_n,B_n]\|_D^2
 =e^{\beta n/2}{1-e^{-\beta n}\over
 (1+e^{-\beta n})\beta n}\to\infty.                \tag{7.1}
\]

Thus static tail smallness and even a zero modular derivative do not form an
ideal under arbitrary contractions.  The projected or structured multiplier
condition is load-bearing.

\section{Coordinate-only cutoff}

The exact Q3LOCK hypothesis map in \texttt{EXP-000781} imports the
infinite-volume tempered-DLR one-site exponential path estimate of
Kozitsky--Pasurek.  For the finite periodic-volume bridge define

\[
 A_x=\lambda_\sigma\|\omega_x\|_{C^\sigma}^2
       +\kappa\|\omega_x\|_2^2.                       \tag{8.1}
\]

Finite-volume confinement makes the exponential moment finite.  Condition the
periodic Gibbs law at $x$, apply their Lemma 4.1, integrate, and use generalized
Holder with $r_y=\vartheta|J^\Lambda_{xy}|/\kappa$ and
$\sum_y r_y<1$.  This gives

\[
 e^{n_x}\le e^C\prod_y e^{r_yn_y},
 \qquad n_x=\log\mu_{\Lambda,h}^{\rm per}(e^{A_x}),   \tag{8.2}
\]

and hence

\[
 n_x\le C+{\vartheta\over\kappa}\sum_y|J^\Lambda_{xy}|n_y.
                                                               \tag{8.3}
\]

Here $\widehat J_\Lambda\le6c$.  Periodic translation invariance and
$\vartheta\widehat J_\Lambda<\kappa$ give
$n_x\le C/(1-\vartheta\widehat J_\Lambda/\kappa)$.  The compact-source
uniformity of $C$ is inherited from the stability constants checked in
\texttt{EXP-000781}.  For $0<r\le\beta$,

\[
 |\omega(0)|^2\le2r^{-1}\|\omega\|_2^2
                 +2r^{2\sigma}[\omega]_\sigma^2.      \tag{8.4}
\]

Given $a>0$, choose $r\le\beta$ with
$2ar^{2\sigma}\le\lambda_\sigma$ and then the freely available $L^2$
coefficient $\kappa\ge2a/r$.  The recursion and Holder control imply, at fixed
$\beta$ and on compact source intervals,

\[
 \sup_{\Lambda,h,x}\varphi_{\Lambda,h}(e^{a|q_x|^2})<\infty
 \qquad\hbox{for every }a>0.                         \tag{8.5}
\]

Choose $Q_L(q)=\eta(|q|/L)q$, equal to $q$ on the $L$-ball and zero outside
the $2L$-ball, and truncate only the intersite coordinate.  The truncated bond
has norm $O(L^2)$.  With $\rho=\rho_{\Lambda,h}$, its tail $W_L$ and gradient
over both bond endpoints and all components are polynomial factors on
$\max(|q_x|,|q_y|)>L$, so

\[
 \|W_L\|_D^2+\|[\log\rho,W_L]\|_D^2
 \le C_{a,N}L^Ne^{-aL^2}.                            \tag{8.6}
\]

To justify the identity, first impose a bounded outer cutoff $W_{L,M}$ on the
common Schrodinger core and then send $M\to\infty$ by exponential domination.
The exact Dirichlet identity is

\[
 \|[\log\rho,W_L]\|_D^2
 ={\beta\hbar^2\over\chi}
   \varphi(|\nabla_{(x,y)}W_L|^2).                  \tag{8.7}
\]

If the missing structured comparison grows at most as
$\operatorname{poly}(L)e^{C_0TL^2}$, choose $L_m=m^\alpha$,
$0<\alpha<1/2$, and $a>C_0T$.  The static/modular tail and the bounded-
interaction factorial error then both vanish.  Constants depend on fixed
$\beta,a,h_0$ and model data; no ground-limit uniformity is asserted.  This
scale arithmetic is conditional on the projected or multiplier estimate.

Primary source: Y. Kozitsky and T. Pasurek,
\url{https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0609045}.

\section{Fixed-beta OS mixture precursor}

For $0<\lambda<1$, assume the same reflection, underlying field space and
positive-time test algebra.  For
$\mu_0=\lambda\mu_++(1-\lambda)\mu_-$, reflection positivity gives

\[
 q_0=\lambda q_++(1-\lambda)q_-,\qquad
 N_0=N_+\cap N_-.                                   \tag{9.1}
\]

The map

\[
 [F]_0\mapsto(\sqrt\lambda[F]_+,\sqrt{1-\lambda}[F]_-)
\]

is an isometry into the direct sum of the phasewise OS spaces.  It need not be
surjective.  A common normal W-star interpretation additionally needs
reconstruction functoriality on one formal field algebra.  Central phase
projections require disjointness; extremality helps only with a common-KMS-
simplex theorem that implies it.  The mixture $\mu_0$ has not been proved to be
the zero-source periodic finite-volume limit.  This is only a fixed-beta
envelope precursor, not a beta-independent Hamiltonian common alpha.

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The star counterexample might be misread as nonexistence of the dynamics.
This objection is upheld as a scope warning: equation (3.4) proves the same
subflow is exactly unitary after all-order resummation.
\item The tree identity might be copied directly to the cubic lattice.  This is
dismissed by the square, where a second endpoint path remains after the chosen
backbone is removed.
\item Static Gibbs tails might be assumed stable under every bounded evolved
observable.  This is dismissed by equation (7.1); modular half-strip or direct
projected control is essential.
\item The OS mixture might be called the common Hamiltonian dynamics.  This is
dismissed: it is fixed-beta, its image need not be the full direct sum, and the
Hamiltonian, central-decomposition and zero-temperature gates remain open.
\end{enumerate}
