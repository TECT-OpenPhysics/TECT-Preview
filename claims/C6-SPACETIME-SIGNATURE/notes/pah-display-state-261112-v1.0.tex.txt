$d(x)=(s,\psi,U)$ is a sufficient state for an exact time-homogeneous
Markov description of the unchanged PAH-v2 counting process. We disprove
this at one already-admitted finite instance, including its original
stationary Gibbs law. We do not reject the full counting process.

\section*{2. Exact admitted witness}
Use the adopted Morton square $G_{0,0}$, width $2$, vertices
$0=(0,0),1=(1,0),2=(0,1),3=(1,1)$, edges
$01,02,13,23$, and the one closed square face. The anchors are
$O=\{0\}$ and $C=\{1\}$; no field is clamped. This is the original open
boundary. Set $r=h=N=0$ and $m_0=q_0=R_0=2$. The source schedule gives
$K=2,M_s=1,M_\psi=2,Q=2,R_{\max}=2$.
Take $\epsilon=1/2$, $\beta=\nu=1$, all positive functional couplings
equal to $1$, and $m^2=\lambda_4=0$. These are admissible witness inputs,
not a fit or a new source law. Every parameter is fixed; no limit is taken.

Consider the four counting states
\[
 j=(1,1,1,1),\quad \ell=(0,1,1,0),\quad
 n=(a,0,0,b),\quad u=(0,0,0,0),\qquad a,b\in\{0,1\}.
\]
They have identical display $s_v=1$, $\psi=(0,1,1,0)$, $U_e=1$.
This is the whole exact-display fibre: positive amplitudes fix $n_1,n_2$,
zero amplitudes leave $n_0,n_3$ free, and $\epsilon<1$ fixes every $j$.
Use $f=|\psi_O|^2=\ell_0^2$, a gauge- and anchor-invariant member of
the existing full finite algebra. It is not a replacement observable domain.

Each initial energy is $F=4/3+2=10/3$: the first term is onsite and
the second is the covariant gradient contribution. PH, LK and AP leave
$f$ unchanged. Of the four valid TR roots, only $1\to0$ and $2\to0$
change $f$; each increases it by one. Their mobility is one. TR retains
all phase labels, including $a$ at the initially empty vertex. The two
target energies are therefore
\[
 F_{\rm target}=\begin{cases}4/3+1=7/3,&a=0,\\
                              4/3+3=13/3,&a=1.\end{cases}
\]
Consequently the original midpoint rate gives, exactly,
\[
 (Lf)(x_{0b})=2e^{1/2},\qquad
 (Lf)(x_{1b})=2e^{-1/2}.                         \tag{1}
\]
Each initial state has PH:8, TR:4, LK:8, AP:4 valid labelled incidences.
K=2 duplicate signs are retained. No invalid root or root multiplicity is
silently changed. As full-functional regression controls, the AP-minus
energy increments at empty $O$ and occupied $C$ are $17/24$ and $1/3$;
their mobility is $1/\sqrt2$, but their $f$ increments are zero.

If a generator $\bar L$ on displays satisfied
$L(\bar f\circ d)=(\bar L\bar f)\circ d$ for every displayed observable,
its right side would be constant on a display fibre. Equation (1)
contradicts this. The universal displayed-state closure route is therefore
false at this admitted instance. This is not an all-regulator assertion.

\section*{3. Original stationary Gibbs law does not remove the defect}
Let $\pi(x)=Z^{-1}e^{-F(x)}$ on the complete finite counting space.
For each valid signed incidence, inverse validity and symmetric mobility
give
\[
 \pi(x)c_r(x)=Z^{-1}m_r(x)e^{-[F(x)+F(rx)]/2}
             =\pi(rx)c_{r^{-1}}(rx).
\]
Summation preserves all duplicate labels. Hence $L=L^*$ in $L^2(\pi)$,
$S_t=e^{tL}=S_t^*$, and $\pi$ has full support. Let
$P=\mathbb E_\pi[\,\cdot\mid d]$, the orthogonal projection onto display
functions, solely for this proof. Under the unchanged stationary path law,
the displayed two-time conditional transitions are
$C_t=PS_tP$ on $\operatorname{ran}P$. An autonomous stationary Markov
description would require $C_{2t}=C_t^2$.

Finite matrix calculus and orthogonality instead yield the exact identity
\[
 C_{2t}-C_t^2=PS_t(I-P)S_tP,\qquad
 \langle f,(C_{2t}-C_t^2)f\rangle_\pi
       =\|(I-P)S_tf\|_\pi^2.                    \tag{2}
\]
All four fibre states have equal Gibbs weight. From (1),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_\pi(Lf\mid d=d(x_{00}))
     =(e^{1/2}-e^{-1/2})^2>0.
\]
Their total mass is $4e^{-10/3}/Z>0$, so
\[
 \|(I-P)Lf\|_\pi^2\ge
 \frac{4e^{-10/3}}{Z}(e^{1/2}-e^{-1/2})^2>0.     \tag{3}
\]
Since $Pf=f$ and the finite matrix exponential is analytic,
$(I-P)S_tf=t(I-P)Lf+O(t^2)$ in the fixed finite norm. Equations (2)--(3)
give a positive Chapman--Kolmogorov defect for all sufficiently small
$t>0$. Thus the actual stationary displayed process is not a
time-homogeneous Markov chain. Merely averaging instantaneous rates over
the Gibbs fibre cannot reproduce its finite-time law.
There is no quantitative cutoff-uniform lower bound in (3), no change of
initial state, and no interchange of time or regulator limits.

\paragraph{Gauge quotient.}
This is not a gauge artefact. Preserving all displayed $U=1$ on the
connected square forces a constant gauge label, and the two occupied
phases then force that label to zero. The four hidden states are not
identified by this stabilizer. The gauge orbit has 16 exact-display
representatives, each with the same four-state conditional split because
$F$ and $Lf$ are invariant. The conditional variance therefore persists
also on the gauge-orbit display fibre. The two adjacent marked anchors
make the cell automorphism group trivial. This additional observation
does not assert that every arbitrary further quotient is non-Markov.
