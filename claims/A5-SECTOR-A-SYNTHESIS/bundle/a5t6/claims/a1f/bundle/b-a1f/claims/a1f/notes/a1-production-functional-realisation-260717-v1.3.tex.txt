\section*{2. Frozen functional and implementation}
For $\rho=\Psi^\dagger\Psi$, $m_A=\Psi^\dagger T_A\Psi$,
$J_A=\nabla m_A$, and
$K_A=J_A-(m_A/(\rho+10^{-12}))\nabla\rho$, the backend implements
\[
 F=F_{\rm Braz}+F_{\rm family}+F_{\rm lock}+F_{\rm shell}
 +\int\left(\frac\lambdA4-SCALAR-SPECTRAL-CONSTRUCTIVE-MEASURE\rho^2+\frac\gamma6\rho^3\right)
 +\sum_A\int\left(\frac A2-FULL-PRODUCTION-WELLPOSED|J_A|^2
 +b\operatorname{Re}(\bar J_A\cdot K_A)+\frac c2|K_A|^2\right),
\]
where $a=c_{JJ}\alpha_X^2/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$,
$b=c_{JK}\alpha_X\beta_X/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$, and
$c=c_{KK}\beta_X^2/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$.  The manifest fixes the Brazovskii
term, family mass, internal lock, shell bias, quartic and sextic terms,
all three Class-II coefficients, density regularizers, projector, and real
pairing convention.  The backend, verifier, and collector are SHA-256 pinned.
