\section{Purpose and scope}

R-097 reduced the complete heat-lifted current problem to one terminal
posterior bracket
\[
 \mathfrak C_{\rm post}(B;R)
 =\sum_i\int\left\{
   \E_{\cF}[B:(G_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_i)]
   -q_i^T(\overline B+2R)^{-1}q_i
 \right\}\dd x,                                         \tag{1.1}
\]
where $\overline B=\E_{\cF}B$ and $q_i=\E_{\cF}(BG_i)$.
It also left the standalone Cartan one-use ledger R-085 (4.11) and the
rational shifted-Hessian form R-085 (6.5) open.  This note tests those three
surfaces together, with the complete signed endpoint retained.

Five exact conclusions result.

First, the posterior bracket is superadditive under a matching split of the
matrix payment.  This makes a rowwise lower estimate legal, although it may
discard helpful cross-row compensation and gives no positivity.

Second, the production rational rank-one Gram has an exact conditional
ridge-regression form.  It identifies the missing analytic estimate without
revealing the coefficient or differentiating an adapted selector.

Third, R-085 (6.5) is recovered exactly from the R-097 terminal identity only
after the five unshifted families, the low endpoint, and the Cameron--Martin
matrix payment are restored once.  A lower estimate for the unshifted
families is not the upper estimate needed by this recovery.

Fourth, a genuine production Cartan slice gives an anisotropic reverse
partition of one source-block visit.  Each oppositely oriented subvisit has a
nonzero arbitrarily far harmonic whose squared atom is order $A^2$, while the
mixed energy--terminal-sextic budget is only order $A$.  The two signed atoms
cancel exactly before squaring.  Therefore no per-subvisit nonnegative
R-085/R-088 atomization can be stable under this partition refinement while
retaining only the mixed payload.  This does not address two visits carrying
distinct temporal roots or heat operators, and it does not touch the regular
one-shot theorem.

Fifth, independent-root resampling gives a derivative-free successor.  The
production Gram obeys a global finite-difference inequality and the bare
future-control kernel is
\[
 \sum_{j<k}2^j2^{-4k}<2^{-3k}.                           \tag{1.2}
\]
The remaining work is to prove the spatial mixed-norm estimate with the
Schur mean, sextic multiplier, trace, heat, low endpoint, and R-063 forest
each retained once.

These statements do not prove the production posterior lower form, the
standalone Cartan one-shot ledger, rational (6.5), complete $H_N$, REG,
arbitrary progressive assembly, $\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$, the
$q=10/9$ Nelson estimate, a measure theorem, or Sector-A closure.  Tier
stays T4.

\section{Posterior bracket and payment-split superadditivity}

Fix one conditional fibre and suppress $i,x$.  For a random positive
semidefinite matrix $B$, a deterministic $R\succ0$, and a square-integrable
vector $G$, assume that every displayed expectation below is finite (this is
automatic at finite production cutoff), and put
\[
 \mathcal C(B;R)
 =\E_{\cF}[B:(G^{\otimes2}-\Gamma)]
  -q_B^T(\overline B+2R)^{-1}q_B,
 \qquad q_B=\E_{\cF}(BG).                               \tag{2.1}
\]
This is the shorter form of the R-097 identity
$J_B+\E_{\cF}[B:(V_B-\Gamma)]$.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-2.1-payment-split-superadditivity
\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (payment-split posterior superadditivity)}

Let $B=\sum_{a=1}^rB_a$ and $R=\sum_{a=1}^rR_a$, with
$B_a\succeq0$ and $R_a\succ0$.  Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal C(B;R)\geq
        \sum_{a=1}^r\mathcal C(B_a;R_a).}               \tag{2.2}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Set $A_a=\E_{\cF}B_a+2R_a$, $q_a=\E_{\cF}(B_aG)$,
$A=\sum_aA_a$, and $q=\sum_aq_a$.  With $y=A^{-1}q$,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_aq_a^TA_a^{-1}q_a-q^TA^{-1}q
 &=\sum_a\left\{
 q_a^TA_a^{-1}q_a-2y^Tq_a+y^TA_ay\right\}\\
 &=\sum_a\|A_a^{-1/2}q_a-A_a^{1/2}y\|^2\geq0.
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{2.3}
\]
The Wick part of (2.1) is linear in $B$, so (2.3) proves (2.2).
\hfill$\square$

The same calculation shows that increasing $R$ increases $\mathcal C$.
This is not a free improvement in the original action: the terminal Schur
identity simultaneously spends the actual term $\int c^TRc$.  Equation
(2.2) permits a lower proof by rows, but the full bracket can be strictly
larger because it keeps cross-row regression compensation.

\section{The rational row is an exact ridge deficit}

For the production rational row write
\[
 B_R=b\otimes b,\qquad
 b=2\sqrt{c_1}\,g(Z_*),\qquad s_i=b^TG_i.              \tag{3.1}
\]
Condition on a sigma-field making the terminal control and $R$ measurable,
assume the displayed conditional moments are finite, and put
\[
 K=\E_{\cF}(bb^T),\qquad
 q_i=\E_{\cF}(b s_i),\qquad A_R=K+2R.                  \tag{3.2}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-3.1-rational-ridge-deficit
\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (conditional rational ridge normal form)}

For every derivative direction $i$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal C_i(B_R;R)
 &=\E_{\cF}[s_i^2-b^T\Gamma_i b]-q_i^TA_R^{-1}q_i\\
 &=\inf_{h\in\mathbb R^6}\left\{
   \E_{\cF}(s_i-b^Th)^2+2h^TRh
   -\E_{\cF}(b^T\Gamma_i b)\right\}.
 \end{aligned}}                                         \tag{3.3}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
The first equality is (2.1) with $B=b\otimes b$.  Expanding the expression
inside the infimum gives
\[
 \E_{\cF}s_i^2-2h^Tq_i+h^T(K+2R)h
 -\E_{\cF}(b^T\Gamma_i b).                             \tag{3.4}
\]
Its unique minimizer is $h=A_R^{-1}q_i$, which gives (3.3).
\hfill$\square$

Thus the missing sign is a weighted conditional ridge deficit: how much of
the derivative carrier $s_i=b^TG_i$ can be predicted by the random ridge
$b^Th$, compared with the covariance-normal trace.  Positivity of $B_R$ does
not decide that deficit.

\section{Exact recovery boundary for R-085 (6.5)}

Use the R-085 notation
\[
 F_{6.5}=\mathcal H_R+{1\over2}\sum_i\int c_i^TB_1c_i\dd x,
 \qquad
 W_0={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 B_0:(G_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_i)\dd x,                   \tag{4.1}
\]
and let $\mathcal U_R$ be the sum of the five unshifted families.  The exact
Taylor identity is
\[
 \Delta W_R=\mathcal U_R+F_{6.5}.                       \tag{4.2}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-4.1-rational-standalone-recovery
\subsection*{Theorem 4.1 (standalone recovery from the terminal bracket)}

With $\overline{\mathcal A}=\E_{\cF}B_1+2R$ and
$q_i=\E_{\cF}(B_1G_i)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \E_{\cF}F_{6.5}
={}&{1\over2}\sum_i\int
 \|c_i+\overline{\mathcal A}^{-1}q_i
 \|_{\overline{\mathcal A}}^2\dd x
 +{1\over2}\mathfrak C_{\rm post}(B_R;R)\\
 &-\sum_i\int c_i^TRc_i\dd x
 -\E_{\cF}W_0-\E_{\cF}\mathcal U_R.
 \end{aligned}}                                         \tag{4.3}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
The R-097 terminal Schur identity says
\[
 \E_{\cF}W_R(A^*)+\sum_i\int c_i^TRc_i
 ={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 \|c_i+\overline{\mathcal A}^{-1}q_i
 \|_{\overline{\mathcal A}}^2
 +{1\over2}\mathfrak C_{\rm post}.                    \tag{4.4}
\]
Insert $W_R(A^*)=W_0+\mathcal U_R+F_{6.5}$ from (4.2) and rearrange.
\hfill$\square$

Every owner in (4.3) occurs once.  In particular, a lower bound on
$F_{6.5}$ requires an \emph{upper} form bound on $\mathcal U_R$, because it
appears with a minus sign.  R-085 records a lower bound for its five-family
sum.  Its familywise absolute estimates suggest a two-sided statement, but
that upper statement is not a current accepted authority and is not inferred
here.  R-097 terminalizes $\mathcal U_R+F_{6.5}$, not $F_{6.5}$ alone.

\section{Exact rational production-fibre sign}

On the pure-doublet ray the rational coefficient is
\[
 B_R(u)=4c_1\left(u-{5\over9}{u^3\over u^2+e}\right)^2,
 \qquad c_1={243\over8000P}.                            \tag{5.1}
\]
It is strictly increasing for $u>0$: after writing $u=t\sqrt e$, the
derivative of the dimensionless squared factor is
\[
 {2t(4t^2+9)(2t^2-3t+3)(2t^2+3t+3)
  \over81(t^2+1)^3}>0\qquad(t>0).
\]
At the two audit points
$u_-={1\over2}\sqrt e$ and $u_+={3\over2}\sqrt e$,
\[
 B_-={3e\over125P},\qquad
 B_+={2187e\over21125P},\qquad
 \Delta=B_+-B_-={1680e\over21125P}.                    \tag{5.2}
\]
Use the positive-branch inverse to construct the bounded even selector for
which
\[
 B_R(u(G^2))=B_-+{\Delta\over1+G^2}.                    \tag{5.3}
\]
Let $G=0$ with probability $1/2$ and $G=\pm\sqrt2$ with probability $1/4$
each.  Then $\Gamma=1$ and symmetry gives $q=r=0$ for every $R>0$.  Hence
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal C(B_R;R)
 =\E[B_R(u(G^2))(G^2-1)]
 =-{\Delta\over3}=-{560e\over21125P}<0.}               \tag{5.4}
\]
This is an exact production-coefficient fibre obstruction to automatic
ridge positivity.  It has no torus control map, spatial derivative
$c=Da$, heat/low packet, or Cameron--Martin realization, and therefore is
not a counterexample to a paid production lower form.

The complete four-row pure-doublet coefficient from R-093 gives an even

\section{An anisotropic Cartan partition-refinement obstruction}

Fix the production Cartan generator
\[
 S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)                            \tag{6.1}
\]
inside one active real doublet.  Let $n_j\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$ be an
admissible torus carrier whose physical frequency
$N_j=2\pi|n_j|/L$ lies in shell $j$, let $0<\epsilon<1$, and set
\[
 f_\epsilon(\theta)=(\sin\theta,\epsilon\cos\theta),
 \qquad \theta={2\pi n_jx_1\over L}.                   \tag{6.2}
\]
Since $|f_\epsilon|\geq\epsilon$, this path remains uniformly away from the
cone tip at every fixed positive floor.  Refine one deterministic source-
block secant into two oppositely oriented subvisits, both evaluated with the
same fixed target heat and the same root derivative, with
\[
 a_+=Af_\epsilon,\qquad a_-=-Af_\epsilon,              \tag{6.3}
\]
and use the fresh Cameron--Martin direction
$v=\sqrt{\lambda_{n_j}}f_\epsilon$.

For the production heat-lifted Cartan one-form
$\mathcal C_\Phi(z)=\Phi(z)^TDz$, define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_A^+&=\mathbb D_j[\mathcal C_\Phi(w+Af_\epsilon)
                     -\mathcal C_\Phi(w)][v],\\
 T_A^-&=\mathbb D_j[\mathcal C_\Phi(w)
                     -\mathcal C_\Phi(w+Af_\epsilon)][v].
 \end{aligned}                                          \tag{6.4}
\]
Endpoint reversal gives the pathwise identity
\[
 \boxed{T_A^-=-T_A^+.}                                  \tag{6.5}
\]

Put
\[
 d_\epsilon(\theta)=\sin^2\theta+\epsilon^2\cos^2\theta,
 \qquad
 q_\epsilon(\theta)
 ={\sin^2\theta-\epsilon^2\cos^2\theta
   \over d_\epsilon(\theta)}.                          \tag{6.6}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-6.1-cartan-reverse-visit
\subsection*{Theorem 6.1 (nonzero far harmonic and signed cancellation)}

At fixed cutoff, fixed future heat, and fixed positive floor,
\[
 {T_A^+\over AN_j\sqrt{\lambda_{n_j}}}
 \longrightarrow
 \tau_\epsilon(\theta)
 :=q_\epsilon(\theta)d_\epsilon'(\theta)
 \quad\hbox{in }L^2(\Omega\times\mathbb T^3)           \tag{6.7}
\]
as $A\to\infty$.  If
\[
 \rho={1-\epsilon\over1+\epsilon},\qquad \phi=2\theta,
\]
then
\[
 q_\epsilon
 =\rho-{4\epsilon\over1-\epsilon^2}
       \sum_{n\geq1}\rho^n\cos(n\phi),                \tag{6.8}
\]
and, for every $k\geq2$, the sine-series coefficient is
\[
 \boxed{
 [\sin(k\phi)]\tau_\epsilon
 =-2\epsilon(1-\rho^2)\rho^{k-1}\neq0.}               \tag{6.9}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Write the positive production floor as $\eta>0$ and
$n_\epsilon=\sin^2\theta-\epsilon^2\cos^2\theta$, so the actual scalar
coefficient on the radial slice is
$A^2n_\epsilon/(A^2d_\epsilon+\eta)$.  At zero base and zero fixed heat
displacement, direct radial differentiation of the complete one-form gives
the finite-$A$ identity
\[
 {T_A^+\over AN_j\sqrt{\lambda_{n_j}}}
 =\tau_\epsilon(\theta)
  \left\{1-{\eta^2\over(A^2d_\epsilon(\theta)+\eta)^2}\right\}. \tag{6.7a}
\]
Thus the floor correction is nonzero at finite $A$---the floorless
homogeneity shortcut would be false---but it is uniformly $O(A^{-4})$
because $d_\epsilon\geq\epsilon^2$.  In the leading floorless limit the two
chain-rule lines $D\Phi[v]^TDz$ and $\Phi^TDv$ each contribute half of
$AN_j\sqrt{\lambda_{n_j}}q_\epsilon d_\epsilon'$.  A fixed base and fixed
heat displacement are lower order on $|Af_\epsilon|\geq A\epsilon$; the
global production derivative bounds and the finite-cutoff moment bounds give
$L^2$ domination.  This proves (6.7).

Writing $d_\epsilon$ as an affine function of $\cos\phi$ and using the
Poisson-kernel series gives (6.8).  Multiplication by
$d_\epsilon'=(1-\epsilon^2)\sin\phi$ and the elementary product-to-sum
identity gives (6.9) for $k\geq2$. \hfill$\square$

For the audit value $\epsilon=1/2$, $\rho=1/3$ and the corrected closed form
is
\[
 [\sin(2k\theta)]\tau_{1/2}=-{8\over3^{k+1}},
 \qquad k\geq2.                                        \tag{6.10}
\]
In particular the $k=2,3$ coefficients are $-8/27$ and $-8/81$.  The
power $k+1$ in (6.10) is independently recomputed rather than inferred from
a decimal fit.

Fix a gap $C\geq5$.  Choose $k_C$ so that the physical carrier $2k_CN_j$ lies in a
nonzero smooth Littlewood--Paley block $\Pi_m$ with $m\geq j+C$.  The leading
term in (6.7) is independent of the fresh probability root, so its OU image
is unchanged.  Since $\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}\dd t=1/2$, (6.7)--(6.9) imply
that for all sufficiently large finite $A$,
\[
 \int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
 \E\|\Pi_mP_t^{(j)}T_A^\pm\|_{\rm HS}^2\dd t
 \geq c_{C,\epsilon,n_j}A^2,                           \tag{6.11}
\]
where $c_{C,\epsilon,n_j}>0$.  The one-direction limiting coefficient before
the fixed LP multiplier is
\[
 N_j^2\lambda_{n_j}\epsilon^2(1-\rho^2)^2
 \rho^{2(k_C-1)}.                                      \tag{6.12}
\]

% evidence-anchor: corollary-6.2-nonnegative-per-subvisit-no-go
\subsection*{Corollary 6.2 (nonnegative per-subvisit atomization fails)}

Suppose an extension of R-085 (4.10)--(4.11), or of the corrected R-088
direct-root analogue, assigns nonnegative atom ledgers $q_+,q_-$ to these
two subvisits, is stable under refinement of one source-block secant, and has
a constant uniform in $A$.  Equation (6.11) forces
\[
 q_++q_-\geq cA^2.                                     \tag{6.13}
\]
The complete loop has terminal shift zero.  With the fixed positive source-
cost coefficient
\[
 \kappa_{n_j,\epsilon}:=\|K_{n_j}^{-1}f_\epsilon\|_{\mathcal H}^2,
\]
its cost is $X=2\kappa_{n_j,\epsilon}A^2$, while its terminal sextic $Y$ is
independent of $A$.
Consequently
\[
 1+\E[X^{1/2}Y^{1/2}]=O(1+A),                          \tag{6.14}
\]
contradicting a uniform bound of $q_++q_-$ by (6.14).

There is no contradiction after signed grouping: (6.5) gives
$T_A^++T_A^-=0$ before any square.  Nor is there a contradiction if one
allows a once-only pure $X$ payment, since (6.13) is then critical rather
than supercritical.  Distinct temporal visits need not share the target heat
or derivative root, so (6.5) is not asserted for them.  The result is exactly
a no-go for refinement-stable nonnegative per-subvisit atomization;
R-085's regular physical-shell one-shot target and R-092's regular $H_C$
theorem remain intact.

\section{Derivative-free causal finite differences}

The exact production frame bounds are
\[
 \|M_A(z)\|_F\leq\sqrt{20}|z|,
 \qquad
 \|M_A(z)-M_A(w)\|_F\leq\sqrt{68}|z-w|.                \tag{7.1}
\]
For
\[
 B_{\rm fr}(z)=\sum_{A=1}^3M_A(z)Q_{\rm II}M_A(z)^T,   \tag{7.2}
\]
polarization gives the global secant estimate
\[
 \boxed{
 \|B_{\rm fr}(z)-B_{\rm fr}(w)\|_F
 \leq C_B(|z|+|w|)|z-w|,
 \quad C_B=3\|Q_{\rm II}\|_{\rm op}\sqrt{20\cdot68}.} \tag{7.3}
\]

Let $h_k^{(j)}$ be obtained from a strict-past source $h_k$ by independently
resampling root $j<k$.  Here $\operatorname{Var}_j$ is the Efron--Stein
conditional variance with all roots other than $j$ held fixed; it is not the
Doob variance conditional only on $\mathcal F_{j-1}$.  Conditional variance
gives
\[
 \E\|h_k-h_k^{(j)}\|^2
 =2\E\operatorname{Var}_j(h_k)\leq2\E\|h_k\|^2.       \tag{7.4}
\]
If $a_k=K_kh_k$ and $\|K_k\|_{L^2\to L^2}\leq
\kappa_K2^{-2k}$, then
\[
 \E\|a_k-a_k^{(j)}\|_2^2
 \leq2\kappa_K^2 2^{-4k}\E\|h_k\|^2.                \tag{7.5}
\]
For $k>j_0$, after the displayed resampling prefactor $2\kappa_K^2$ is kept
outside, the derivative-root covariance weights have the exact scalar Hardy
mass
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{j=j_0}^{k-1}2^j2^{-4k}
 =(2^k-2^{j_0})2^{-4k}<2^{-3k}.}                        \tag{7.6}
\]

Equations (7.3)--(7.6) require no Malliavin derivative of $h_k$.  They provide
a candidate successor to per-subvisit nonnegative atomization, but are not
yet an ANOVA decomposition or a once-only covariance ledger: summing raw
root influences counts a Hoeffding component once for every root in its
support.  They also do not control the product multiplier
$|Z|+|Z^{(j)}|$, the conditional Schur mean
$q^T(\overline B+2R)^{-1}q$, or the spatial low-Hermite/Wick pairing.  The
next theorem must combine those objects before using (7.6).

\section{Devil's-advocate self-test}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: posterior superadditivity proves positivity.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  It only compares the full bracket with the sum of row
brackets.  The rational production fibre (5.4) is negative.

\item \textbf{Objection: a larger matrix payment makes the bracket positive
for free.}  \textbf{DISMISSED.}  The bracket is monotone in $R$, but the
original action simultaneously subtracts the once-only cost $\int c^TRc$.

\item \textbf{Objection: R-097 already proves R-085 (6.5).}
\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  It terminalizes
$\mathcal U_R+F_{6.5}$.  Equation (4.3) retains
$-\E\mathcal U_R$, so a suitable upper form is still required.

\item \textbf{Objection: the rational sign fixture disproves the paid torus
packet.}  \textbf{DISMISSED.}  It has no spatial control realization, low
endpoint, heat packet, forest, or Cameron--Martin cost.

\item \textbf{Objection: reverse subvisits contradict the exact full action.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  The two atoms cancel identically in (6.5).  The
contradiction applies only after squaring and assigning nonnegative ledgers
to the visits separately.

\item \textbf{Objection: the far harmonic disappears at a positive floor or
after target heat.}  \textbf{DISMISSED.}  The anisotropic path stays at
distance at least $A\epsilon$ from the tip, and (6.7) is an $L^2$ limit with
fixed heat and floor.  Every fixed gap has a nonzero coefficient (6.9).

\item \textbf{Objection: at $\epsilon=1/2$ the coefficient is
$-8/3^{k+2}$.}  \textbf{DISMISSED BY INDEPENDENT FACTOR AUDIT.}  Substitution
in (6.9) gives $-8/3^{k+1}$; direct quadrature gives $-8/27$ at $k=2$ and
$-8/81$ at $k=3$.

\item \textbf{Objection: the resampling kernel closes the posterior form.}
\textbf{UPHELD as premature.}  Equation (7.6) is the bare covariance/control
kernel.  The spatial multiplier, low-Hermite pairing, Schur mean, sextic
growth, trace, heat, low endpoint, and forest still require one integrated
estimate.

\item \textbf{Objection: the new no-go withdraws R-092 regular $H_C$.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  The fixture refines one source-block secant into opposite
subvisits with the same heat/root direction.  R-092 is explicitly a mutually
orthogonal no-revisit one-shot theorem.
\end{enumerate}

External review should focus on the radial-chain factor in (6.7), the
Fourier normalization in (6.9)--(6.10), the payment split in (2.2), and the
minus sign on $\mathcal U_R$ in (4.3).
