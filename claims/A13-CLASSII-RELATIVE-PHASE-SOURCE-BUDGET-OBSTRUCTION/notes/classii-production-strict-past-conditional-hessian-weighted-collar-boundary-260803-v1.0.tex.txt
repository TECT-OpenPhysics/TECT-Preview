\section*{1. Purpose and scope: identify the actual conditional owner}
\hypertarget{section-1-purpose}{ }

R-151 proves a strict local Hessian gap at zero deterministic strict past for
the complete linear $p:2p$ endpoint.  R-152 derives an affine-past chain rule,
a conditional absolute collar, and the abstract nonlinear and multi-root
acceptance gates.  It deliberately stops before identifying the conditional
operator on actual production incidences.  The present result performs that
identification for the last fresh root and records the strongest consequence
that follows without inventing a new owner.

There are four conclusions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item After conditioning on the strict past, the fresh current covariance
cancels exactly against its part of the A7 trace counterterm.  The remaining
endpoint contains the past covariance with a minus sign.  Its first variation
and complete symmetric bilinear Hessian are displayed below.
\item The terminal sixth-power and Cameron--Martin source Hessians have the
exact factors $9/10$ and $9/10$.  A curved control chart adds the first
variation applied to the chart acceleration; fixed-law differentiation does
not create an unlabelled Malliavin term.
\item Replacing the R-152 spatial essential suprema by spatial $L^2$ norms
gives a strictly more flexible pathwise collar.  The exact A1 source symbol
also gives the control-generated mean bound
\[
 \|\nabla m_h\|_2^2 < {41\over20}\|h_-\|_{\mathcal S}^2.
\]
Neither statement controls the revealed Gaussian past unconditionally.
\item A nondegenerate derivative-active Gaussian past has unbounded support,
so neither the R-152 absolute collar nor the new $L^2$ collar can hold with a
fixed almost-sure threshold in general.  Event-localized predictable controls
show that an all-directions weighted-average version is equivalent to the
fiberwise bound.  The signed covariance-normal completion remains necessary.
\end{enumerate}

This is a scoped T4 boundary.  It does not construct the earlier-root
feedback, complete forest/low/balanced assembly, or the multi-root production
matrix.  T-050, A13, Nelson, and Sector A remain open.  No phase or PDE verdict
follows.

\section*{2. Exact strict-past reduction}
\hypertarget{section-2-reduction}{ }

Fix the A7 six-real convention, positive coefficient floor, finite cutoff,
and one common real-even regulator.  Condition on the strict-past sigma-field
$\mathcal F_-$.  For almost every past realization write
\[
 W=m+X,
 \qquad
 V_i=n_i+Y_i,
 \]
where $(X,Y_i)$ is the centered fresh Gaussian field-current pair.  Assume
the production same-point identities
\[
 \Ef[XY_i^T]=0,
 \qquad
 \Ef[Y_iY_i^T]=\Gamma_{>,i},
 \qquad
 \Gamma_{\Lambda,i}=\Gamma_{<,i}+\Gamma_{>,i}.       \tag{2.1}
\]
For a whitened source direction $a$, let the actual final-root incidences be
\[
 z_a=S_xa,
 \qquad
 u_{i,a}=S_{v,i}a.                                   \tag{2.2}
\]
Production links these quantities:
\[
 n_i=\partial_i m,
 \qquad
 S_{v,i}=\partial_iS_x.                              \tag{2.3}
\]
They are not freely variable model parameters.

Let $B=C_6^*C_6$ be the complete positive Gram coefficient and define
\[
 \overline B_a(x)=\Ef B(m+X+z_a).                    \tag{2.4}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (future-current/trace cancellation)}
\hypertarget{theorem-2-1-cancellation}{ }

The conditional A7 endpoint for the declared final fresh root is exactly
\[
\boxed{
 \cR_-(a)={1\over2}\int\sum_i
 \left[
 (n_i+u_{i,a})^T\overline B_a(n_i+u_{i,a})
 -\Tr(\overline B_a\Gamma_{<,i})
 \right]dx .}                                       \tag{2.5}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Before integrating the fresh current, the $i$th A7 contribution is
\[
 {1\over2}\Ef\left[
 (n_i+u_i+Y_i)^TB(m+X+z)(n_i+u_i+Y_i)
 -\Tr\{B(m+X+z)\Gamma_{\Lambda,i}\}
 \right].                                           \tag{2.6}
\]
Joint Gaussianity and the first identity in (2.1) make $X$ and $Y_i$
independent.  Thus the linear $Y_i$ terms vanish and
\[
 \Ef[Y_i^TB(m+X+z)Y_i]
 =\Tr(\overline B_z\Gamma_{>,i}).                    \tag{2.7}
\]
The future trace cancels against the future part of
$\Gamma_{\Lambda,i}$, leaving
\[
 \Tr(\overline B_z\Gamma_{>,i})
 -\Tr\{\overline B_z(\Gamma_{<,i}+\Gamma_{>,i})\}
 =-\Tr(\overline B_z\Gamma_{<,i}).                  \tag{2.8}
\]
This proves (2.5). \hfill$\square$

The cancellation is signed and owner-complete for this conditional endpoint.
It must occur before any absolute-value estimate.  The result would change if
the regulator were asymmetric, the same-point field-current covariance were
nonzero, or the conditional law moved with $a$.

\section*{3. First variation, Hessian, and form criterion}
\hypertarget{section-3-hessian}{ }

At $a=0$ put $W=m+X$.  For directions $\alpha,\beta$, abbreviate
\[
 z_\alpha=S_x\alpha,
 \quad u_{i,\alpha}=S_{v,i}\alpha,
 \quad
 \overline{DB[z_\alpha]}=\Ef DB(W)[z_\alpha],       \tag{3.1}
\]
and similarly for $D^2B$.

\subsection*{Proposition 3.1 (complete first and second variations)}

The first variation is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 D\cR_-(0)[\alpha]=\int\sum_i\bigg[
 &u_{i,\alpha}^T\overline B_0n_i\\
 &+{1\over2}\Tr\!\left(
 \overline{DB[z_\alpha]}(n_in_i^T-\Gamma_{<,i})
 \right)\bigg]dx .
\end{aligned}}                                      \tag{3.2}
\]
The symmetric bilinear Hessian is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 D^2\cR_-(0)[\alpha,\beta]=\int\sum_i\bigg[
 &u_{i,\alpha}^T\overline B_0u_{i,\beta}\\
 &+u_{i,\alpha}^T\overline{DB[z_\beta]}n_i
  +u_{i,\beta}^T\overline{DB[z_\alpha]}n_i\\
 &+{1\over2}\Tr\!\left(
 \overline{D^2B[z_\alpha,z_\beta]}
 (n_in_i^T-\Gamma_{<,i})
 \right)\bigg]dx .
\end{aligned}}                                      \tag{3.3}
\]
On the diagonal, the two cross orientations combine to coefficient two.
The past-covariance contribution retains coefficient $-1/2$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Differentiate the one scalar endpoint (2.5).  The quadratic current factor
contributes two first-current orientations and one current-current term.  The
field dependence contributes one $DB$ and one $D^2B$.  Polarization gives
(3.3), including the two separately oriented cross terms. \hfill$\square$

Equation (3.2) is generally nonzero.  Positive Hessian curvature alone does
not prove that the base point is stationary or a local minimum.

\subsection*{Exact sixth-power and source owners}

For
\[
 \cS_6(a)={3\over20}\int\Ef|W+z_a|^6dx,
\]
the exact derivatives are
\[
 D\cS_6(0)[\alpha]
 ={9\over10}\int\Ef[|W|^4W\mathbin{\cdot}z_\alpha]dx,       \tag{3.4}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 D^2\cS_6(0)[\alpha,\beta]
 ={9\over10}\int\Ef\big[&|W|^4z_\alpha\mathbin{\cdot}z_\beta\\
 &+4|W|^2(W\mathbin{\cdot}z_\alpha)
              (W\mathbin{\cdot}z_\beta)\big]dx .
\end{aligned}}                                      \tag{3.5}
\]
This Hessian is positive semidefinite, but it need not be strictly positive.
In whitened source coordinates the Cameron--Martin action is
\[
 \cC(a)={9\over20}|a|^2,
 \qquad D^2\cC={9\over10}\Id.                       \tag{3.6}
\]
For nonwhitened coordinates, $\Id$ is replaced by the actual source Gram.

Let $K_E$ and $K_6$ denote the conditional endpoint and sixth-power forms
without the source term.  The requested one-tenth curvature gap is exactly
\[
\boxed{
 K_E+K_6\succeq-{4\over5}\Id
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 K_E+K_6+{9\over10}\Id\succeq{1\over10}\Id.}       \tag{3.7}
\]
This is stronger than mere nonnegative curvature, whose endpoint-plus-sixth
threshold is $-9\Id/10$.

\subsection*{Connection and domain firewall}

For a curved source path
\[
 a(t)=a_0+t\alpha+{1\over2}t^2\eta+o(t^2),
\]
the chain rule is
\[
 {d^2\over dt^2}\cA(a(t))\big|_{t=0}
 =D^2\cA(a_0)[\alpha,\alpha]+D\cA(a_0)[\eta].       \tag{3.8}

The source connection is $9a_0\mathbin{\cdot}\eta/10$.  Endpoint and
sixth-power connections need not vanish.  Gaussian-source pullback has its

Hessian (3.3).

For the strict-past measurable matrix $K=K_E+K_6$, the natural absolute form

 \cD_{|K|^{1/2}}
 =\{\phi:\E|\phi|^2+\E[\phi^T|K|\phi]<\infty\}.     \tag{3.9}

At finite cutoff and fixed floor, bounded localized simple controls form the

 \E[\phi^T(K+4\Id/5)\phi]\ge0

\section*{4. Spatially weighted collar}
\hypertarget{section-4-collar}{ }

Conditioned on the past, define the unnormalized spatial quantities
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A_n^2&=\int\sum_i|n_i|^2dx,\\
 A_W^2&=\int\Ef|m+X|^2dx,\\
 A_Y^2&=\int\Ef\sum_i|Y_i|^2dx.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{4.1}
\]
Let $V$ be the torus volume, $\lambda_2=\lambda_{\min}A(2p)$, and retain the
exact R-130 envelopes
\[
 L_6={1143\over250P},
 \qquad H_6={7083\over500P},
 \qquad P=4+10^{-12}.                                \tag{4.2}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 4.1 (conditional spatial L2 collar)}

Define
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 C_{2,p}={}&{8\sqrt3L_6|p|\over V\lambda_2}A_nA_W
 +{4\sqrt3H_6\over V\lambda_2}A_nA_Y\\
 &+{2H_6\over V\lambda_2}A_n^2.
\end{aligned}}                                      \tag{4.3}
\]
Then the retained R-152 fixed-law chart obeys
\[
 \cA''(0)[H,H]>
 \left({7\over50}-C_{2,p}\right)\|H\|_{\Frob}^2.  \tag{4.4}
\]
In particular, $C_{2,p}\le1/25$ preserves curvature above $1/10$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Spatial Cauchy--Schwarz applied to the three R-152 current-mean terms gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 |T_1|&\le2\sqrt3L_6beA_nA_W\|H\|_{\Frob}^2,\\
 |T_2|&\le2\sqrt3H_6b^2A_nA_Y\|H\|_{\Frob}^2,\\
 |T_3|&\le H_6b^2A_n^2\|H\|_{\Frob}^2.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{4.5}
\]
Here $b^2\le2/(V\lambda_2)$ and $e=2|p|b$.  Substitution gives (4.3), and
R-152 supplies the $7/50$ base gap. \hfill$\square$

For normalized RMS quantities $a_n=A_n/\sqrt V$, $a_W=A_W/\sqrt V$, and
$a_Y=A_Y/\sqrt V$, the explicit $1/V$ disappears.  Also
$A_n\le\sqrt V N$, $A_W\le\sqrt V M_W$, and
$A_Y\le\sqrt V M_Y$, so (4.3) is no larger than the R-152 essential-supremum
collar.  Its coefficients are exact for the declared R-130 plus
Cauchy--Schwarz envelope; optimality is not claimed.

For a fixed predictable direction $H$, a weighted condition
\[
 \E[C_{2,p}\|H\|_{\Frob}^2]
 \le {1\over25}\E\|H\|_{\Frob}^2                  \tag{4.6}
\]
is sufficient.  If (4.6) is demanded for every event-localized predictable
$H$, however, it is equivalent to $C_{2,p}\le1/25$ almost surely.  Therefore
the new collar is spatially weaker but not probabilistically weaker as a
uniform all-directions theorem.

\section*{5. Exact A1 control-mean gradient constant}
\hypertarget{section-5-gradient}{ }

For the canonical control-generated mean $m_h=Lh_-$, Parseval and
$LL^*\preceq A^{-1}$ give
\[
 \|\nabla m_h\|_2^2
 \le\sup_{x\ge0}{x\over f(x)}\|h_-\|_{\mathcal S}^2,
 \qquad
 f(x)=x^2-ax+c,                                      \tag{5.1}
\]
where
\[
 a={4626377063\over5000000000},
 \qquad
 c={5020336473\over10000000000}.                    \tag{5.2}
\]
Since
\[
 {d\over dx}{x\over f(x)}={c-x^2\over f(x)^2},      \tag{5.3}
\]
the exact maximum is
\[
 c_{\rm grad}={1\over2\sqrt c-a}
 =2.033300406271083\ldots .                          \tag{5.4}
\]
The rational certificate
\[
 {41\over20}f(x)-x
 ={410000000000x^2-579362919166x+205833795393
   \over200000000000}                               \tag{5.5}
\]
has discriminant
\[
 -{476508084992737466111\over10^{22}}<0.             \tag{5.6}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{
 \|\nabla m_h\|_2^2
 \le c_{\rm grad}\|h_-\|_{\mathcal S}^2
 <{41\over20}\|h_-\|_{\mathcal S}^2.}             \tag{5.7}
\]
This bound concerns the mean generated by the control and its raw source
norm.  It does not apply to a revealed Gaussian mean, does not include the
$9/20$ action coefficient, and does not by itself pay a product with the
current direction.

\section*{6. Gaussian-past and ultraviolet obstruction}
\hypertarget{section-6-obstruction}{ }

\subsection*{Proposition 6.1 (no deterministic absolute collar for a
nondegenerate derivative-active Gaussian past)}

Assume one uncancelled revealed root has coefficient
$g\sim\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma>0$ and spatial profile $\varphi$
such that $\|\nabla\varphi\|_\infty>0$.  Condition on all other roots and
write
\[
 m=m_0+g\varphi,
 \qquad n=\nabla m.                                  \tag{6.1}
\]
Then
\[
 N(g)\ge |g|\|\nabla\varphi\|_\infty
       -\|\nabla m_0\|_\infty.                       \tag{6.2}
\]
The R-152 collar contains the strictly positive term
$2H_6N(g)^2/\lambda_2$.  Hence
\[
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_g C_{\rm past}(g)=\infty,
 \qquad
 \Pr\{C_{\rm past}>K\mid m_0\}>0                  \tag{6.3}
\]
for every finite $K$.  The same conclusion applies to $C_{2,p}$ when the
$L^2$ derivative profile is nonzero.  Therefore the deterministic
almost-sure threshold $C\le1/25$ cannot close the general Gaussian strict
past.

The conclusion is unbounded support and positive-probability violation, not
almost-sure violation.  Small $|g|$ can satisfy the collar with positive
probability.  A zero mode, a derivative-inactive root, or an exact signed
feedback cancellation lies outside this fixture.

Event localization gives the companion logical boundary.  If
\[
 \E[C_{2,p}\|H\|^2]\le\kappa\E\|H\|^2              \tag{6.4}
\]
holds for every bounded predictable direction and the core admits
$H=H_0\mathbf1_E$, take
$E=\{C_{2,p}>\kappa+\epsilon\}$.  Equation (6.4) fails unless this event has
zero probability.  Thus an averaged multiplier cannot evade (6.3) for the
full predictable class.

There is a separate ultraviolet warning.  In the A1 sharp-cube covariance,
\[
 \Tr\Gamma(k)={6\over Y_{\rm kin}|k|^4}+O(|k|^{-6}),
\]
so the separated future-current envelope obeys
\[
 A_{Y,\Lambda}^2
 =\sum_{k\in\text{cube}}|k|^2\Tr\Gamma(k)
 =\Theta(V\Lambda),
 \qquad
 A_{Y,\Lambda}=\Theta((V\Lambda)^{1/2}).             \tag{6.5}
\]
Indeed the exact max-norm shell has $24r^2+2$ lattice points, giving
\[
 8N\le
 \sum_{0<\|n\|_\infty\le N}{1\over|n|^2}
 \le26N.                                             \tag{6.6}
\]
Indexing and cutoff conventions change the leading constant, not the linear
growth for a positive-density future tail.  This is the already registered
separated-current obstruction, not a divergence of the covariance-normal
endpoint: the signed trace cancellation in Theorem 2.1 removes the primitive
future-current divergence.

Finally, $B\succeq0$ does not sign (3.3).  In the scalar quadratic row
$B(w)=\kappa w^2$, the matrix in the $(z,u)$ variables is
\[
 \kappa\begin{pmatrix}
 n^2-\sigma_{<}^2&2mn\\
 2mn&m^2+\sigma_X^2
 \end{pmatrix}.                                     \tag{6.7}
\]
At $m=n=\sigma_X^2=1$ and $\sigma_<^2=1/2$, its determinant is $-3\kappa^2$.
This is a logical sign fixture, not a production counterexample.  It explains
why the covariance deficit and $DB$ cross terms must remain in one signed
operator.

\section*{8. Devil's-advocate review}
\hypertarget{section-8-devils-advocate}{ }

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: zero field-current covariance is only uncorrelated,
not independent.} \textbf{DISMISSED under the declared hypothesis.}  The
fresh pair is jointly Gaussian, so zero cross covariance gives independence.
The result does not extend this inference to non-Gaussian laws.

\item \textbf{Objection: $m,n,z,u$ were varied independently.}
\textbf{VALID-with-mitigation.}  Equations (2.2)--(2.3) retain the production
incidences $n=\nabla m$ and $u=\nabla z$.  Independent scalar fixtures audit

\item \textbf{Objection: the positive sixth-power Hessian makes the full
operator positive.} \textbf{UPHELD against that inference.}  It may vanish,
and no registered theorem compares it uniformly with the signed covariance
deficit and cross terms.

\item \textbf{Objection: the spatial $L^2$ collar solves the random-past
problem by averaging.} \textbf{UPHELD.}  It improves only the spatial norm.
For all predictable directions, event localization restores the almost-sure
multiplier condition.

\item \textbf{Objection: Gaussian support proves the collar is violated
almost surely.} \textbf{UPHELD against that stronger wording.}  The proved
statement is unbounded essential support and positive-probability violation.

\item \textbf{Objection: the $41/20$ bound pays the full source budget.}
\textbf{UPHELD.}  It bounds only the control-generated mean gradient in the
raw source norm.  It is not the $9/20$ action payment and does not control the
revealed past.

\item \textbf{Objection: $A_Y\sim\sqrt\Lambda$ proves the PDE or A7 composite
diverges.} \textbf{UPHELD against that inference.}  Only the separated
absolute current envelope diverges.  The signed future-current/trace
cancellation is exact.

\item \textbf{Objection: positive curvature proves a local minimum.}
\textbf{UPHELD.}  The first variation (3.2) and chart connections may be
nonzero.

\item \textbf{Objection: this result selects BCC, a uniform phase, or another
PDE.} \textbf{UPHELD against every such inference.}  R-153 is phase-neutral
conditional mathematics and supplies no physical model-selection verdict.
\end{enumerate}
