\section*{1. Purpose and scope}

R-133 isolated three possible successors: a blockwise score theorem, a direct
fixed-law estimate, and an aggregate positive-exponent collar estimate.  R-134
tests all three against the complete six-row current and the existing
root/visit/forest ownership.

The outcome is a real advance but not production closure.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The last independent six-real Gaussian source block in a fixed R-104
chart can regularise the rational coefficient without an inverse feedback
Jacobian, provided its pointwise covariance has an added uniform full-rank
lower bound.  Sharp six-dimensional negative moments control the second and
third quotient jets.  The fourth jet is critical and has an explicit
logarithmic $L^2$ divergence.
\item The density floor can be removed at the action level before any
differentiation.  The complete Pauli sum improves the naive rowwise constant:
\[
 \|C_{6,e}(w)v-C_{6,0}(w)v\|
 \le\alpha\sqrt{c_1e}\,|v|,
 \qquad \alpha^2c_1={3\over320P}.                 \tag{1.1}
\]
After all owners are recombined, one Hilbert direct-sum inequality has no
root or visit multiplicity.  It reduces the action problem to one signed
forest-minus-weighted-current form.
\item The weighted current energy cannot then be paid separately by source
and sextic.  The uncontrolled covariance-normal Gaussian has bounded value
moments but linearly divergent gradient energy.  Separating the forest
therefore destroys the Wick cancellation which the forest was built to keep.
\item A conditional root-atom estimate with spatial exponent $s>7/12$ does
sum to the accepted $\cB_{7/12}^{\rm out}$ ledger with an exact geometric
constant.  At $s=2/3$ the square constant is
$0.658881625872614\ldots$.  A direct fixed-collar form has the smaller
constant $0.285928885855479\ldots$.  Neither conditional estimate supplies
its own production atom or one-use $q_k$ ledger.
\end{enumerate}

The result is T4 at fixed finite cutoff and positive floor.  It does not prove
a terminal covariance lower bound uniform across cutoffs, charts,
subdivisions, or refinements; an owner-preserving terminal disintegration; the complete signed
forest-current inequality; the production one-use atom hypotheses; a
collar-uniform near/balanced headroom; the low matrix, matching energy, strict
gap, or absolute anchor; either A13 gate; $\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$;
Nelson; removals; an interacting measure; or Sector-A closure.

\section*{6. Sharp six-real Gaussian negative moments}

Let $G$ be standard Gaussian in $\mathbb R^6$ and
\[
 X=m+\Gamma^{1/2}G,\qquad \Gamma\succeq\lambda I_6,
 \qquad \lambda>0.                                  \tag{6.1}
\]

\paragraph{Theorem 6.1 (terminal Gaussian anti-concentration).}
For every $e\ge0$ and $0<q<6$,
\[
\boxed{
 \E(e+|X|^2)^{-q/2}
 \le C_{6,q}\lambda^{-q/2},\qquad
 C_{6,q}={\Gamma(3-q/2)\over2^{1+q/2}}.}           \tag{6.2}
\]
The constant is sharp as $e\downarrow0$, $m=0$, and
$\Gamma=\lambda I_6$.  In particular,
\[
                   C_{6,2}={1\over4},\qquad
                   C_{6,4}={1\over8}.               \tag{6.3}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Write $\Gamma=\lambda I_6+\Sigma$ and condition on the independent Gaussian
with covariance $\Sigma$.  The function $(e+|x|^2)^{-q/2}$ is radial and
decreasing.  Anderson's inequality, equivalently its layer-cake form for
centred Gaussian balls, makes every conditional translation no larger than
the centred $N(0,\lambda I_6)$ value.  For $R=|G|$,
\[
 \E R^{-q}
 =2^{-q/2}{\Gamma((6-q)/2)\over\Gamma(3)}
 ={\Gamma(3-q/2)\over2^{1+q/2}}.                   \tag{6.4}
\]
This proves (6.2).  Equality in the limiting centred isotropic case proves
sharpness. \hfill$\square$

At $q=6$ the Gamma factor has a pole.  Radially, the squared critical jet has
the integral $\int_0^1r^5r^{-6}\,dr$, hence logarithmic divergence.

\section*{7. Normalized quotient jet threshold}

For $S=S^T$, $\|S\|\le1$, let
\[

\[

 \|D\mathcal J_S\|\le2+4+4+4=14,\qquad
 \|D^2\mathcal J_S\|\le2+4+4+8+8+4=30.            \tag{7.2}

Moreover
\[
