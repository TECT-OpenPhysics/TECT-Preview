\section{Purpose and scope}

R-080 left three analytic inputs between the exact R-079 complete packet and
the controlled-shell one-use theorem: FAR, NEAR, and the full
progressive/revisit extension.  This note does not declare any of those three
inputs complete.  It replaces each broad label by a sharper theorem or
obstruction and proves every step that does not require the missing
root-resolved production estimate.

The new positive statements are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the production current splits exactly into a spatially quadratic
      polynomial channel and one heat-averaged nonlinear Cartan channel;
\item the polynomial channel vanishes identically at sufficiently separated
      output shells;
\item every band-limited deterministic Cartan current has a genuine relative-gap
      tail of order $N^{-2s}$ for $0<s<1$, with the exact critical budget
      $X^{1/2}Y^{1/2}$;
\item predictable fixed-coefficient Gaussian injection reduces to a
      triangular half-derivative coefficient norm;
\item an explicitly factorised first-order NEAR martingale response has the
      required vector-valued Cameron--Martin and terminal-sixth-moment
      budgets, and therefore its conditional gain ledger is exact on the
      stochastic-input side;
\item every bounded simple progressive time packet admits strict-past
      covariance factorisation, and the complete R-079 algebra extends to
      overlapping temporal packets.
\end{enumerate}

The new negative statements are equally load-bearing.  Summing the
deterministic FAR estimate separately over probability roots repeats a
critical global budget.  Subcritical $H^{1/2-}$ coefficient control does not
bound the triangular injection norm.  A complete nonlinear coefficient
innovation does not factor through $D_j=d_jA^*$ alone; its exact Jensen defect
can be nonzero when $D_j=0$.  An absolute control--control pair-high
extraction is supercritical for the entire candidate NEAR gain range.
Finally, the R-075 one-shot graph is not dense in the progressive class even
at one scalar mode and in terminal $L^2$.

All statements are at finite cutoff, fixed positive production floor, common
even regulator, exact products, and the hash-pinned A1 production frame.
No root-resolved FAR bound, adapted lower-chaos-complete NEAR operator bound,
signed control--control NEAR theorem, overlap-stable progressive packet lower
bound, controlled-shell one-use estimate, Nelson bound, measure theorem,
removal limit, or Sector-A closure is asserted.  The claim remains T4.

\section{Authority and production current coordinates}

Suppress the spatial direction and the positive $Q_{\rm II}$ contraction.
For each symmetric production generator $S_A$, define
\[
 \rho_e(z)=|z|^2+e,\qquad n_A(z)=z^TS_Az,
 \qquad q_A(z)={n_A(z)\over\rho_e(z)},                    \tag{2.1}
\]
and
\[
 p_A(z)=2S_Az,\qquad
 r_A(z)=(S_A-q_A(z)\Id)z,\qquad
 v_A(z)=2r_A(z).                                          \tag{2.2}
\]
The exact production frame is $M_A=[p_A,v_A]$.  The R-068 production
identities give
\[
 |q_A(z)|\le1,\qquad |r_A(z)|\le|z|,
 \qquad Dq_A(z)[h]={2r_A(z)\cdot h\over\rho_e(z)}.       \tag{2.3}
\]
R-079 supplies the complete expectation-level current/feedback identity,
R-080 its regular low bounds and far square completion, and R-063 the
deterministic translated balanced-jet reconstruction.  Those authorities are
used only within their declared scopes.

Introduce the quotient vector
\[
 F_A(z)=q_A(z)z.                                          \tag{2.4}
\]
It is odd, vanishes at zero, and is globally Lipschitz uniformly in the
positive floor.  Indeed
\[
 DF_A(z)[h]=q_A(z)h+zDq_A(z)[h]
\]
and (2.3) gives
\[
 |DF_A(z)[h]|
 \le |h|+2{|z|^2\over\rho_e(z)}|h|\le3|h|.              \tag{2.5}
\]
For any centered target Gaussian covariance $\Sigma$, translation coupling
therefore gives
\[
 \operatorname{Lip}(P_\Sigma F_A)\le3,\qquad
 (P_\Sigma F_A)(0)=0.                                    \tag{2.6}
\]
The constants in later generic current estimates may depend on the fixed
floor through higher frame derivatives, but (2.5)--(2.6) do not.

\section{Exact polynomial--Cartan current split}

\textbf{Theorem 3.1 (production current split).}
For every spatially differentiable $z$,
\[
 p_A(z)^TDz=Dn_A(z),                                     \tag{3.1}
\]
and
\[
 v_A(z)^TDz
 =Dn_A(z)-q_A(z)D\rho_e(z)
 =\rho_e(z)Dq_A(z).                                      \tag{3.2}
\]
Moreover, for centered target heat,
\[
 P_\Sigma p_A=p_A,\qquad
 P_\Sigma v_A=p_A-2P_\Sigma F_A.                        \tag{3.3}
\]
Consequently the two columns of the heat-averaged spatial current are
\[
 Dn_A(z),\qquad
 Dn_A(z)-2(P_\Sigma F_A)(z)^TDz.                         \tag{3.4}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Symmetry of $S_A$ gives
$Dn_A=2(S_Az)^TDz=p_A^TDz$.  Since
$D\rho_e=2z^TDz$ and
$v_A=p_A-2q_Az$, the first equality in (3.2) follows.
Differentiating $n_A=q_A\rho_e$ gives the second.  The map $p_A$ is linear
and the target Gaussian is centered, while
$v_A=p_A-2F_A$; this proves (3.3) and (3.4). \hfill$\square$

If $z$ has spatial Fourier support in $|k|\le c\Lambda$, then $Dn_A(z)$
has support in $|k|\le2c\Lambda$.  Hence for a sufficiently large fixed
dyadic separation $C_0$,
\[
 \Pi_mDn_A(z)=0\qquad(m\ge j+C_0,\ \Lambda=2^j).         \tag{3.5}
\]
Thus R-080's far loss has no genuinely nonlinear contribution from the
$p_A$ column or the $Dn_A$ part of the $v_A$ column.  The sole load-bearing
spatial tail is the heat-averaged quotient-vector, or Cartan, part of the
second column.  This is an exact production simplification, not a bound for
the full $S_C$ sum.

\section{Deterministic FAR theorem and its critical ledger}

Let $\Pi_{\ge N\Lambda}$ be a smooth high-frequency projector, let
$0<s<1$, and let $z$ be spatially band-limited to $c\Lambda$.  Put
$\overline F_A=P_\Sigma F_A$ for any centered target covariance.  This is
the nonlinear Cartan coefficient isolated in (3.4), not the full two-column
frame $M_A$.

\textbf{Theorem 4.1 (relative-gap current tail).}
At the fixed floor,
\[
 \|\Pi_{\ge N\Lambda}[\overline F_A(z)^TDz]\|_2^2
 \le C_{e,s}N^{-2s}\|z\|_6^2\|Dz\|_3^2                \tag{4.1}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \|\Pi_{\ge N\Lambda}[\overline F_A(z)^TDz]\|_2^2
 \le C_{e,s}N^{-2s}\|z\|_{H^2}\|z\|_6^3.             \tag{4.2}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Oddness gives $\overline F_A(0)=0$, and (2.6) gives the uniform Lipschitz
ceiling three after target convolution.  The fractional product and Nemytskii
estimates give
\[
 \|\overline F_A(z)^TDz\|_{H^s_2}
 \lesssim
 \|\overline F_A(z)\|_6\|Dz\|_{W^{s,3}}
 +\|\overline F_A(z)\|_{W^{s,6}}\|Dz\|_3.            \tag{4.3}
\]
Lipschitz composition and Bernstein bound the right side by
$C_{e,s}\Lambda^s\|z\|_6\|Dz\|_3$.  The high-frequency tail estimate
$\|\Pi_{\ge N\Lambda}f\|_2\lesssim(N\Lambda)^{-s}
\|f\|_{H^s_2}$ proves (4.1).  Finally the three-dimensional interpolation
\[
 \|Dz\|_3\lesssim
 \|z\|_{H^2}^{1/2}\|z\|_6^{1/2}                       \tag{4.4}
\]
gives (4.2). \hfill$\square$

With $X=\|z\|_{H^2}^2$ and $Y=\|z\|_6^6$, the right side of (4.2) is
\[
 C_{e,s}N^{-2s}X^{1/2}Y^{1/2}.                          \tag{4.5}
\]
There is a genuine and arbitrarily high subunit relative-gap exponent in
$N$, but the energy ledger at one probability root is exactly critical.
If a root-resolved theorem allowed the factor $2^{-\theta C}$ to remain
after the $j$ sum, a sufficiently large fixed $C$ and scalar Young would pay
arbitrary $\eta\E X+\zeta\E Y+C_{\eta,\zeta}$.  Applying (4.2)
independently for every $j$ instead repeats the same global $X/Y$ payment and
does not prove R-080's
\[
 S_C=\E\sum_j\sum_{m\ge j+C}
       \|\Pi_m d_j\cJ_j\|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2.               \tag{4.6}
\]

The precise sufficient FAR successor is a martingale-root-resolved estimate
of the form
\[
 \sum_j\E\|\Pi_{\ge j+C}d_j\cJ_j\|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2
 \le C2^{-\theta C}
 \{1+\E[X^{1/2}Y^{1/2}]\},\qquad\theta>0,               \tag{4.7}
\]
with derivative injection, value innovation, and heat compensator retained
together.  Neither R-050's unshifted enhancement nor R-063's deterministic
translation theorem supplies (4.7) for random predictable translations.

\section{Fixed-coefficient injection and the half-derivative endpoint}

The predictable part of derivative injection can be isolated exactly.  Let
$g_j$ be a fresh Gaussian shell and let $f$ be independent of $g_j$.  Fourier
support gives, for $m\ge j+C$,
\[
 \Pi_m(fDg_j)=\Pi_m\{(\widetilde\Pi_m f)Dg_j\}.          \tag{5.1}
\]
Using the derivative-shell covariance
$\E|Dg_j(x)|^2\lesssim2^j$ yields
\[
 \E_{g_j}\|\Pi_m(fDg_j)\|_2^2
 \lesssim2^j\|\widetilde\Pi_m f\|_2^2.                 \tag{5.2}
\]
If the same coefficient $f$ occurs at every $j$, summing first in $j$ gives
\[
 \sum_m\sum_{j\le m-C}
 \E\|\Pi_m(fDg_j)\|_2^2
 \lesssim2^{-C}\|f\|_{H^{1/2}}^2.                      \tag{5.3}
\]
For predictable coefficients $f_j$, the exact sufficient object becomes the
triangular norm
\[
 \mathfrak C_C(f_\bullet)
 =\sum_m\sum_{j\le m-C}2^j
   \E\|\widetilde\Pi_m f_j\|_2^2.                       \tag{5.4}
\]
For production the natural predictable coefficient is
$f_j=(P_{\Sigma_{j+1:J}}F_A)(Z_{j-1})$.  The rest of the base increment still
contains the same-shell nonlinear difference and the heat compensator, so
(5.4) is a sharp subchannel reduction, not an identity for all of $S_C$.

The exponent $1/2$ in (5.3) is a true endpoint.  Take orthogonal dyadic
coefficients with
\[
 \|\Pi_m f^{(J)}\|_2^2=2^{-m},\qquad1\le m\le J.         \tag{5.5}
\]
For every $\delta>0$,
\[
 \sup_J\|f^{(J)}\|_{H^{1/2-\delta}}^2
 =\sup_J\sum_{m=1}^J2^{-2\delta m}<\infty,              \tag{5.6}
\]
whereas (5.4) with the same coefficient at every root grows as
\[
 \sum_{m>C}2^{-m}\sum_{j\le m-C}2^j
 \asymp2^{-C}J.                                          \tag{5.7}
\]
Therefore an $H^{1/2-}$ coefficient theorem alone cannot close FAR.  The
balanced R-063 subtraction, square--trace cancellation, or a genuine
martingale Carleson estimate must create the missing root summation.  This is
a method no-go, not a counterexample to the complete production packet.

\section{NEAR: vector budget and nonlinear factorisation boundary}

Let $\ell$ be the fixed low cutoff and define the high terminal control
martingale differences
\[
 D_j=d_j(A^*-P_\ell A^*),\qquad D=(D_j)_j.               \tag{6.1}
\]
All spatial norms below are taken with values in $\ell^2_j$.  Hilbert-valued
Doob orthogonality and the control-energy identification give
\[
 \E\|D\|_{H^2_x(\ell^2)}^2\lesssim\E X.                 \tag{6.2}
\]
Pointwise scalar Burkholder followed by spatial integration gives, up to the
already fixed-low term,
\[
 \E\|D\|_{L^6_x(\ell^2)}^6\lesssim\E Y.                 \tag{6.3}
\]
The vector-valued version of the R-079 interpolation is
\[
 \|D\|_{H^{2/5}_x(\ell^2)}
 \lesssim
 \|D\|_{H^2_x(\ell^2)}^{1/5}
 \|D\|_{L^6_x(\ell^2)}^{4/5}.                           \tag{6.4}
\]
Thus every response branch already factorised linearly through $D_j$ has the
exact stochastic input cost
\[
 X_D^{1/10}Y_D^{2/15}.                                   \tag{6.5}
\]

It is essential not to promote (6.5) to the whole nonlinear future
coefficient.  Such a coefficient is not determined by $D_j$.  Let
$\varepsilon_r=\operatorname{sgn}g_r$, where $g_j,g_{j+1}$ are independent
fresh Gaussian roots (bounded smooth approximants give the same limit), let
$\psi_p$ be a nonzero bounded deterministic carrier in a later shell
$p>j+1$, and take the progressive control
\[
 A(x)=\lambda\varepsilon_{j+1}(1+c\varepsilon_j)\psi_p(x)e_1,
 \qquad 0<c<1.                                           \tag{6.6}
\]
Then
\[
 d_jA=d_jDA=0,\qquad
 d_j|A|^2=2c\lambda^2\varepsilon_j|\psi_p|^2.
                                                                \tag{6.7}
\]
This blind spot occurs in the actual production coefficient.  With
$\alpha=5/9$ and the positive diagonal current weights $c_0,c_1$, the
pure-doublet production trace is
\[
 h(t):=\operatorname{Tr}B(te_1)
 =12c_0t^2+c_1\left\{12t^2-{8\alpha t^4\over t^2+e}
 +{4\alpha^2t^6\over(t^2+e)^2}\right\}.                 \tag{6.8}
\]
Consequently
\[
 d_jh(A)={\varepsilon_j\over2}
 \{h(\lambda(1+c)\psi_p)-h(\lambda(1-c)\psi_p)\}
 =24(c_0+c_1)c\lambda^2\varepsilon_j\psi_p^2
  +O_e(\lambda^4|\psi_p|^4)\ne0                       \tag{6.9}
\]
as a nonzero function in $L^1_x$ for all sufficiently small nonzero
$\lambda$.  Thus this production trace component is not determined by $D_j$.
The witness does not by itself exclude cancellation after recombination with
the complete square--trace--forest packet; rather, no componentwise or
complete representation can be inferred from $D_j$ alone without first
proving cancellation of this Jensen defect.

There is a canonical nonduplicating split of this blind spot.  Put
$a_j=P_jA^*$ and, for a deterministic $C^1$ coefficient $H$, define its
conditional Jensen defect
\[
 \mathfrak D_j^H=P_jH(A^*)-H(a_j).                       \tag{6.10}
\]
For every active root, $D_j=a_j-a_{j-1}$ and exactly
\[
 d_jH(A^*)=
 \int_0^1DH(a_{j-1}+tD_j)D_j\dd t
 +(\mathfrak D_j^H-\mathfrak D_{j-1}^H).                \tag{6.11}
\]
The first term is the genuine $D_j$ secant.  The second is an upper-triangular
conditional-variance/all-higher-chaos feedback generated by later roots; it
is the nonzero term in (6.9).  Production rational coefficients have no
finite-chaos truncation, so this Jensen-defect branch must remain complete.
Identity (6.11) is for one fixed deterministic or predictable $H$.  If the
coefficient family itself changes with the root, the additional heat or
projector compensator
$[H_j-H_{j-1}](a_{j-1})$ must also be retained.

There is nevertheless a precise conditional first-order route.  If an
explicitly projected response branch $R_j^{(1)}$ has the exact factorisation
$R_j^{(1)}=\mathbb K_j^{\rm near}[D_j]$, a sufficient vector-valued adapted
estimate is
\[
 \|\{\mathbb K_j^{\rm near}[D_j]\}_j\|_
       {H^{-3/5+\gamma}_x(\ell^2)}
 \le R_\gamma\|D\|_{H^{2/5}_x(\ell^2)},
 \qquad R_\gamma\in\bigcap_{p<\infty}L^p.               \tag{6.12}
\]
If (6.12) holds for any $\gamma>0$, pairing it with the R-078 payload at
$\beta=3/5-\gamma$ gives
\[
 R_\gamma X^{1/2-\gamma/4}Y^{1/2+\gamma/12}.            \tag{6.13}
\]
The exact ledger is
\[
 a={1\over2}-{\gamma\over4},\qquad
 b={1\over2}+{\gamma\over12},\qquad
 c=1-a-b={\gamma\over6},\qquad
 p_R={6\over\gamma}.                                    \tag{6.14}
\]
At $\gamma=1/20$ this is
\[
 (a,b,c,p_R)=
 \left({39\over80},{121\over240},{1\over120},120\right).
                                                               \tag{6.15}
\]
The budgets (6.2)--(6.5) and ledger (6.13)--(6.15) are proved for that
declared factorised branch,
conditional on (6.12).  The remaining nonlinear oscillation coordinate
\[
 \Omega_j(H)=d_jH(A^*)                                  \tag{6.16}
\]
must be estimated as a coefficient/complete-packet martingale square

Strict-past measurability does not freeze the coefficient at probability root

Its canonical weight

Hence the hidden future coefficient in R-080 cannot be replaced by an

\section{The control--control pair-high branch must remain signed}

A fresh-Gaussian/control high pair may be compatible with (6.12), because the
balanced Gaussian jet can in principle create gain without spending another
control factor.  The pure control--control pair-high route is different.
Suppose an absolute estimate exposes one additional comparable control factor
with interpolation fraction $\vartheta\in[0,1]$.  Its budget is
\[
 2^{-\vartheta p}X^{\vartheta/2}
 Y^{(1-\vartheta)/6}.                                    \tag{7.1}
\]
Adding (7.1) to the first-order gain ledger (6.13) changes the Young slack to
\[
 c_{\rm pair}
 ={\gamma\over6}-{\vartheta\over2}
  -{1-\vartheta\over6}
 ={\gamma-1-2\vartheta\over6}<0                         \tag{7.2}
\]
for every $0<\gamma<1/10$ and every $\vartheta\ge0$.
At $\vartheta=0$ the loss is already $(\gamma-1)/6$.

Therefore the control--control pair-high branch cannot be closed by exposing
and absolutely paying another control factor.  It must remain signed with the
terminal current square, covariance trace, paid subtraction, and complete
R-063 forest.  R-080's exact negative root fixture already excludes universal
pointwise square--trace positivity.  The precise second NEAR successor is thus
a production-specific signed control--control balanced-root lemma.  Equation
(7.2) forbids one architecture; it does not falsify that signed lemma or the
complete one-use estimate.

\section{Progressive temporal packet factorisation}

Let $I_k=(t_{k-1},t_k]$ be one interval of a finite time partition and let
$u_t=u_k$ there, with $u_k$ bounded, cylindrical, and
$\cF_{t_{k-1}}$-measurable.  Put
\[
 \Delta C_k=\int_{I_k}J_tJ_t^*\dd t,\qquad
 L_k=\int_{I_k}J_t\dd t,\qquad a_k=L_ku_k.              \tag{8.1}
\]

\textbf{Theorem 8.1 (strict-past temporal factorisation).}
There is a predictable $h_k$ such that
\[
 a_k=\Delta C_k^{1/2}h_k,
 \qquad\|h_k\|^2\le |I_k|\|u_k\|^2.                    \tag{8.2}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Operator Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 L_kL_k^*\preceq |I_k|\Delta C_k.                        \tag{8.3}
\]
Douglas factorisation writes
$L_k=\Delta C_k^{1/2}R_k$ with
$\|R_k\|\le|I_k|^{1/2}$.  Take $h_k=R_ku_k$. \hfill$\square$

The Gaussian increment on $I_k$ has covariance $\Delta C_k$ and is
independent of $\cF_{t_{k-1}}$, so every bounded simple progressive packet
enters the R-064 strict-past causalisation exactly.  The ranges
$\operatorname{Ran}\Delta C_k$ for different $k$ may overlap or coincide.
That overlap does not damage Hilbert martingale orthogonality, the deterministic
covariance split, or the simultaneous complete-coefficient split.  Therefore
the complete-current and complete-safe-packet algebra of R-079 temporalises
verbatim, including the $f_k$--$i_k$ cross term.

What does not temporalise is just as important.  The R-075 whole-shell graph,
R-066 cumulative orthogonal-energy calculation, and R-080 separate low and
far estimates use mutually orthogonal physical ranges.  They cannot be
applied packet by packet after the ranges overlap.  The complete endpoint
must first be recombined, and only then estimated uniformly in time mesh and
revisit multiplicity.

\section{Exact non-density of the one-shot graph}

The progressive gap is not a technical density omission.  It is already
visible at one scalar cutoff.  Let $J_t=1$ and let $W$ be one Brownian mode.
The bounded simple progressive control
\[
 u_t=\tanh(W_{1/2})\mathbf1_{(1/2,1]}(t)                 \tag{9.1}
\]
has terminal displacement
\[
 A(u)={1\over2}\tanh(W_{1/2}).                           \tag{9.2}
\]
Any regular one-shot displacement in this only physical range is
$\cF_0$-measurable.  Even if independent auxiliary randomness is placed in
$\cF_0$, it remains independent of $W_{1/2}$.  Since $\E A(u)=0$, for every
such displacement $a$,
\[
 \E|A(u)-a|^2
 =\E|A(u)|^2+\E|a|^2
 \ge\E|A(u)|^2>0.                                       \tag{9.3}
\]
Thus there is no terminal-$L^2$ approximation, hence no approximation in the
stronger R-075 graph containing terminal $L^6$.

Refining time makes each temporal packet strict-past but leaves every packet
in the same physical covariance range.  Orthogonalising the noise coordinates
does not make those physical ranges orthogonal while preserving the original
filtration and causality.  This proves that regular one-shot graph density is
not a route to the full progressive theorem.

The exact progressive successor is an overlap-stable complete-packet bound.
Writing
\[
 G_J(\phi)=V_J^{\rm ren}(\phi)+\epsilon_6\|\phi\|_6^6,
\]
one must prove, uniformly in $J$, in the time partition, and in revisit
multiplicity,
\[
 \E G_J(X_J+h_J(u))
 +\epsilon_v\E\sum_k|I_k|\|u_k\|^2\ge-C               \tag{9.4}
\]
for every bounded cylindrical simple progressive control.  Only after (9.4)
may one use fixed-cutoff admissible-core truncation, auxiliary conditioning,
terminal graph continuity, and lower semicontinuity to reach every finite-
energy Boue--Dupuis control.  Predictable $L^2$ density alone does not control
the terminal $L^6$ coordinate or an infinity-minus-infinity limit.
