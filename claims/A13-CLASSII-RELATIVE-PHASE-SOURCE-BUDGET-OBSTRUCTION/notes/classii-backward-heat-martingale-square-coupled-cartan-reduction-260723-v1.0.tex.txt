\section*{Purpose and scope}

This note continues the A13 strict-past proof without creating a new peer
claim.  It corrects the remaining charge by retaining the completed square,
removes the apparent shellwise heat-current drift by a terminal-backward Doob
martingale, and proves that the finite low block and the high-frequency
counterterm secants are not the final obstruction.  The complete finite-cutoff
problem is reduced to one averaged raw-current/Cartan secant with its positive
Gauss--Newton square retained.

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.91\textwidth}{
\textbf{Closed here.}  Exact square-coupled and Gibbs--Pythagoras identities;
the terminal-backward heat martingale and controlled telescope; uniform
$C^{1,1}$ heat regularity; exact scalar shellwise no-gos; the averaged
production frame/Cartan secant; a finite-low-block bound; and a summable
high-shell covariance-trace loss using the control energy once.

\medskip
\textbf{Still open.}  A cutoff-uniform lower bound for the remaining averaged
raw-current/Cartan--Jacobi secant under every regular declared strict-past
control.
Consequently no controlled-shell one-use theorem, Nelson moment, interacting
measure, regulator removal, or tier promotion is asserted.
}}
\end{center}

All exact identities are finite-dimensional and cutoff-wise.  The analytic
tail bounds use the hash-pinned $L=16$ A1 symbol, the common real-even sharp
spectral shells, one strict-past control block per mutually orthogonal shell,
the fixed positive density floor, and the six-real convention.  A general
progressive covariance flow which revisits the same Fourier range is not
silently identified with this ordered block class.  The controlled identities
and bounds are stated for bounded cylindrical controls, or more generally for
strict-past controls for which every displayed product is integrable.  An
extension to arbitrary finite-energy drifts by localization and lower
semicontinuity belongs to the successor gate and is not asserted here.

\section{The full score and the square that must remain}

Use the A11--A13 strict-past notation.  Conditional on $\cF_{j-1}$, the exact
shell increment is
\[
 F_j(u)=I_j(u)+C_j(u)
 ={1\over2}\{u^TT_ju-\Tr T_j\}+\langle s_j,u\rangle+N_j(u),
 \qquad s_j=\ell_j+m_j,                                      \tag{1.1}
\]
where $T_j\ge0$, $C_j(u)=\langle m_j,u\rangle+N_j(u)$, and
$N_j(0)=DN_j(0)=0$.  The A11 telescope says
\[
 V_J^{\rm ren}=\sum_{j\le J}F_j.                            \tag{1.2}
\]
If $\mathcal K_j u=(K_ju,DK_ju)$ denotes the physical value/derivative
displacement, then more is true:
\[
 F_j(u)=V_j^{\rm ren}(Z_{j-1}+\mathcal K_ju)
       -V_{j-1}^{\rm ren}(Z_{j-1}).                          \tag{1.3}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \langle s_j,v\rangle
 =DV_j^{\rm ren}(Z_{j-1})[\mathcal K_jv].                   \tag{1.4}
\]
Thus the full score is conservative at the energy-gradient level.  The
non-exact target-space current one-form found earlier is still present inside
the frame representation; it is not a curl of the complete shell energy.

Let $q>0$,
\[
 R_j=(\Id+qT_j)^{-1},\qquad H_j=T_j+q^{-1}\Id,              \tag{1.5}
\]
and let $\xi_j$ be standard Gaussian.  Put
\[
 \overline F_j(h)=\E_jF_j(\xi_j+h),\qquad
 \overline N_j(h)=\E_jN_j(\xi_j+h),                         \tag{1.6}
\]
\[
 P_j(h)={1\over2}\left|H_j^{1/2}(h+qR_js_j)\right|^2.       \tag{1.7}
\]
Exact metric completion gives
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \overline F_j(h)=-{|h|^2\over2q}-\mathcal S_j^\sharp(h),
 \quad
 \mathcal S_j^\sharp(h)
 ={q\over2}\langle s_j,R_js_j\rangle
 -\overline N_j(h)-P_j(h).
 \quad}                                                     \tag{1.8}
\]
The square $P_j$ is part of the charge.  Bounding
$q\langle s_j,R_js_j\rangle/2-\overline N_j$ alone is a
strictly stronger statement and repeats the earlier source-square error.
At $h=0$,
\[
 P_j(0)={q\over2}\langle s_j,R_js_j\rangle,\qquad
 \mathcal S_j^\sharp(0)=-\E_jN_j(\xi_j)=-\E_jF_j(\xi_j).   \tag{1.9}
\]
After averaging and summing, (1.2) gives
\[
 \E\sum_{j\le J}\mathcal S_j^\sharp(0)
 =-\E V_J^{\rm ren}(X_J)=0.                                \tag{1.10}
\]
Any shellwise positive-part or absolute-value estimate destroys this exact
base-law cancellation.

\subsection*{Conditional Gibbs--Pythagoras identity}

Assume first that the displayed exponential normalizer is finite and that the
shifted Gaussian has finite relative entropy against the conditional tilted
law.  The identities below then extend to the usual localized/truncated class
whenever both sides are finite.

Define
\[
 \kappa_j={1\over q}\log\E_j e^{-qF_j},\qquad
 \nu_j(\dd u)=e^{-qF_j(u)-q\kappa_j}\,\gamma_j(\dd u).       \tag{1.11}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \mathcal S_j^\sharp(h)
 =\kappa_j-{1\over q}H(\gamma_h\mid\nu_j).
 \quad}                                                     \tag{1.12}
\]
Indeed,
\[
 H(\gamma_h\mid\nu_j)
 ={|h|^2\over2}+q\overline F_j(h)+q\kappa_j,                \tag{1.13}
\]
and (1.12) follows from (1.8).  Completing the conditional Gaussian also
gives the exact non-separated normalizer
\[
\begin{split}
 \kappa_j={}&-{1\over2q}\log\det{}_2(\Id+qT_j)
 +{q\over2}\langle s_j,R_js_j\rangle\\
 &+{1\over q}\log
 \E_{\mathcal N(-qR_js_j,R_j)}e^{-qN_j}.
\end{split}                                                  \tag{1.14}
\]

For a triangular shifted law $Q$ and the sequentially normalized law
$P=\prod_j\nu_j$, the entropy chain rule yields
\[
 \boxed{
 \E_QV_J^{\rm ren}
 =-{1\over2q}\E_Q\sum_j|\widehat h_j|^2
  -\E_Q\sum_j\kappa_j+{1\over q}H(Q\mid P).}               \tag{1.15}
\]
Here $\widehat h_j$ is the conditional mean as a function of the \emph{state}
past.  If $Z_j=\xi_j+h_j(\xi_{<j})$, then
\[
 \widehat h_j(z_{<j})=h_j(\mathcal T_{<j}^{-1}z_{<j}).       \tag{1.16}
\]
The executable negative control shows that replacing (1.16) by
$h_j(z_{<j})$ changes the chain identity.  Also, $P$ is not generally the
global Gibbs law: its past-dependent conditional normalizers do not telescope.
Thus (1.15) is an exact geometric identity, not a Nelson estimate.

\subsection*{Fast quartic carrier: factor four is reassembled}

For the scalar principal carrier $f(A)=cA^4$, $c\ge0$, one has
\[
 T=2cA^2,\qquad \ell=m=2cA^3,\qquad s=4cA^3,                \tag{1.17}
\]
\[
 N(u)=cu^2(u^2+4Au+5A^2)
 =cu^2\{(u+2A)^2+A^2\}\ge0.                                \tag{1.18}
\]
With $R=(1+qT)^{-1}$ and
$P(u)=(T+q^{-1})(u+qRs)^2/2$, direct expansion gives
\[
 {q\over2}s^2R-N(u)-P(u)
 =f(A)-f(A+u)-{u^2\over2q}.                                 \tag{1.19}
\]
The ratio $s^2/\ell^2=4$ is therefore exactly neutralized by the nonlinear
remainder and retained square.  At $u=-A$ the right side is
$cA^4-A^2/(2q)$, so a shellwise terminal-only estimate is still false for
large $A$.  Equation (1.19) is pointwise.  It must not be confused with the
Gaussian-averaged charge in (1.8); for example
$\mathcal S^\sharp(0)=-c(5A^2+3)$ in the standard scalar Gaussian fixture.

\section{Terminal-backward heat martingale}

Let $(g_j,d_{j,i})$ be the value and $i$th derivative fields of the $j$th
Gaussian shell.  At each spatial point assume
\[
 \E[g_j\otimes g_j]=\Sigma_j,\qquad
 \E[d_{j,i}\otimes d_{j,i}]=\Delta\Gamma_{j,i},\qquad
 \E[g_j\otimes d_{j,i}]=0.                                 \tag{2.1}
\]
Joint Gaussianity and the pinned common-even covariance make the value and
derivative variables independent at the same point.  Write
\[
 \Gamma_{j,i}=\sum_{k\le j}\Delta\Gamma_{k,i},\qquad
 \Sigma_{r:s}=\sum_{k=r}^s\Sigma_k.                         \tag{2.2}
\]
For a fixed terminal shell $J$, define the backward coefficient and energy
\[
 B_j^{(J)}=P_{\Sigma_{j+1:J}}B,                              \tag{2.3}
\]
\[
 W_j^{(J)}(x,y)
 ={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 B_j^{(J)}(x):(y_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j,i})\dd r.            \tag{2.4}
\]
The integral in (2.4) is the spatial integral; the heat semigroup acts in the
six-real target variable at each point.

\textbf{Theorem 2.1 (backward heat martingale).}
For every finite cutoff and every deterministic past state $(x,y)$,
\[
 \E_jW_j^{(J)}(x+g_j,y+d_j)=W_{j-1}^{(J)}(x,y).              \tag{2.5}
\]
If $(a_j,b_j)$ is $\cF_{j-1}$-measurable, then
\[
 \E_jW_j^{(J)}(x+a_j+g_j,y+b_j+d_j)
 =W_{j-1}^{(J)}(x+a_j,y+b_j).                               \tag{2.6}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Independence in (2.1) factors the same-point expectation.  The derivative
average changes $y_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j,i}$ into
$y_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j-1,i}$, while the value average changes
$B_j^{(J)}$ into
$P_{\Sigma_j}B_j^{(J)}=B_{j-1}^{(J)}$.  This proves (2.5).
Conditioning on the strict past makes $(a_j,b_j)$ deterministic, which proves
(2.6). \hfill$\square$

Let the controlled state satisfy
\[
 Z_j=Z_{j-1}+a_j+g_j,\qquad
 DZ_j=DZ_{j-1}+b_j+d_j,\qquad Z_0=DZ_0=0.                   \tag{2.7}
\]
Since $W_J^{(J)}=V_J^{\rm ren}$ and $W_0^{(J)}(0,0)=0$, the
tower property gives the exact controlled discrete Ito identity
\[
 \boxed{
 \E V_J^{\rm ren}(Z_J)
 =\E\sum_{j\le J}
 \left\{W_{j-1}^{(J)}(Z_{j-1}+a_j,DZ_{j-1}+b_j)
              -W_{j-1}^{(J)}(Z_{j-1},DZ_{j-1})\right\}.}    \tag{2.8}
\]
This is the nonlocal reorganization missing from the forward conditional
heat-current calculation.  Every uncontrolled $O(1)$-per-shell heat drift is
removed before an inequality is applied.

\section{Why the forward heat term cannot be bounded shellwise}

The production coefficient is globally $C^{1,1}$ uniformly in the positive
floor.  If the floor is $e>0$ and $X=\sqrt e\,Y$, then
\[
 B_e(\sqrt e\,Y)=eB_1(Y),\qquad
 D_X^2B_e(\sqrt e\,Y)=D_Y^2B_1(Y).                          \tag{3.1}
\]
The rational $B_1$ is smooth on bounded sets and is asymptotically degree two
with a smooth angular coefficient.  Hence
\[
 L_B:=\sup_{e>0}\sup_X\|D^2B_e(X)\|<\infty.                \tag{3.2}
\]
Taylor's formula gives
\[
 \|P_\Sigma B(X)-B(X)\|
 \le {L_B\over2}\Tr\Sigma.                                \tag{3.3}
\]
This correct estimate is not summable after taking an absolute contraction
with $DX^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{<j}$: sharp dyadic scaling gives
$\Tr\Sigma_j\asymp2^{-j}$ and
$\Tr\Gamma_{<j}\asymp2^j$.

The failure is exact already for the scalar coefficient $B(x)=x^2$.  Take
\[
 \sigma_j=2^{-j},\qquad \gamma_{<j}=2^j.                   \tag{3.4}
\]
At $x=Dx=h=0$ the score and completed square vanish, but
\[
 \E_jC_j=-{1\over2}\sigma_j\gamma_{<j}=-{1\over2}          \tag{3.5}
\]
for every shell, whereas
\[
 \E|g_j|^6=15\,2^{-3j}.                                    \tag{3.6}
\]
For a Gaussian past derivative the centered quadratic in (3.5) has standard
deviation $1/\sqrt2$, again independent of $j$.  Thus shellwise absolute
values, positive parts, or sextic domination are impossible.

The executable production audit confirms that this is not an artifact of the
scalar model.  It computes the exact A1 sharp-cube value/derivative
covariances and averages the full rational six-real $B$ at two Gauss--Hermite
orders.  The origin deficits are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 &N=64&N=128\\ \hline
 {\rm GH}\,7&2.8680745298\times10^{-4}&2.8526808367\times10^{-4}\\
 {\rm GH}\,9&2.8689027298\times10^{-4}&2.8535053764\times10^{-4}
\end{array}                                                  \tag{3.7}
\]
The registered assertions are the conservative interval
$2.84\times10^{-4}< -\E C_{128}<2.87\times10^{-4}$, a relative
$64/128$ gap below two percent, and an order-seven/order-nine relative gap
below $5\times10^{-4}$ at both cutoffs.  This is cutoff- and
quadrature-stable finite evidence for the pinned production coefficients, not
an interval proof of a continuum limit.

There is also an analytic zero-floor diagnostic.  Write
$R=|\psi_1|^2+|\psi_2|^2$ and $\rho=R+|\psi_3|^2$.  Direct Pauli algebra gives
\[
 \Tr B_e=12(a+2b+c_{\rm II})R-{8bR^2\over\rho+e}
 -{4c_{\rm II}R^2(\rho+2e)\over(\rho+e)^2}.                \tag{3.8}
\]
Set $e=0$ first (equivalently, take the high-variance limit
$e/c_{\rm sh}\downarrow0$).  If
$\E\psi\psi^*=c_{\rm sh}\Id_3$ and the one-direction derivative covariance
is $d\Id_3$, Pauli identities then give
\[
 \E\Tr B
 =6c_{\rm sh}(4a+6b+3c_{\rm II}),                         \tag{3.9}
\]
so the three-direction origin deficit coefficient is
\[
 {3\over4}d\,\E\Tr B
 \simeq0.163265625\,c_{\rm sh}d.                          \tag{3.10}
\]
At the production values, $b,c_{\rm II}>0$, so (3.8) is no smaller at
$e>0$ than at $e=0$.  Hence (3.10) is also a positive-floor lower-bound
coefficient, although equality is reserved for the zero-floor limit.  The
product $c_{\rm sh}d$ is scale zero.  Equations (3.5)--(3.10) explain why the
backward martingale, rather than a sharper modulus estimate in (3.3), is
load-bearing.  The executable fixed-floor computation (3.7), rather than the
zero-floor equality (3.9), is the finite-floor numerical evidence.

\section{Averaged frame secant and Cartan form}

Recall the production frame
\[
 B(X)=\sum_{A=1}^3M_A(X)Q_{\rm II}M_A(X)^T,\qquad
 M_A=[p_A,v_A],\qquad Q_{\rm II}\succeq0,                   \tag{4.1}
\]
\[
 p_A=2S_AX,\qquad
 v_A=2(S_A-q_A\Id)X,\qquad
 q_A={X^TS_AX\over |X|^2+e}.                               \tag{4.2}
\]
Heat averaging preserves both positivity and the frame representation:
\[
 B_{j-1}^{(J)}(x)
 =\int\sum_AM_A(x+r)Q_{\rm II}M_A(x+r)^T\dd\mu_{j:J}(r),    \tag{4.3}
\]
where $\mu_{j:J}=\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_{j:J})$.  The variable $r$ in
(4.3) is a target-space heat dummy held fixed under spatial differentiation;
it is not identified pathwise with the actual future Gaussian field.

For a stage state $(x,y_i)$ and physical control $(a,b_i)$ put
\[
 w_{A,i,r}=M_A(x+r)^Ty_i,                                   \tag{4.4}
\]
\[
 \Delta w_{A,i,r}
 =M_A(x+a+r)^T(y_i+b_i)-M_A(x+r)^Ty_i,\qquad
 \Delta M_{A,r}=M_A(x+a+r)-M_A(x+r).                        \tag{4.5}
\]
Direct expansion of (2.4) gives the exact averaged secant
\[
\begin{split}
 \Delta W_{j-1}^{(J)}
 =\sum_{i,A}\int\!\int\{&
 w_{A,i,r}^TQ_{\rm II}\Delta w_{A,i,r}
 +{1\over2}\Delta w_{A,i,r}^TQ_{\rm II}\Delta w_{A,i,r}\\
 &-\Tr(Q_{\rm II}M_A(x+r)^T\Gamma_{j-1,i}\Delta M_{A,r})\\
 &-{1\over2}\Tr(Q_{\rm II}\Delta M_{A,r}^T
                 \Gamma_{j-1,i}\Delta M_{A,r})\}\dd x\dd\mu_{j:J}(r).
\end{split}                                                    \tag{4.6}
\]
The second term is the retained positive Gauss--Newton square.  The last term
is negative; its scale will be shown summable rather than assigned a false
sign.

For the non-exact current one-form
\[
 \omega_A=\dd m_A-q_A\dd\rho,\qquad K_A(X)=X^*\omega_A,      \tag{4.7}
\]
the prior exact identity remains valid under the heat average:
\[
 K_A(x+a+r)-K_A(x+r)
 =\dd_x\int_0^1\omega_A(x+r+ta)[a]\dd t
 +\int_0^1\dd\omega_A(x+r+ta)[a,y+tb]\dd t,                \tag{4.8}
\]
\[
 \dd\omega_A={\dd\rho\wedge\dd m_A\over\rho+e},\qquad
 \|\dd\omega_A\|_{\rm op}\le4.                           \tag{4.9}
\]
Thus (4.6) is precisely an averaged exact-current plus Cartan-curvature
secant.  In second-variation language, if
$w_t=M_A(x+r+ta)^T(y_i+tb_i)$, then
\[
 {\dd^2\over\dd t^2}{1\over2}|w_t|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2
 =|\dot w_t|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2
  +\langle w_t,Q_{\rm II}\ddot w_t\rangle.                 \tag{4.10}
\]
The first term is positive; the second is the signed Cartan--Jacobi term.
They may not be estimated independently at the critical regularity.

\section{The covariance-trace channels are summable}

Let $\Lambda_j=2^j$, $a_j=K_jh_j$, and $b_{j,i}=D_iK_jh_j$.  The A1 symbol lower
bound and the sharp-cube count imply, after enlarging constants for finitely
many low shells,
\[
 \|K_j\|_{\cH_j\to L^2}\le C_K\Lambda_j^{-2},\qquad
 \|DK_j\|_{\cH_j\to L^2}\le C_G\Lambda_j^{-1},           \tag{5.1}
\]
\[
 \max_i\|\Gamma_{j-1,i}\|_{\rm op}\le C_\Gamma\Lambda_j. \tag{5.2}
\]
Here $M_R:=\sup_{\Lambda,n}|R_\Lambda(n)|$ is the common multiplier bound,
and $c_{\rm sym}>0$ is a pinned high-frequency symbol constant such that
$\lambda_{\min}A(k)\ge c_{\rm sym}\langle k\rangle^4$ on every shell mode.
One admissible set is
\[
 C_K=4M_Rc_{\rm sym}^{-1/2}\alpha^{-2},\qquad
 C_G=2M_Rc_{\rm sym}^{-1/2}\alpha^{-1},                    \tag{5.3}
\]
\[
 C_\Gamma=L^{-3}M_R^2c_{\rm sym}^{-1}\alpha^{-2}
 \left(24+{\pi^2\over3}\right),\qquad \alpha={2\pi\over L}.\tag{5.4}
\]
For (5.4), group lattice points by $\|n\|_\infty=m$, use
$\#\{\|n\|_\infty=m\}=24m^2+2$, and bound
$k_i^2\langle k\rangle^{-4}\le(\alpha m)^{-2}$.

The production frames have floor-uniform deterministic bounds
\[
 |M_A(z)|_F\le\sqrt{20}|z|,\qquad
 |M_A(z+a)-M_A(z)|_F\le\sqrt{68}|a|.                        \tag{5.5}
\]
To see the second bound, write $r_A=(S_A-q_A\Id)z$.  Since
$S_A^2\le\Id$ and $m_A=q_A(|z|^2+e)$,
\[
 |r_A|^2\le |z|^2,\qquad
 \nabla q_A={2r_A\over |z|^2+e},\qquad
 |z|\,|Dq_A(z)[a]|\le2|a|.                                \tag{5.6}
\]
Hence $|Dp_A[a]|\le2|a|$ and $|Dv_A[a]|\le8|a|$, proving
$\sqrt{2^2+8^2}=\sqrt{68}$.

Let $S_j$ denote the positive magnitude of the last trace term in (4.6):
\[
 S_j={1\over2}\sum_{i,A}\int\!\int
 \Tr(Q_{\rm II}\Delta M_{A,j,r}^T
                 \Gamma_{j-1,i}\Delta M_{A,j,r})\dd x\dd\mu.\tag{5.7}
\]
Equations (5.1), (5.2), and (5.5) give pathwise
\[
 S_j\le C_S\Lambda_j^{-3}\|h_j\|^2,\qquad
 C_S={9\over2}\|Q_{\rm II}\|_{\rm op}(68)C_\Gamma C_K^2. \tag{5.8}
\]
Thus
\[
 \sum_{j\ge j_0}S_j
 \le C_S2^{-3j_0}\sum_{j\ge j_0}\|h_j\|^2.                \tag{5.9}
\]

For the mixed trace term put
\[
 L_j=\sum_{i,A}\int\!\int
 \Tr(Q_{\rm II}M_A(x+r)^T\Gamma_{j-1,i}
                 \Delta M_{A,j,r})\dd x\dd\mu,             \tag{5.10}
\]
and
\[
 A_j^2=\int\|Z_{j-1}+r\|_2^2\dd\mu_{j:J}(r).               \tag{5.11}
\]
Then
\[
 |L_j|\le C_L\Lambda_j^{-1}\|h_j\|A_j,\qquad
 C_L=9\|Q_{\rm II}\|_{\rm op}\sqrt{20}\sqrt{68}
       C_\Gamma C_K.                                        \tag{5.12}
\]
It is unnecessary to identify $r$ with a future controlled field.  Bounded
total value covariance, (5.1), and shell orthogonality give directly
\[
\begin{split}
 \E A_j^2
 &\le2\E\|Z_{j-1}\|_2^2+2L^3\Tr\Sigma_{j:J}\\
 &\le4\E\|X_{<j}\|_2^2
     +4\E\left\|\sum_{k<j}K_kh_k\right\|_2^2+C\\
 &\le C_A\left(1+\E\sum_k\|h_k\|^2\right).
\end{split}                                                  \tag{5.13}
\]
The last line uses the mutually orthogonal one-shot shell ranges pathwise;
it is not a future-field substitution.
Cauchy--Schwarz over probability and shells therefore yields
\[
 \E\sum_{j\ge j_0}|L_j|
 \le C2^{-j_0}
 \left\{1+\E\sum_k\|h_k\|^2\right\}.                     \tag{5.14}
\]
Combining (5.9)--(5.14), for every $\eta>0$ one may choose a fixed $j_0$
large enough that, uniformly in $J$,
\[
 \E\sum_{j\ge j_0}(|L_j|+S_j)
 \le\eta\E\sum_k\|h_k\|^2+C_{\eta,j_0}.                  \tag{5.15}
\]
This spends the control energy once.  It does not use the desired Nelson
moment.

\section{The finite low block and the exact remaining form}

Fix the threshold $j_0$ selected above.  Summing (2.8) only over
$j<j_0$ gives exactly
\[
 \E\sum_{j<j_0}\Delta_j^{\rm ctrl}
 =\E W_{j_0-1}^{(J)}(Z_{j_0-1},DZ_{j_0-1}).                 \tag{6.1}
\]
Positivity of $B_{j_0-1}^{(J)}$, quadratic growth of $B$, bounded total value
covariance, and the fixed matrix $\Gamma_{j_0-1}$ imply
\[
 W_{j_0-1}^{(J)}(z,Dz)\ge-C_{j_0}(1+\|z\|_2^2),             \tag{6.2}
\]
uniformly in $J$.  In the declared orthogonal one-shot shell class,
$Z_{j_0-1}=P_{<j_0}Z_J$.  The fixed low projection is bounded from $L^6$ to
$L^2$, and scalar Young gives, for every $\zeta>0$,
\[
 \E\sum_{j<j_0}\Delta_j^{\rm ctrl}
 \ge-\zeta\E\|Z_J\|_6^6-C_{\zeta,j_0}.                   \tag{6.3}

\section{What A9 imports, and what it cannot supply}

The backward coefficient $B_{j-1}^{(J)}$ is positive and satisfies
\[
 \|B_{j-1}^{(J)}(z)\|_F
 \le\beta_B(|z|^2+\kappa_0).                                \tag{7.1}
\]
Therefore the A9 arbitrary-source frozen-shell theorem applies conditionally
to its PSD quadratic block, and its Schatten estimate gives
\[
 \|T_{j,B_{j-1}^{(J)}(z)}\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2
 \le C2^{-j}(\|z\|_4^4+L^3\kappa_0^2).                     \tag{7.2}
\]
This is a valid reusable summable determinant cost.

It does not bound (6.4).  Once the present shell has been conditioned out,
$w_{A,i,r}$ and $\Delta w_{A,i,r}$ depend on the same past noise and adapted
control.  A9's base-Gaussian $L^2$ estimates do not control every such tilted
law; direct Malliavin integration by parts also creates a derivative of the
control which the Boue--Dupuis cost does not bound.  A new global relative-form
or backward log-Laplace theorem for the full Cartan secant is therefore
load-bearing.  Rewriting (6.4) as entropy, HJB, or a Follmer drift without a
new estimate is circular.
