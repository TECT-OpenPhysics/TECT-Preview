\section{The unconditional augmented normal form}

Let $\mathcal F$ be the past.  Write $z=G_0+g$, where $G_0$ is
$\mathcal F$-measurable,
\[
 \mathbb E_{\mathcal F}g=0,

\[
 A=B+2R,\qquad \bar B=\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}B,

\[
 q_B=\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}(Bz),\qquad

\]
and
\[
 \Theta_R(B)=B-B(B+2R)^{-1}B,\qquad
 Y=\Theta_R(B)^{1/2}z,\qquad r=\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}Y. \tag{2.4}
\]
The principal square root is well-defined even when $B$ and $R$ do not
commute.  Indeed,
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}B&B\\B&B+2R\end{pmatrix}\succeq0,
 \qquad
 (x,y)\longmapsto (x+y)^TB(x+y)+2y^TRy,
\]
so the Schur complement of $B+2R\succ0$ gives
$\Theta_R(B)\succeq0$; also $\Theta_R(B)\preceq B$ because
$B(B+2R)^{-1}B\succeq0$.
All spaces in this theorem are finite-dimensional.  Assume the displayed
conditional moments are finite, in particular
$\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}\|B\|$, $\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}z^TBz$,
$\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}\|Bz\|$, and
$\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}\|Y\|^2$; hence $\bar B,q_B,r$ and every term below

\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (complete augmented one-reveal normal form)}

The complete R-092 augmented density equals
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}

 :={}&\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}\!\left[
   (m-m_0)^TA(m-m_0)+|Y-r|^2\right]-\bar B:\Gamma\\
 ={}&\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}(z^TBz)
   -\bar B:\Gamma-q_B^T\bar A^{-1}q_B-|r|^2 .
\end{aligned}}                                         \tag{2.5}
\]
No endpoint square is duplicated in this identity.

\emph{Proof.}
Since $Am=Bz$ and $\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}(Am)=q_B=\bar A m_0$,
\[
 \mathbb E_{\mathcal F}(m-m_0)^TA(m-m_0)
 =\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}z^TBA^{-1}Bz
  -q_B^T\bar A^{-1}q_B.                                \tag{2.6}
\]
Also
\[
 \mathbb E_{\mathcal F}|Y-r|^2
 =\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}z^T\Theta_R(B)z-|r|^2.         \tag{2.7}
\]
The pointwise matrix identity
\[
 B(B+2R)^{-1}B+\Theta_R(B)=B                           \tag{2.8}
\]
then gives (2.5). \hfill$\square$

The theorem is the unconditional compression of R-092 (9.11).  It keeps the
Schur square, the terminal $\Theta_R$ martingale energy, and the covariance
subtraction together.  In particular no conclusion can be drawn from the
sign of any one of them separately.

\subsection*{Corollary 2.2 (even-reveal covariance classifier)}

Assume $G_0=0$, the conditional law of $g$ is centrally symmetric, and
$B(-g)=B(g)$.  Then $q_B=r=0$, and
\[

 =\mathbb E_{\mathcal F}\!\left[B(g):(g\otimes g-\Gamma)\right].}
 \tag{2.9}
\]
For an independent copy $g'$ this can also be written
\[

\]

sign is strict when $g^2$
is nondegenerate and $\beta$ is strictly monotone on its essential range;

$(\beta(g^2)-\beta(g'^2))(g^2-g'^2)$ is nonzero with positive probability.

(2.5), not term by term.
