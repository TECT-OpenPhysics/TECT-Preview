\section{Direct-root nonorthogonal Schur theorem}

\subsection*{Theorem 3.1}

Let $v_{A,j,k,t}$ be Hilbert-valued atoms for $k\le j$, and suppose that for
$m\ge j+C_0$,
\[
 \sum_A\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
 \mathbb E\|\Pi_mv_{A,j,k,t}\|_{\rm HS}^2dt
 \le 2^{-2s(m-k)}q_k
 \tag{3.1}
\]
with $s>0$ and deterministic $q_k\ge0$.  For every $C\ge C_0$ and
$0<\eta<2s$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sum_{A,j}\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
 \mathbb E\left\|
 \mathrm Q_{j,C}\sum_{k\le j}v_{A,j,k,t}
 \right\|_{\rm HS}^2dt\\
 &\quad\le
 {2^{-2sC}\over
 (1-2^{-\eta})(1-2^{-2s})(1-2^{\eta-2s})}
 \sum_kq_k .
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{3.2}
\]
No orthogonality between different $k$ is assumed.

\emph{Proof.}
For fixed $A,j,m,t$, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k\le j}\Pi_mv_{A,j,k,t}\right\|^2
 \le {1\over1-2^{-\eta}}
 \sum_{k\le j}2^{\eta(j-k)}
 \|\Pi_mv_{A,j,k,t}\|^2.
 \tag{3.3}
\]
Insert (3.1), exchange nonnegative sums, and put $r=j-k$.  Then
\[
 \sum_{m\ge j+C}2^{-2s(m-k)}
 ={2^{-2s(C+r)}\over1-2^{-2s}},
 \tag{3.4}
\]
while
\[
 \sum_{r\ge0}2^{(\eta-2s)r}
 ={1\over1-2^{\eta-2s}}.
 \tag{3.5}
\]
The latter converges exactly when $\eta<2s$.  Combining
(3.3)--(3.5) proves (3.2). \hfill$\square$

\[

The threshold $s>0$ is sharp under only (3.1).  At $s=0$, one fixed

\subsection*{Lemma 5.1}

Let $0<s<1$ and
$A\in H^2(\mathbb T_L^3)\cap L^6(\mathbb T_L^3)$.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \|A^{\otimes3}\otimes DA\|_{B^s_{1,1}}
 \le C_{s,L}\|A\|_{H^2}\|A\|_6^3.}
 \tag{5.1}
\]
The statement applies componentwise to the production vector field.

\emph{Proof.}
Use a smooth inhomogeneous Littlewood--Paley partition and assign ties by a
fixed ordering.  If the differentiated factor has largest frequency $j$,
Holder gives
\[
 \|\Delta_jDA\,A^3\|_1
 \le C2^j\|\Delta_jA\|_2\|A^3\|_2
 \le C2^j\|\Delta_jA\|_2\|A\|_6^3.
 \tag{5.2}
\]
If an undifferentiated factor $\Delta_jA$ has largest frequency, two other
undifferentiated factors are placed in $L^6$, and Bernstein gives
\[
 \|DS_{j+c}A\|_6\le C2^j\|A\|_6.
 \tag{5.3}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \|\Delta_jA\,A\,A\,DS_{j+c}A\|_1
 \le C2^j\|\Delta_jA\|_2\|A\|_6^3.
 \tag{5.4}
\]
Let $P_j$ denote any product family whose largest input scale is $j$.
Although its output may lie arbitrarily below $j$ through high--high-to-low
interactions, it always has output scales $n\le j+c$.  The uniform
$L^1$ multiplier bound and $s>0$ give the geometric low-output summation
\[
 \sum_{n\le j+c}2^{sn}\|\Delta_nP_j\|_1
 \le C_s2^{sj}\|P_j\|_1.
 \tag{5.5}
\]
Combining (5.2)--(5.5) over the finitely many largest-input product families
reduces the Besov norm to
\[
 \sum_j2^{(s+1)j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2.
 \tag{5.6}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_j2^{(s+1)j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2
 &=\sum_j2^{-(1-s)j}
 \{2^{2j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2\}\\
 &\le
 \left(\sum_j2^{-2(1-s)j}\right)^{1/2}\|A\|_{H^2}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{5.7}
\]
The final series converges exactly when $s<1$; the low-output sum used
$s>0$.  This proves (5.1), including high--high-to-low outputs.
\hfill$\square$

With

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{``The direct-root theorem dropped a required $2^j$.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED within the stated target.}
Equation (2.2) is the exact R-084 expression and has no such factor.  The
R-085 theorem remains valid for (2.4).  Any rescaling must be exhibited
explicitly.

\item \textbf{``The weaker threshold proves Cartan CFAR.''}
\textbf{UPHELD as an overclaim.}
Theorem 3.1 only performs the triangular summation.  The production bridge
(4.11)--(4.12), or a direct integrated substitute, remains open.

\item \textbf{``R-087's old $q_k^{\rm mod}$ now satisfies the ledger.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
The rare branch gives $q_k^{\rm mod}\asymp N^5$.  The repair saves one
power but does not make the extracted majorant bounded.

\item \textbf{``The rare branch refutes the exact Cartan atom.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
It evaluates the generic $Z^6$ majorant, not the exact atom.  The
$N^{-5/6}$ arithmetic uses the explicitly unproved toy ansatz (6.5) and
cannot be used as positive evidence either.

\item \textbf{``Sequential telescoping makes control shells orthogonal.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
The telescope occurs before the Hilbert--Schmidt square.  Cross-$k$ terms
remain and are handled only conditionally by Theorem 3.1.

\item \textbf{``The quartic Besov lemma proves the literal terminal
ledger.''}
\textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION.}
It proves the pure-control critical product.  Passing to $U+A$ adds
(5.11)--(5.13) on the stated one-shot class, which is form-payable but not
literally (4.12).

\item \textbf{``The trace debt can be paid separately by Young.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
Equation (7.4) and the fixed-target-dimension fixture (7.10) show that the
debt is the variance part of the retained square.  Separating it can create
a cutoff-covariance-divergent loss from a zero-mean packet.

\item \textbf{``Conditional centering closes the rational remainder.''}
\textbf{UPHELD as an overclaim.}
The centered covariance-matched branch was already closed by R-077.  The
coefficient-dominant residual can have nonzero conditional mean, covariance
defect, and same-root lower chaoses.

\item \textbf{``Positive Jensen defect gives a favorable Wick sign.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
A PSD matrix contracted with $Q=GG^T-\Gamma$ has no fixed sign.

\item \textbf{``The value $1/220$ caps every completion parameter.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}
It caps only an additional charge if strict $p<q_\eta$ is required.  An
internal share of the pinned $9/20$ leaves $q=10/9$ unchanged.
\end{enumerate}
