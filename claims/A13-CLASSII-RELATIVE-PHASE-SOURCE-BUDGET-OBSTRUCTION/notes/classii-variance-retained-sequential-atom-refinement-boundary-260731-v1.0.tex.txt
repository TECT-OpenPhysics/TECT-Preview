\section*{1. Purpose and scope}

R-134 left two obligations adjacent but logically distinct.  The first was an
owner-preserving passage from the R-087 self atom to the R-088 sequential
atom, with the conditional future variance and the R-063 forest retained.
The second was a terminal Gaussian reservation with enough joint spatial data
to support the conditional $\gamma=7/12$ route.  This note audits both
obligations against R-104, R-125, and the directed source union.

The conclusions are deliberately asymmetric.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The R-125 future-variance rebate gives the exact owner coordinate.  It
must remain inside the sequential atom sum; replacing it by an uncentred
second-moment envelope can erase an exact cancellation before any shell
estimate is attempted.
\item R-088 gives an exact sequential physical-shell atom.  The R-087 spatial
proof reruns directly on that atom because its band-limited coefficient path
has exactly the same three-channel integrand.  This proves the uncentred
far-output atom estimate, but not the centred conditioning step or the
once-owned $\sum_k q_k^{\rm seq}$ bound.
\item R-087 self atoms and R-088 sequential atoms are two path decompositions
of the same endpoint.  An exact two-parameter homotopy identifies their
termwise difference.  Equality of the total sums does not give termwise
domination, but that comparison is unnecessary for the direct spatial reproof;
the remaining production obstruction is the centred owner conversion and
summability of the sequential atom costs.
\item The extracted translated-model $Z^6$ route still fails on the accepted
predictable rare event.  The failure is an envelope no-go, not a
counterexample to the exact sequential atom.
\item A nonzero Gaussian block cannot remain independent and uniformly
six-real elliptic for every chart in the full R-093 directed temporal
refinement union.  Its terminal-tail covariance converges to zero as the last
mesh interval shrinks.  This is a structural no-go for the refinement-uniform
last-block architecture, not merely an absent estimate.
\item The viable successor is therefore fixed-chart or shell-weighted: keep a
finite unrevealed aggregate when its chart-dependent covariance is elliptic,
or absorb shell-dependent inverse-covariance and spatial costs into a
once-owned $q_k$ ledger.  Neither successor is proved for the complete
production owners here.
\end{enumerate}

The scope is one fixed finite cutoff, one positive density floor, one common
legal heat, and one declared R-093/R-104 temporally faithful chart unless a
statement explicitly quantifies over refinements.  All
representation-preserving same-root visits are recombined.  Output clusters
are contraction closed, orthogonal, and exhaustive.  The source energy and
terminal sextic remain outside the owner identity and may be appended only
once after all owners are summed.

The binding authorities are R-087, R-088, R-091, R-092, R-104, R-116,
R-119, R-123, R-125, R-129, R-133, and R-134.  No statement below changes their tiers or
promotes R-135 beyond its registered scoped T4 authority.

\section*{2. Exact owner identity and the variance firewall}

Fix a recombined root--spatial owner $o$.  Let $\cC$ range over its exhaustive

\[

\[

 \qquad

\[

\[
 \boxed{

 ={1\over2}\Forest_{063,o}^{\rm ad}
  -{1\over2}\Var_f(X_o).}                              \tag{2.4}

Equivalently, for the moving endpoint,
\[

The factor $1/2$ in (2.4) is load bearing.  The cluster direct sum must be
formed before the variance is taken.  No forest share is allocated to one

The elementary identity

and discards the positive term $\|\E_fX_o\|^2/2$.  That discarded term is not

Let $\mathcal O_{\rm full}$ include every recombined root owner together with

\section*{4. Exact sequential Cartan atom}

Fix a production generator $A$ and probability root $j$.  Write
\[
 \Phi=\Phi_{A,j},
 \qquad \cC_\Phi(z)=\Phi(z)^TDz,                        \tag{4.1}
\]
and let the physical control shells be
\[
 A^{(k)}=A^{(\ell)}+\sum_{r=j_0}^ka_r,
 \qquad B_{j,k}=w_j+A^{(k-1)}.                          \tag{4.2}
\]
The sequential endpoint increment is
\[
 \Theta_{A,j,k}
 =\cC_\Phi(B_{j,k}+a_k)-\cC_\Phi(B_{j,k}),             \tag{4.3}
\]
and telescopes exactly:
\[
 \sum_{k=j_0}^j\Theta_{A,j,k}
 =\cC_\Phi(w_j+A^{(j)})
  -\cC_\Phi(w_j+A^{(\ell)}).                           \tag{4.4}
\]
Since $a_k$ is strict-past for the fresh root $j$, its fresh-root derivative
vanishes.  Differentiating (4.3) in a fresh Cameron--Martin direction $v$
gives the complete three-channel atom
\[
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 T_{A,j,k}[v]={}&
 \{D\Phi(B_{j,k}+a_k)-D\Phi(B_{j,k})\}[v]^TDB_{j,k}\\
 &+D\Phi(B_{j,k}+a_k)[v]^TDa_k\\
 &+\{\Phi(B_{j,k}+a_k)-\Phi(B_{j,k})\}^TDv.
 \end{aligned}}                                        \tag{4.5}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T_{A,j,k}[v]=\int_0^1\{&
 D^2\Phi(B_{j,k}+ra_k)[a_k,v]^TD(B_{j,k}+ra_k)\\
 &+D\Phi(B_{j,k}+ra_k)[v]^TDa_k\\
 &+D\Phi(B_{j,k}+ra_k)[a_k]^TDv\}\,dr .
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{4.6}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \sum_{k=j_0}^jT_{A,j,k}
 =\mathbb D_j\{\cC_\Phi(w_j+A^{(j)})
                 -\cC_\Phi(w_j+A^{(\ell)})\}.         \tag{4.7}
\]
Every future physical control shell is absent from the coefficient path of
the $k$th atom.  With the R-081 bounds
$\|D\Phi\|_\infty\le3$ and
$\|D^2\Phi\|_\infty\le M_{e,2}$,
\[
 |T_{A,j,k}[v]|
 \le M_{e,2}|a_k||v||DB_{j,k}|
      +3|v||Da_k|+3|a_k||Dv|.                           \tag{4.8}
\]
For a root basis satisfying
\[
 \sum_h|v_h|^2\le\kappa_02^{-j},
 \qquad
 \sum_h|Dv_h|^2\le\kappa_12^j,                        \tag{4.9}
\]
one obtains the pointwise envelope
\[
 \sum_h|T_{A,j,k}[v_h]|^2
 \le C_e\{2^{-j}|a_k|^2|DB_{j,k}|^2
          +2^{-j}|Da_k|^2+2^j|a_k|^2\}.                \tag{4.10}
\]
Equations (4.5)--(4.10) are exact or fixed-floor local estimates.  They do
not by themselves make distinct $k$ atoms orthogonal.

\section*{5. Variance-retained sequential summation}

Let $\cY_{A,j,k,t}$ be the exhaustive output-cluster direct sum of the legal
projection of $P_t^{(j)}T_{A,j,k}$.  Define its conditionally centred form
\[
 \widetilde\cY_{A,j,k,t}
 =\cY_{A,j,k,t}-\E_f\cY_{A,j,k,t}.                      \tag{5.1}
\]
Whenever the deterministic spatial and OU operators commute with the legal
future conditioning,
\[
 \E\|\Pi_m\widetilde\cY_{A,j,k,t}\|^2
 \le \E\|\Pi_m\cY_{A,j,k,t}\|^2.                      \tag{5.2}
\]
If that commutation has not been established, the centred estimate below
must be assumed directly.

\subsection*{Theorem 5.1 (conditional variance sequential atom theorem)}

Assume that for some $s>0$, every $k\le j$, and every $m\ge j+5$,
\[
 \sum_A\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
 \E\|\Pi_m\widetilde\cY_{A,j,k,t}\|^2\,dt
 \le2^{-2s(m-k)}q_k,                                   \tag{5.3}
\]
where $q_k\ge0$ is deterministic and
\[
 \sum_kq_k\le Q.                                       \tag{5.4}
\]
For $0<\gamma<s$, define the variance-retained output ledger
\[
 \cB_{\gamma}^{\rm var}
 :=\sum_{A,j}\sum_{m\ge j+5}2^{2\gamma(m-j-5)}
   \int_0^\infty e^{-2t}
   \E\Var_f\!\left(\Pi_m\sum_{k\le j}
                 \cY_{A,j,k,t}\right)dt.               \tag{5.5}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \cB_{\gamma}^{\rm var}
 \le {2^{-10s}\over
 (1-2^{-s})^2(1-2^{-2(s-\gamma)})}\,Q.}                \tag{5.6}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Conditional Pythagoras gives
\[
 \Var_f\!\left(\Pi_m\sum_{k\le j}\cY_k\right)
 =\E_f\left\|\Pi_m\sum_{k\le j}\widetilde\cY_k
       \right\|^2.                                     \tag{5.7}
\]
For fixed $j,m,t$, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k\le j}\Pi_m\widetilde\cY_k\right\|^2
 \le{1\over1-2^{-s}}
     \sum_{k\le j}2^{s(j-k)}
     \|\Pi_m\widetilde\cY_k\|^2.                     \tag{5.8}
\]
Insert (5.3), use Tonelli, and put $r=j-k$ and $d=m-j\ge5$.
The $r$ series contributes $(1-2^{-s})^{-1}$, while
\[
 \sum_{d\ge5}2^{2\gamma(d-5)}2^{-2sd}
 ={2^{-10s}\over1-2^{-2(s-\gamma)}}.                   \tag{5.9}
\]
Equations (5.4), (5.8), and (5.9) prove (5.6). \hfill$\square$

At
\[
 s={2\over3},
 \qquad \gamma={7\over12},                             \tag{5.10}
\]
the square constant is
\[
 \boxed{
 C_{2/3,7/12}^{\rm var}
 ={2^{-20/3}\over
 (1-2^{-2/3})^2(1-2^{-1/6})}
 =0.658881625872614\ldots .}                            \tag{5.11}
\]
No cross-$k$ orthogonality is used, and each $q_k$ is spent once.  The theorem
does not prove (5.4) or the identification of the complete R-125 owner current
with the projected R-088 atom sum.  Theorem 4A.1 supplies the uncentred spatial
version of (5.3) with $q_k=q_k^{\rm seq}$.  The centred version follows from
(5.2) only after the legal conditioning commutation is certified; otherwise it
remains a separate production input.  The once-owned $q_k^{\rm seq}$ sum and
owner intertwiner remain open.

\section*{6. Self atoms versus sequential atoms: an exact homotopy}

R-087 and R-088 atomise the same endpoint along different paths.  The
difference can be located without guessing a sign.

Let $E$ and $H$ be finite-dimensional real Hilbert spaces, let
$F:E\to H$ be $C^2$, and let
\[
 a=\sum_{k=1}^Ka_k,
 \qquad p_{k-1}=\sum_{r<k}a_r.                          \tag{6.1}
\]
Define the straight-path self atoms and polygonal sequential atoms by
\[
 M_k=\int_0^1DF(ra)[a_k]\,dr,                           \tag{6.2}
\]
\[
 T_k=\int_0^1DF(p_{k-1}+ra_k)[a_k]\,dr.                \tag{6.3}
\]
Both decompositions telescope:
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_kM_k=F(a)-F(0)=\sum_kT_k.}                        \tag{6.4}
\]
For the exact termwise difference put
\[
 H_k(r)=p_{k-1}+ra_k-ra
       =(1-r)p_{k-1}-r\sum_{q>k}a_q,                    \tag{6.5}
\]
\[
 \Gamma_k(r,\tau)=ra+\tau H_k(r).                      \tag{6.6}
\]
The fundamental theorem of calculus in $\tau$ gives
\[
 \boxed{
 T_k-M_k
 =\int_0^1\!\int_0^1
 D^2F(\Gamma_k(r,\tau))[H_k(r),a_k]\,d\tau\,dr.}      \tag{6.7}
\]
Summing (6.7) gives zero by (6.4), but no summand has a fixed sign.

For the production comparison take
\[
 F(A)=\mathbb D_j\cC_{\Phi_{A,j}}(w_j+A),              \tag{6.8}
\]
with the finite-low shift absorbed into the origin.  Then (6.2) is the R-087
self atom and (6.3) is the R-088 sequential atom.  Equation (6.5) shows the
precise trade: the sequential path removes future controls from its $k$th
coefficient, while the self-to-sequential homotopy contains past-prefix and
future-tail mixed Hessian terms.  Consequently
\[
 \sum_k\|T_k\|^2\lesssim\sum_k\|M_k\|^2               \tag{6.9}
\]
does not follow from endpoint exactness.  A proof of (6.9), a weighted
variant, or any later attempt to compare the two decompositions must estimate
the strip (6.7) with the R-125 variance and forest owners retained.  Theorem
4A.1 does not require (6.9): it proves the sequential spatial estimate directly
from the common three-channel integrand.  The remaining burden is to sum its
actual $q_k^{\rm seq}$ costs and intertwine the legally centred full owners.

\section*{7. Extracted-$Z^6$ rare-event no-go}

The R-087 straight-path spatial estimate introduced
\[
 q_k^{\rm mod}
 =C_e2^{-(6\alpha-1)k}
  \sup_{j\ge k}\sum_A\E\int_0^1
  Z_{j,r}^6\{\|a_k\|_2^2+\|Da_k\|_2^2\}\,dr.          \tag{7.1}
\]
The R-088 direct-root theorem needs only $\sum_kq_k$, but this particular
extraction still fails.  Put $N=2^k$, choose
$E\in\cF_{k-1}$ with $\mathbb P(E)=p=N^{-6}$, and set
\[
 h_k=N^3\mathbf1_Ee_N,
 \qquad a_k=K_kh_k=N\mathbf1_Ee_N.                     \tag{7.2}
\]
Then
\[
 \E X\asymp\E Y\asymp\E\sqrt{XY}\asymp1,            \tag{7.3}
\]
whereas, with $\beta=6\alpha-1$,
\[

 =N^5=p^{-5/6}.}                                       \tag{7.4}
\]
The older $2^k$-weighted extraction grows as $N^6=p^{-1}$.  Thus neither the
weighted nor unweighted theorem converts this $Z^6$ envelope into a one-use
ledger.

This fixture does not evaluate the exact atom (4.5).  Indeed, the exact scalar
production saturation analysed in R-091 has a fixed-gap quantity of order
$N^{-4}$ on the same rare event.  Therefore (7.4) proves only
\[
 \text{extracted translated-model }Z^6\text{ majorants are too coarse};
                                                               \tag{7.5}
\]

\section*{8. Conditioning, rank, and spatial regularity}

For a jointly Gaussian finite-dimensional pair $(Z,Y)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \Cov(Z\mid Y)
 =\Gamma_Z-\Gamma_{ZY}\Gamma_Y^\dagger\Gamma_{YZ}.}    \tag{8.1}
\]
If $Y=AZ$, then
\[
 \Cov(Z\mid AZ)
 =\Gamma_Z^{1/2}
  (\Id-P_{\Ran(\Gamma_Z^{1/2}A^*)})
  \Gamma_Z^{1/2}.                                      \tag{8.2}
\]
Thus independent conditioning preserves covariance, while noiseless
observation can remove rank, including all of it when $A=\Id$.

For a Gaussian field $\zeta$ with covariance kernel $K$, a finite Gaussian
observation $Y$ gives
\[
 \E[\zeta(x)\mid Y]
 =m(x)+K_{\zeta Y}(x)K_Y^\dagger(Y-\E Y),              \tag{8.3}
\]
\[
 K_{\mid Y}(x,y)
 =K(x,y)-K_{\zeta Y}(x)K_Y^\dagger K_{Y\zeta}(y).      \tag{8.4}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \Cov(\partial_i\zeta(x)\mid Y)
 =\partial_{x_i}\partial_{y_i}K_{\mid Y}(x,y)|_{y=x}, \tag{8.5}
\]
\[
 \E[|\zeta(x)-\zeta(y)|^2\mid Y]
 =\Tr\{K_{\mid Y}(x,x)+K_{\mid Y}(y,y)
                  -2K_{\mid Y}(x,y)\}.                \tag{8.6}
\]
The residual covariance decreases as a quadratic form, but its smallest
eigenvalue can collapse and the regression mean contains the pseudoinverse
$K_Y^\dagger$.  Conditioning on a nonlinear same-root coefficient need not
leave a Gaussian conditional law, so (8.1)--(8.6) then require a separate
affine representation theorem.

The sharp spatial diagnostic is
\[
 \zeta_N^a(x)=G_a\cos(Nx)+H_a\sin(Nx),
 \qquad a=1,\ldots,6,                                  \tag{8.7}
\]
with independent standard $G_a,H_a$.  It has
\[
 K_N(x,y)=\cos(N(x-y))\Id_6,
 \qquad K_N(x,x)=\Id_6,                                \tag{8.8}
\]
but its $W^{\sigma,3}$ norm grows as $N^\sigma$.  After conditioning on
$G=(G_a)$,
\[
 \E[\zeta_N(x)\mid G]=G\cos(Nx),
 \qquad
 \Cov(\zeta_N(x)\mid G)=\sin^2(Nx)\Id_6,              \tag{8.9}
\]
\[
 \Cov(\partial_x\zeta_N(x)\mid G)
 =N^2\cos^2(Nx)\Id_6.                                  \tag{8.10}
\]
Hence $\inf_x\lambda_{\min}=0$ after conditioning despite exact unit
pointwise covariance before conditioning.  R-116 additionally forbids
splitting the production sine/cosine and polarization partners into an
artificial standalone root; they must remain in one legal conditional
cluster.  The complete pair still proves that pointwise ellipticity alone
does not imply a spatial fractional estimate.

\section*{9. Directed-refinement terminal no-go}

R-104 starts from a finite temporal partition and defines
\[
 \Delta C_k=\int_{I_k}J_tJ_t^*\,dt,
 \qquad S_k=\Delta C_k^{1/2},
 \qquad X_J\stackrel{\rm law}=\sum_kS_k\xi_k.           \tag{9.1}
\]
Singular $\Delta C_k$ are allowed.  Let a refinement have final cut $s<1$.
The Gaussian source component independent of $\cF_s$ and contained in the
physical terminal source is
\[
 Y_s=\int_s^1J_t\,dW_t,
 \qquad C_s=\Cov(Y_s)=\int_s^1J_tJ_t^*\,dt.             \tag{9.2}
\]
Let $E_x:H_J\to\mathbb R^6$ be point evaluation at fixed cutoff and put
\[
 \Gamma_s(x)=E_xC_sE_x^*.                               \tag{9.3}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 9.1 (no refinement-uniform last innovation)}

For every fixed cutoff and point,
\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_{\min}\Gamma_s(x)
 \le{1\over6}\Tr\Gamma_s(x)
 ={1\over6}\int_s^1\|J_t^*E_x^*\|_{\rm HS}^2\,dt
 \longrightarrow0\quad(s\uparrow1).}                 \tag{9.4}
\]
Therefore no $\lambda_*>0$ can satisfy
\[
 \Gamma_s(x)\succeq\lambda_*\Id_6                    \tag{9.5}
\]
uniformly in the R-093 directed temporal refinements.  If the integrand in
(9.4) is bounded by $M_{J,x}^2$, then
\[
 \lambda_{\min}\Gamma_s(x)
 \le{1-s\over6}M_{J,x}^2.                               \tag{9.6}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
The trace bound is elementary.  The final integral tends to zero by absolute
continuity of the finite-cutoff covariance integral. \hfill$\square$

There is also an invariant Gaussian-Hilbert-space formulation.  A Gaussian
linear functional independent of every $\cF_s$, $s<1$, must have a source
kernel supported in
\[
 \bigcap_{s<1}(s,1]=\{1\},                              \tag{9.7}
\]
which is null for the time covariance measure; the functional is therefore
zero in $L^2$.  Freezing a positive-length last interval avoids (9.4) only by
removing late predictable controls and is not cofinal in the full progressive
core.

The increasing sharp-cube root filtration gives the same obstruction in
discrete form.  The accepted production value-root estimate is
\[
 \Tr\Sigma_j^{\rm val}(x)\le\kappa_02^{-j}.             \tag{9.8}
\]
For the future tail
\[
 \Gamma_{>j}^{\rm val}(x)=\sum_{r>j}\Sigma_r^{\rm val}(x),
\]
one has
\[
 \lambda_{\min}\Gamma_{>j}^{\rm val}(x)
 \le{\kappa_0\over6}2^{-j},
 \qquad \Gamma_{>J}^{\rm val}=0.                       \tag{9.9}
\]
Moreover, for a centred packet $Z_j$ and $q>0$, Jensen gives

 \ge(\E|Z_j|^2)^{-q/2}.                                \tag{9.10}
\]
At the R-134 inverse orders $q=9/4$ and $q=9/2$, a value covariance of scale
$2^{-j}$ therefore carries growth $2^{9j/8}$ and $2^{9j/4}$, respectively.
The sharp R-134 upper constants are

 \qquad
 C_{6,9/2}={\Gamma(3/4)\over2^{13/4}}.                 \tag{9.11}
\]
Full rank at each fixed $j$ cannot turn these shell-dependent constants into
an owner-uniform one.

Finally, the current R-125 conditioning retains the current root and
