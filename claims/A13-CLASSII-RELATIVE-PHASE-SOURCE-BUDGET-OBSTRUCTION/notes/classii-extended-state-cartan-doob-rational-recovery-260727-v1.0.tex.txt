\section{Purpose and scope}

R-098 left three closely related questions at the A13 frontier.  Could a
signed Cartan visit be grouped when its target heat changes?  Could the bare
future-control response be assigned once without differentiating an adapted
control?  Could the exact R-085 rational recovery be completed once an upper,
rather than merely lower, estimate for its five unshifted families is known?

This note answers all three questions at their honest scope and records two
route exclusions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A complete Cartan edge in the extended state $(\Sigma,z)$ telescopes
exactly within one fixed Malliavin root.  The heat-change bracket is mandatory.
Any common linear root derivative, Ornstein--Uhlenbeck operator, and FAR
projection preserve the already-grouped identity.

\item A later inverse visit carried by a fresh temporal root does not cancel
the earlier nonnegative root square.  A production Cartan harmonic therefore
rules out a cutoff/partition/revisit-uniform extension of the R-085 mixed-only
ledger to arbitrary progressive controls.  The result does not touch R-092's
regular no-revisit theorem and does not exclude a once-only pure-$X$ allowance.

\item Chronological Doob differences give an exact derivative-free
Doob--Hardy theorem.  Each Hoeffding component is owned at its last revealed
root and all later smoothing shells are grouped before squaring.  This improves
ownership and signed grouping; it does not invalidate the already-correct
weighted resampling estimate of R-098.

\item The complete nonlinear frame has an exact ordered-reveal decomposition
into a mean secant and a Jensen residual.  A finite product fixture proves that
the residual cannot be controlled by the same-level shift increment alone,
and a rare-event spike rules out squaring the spatial frame secant before its
signed cross pairing.

\item The five R-085 unshifted rational families satisfy a two-sided form bound
on the accepted fixed-floor regular strict-past one-shot class.  An exact
payment-gauge identity then reduces rational (6.5) to a lower form for the
complete owner packet, which is weaker than positivity of the posterior
bracket alone.  The latter lower form is still open.
\end{enumerate}

The result is a T4 analytic/exact/executed advance and method boundary.  It
does not prove the coefficient-unconditioned production posterior lower form,
rational (6.5), complete $H_N$, REG, arbitrary progressive $H_A$,
$\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$, the $q=10/9$ Nelson estimate, cutoff or floor
removal, an interacting measure, or Sector-A closure.

\section{The extended-state signed Cartan telescope}

Fix one Malliavin root $j$ and a finite path
$(\Sigma_\ell,z_\ell)_{\ell=0}^L$.  Define the complete edge
\[
\begin{split}
 E_\ell={}&J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_\ell)
             -J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_{\ell-1})\\
 &+J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_{\ell-1})
             -J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}(z_{\ell-1}).
\end{split}                                            \tag{3.1}
\]
The first line is the value/source edge at the new heat and the second line is
the target-heat compensator.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-3.1-extended-state-cartan-telescope
\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (complete common-root telescope)}

For every finite path, without a sign or monotonicity assumption,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{\ell=1}^L E_\ell
 =J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0).}              \tag{3.2}
\]
Consequently, for a common root derivative, OU time, and FAR projector,
\[
 Q_{j,C}P_t^{(j)}\mathbb D_j\sum_{\ell=1}^LE_\ell
 =Q_{j,C}P_t^{(j)}\mathbb D_j
   \{J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0)\}.          \tag{3.3}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Each complete edge is already
$J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_\ell)-J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}(z_{\ell-1})$.
Summation gives (3.2).  Equation (3.3) follows by applying the same linear
operator to the grouped identity.  \hfill$\square$

Thus arbitrary signed subdivisions inside one root are partition invariant.
Equality of both extended endpoints guarantees cancellation, but it is not
necessary because $(\Sigma,z)\mapsto J_\Sigma(z)$ need not be injective; for
example, distinct heats agree at $z=0$.  Equality of the value endpoints alone
does not guarantee cancellation, and a heat change generically leaves the
defect in (3.4).  At fixed heat the compensators vanish and Theorem 3.1 reduces
to the R-098 same-root reverse cancellation.  Identifying
the endpoint derivative with the accepted R-084 three-channel formula also
requires the endpoint shift to be $\cF_{j-1}$-measurable; otherwise
$\mathbb D_j a$ is a new channel.

\subsection*{Omitted-heat defect}

If the heat compensators are dropped, the source-only sum is
\[
\begin{split}
 S_{\rm src}
 ={}&J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0)\\
 &-\sum_{\ell=1}^L
   [J_{\Sigma_\ell}-J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}](z_{\ell-1}).
\end{split}                                            \tag{3.4}
\]
The last line is an exact defect, not an error term.

For the production vector $F_S$ with $\operatorname{tr}S=0$, its cubic
Taylor term gives
\[
 D_z(\Delta F_S)(0)={4S\over e},
 \qquad
 D_z(P_{\tau I}F_S)(0)={2\tau S\over e}+O(\tau^2).   \tag{3.5}
\]
Indeed,
$\Delta[(z^TSz)z]=2(\operatorname{tr}S)z+4Sz$.
For $f_\epsilon=(\sin\theta,\epsilon\cos\theta)$,
$z=\delta f_\epsilon$, and a root direction
$v=\sqrt\lambda f_\epsilon$, the leading omitted-heat derivative is
\[
 {2(\tau_1-\tau_2)\over e}\,
 \delta\sqrt\lambda\,D_x(f_\epsilon^TSf_\epsilon)
 +O\{\delta(\tau_1^2+\tau_2^2)+\delta^3\}.           \tag{3.6}
\]
For the selected Cartan harmonic,
$D_x(f_\epsilon^TSf_\epsilon)$ is a nonzero multiple of
$(1+\epsilon^2)\sin(2\theta)$.  Hence (3.4) is nonzero in the production
current.  This low-harmonic diagnostic proves that deleting the heat owner is
false; it is not by itself a FAR counterexample.

\section{Causal Doob--Hardy ownership}

Let
\[
 A^*=\sum_{k=j_0}^{J+1}K_kh_k,
 \qquad
 d_j=\E[\,\cdot\mid\cF_j]-\E[\,\cdot\mid\cF_{j-1}], \tag{5.1}
\]
with deterministic $K_k:H\to V$, $h_k\in L^2(\cF_{k-1};H)$, and
$\|K_k\|\leq\kappa2^{-\alpha k}$.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-5.1-causal-doob-hardy-one-use
\subsection*{Theorem 5.1 (last-root Doob--Hardy theorem)}

For every $\beta>0$, set
$S_\beta=\sum_{r\geq1}2^{-\beta r}=(2^\beta-1)^{-1}$.
Then
\[
 d_jA^*=\sum_{k>j}K_kd_jh_k                                      \tag{5.2}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
 \sum_j2^{\beta j}\E\|d_jA^*\|_V^2
 \leq S_\beta\kappa^2
 \sum_k2^{-(2\alpha-\beta)k}
       \sum_{j<k}\E\|d_jh_k\|_H^2.}                  \tag{5.3}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\boxed{
 \sum_j2^{\beta j}\E\|d_jA^*\|_V^2
 \leq S_\beta\kappa^2
 \sum_k2^{-(2\alpha-\beta)k}
 \E\|h_k-\E_{j_0-1}h_k\|_H^2.}                     \tag{5.4}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
If $k\leq j$, then $h_k$ is already $\cF_{j-1}$-measurable, proving (5.2).
For fixed $j$, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k>j}K_kd_jh_k\right\|^2
 \leq \kappa^2
 \left(\sum_{k>j}2^{-\beta(k-j)}\right)
 \sum_{k>j}2^{\beta(k-j)}2^{-2\alpha k}\|d_jh_k\|^2.
\]
Multiplying by $2^{\beta j}$ and summing proves (5.3).  Martingale
orthogonality for each $h_k$ proves (5.4).  \hfill$\square$

For the production $L^2$ smoothing exponent $\alpha=2$ and $\beta=1$,
$S_1=1$, so
\[
 \sum_j2^j\E\|d_jA^*\|_2^2
 \leq\kappa_K^2\sum_k2^{-3k}
 \E\|h_k-\E_{j_0-1}h_k\|_2^2.                       \tag{5.5}
\]
The constant one is sharp in the limiting finite-array sense.  In three
spatial dimensions, the accepted estimate
\[
 \|K_k\|_{L^2\to L^r}\lesssim
 2^{-(1/2+3/r)k},\qquad2\leq r\leq6,                 \tag{5.6}
\]
gives
\[
 \sum_j2^j\E\|d_jA^*\|_r^2
 \lesssim\sum_k2^{-(6/r)k}
 \E\|h_k-\E_{j_0-1}h_k\|_2^2.                      \tag{5.7}
\]
The decay powers at $r=2,3,6$ are $3,2,1$.

\subsection*{Exact Hoeffding accounting}

Write the orthogonal Hoeffding expansion over roots before $k$ as
\[
 h_k=\sum_{S\subset\{j_0,\ldots,k-1\}}h_{k,S}.        \tag{5.8}
\]
Chronological revelation yields
\[
 d_jh_k=\sum_{\max S=j}h_{k,S};                       \tag{5.9}
\]
each nonconstant component is owned exactly once.  Coordinate resampling
instead satisfies
\[
 {1\over2}\E\|h_k-h_k^{(j)}\|^2
 =\sum_{S\ni j}\|h_{k,S}\|_2^2,                     \tag{5.10}
\]
so its unweighted accounting sees a component $|S|$ times.  This is not a
defect in R-098: the existing dyadic weights obey
$\sum_{j\in S}2^j<2^k$ and retain its $2^{-3k}$ conclusion.  The new gain is
chronological ownership, removal of the resampling factor, and signed grouping
of every future $k$ before squaring.

\section{The complete-frame ordered reveal}

Let $Z$ be the terminal value field, assume

 Z_j=\E_jZ,\qquad

% evidence-anchor: theorem-6.1-complete-frame-ordered-reveal
\subsection*{Theorem 6.1 (mean secant plus Jensen residual)}

For every reveal,
\[
 \boxed{d_j\widehat B
 =B_{\rm fr}(Z_j)-B_{\rm fr}(Z_{j-1})
   +\mathcal J_j-\mathcal J_{j-1}.}                   \tag{6.2}
\]
If $Q_j$ is the R-097 quadratic-Wick martingale, then for any base $\ell$,
\[
\begin{split}
 \E\langle B_{\rm fr}(Z),Q_J\rangle
 ={}&\E\langle\widehat B_\ell,Q_\ell\rangle\\
 &+\sum_{j>\ell}\E\langle
 B_{\rm fr}(Z_j)-B_{\rm fr}(Z_{j-1})
 +\mathcal J_j-\mathcal J_{j-1},d_jQ\rangle.
\end{split}                                            \tag{6.3}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Equation (6.2) follows by adding and subtracting the two mean frames.  Equation
(6.3) is martingale orthogonality.  \hfill$\square$

The decomposition is invariant under subdivisions that leave both the
terminal field $Z$ and the filtration/reveal order unchanged.  It cannot be
declared invariant after adding fresh roots or changing their order.  The
global Schur mean
\[
 -q^T(\overline B+2R)^{-1}q                            \tag{6.4}
\]
also remains one unsplit owner; one must not condition on the full terminal
control and then reuse the original root independence.

For the quadratic Gram $B(z)=z^{\otimes2}$,
$\mathcal J_j=\operatorname{Cov}(Z\mid\cF_j)$.  With
$u_j=d_jZ$,
\[
\begin{split}
 d_j\widehat B={}&Z_{j-1}\otimes u_j+u_j\otimes Z_{j-1}
 +u_j\otimes u_j\\
 &+\operatorname{Cov}(Z\mid\cF_j)
  -\operatorname{Cov}(Z\mid\cF_{j-1}).
\end{split}                                            \tag{6.5}
\]
The last line is a mandatory Jensen/covariance residual.  It is structurally
the kind of residual the posterior/forest architecture must own, but this note

\subsection*{A finite exponential separation}

Let independent $\xi_r$ have
\[
 \Pr(\xi_r=\pm2)={1\over8},\qquad
 \Pr(\xi_r=0)={3\over4}.                              \tag{6.6}
\]
Then $\E\xi_r=0$, $\E\xi_r^2=1$, and $\E\xi_r^4=4$.
For $Z=\prod_{r=1}^n\xi_r$, one has $d_jZ=0$ for $j<n$ and
$d_nZ=Z$, while
\[
 d_j\widehat B=\left(\prod_{r<j}\xi_r^2\right)(\xi_j^2-1),
 \qquad
 \E|d_j\widehat B|^2=3\,4^{j-1}.                     \tag{6.7}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^n\E|d_j\widehat B|^2=4^n-1,
 \qquad
 \sum_{j=1}^n\E|d_jZ|^2=1.                           \tag{6.8}
\]
No cutoff-uniform nonlinear-frame estimate can use only the same-level mean
shift $d_jZ$.

\subsection*{Why the frame secant must remain linearly paired}

Thus

 \|B_{\rm fr}(Z_j)-B_{\rm fr}(Z_{j-1})\|_{L_x^2}

Keeping (6.10) linearly paired with $d_jQ$ has formal payload

This loss is real.  On an event $E$ with probability $N^{-6}$ set

Then

\section{Once-only owner ledger and next theorem}

The combined result fixes the following order of operations.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Inside one root, group the complete $(\Sigma,z)$ Cartan edge, including
the heat compensator, before root differentiation, OU evolution, projection,
or squaring.

\item Across roots, use the actual Cameron--Martin cost $X$ once.  The later
inverse visit does not erase an earlier martingale square, and the terminal
mixed payload alone is not a progressive theorem.

\item In control coordinates, use Theorem 5.1 to group all future smoothing
shells before squaring.  Do not claim that it controls the nonlinear frame.

\item For the frame, retain the mean secant and Jensen residual in the signed
cross-Doob pairing.  Do not square the secant multiplier separately and do not
identify the abstract residual with a production forest without proof.

\item Retain the global Schur mean, low endpoint $W_0$, actual matrix payment,
$q/r$ means, covariance trace, terminal $\Theta_R$ energy, and the R-063 forest
exactly once.  The forest reconstructs the terminal raw $B_{\rm fr}:Q$ owner;
it is not appended per visit.

\item For the rational row, use the two-sided five-family form and the complete
owner target (8.5).  Changing $R$ only repartitions its three matched owners.
\end{enumerate}

The next analytic target is therefore not a standalone progressive extension
of R-085 (4.11).  It is a coefficient-unconditioned, cutoff-uniform lower form
