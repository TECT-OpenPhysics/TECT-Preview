\section*{1. Purpose and scope: result and stopping boundary}
\hypertarget{section-1-purpose}{ }

R-151 proves a strict local Hessian gap for the complete zero-past linear
$p:2p$ endpoint.  It deliberately leaves three lifts open: nonzero strict
past, nonlinear predictable feedback, and owner-complete aggregation over
interacting roots.  This note performs the promised bounded pass on all three
without introducing separately fabricated forest, balanced, trace, or low
reserves.

The outcome has one positive theorem and two exact method boundaries.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A fixed past field mean does not weaken the R-151 covariance-Hessian
bound when the past current mean is zero.  An exact stronger Sturm certificate
gives endpoint loss below $19/25$, hence augmented curvature above $7/50$.
For nonzero past current mean, the three new signed terms are displayed
exactly.  A quantitative once-declared current collar preserves the R-151
$1/10$ margin.
\item For a legal nonlinear fixed-law direction $\phi(\xi_1)$, the exact
criterion is an almost-sure conditional source-space operator lower bound.
The averaged linear tests of R-151 do not imply it.  A smooth translated-bump
fixture proves the non-implication, but is not an A1 production counterexample.
\item Pairwise local gaps do not aggregate merely because source coordinates
are used once.  The full recombined multi-root Hessian must pass one
generalized-eigenvalue or kernel-Schur test.  A rational two-edge fixture has
positive local gaps above $7/50$ and a negative global eigenvalue.
\end{enumerate}

Thus the bounded lift does not reveal an owner-complete global mechanism under
the registered hypotheses.  A13 remains valid conditional mathematics at T4;
T-050, Nelson, and Sector A remain open.  This is the predeclared stopping
boundary for adding further local pair lemmas without the actual production
global operator.

\section*{2. Exact affine-past Hessian}
\hypertarget{section-2-affine-past}{ }

Condition on the strict-past sigma-field $\mathcal F_-$.  For almost every
past realization let $\E_f=\E[\,\cdot\mid\mathcal F_-]$.  In the retained
R-151 antipodal chart write
\[
 W_t=m+X+tZ,
 \qquad
 V_{t,i}=n_i+Y_i+tU_i,                                \tag{2.1}
\]
where
\[
 Z=S_2H\xi_1,
 \qquad
 U_i=D_{2,i}H\xi_1.                                   \tag{2.2}
\]
Here $(X,Y)$ is the centered fresh Gaussian field-current pair,
$m=\E[W_0\mid\mathcal F_-]$ and
$n_i=\E[V_{0,i}\mid\mathcal F_-]$ are fixed after conditioning, and the
centered same-point field-current covariance is zero.  The centered future
covariance and common-even regulator hypotheses are exactly those of R-151.
Let $B=C_6^*C_6$ and let $Q_0$ be the A7
primitive current covariance.  The complete covariance-normal endpoint is
\[
 \cR(t)={1\over2}\int\E_f\left[
   \sum_i V_{t,i}^*B(W_t)V_{t,i}-\Tr\{B(W_t)Q_0\}
 \right]dx.                                           \tag{2.3}
\]

Direct differentiation, before any score transfer, gives
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 \cR''(0)=\int\E_f\bigg[\sum_i\{&U_i^*BU_i
 +2U_i^*DB[Z](Y_i+n_i)\\
 &+{1\over2}(Y_i+n_i)^*D^2B[Z,Z](Y_i+n_i)\\
 &\}\ -{1\over2}\Tr(D^2B[Z,Z]Q_0)\bigg]dx .
\end{aligned}}                                       \tag{2.4}
\]
Separating the centered core from the current mean yields
\[
 \cR''(0)=\mathcal H_{\rm core}(m)+\Delta_n,          \tag{2.5}
\]
where $\mathcal H_{\rm core}(m)$ is the R-151 covariance Hessian with the
field expectation shifted by $m$, and
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_n=\int\E_f\sum_i\{&2U_i^*DB[Z]n_i
 +n_i^*D^2B[Z,Z]Y_i\\
 &+{1\over2}n_i^*D^2B[Z,Z]n_i\}\,dx .
\end{aligned}}                                       \tag{2.6}
\]
The coefficients $2,1,1/2$ in (2.6) are forced by the derivative of the one
scalar endpoint; none is an independently assignable owner.

\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (arbitrary field mean with zero current mean)}
\hypertarget{theorem-2-1-zero-current}{ }

Assume the R-151 retained-lattice chart and a fixed conditional field mean
$m(x)$, but $n=0$.  Then, for every nonzero $H$,
\[
 \mathcal H_{\rm core}(m)[H,H]
 >-{19\over25}\|H\|_{\Frob}^2.                       \tag{2.7}
\]
The exact source Hessian is $9\|H\|_{\Frob}^2/10$, and the terminal sixth-power
Hessian is nonnegative.  Consequently
\[
 \boxed{
 \cA''(0)[H,H]>{7\over50}\|H\|_{\Frob}^2.}          \tag{2.8}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The R-130 bounds for $DB$ and $D^2B$ are global in the field value.  Shifting
the Gaussian expectation by an arbitrary fixed $m$ therefore does not change
the two signed response constants in the R-151 proof.  The positive
$\Tr(Q_2\E B)$ term remains positive.  Section 3 proves the stronger radial
loss bound $19/25$.  Adding the source Hessian and the nonnegative sixth-power
Hessian gives $9/10-19/25=7/50$. \hfill$\square$

This theorem is a curvature statement.  A nonstationary $m$ generally destroys
the R-151 Fourier proof that $\cR'(0)=0$, so (2.8) alone does not make the
origin a local minimum.

The theorem is proved on the enlarged fixed-mean field/current class in which
$m$ and $n$ are algebraically separated.  In the production synthesis one
normally has $n_i=\partial_i m$; hence production $n=0$ normally restricts
$m$ to a spatially constant torus mode.  The enlarged theorem is valid, but
does not assert that every such pair $(m,n)$ is production-realizable.

\subsection*{Theorem 2.2 (conditional past-current collar)}
\hypertarget{theorem-2-2-current-collar}{ }

Pathwise in the strict past, put
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 N&=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_x
 \left(\sum_i|n_i(x)|^2\right)^{1/2},\\
 M_W&=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_x\{\E_f|m+X|^2\}^{1/2},\\
 M_Y&=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_x
 \left\{\E_f\sum_i|Y_i|^2\right\}^{1/2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.9}
\end{equation}
Let $\lambda_2=\lambda_{\min}A(2p)$ and use the exact R-130 constants
\[
 L_6={1143\over250P},\qquad H_6={7083\over500P},
 \qquad P=4+10^{-12}.                                 \tag{2.10}
\]
Define
\[
\boxed{
 C_{\rm past}=
 {8\sqrt3L_6|p|\over\lambda_2}NM_W
 +{4\sqrt3H_6\over\lambda_2}NM_Y
 +{2H_6\over\lambda_2}N^2.}                         \tag{2.11}
\]
Then
\[
 \cA''(0)[H,H]>
 \left({7\over50}-C_{\rm past}\right)\|H\|_{\Frob}^2. \tag{2.12}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{C_{\rm past}\le {1\over25}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \cA''(0)[H,H]>{1\over10}\|H\|_{\Frob}^2.}          \tag{2.13}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
In the spatial-gradient direct sum, the R-130 derivative envelopes and
Cauchy-Schwarz give
\[
 |T_1|\le2\sqrt3L_6N M_Wbe\|H\|_{\Frob}^2,
 \quad
 |T_2|\le2\sqrt3H_6N M_Yb^2\|H\|_{\Frob}^2,
 \quad
 |T_3|\le H_6N^2b^2\|H\|_{\Frob}^2,                 \tag{2.14}
\]
where $b^2\le2/(V\lambda_2)$ and $e=2|p|b$.  The factor $\sqrt3$ is the
joint-Gaussian fourth-moment bound
$\E|Z|^2|U|^2\le3\E|Z|^2\E|U|^2$ and
$\E|Z|^4\le3(\E|Z|^2)^2$.  Integrating over volume $V$ gives (2.11).
Combine it with (2.8).  The implication (2.13) assumes
$C_{\rm past}(\omega)\le1/25$ for almost every past realization.  If $H$ is
past-dependent, an averaged collar alone is insufficient; the corresponding
weighted form estimate must control
$\E[C_{\rm past}\|H\|_{\Frob}^2]$. \hfill$\square$

The registered hypotheses supply no uniform bounds on $N,M_W,M_Y$.  Thus
(2.13) is a real sufficient theorem, but not an unconditional nonzero-past
production theorem.  The missing input is either this collar or a once-owned
source/sextic estimate that pays exactly $\Delta_n$.

\section*{4. Nonlinear predictable directions}
\hypertarget{section-4-nonlinear}{ }

Take a legal fixed-law control direction
\[
 h_2(t,\xi_1)=t\phi(\xi_1),
 \qquad z=S_2\phi,
 \qquad y_i=D_{2,i}\phi.                              \tag{4.1}
\]
The exact endpoint Hessian is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \cR''(0)[\phi,\phi]=\int\E\bigg[&\sum_i\left\{
 y_i^*By_i+2y_i^*DB[z]V_i+{1\over2}V_i^*D^2B[z,z]V_i
 \right\}\\
 &-{1\over2}\Tr\{D^2B[z,z]Q_0\}\bigg]dx .
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.2}
\end{equation}
There is no $D_{\xi_1}\phi$ or feedback-connection term in (4.2): $\phi$ is
held fixed as an element of the predictable control space.

Let $K(\xi_1)$ be the measurable symmetric twelve-real conditional Hessian of
the complete endpoint plus terminal sixth-power term, excluding the source
cost, in the $\xi_2$ source metric.  On its bounded predictable simple form
core,
\[
 \cA''(0)[\phi,\phi]
 =\E\left[\phi^*\left({9\over10}\Id+K(\xi_1)\right)\phi\right]. \tag{4.3}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 4.1 (exact conditional-operator gate)}
\hypertarget{theorem-4-1-conditional-gate}{ }

Subject to the displayed form-domain integrability, the following are
equivalent:
\[
 \cA''(0)[\phi,\phi]\ge{1\over10}\E|\phi|^2
 \quad\hbox{for every bounded predictable simple }\phi, \tag{4.4}
\]
\[
 \boxed{K(\xi_1)\succeq-{4\over5}\Id\quad\hbox{almost surely}.} \tag{4.5}
\]
Sufficiency is immediate.  For necessity, test rational directions $q$ on
measurable sets where
$q^*(K+4\Id/5)q<-\varepsilon$ and truncate to $\|K\|\le M$.  A stronger
uniform strict gap requires $K\succeq(-4/5+\delta)\Id$.  Extension from the
simple core to arbitrary $L^2$ directions requires a declared closed form
domain.

R-151 tests only the finite family $\phi=H\xi_1$ in averaged expectation.  It
does not prove (4.5).  The logical gap is exact.  Let $X\sim N(0,1)$ and on the
natural form domain take
\[
 K(X)=-cX^2,
 \qquad c={1\over5}.                                  \tag{4.6}
\]
For every linear $\phi=HX$, the normalized adverse coefficient is
$3c=3/5<4/5$.  Let $\chi\in C_c^\infty(-1,1)$ be nonzero and
$\phi_R(x)=\chi(x-R)$.  Its ordinary $C^3$ norms are independent of $R$, while
\[
 {\E[K(X)\phi_R(X)^2]\over\E[\phi_R(X)^2]}
 \le-c(R-1)^2.                                       \tag{4.7}
\]
At $R=4$ the source-plus-owner coefficient is at most
$9/10-9/5=-9/10$.  Thus linear testing and ordinary bounded smoothness do not
imply the conditional gate.  This is a one-Gaussian-coordinate logical
non-implication fixture on an unbounded multiplication-form domain, not the
production operator $K$ and not an A1 counterexample.

Two other derivatives must remain separate.  A curved control parametrization
$\Theta$ adds
\[
 D^2(\cA\circ\Theta)[u,u]
 =D^2\cA[D\Theta u,D\Theta u]
 +D\cA[D^2\Theta(u,u)],                               \tag{4.8}
\]
while Gaussian-source differentiation adds the R-128 inverse-Jacobian and
$D\cA\cdot D_\xi^2h$ connection.  Neither term belongs to (4.2), and neither
vanishes without a full criticality theorem.

\section*{5. Multi-root one-use criterion}
\hypertarget{section-5-multiroot}{ }

At the finite cylindrical/root-coordinate matrix level, let
$\mathcal X=\bigoplus_e\mathcal X_e$ be the direct sum of local pair
coordinates and let their actual predictable source incidence be
\[
 J=[J_e]:\mathcal X\longrightarrow\cS,
 \qquad G=J^*J.                                       \tag{5.1}
\]
The source Hessian is $9G/10$, once.  If $E,S,L$ denote the fully recombined
endpoint, sixth-power, and genuine low Hessians, put
\[
 M=E+{9\over10}G+S+L.                                \tag{5.2}
\]
The exact global target is
\[
 \boxed{M\succeq\mu G\quad\hbox{for some }\mu>0.}    \tag{5.3}
\]
For $G\succ0$, this is the generalized-eigenvalue test
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(G^{-1/2}MG^{-1/2})\ge\mu.            \tag{5.4}
\]
If $G$ is singular, decompose
$\mathcal X=\ker J\oplus(\ker J)^\perp$.  The exact kernel-Schur conditions
are
\[
 M_{00}\succeq0,
 \qquad\operatorname{Ran}M_{01}\subseteq
 \operatorname{Ran}M_{00}^{1/2},                     \tag{5.5}
\]
\[
 M_{11}-M_{10}M_{00}^{\dagger}M_{01}
 \succeq\mu G_{11}.                                  \tag{5.6}
\]
An infinite-dimensional predictable-control extension additionally requires
a declared closed form domain, closed-range compatibility, and a well-defined
Moore--Penrose form; fixed Fourier cutoff alone does not supply those facts.

An attempted local source allocation with fractions $0\le\theta_e\le1$ is
legal only if
\[
 G-\operatorname{diag}(\theta_eJ_e^*J_e)\succeq0.    \tag{5.7}
\]
At $\theta_e=1$, the residual has zero diagonal blocks; positivity forces all
off-diagonal $J_e^*J_f$ to vanish.  Thus full local source payments can be
summed only for genuinely orthogonal source incidences.  Canonical Doob
coordinates can diagonalize the source norm, but they do not delete the
cross-root blocks of $E,S,L$.

Even source orthogonality and the strengthened local gap do not imply (5.3).
Take $G=\Id_2$, $S=L=0$, and
\[
 E=\begin{pmatrix}-3/4&-1/5\\-1/5&-3/4\end{pmatrix}.
                                                               \tag{5.8}
\]
Each diagonal local action has gap $-3/4+9/10=3/20>7/50$, but
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}3/20&-1/5\\-1/5&3/20\end{pmatrix}
\quad\hbox{has eigenvalues}\quad -{1\over20},\ {7\over20}. \tag{5.9}
\]
This is an exact linear-algebra non-implication fixture.  It is not the
production cross-root matrix.  Its consequence is that the actual production
$M$ must be constructed before any pairwise R-151/R-152 gaps are aggregated.
