\section{Exact bilinear innovation-bracket lemma}

Let $P_j=\E[\,\cdot\mid\cF_j]$ and $d_j=P_j-P_{j-1}$.  Let $Y_j=d_jY$ be a
complete Hilbert-valued current packet, let $H$ be a terminal adapted
coefficient, let $P$ be $\cF_{j-1}$-measurable, and let $\Lambda$ be any
deterministic continuous bilinear spatial contraction.  Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\Lambda(P,H,Y_j)=\E\Lambda(P,d_jH,Y_j).}             \tag{5.1}
\]
Indeed $P_jH=P_{j-1}H+d_jH$, and the predictable term centers against
$Y_j$.  Equivalently, for $H_j=P_jH$ and $Y_j=P_jY$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \E_{j-1}[\langle H_j,Y_j\rangle-
             \langle H_{j-1},Y_{j-1}\rangle]
 =\E_{j-1}\langle d_jH,d_jY\rangle.}                    \tag{5.2}
\]
Thus, for every component admitting the factorisation in (5.1), complete Doob
projection replaces the terminal coefficient by its innovation and leaves a
predictable quadratic-covariation bracket, not zero and not a Malliavin
derivative.  Equations (4.14) and (5.1) do not by themselves prove that the
terminal square, coefficient curvature, complete Wick forest, trace, and
low-end terms in the entire nonlinear safe packet admit one such bracket
representation.  That exact packet-to-bracket reconstruction remains part of
the successor gate.

If the exact companion in one fixed Hilbert metric is
$\|d_jH\|^2/2$, ordinary polarization gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \langle d_jH,d_jY\rangle+{1\over2}\|d_jH\|^2
 ={1\over2}\{\|d_j(H+Y)\|^2-\|d_jY\|^2\}.}             \tag{5.3}
\]
The final negative term is the fresh injection restored by the R-069/R-070
raw-to-Wick trace.  In an adapted production metric, curvature and Wick
compensators remain; (5.3) supplies no standalone sign.

The Hilbert martingale square function is nevertheless genuinely one-use:
\[
 \sum_j\E\|d_jH\|^2
 =\E\|H-P_{j_0-1}H\|^2\le\E\|H\|^2.                    \tag{5.4}
\]
Conditional projections commute with deterministic smooth spatial blocks,
so the same statement holds after summing their $L_x^2$ squares.  Since
\[
 |\widehat K_t|
 \le t\sup_z\|D^3M(z)\|\,|A|,                            \tag{5.5}
\]
one obtains
\[

 \int_0^1(1-t)\sum_{j,m}\E

 \lesssim_e\E\|A\|_2^2.}                               \tag{5.6}

This controls the unweighted innovation coordinate once.  It does not yet
control the spatially weighted bracket in (4.14).
