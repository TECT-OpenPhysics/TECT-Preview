\section{Purpose and scope}

R-076 left three proposed largest-root classes: a fresh Gaussian root, a
largest explicit control payload, and a coefficient-only balanced root.  The
proposal correctly identified the causal issue, but it was not yet a
disjoint theorem.  Ties between coefficient and payload inputs, and Wick
contractions which remove a nominal top Gaussian pair, make a raw-monomial
ownership rule unsafe.

This note replaces that proposal by two exact operations performed in a
fixed order.

First, every complete terminal control increment is decomposed into its
strict-past conditional mean and complete Doob differences.  The latter are
the canonical fresh-Gaussian packets.  Their signed expectation is exactly
zero at every cutoff.  The cancellation includes the current square,
coefficient Wick term, both restored first variations, every R-063 lower
chaos, and the trace companion; none of those terms centers separately.

Second, inside the remaining predictable endpoint algebra, the only new
R-076 shifted resonance is split by smooth Littlewood--Paley input indices.
If the multiplier/current resonance scale is no larger than the largest
explicit payload scale, the complete block is bounded by the sharpened
R-076 Besov theorem and the already available fifteenth moment.  The
complement is one genuine coefficient-dominant high--high-to-low form.

Thus the old three proposed estimates are replaced by one exact cancellation,
one proved absolute estimate, and one smaller signed successor.  The last
successor, controlled-shell one-use, the $q=10/9$ Nelson theorem, an
interacting measure, floor or regulator removal, and infinite volume are not
proved here.  All statements use the fixed positive production floor,
common real-even regulators, the strict whole-shell filtration, regular
one-shot controls, exact non-aliased products, the R-066 finite-low
convention, and smooth inhomogeneous Littlewood--Paley blocks for analysis.
The physical sharp-cube regulator is not changed.

\section{Canonical complete-packet Doob decomposition}

Fix a terminal shell $J$.  Let $(g_r,d_r)$ be the value and derivative
Gaussian increments and let
\[
 X_r=\sum_{\ell\le r}g_\ell,\qquad
 DX_r=\sum_{\ell\le r}d_\ell .                          \tag{2.1}
\]
Write the partial control shifts, including the fixed low block, as
\[
 A^{(k)}=A_{<j_0}+\sum_{j_0\le\ell\le k}a_\ell,
 \qquad C^{(k)}=DA^{(k)},                               \tag{2.2}
\]
and set $A^{(j_0-1)}=A_{<j_0}$.  Since $a_\ell$ is
$\cF_{\ell-1}$-measurable, $A^{(k)}$ and $C^{(k)}$ are
$\cF_{k-1}$-measurable.

For any shift $H$ measurable before the next Gaussian shell define
\[
 \mathcal W_r^{(J)}(H)
 :=W_r^{(J)}(X_r+H,DX_r+DH),                             \tag{2.3}
\]
where $W_r^{(J)}$ is the R-066 terminal-backward heat energy.  The terminal
increment created by control shell $k$ is
\[
 \Phi_k=\mathcal W_J^{(J)}(A^{(k)})
       -\mathcal W_J^{(J)}(A^{(k-1)}).                  \tag{2.4}
\]

\subsection{Exact theorem}

For $j_0\le k\le r\le J$ put
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak G_{r,k}:={}&
 \{\mathcal W_r^{(J)}(A^{(k)})
   -\mathcal W_r^{(J)}(A^{(k-1)})\}\\
 &-\{\mathcal W_{r-1}^{(J)}(A^{(k)})
   -\mathcal W_{r-1}^{(J)}(A^{(k-1)})\}.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{2.5}
\]
Iterating the R-066 backward-heat martingale from $J$ down to $r$ gives
\[
 \E[\Phi_k\mid\cF_r]
 =\mathcal W_r^{(J)}(A^{(k)})
  -\mathcal W_r^{(J)}(A^{(k-1)}).                       \tag{2.6}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathfrak G_{r,k}
 =\E[\Phi_k\mid\cF_r]-\E[\Phi_k\mid\cF_{r-1}],       \tag{2.7}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 \E[\mathfrak G_{r,k}\mid\cF_{r-1}]=0.}               \tag{2.8}
\]
The finite Doob sum is the exact identity
\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_k=\mathfrak P_k+
          \sum_{r=k}^{J}\mathfrak G_{r,k},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak P_k:=
 \mathcal W_{k-1}^{(J)}(A^{(k)})
 -\mathcal W_{k-1}^{(J)}(A^{(k-1)}).}                   \tag{2.9}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\sum_{k=j_0}^{J}\sum_{r=k}^{J}\mathfrak G_{r,k}=0}
 \quad\hbox{for every }J.                               \tag{2.10}
\]
No dyadic estimate or probability moment is used in (2.10).  Summing the
predictable baselines $\mathfrak P_k$ gives exactly the R-066 controlled
discrete Ito formula.  Thus the fresh-root branch is not a new absolute
Carleson estimate: in the complete signed endpoint it is already the
martingale part removed by the backward-heat coordinate.

This statement is deliberately weaker than
\[
 \sup_J\E\sum_{k\le r}|\mathfrak G_{r,k}|<\infty.        \tag{2.11}
\]
Equation (2.11) is neither needed nor proved.  Taking absolute values before
the conditional cancellation would return to the critical architectures
excluded by R-073 and R-076.

\section{Why the complete Wick forest must stay inside the packet}

At finite cutoff, condition on $\cF_{r-1}$.  Both partial shifts in (2.5)
are now deterministic relative to the fresh shell $r$.  The heat-averaged
coefficient increment has the form
\[
 F_{r,k}^{(J)}(x)
 =B_r^{(J)}(x+A^{(k)})-B_r^{(J)}(x+A^{(k-1)}).           \tag{3.1}
\]
The R-063 Taylor/Wick algebra applies conditionally.  With its root-shell
notation, the complete coefficient bracket is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_\ell\{&F_{r,k}^{(J)}(x_\ell):Q_\ell\\
 &+DF_{r,k}^{(J)}(x_\ell):
       (\mathrm P_3+\mathrm P_1)\\
 &+\tfrac12D^2F_{r,k}^{(J)}(x_\ell):
   (\mathrm P_4+\mathrm P_2^{\rm cross}
      +\Sigma_\ell Q_\ell+\mathrm P_0^{\rm cross})\\
 &+\mathcal R_{3,\ell}^{F_{r,k}^{(J)}}:Q_\ell\}.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{3.2}
\]
Here (3.2) is a finite-cutoff conditional equality, not a claim that an
arbitrary adapted terminal coefficient has a finite global chaos expansion.
It is accompanied by the derivative-current pieces of $\mathcal W$.

The scalar identities expose the issue:
\[
 Z(Y^2-\gamma)=:ZYY:+2kY,                               \tag{3.3}
\]
\[
 Z^2(Y^2-\gamma)=:ZZYY:+\sigma(Y^2-\gamma)
                  +4k:ZY:+2k^2.                        \tag{3.4}
\]
The lower terms in (3.3)--(3.4) are generally nonzero.  A contraction may
turn a nominal top Gaussian pair into $\Sigma Q$, a second chaos, or a zero
chaos.  It is therefore false to give the top chaos one causal owner and
discard or separately center its companions.

R-073 restores both first variations and the terminal square before
raw-to-Wick conversion.  R-066 transports the covariance trace and retains
the finite-low boundary.  Equations (2.5)--(2.10) apply to that complete
packet.  They do not assert
\[
 \E\mathrm P_1=\E\mathrm P_2
 =\E(\Sigma Q)=\E\mathrm P_0=0                          \tag{3.5}
\]
term by term.  This is the precise sense in which the R-063 forest
``reassembles after conditioning.''

Combining (2.10) with the already proved R-066 trace and low-block bounds
gives, for every $\eta,\zeta>0$ and the corresponding fixed $j_0$,
\[
 \E\{\mathcal F_J^{\rm fresh}+\mathcal T_J
        +\mathcal L_{<j_0}\}
 \ge-\eta\E\sum_j\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}^2
     -\zeta\E\|Z_J\|_6^6-C_{\eta,\zeta,j_0},           \tag{3.6}
\]
uniformly in $J$, where
$\E\mathcal F_J^{\rm fresh}=0$.  The signed trace and finite-low estimates,
not a fictional termwise forest centering, supply the right side.

\section{Payload-comparable resonance theorem}

Use a smooth inhomogeneous Littlewood--Paley analysis partition
$(\Delta_m)_{m\ge-1}$.  This is an analysis device; the physical regulator
may remain the pinned sharp cube.  Let
\[
 J=M(U)^TDU,
 \qquad K_t=D^3M(U+tA)-D^3M(U).                          \tag{4.1}
\]
At the fixed positive floor,
\[
 \sup_{t\in[0,1]}\|K_t\|_\infty
 \le2\sup_z\|D^3M(z)\|<\infty.                         \tag{4.2}
\]
R-050/R-075 give, for $s=1/2+\kappa<1$ and every finite $p$,
\[
 R:=\|J\|_{\cC^{-s}}\in L^p(\Omega).                   \tag{4.3}
\]

Let the R-076 input-maximum expansion, with a fixed labelled tie rule, be
\[
 A^3DA=\sum_{r\ge-1}G_r.                                \tag{4.4}
\]
Every $G_r$ contains each four-input monomial exactly once and has Fourier
support in a ball of radius $C2^r$, even when its inputs make a low output.
For a fixed resonance width $L$, define directly at finite cutoff
\[
\begin{aligned}
 T_{\le}:={1\over2}\int_0^1(1-t)^2
 \sum_r\sum_{\substack{|m-n|\le L\\m\le r+L}}
 \int\langle(\Delta_mK_t)(\Delta_nJ),Q_{\rm II}G_r\rangle
 \dd x\dd t .
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{4.5}
\]
Formula (4.5), rather than a prior claim that $K_t\circ J$ exists, is the
definition of the cutoff trilinear form.

\subsection{Block estimate}

Smooth analysis blocks are uniformly bounded on $L^\infty$, and (4.3)
gives
\[
 \|\Delta_mK_t\|_\infty\lesssim_e1,
 \qquad
 \|\Delta_nJ\|_\infty\lesssim2^{sn}R.                  \tag{4.6}
\]
Since $s>0$,
\[
 \sum_{m\le r+L}\sum_{|m-n|\le L}2^{sn}
 \le C_{s,L}2^{sr}.                                    \tag{4.7}
\]
Thus
\[
 \boxed{
 |T_{\le}|
 \le C_{e,s,L}R\sum_r2^{sr}\|G_r\|_1.}                \tag{4.8}
\]
The estimate is pathwise.  It uses no independence, Malliavin derivative,
or control-dependent multiplier norm.  Adaptation is harmless in this
orientation because only the uniform bound (4.2) is used.

R-076 already proved
\[
 \sum_r2^{sr}\|G_r\|_1
 \lesssim\|A\|_{H^2}^{(1+s)/2}\|A\|_6^{(7-s)/2}.        \tag{4.9}
\]
Put $X=\|A\|_{H^2}^2$ and $Y=\|A\|_6^6$.  Then
\[
 |T_{\le}|\lesssim RX^aY^b,
 \quad a={1+s\over4},\quad b={7-s\over12},
 \quad c=1-a-b={1-s\over6}.                            \tag{4.10}
\]
One weighted Young inequality, after both shell sums, yields
\[
 \boxed{
 |T_{\le}|
 \le\eta X+\zeta Y
 +C_{e,s,L}\eta^{-{3(1+s)\over2(1-s)}}
  \zeta^{-{7-s\over2(1-s)}}R^{6/(1-s)}.}                \tag{4.11}
\]
At $\kappa=1/10$ and $s=3/5$,
\[
 (a,b,c)=\left({2\over5},{8\over15},{1\over15}\right),
 \qquad
 \boxed{
 |T_{\le}|\le\eta X+\zeta Y
 +C_e\eta^{-6}\zeta^{-8}R^{15}.}                       \tag{4.12}
\]
The fifteenth moment is already supplied by (4.3).  No shellwise Young
inequality is used, so the Cameron--Martin energy and sextic budget are each
spent once.

The branch $m\le r+L$ includes a unique maximal payload input, payload ties,
mixed coefficient/payload ties, and the old pair-high cases.  A second
explicit high input is not required.  High--high-to-low interactions inside
$G_r$ are retained because (4.8) uses the input scale and $L^1$ norm, not an
output projection.

\subsection{A valid but dominated pair-high refinement}

One explored route first extracted a restricted subfamily with two
comparable explicit high inputs.  If a fraction $\theta$ of the companion
shell's Cameron--Martin gain is used before sequence Holder, it gives
\[
 |T_{\rm pair}|
 \lesssim N_{j_0}^{-\theta}R X^\alpha Y^\beta,
 \quad
 \alpha={1+s+2\theta\over4},\quad
 \beta={7-s-2\theta\over12}.                            \tag{4.13}
\]
The admissible interval is $0<\theta<(1-s)/2$.  Choosing
$\theta=(1-s)/4$ gives, at $s=3/5$,
\[
 \theta={1\over10},\quad
 \alpha={9\over20},\quad
 \beta={31\over60},\quad
 1-\alpha-\beta={1\over30},                             \tag{4.14}
\]
and hence
\[
 |T_{\rm pair}|
 \le\eta X+\zeta Y
 +C\eta^{-27/2}\zeta^{-31/2}N_{j_0}^{-3}R^{30}.         \tag{4.15}
\]
This is a valid restricted refinement and records the earlier no-reuse
search.  It is not the primary proof: (4.12) covers the entire
payload-comparable block with the smaller fifteenth moment and without
extracting a pair.  The refinement may still be useful if an explicit
high-floor decay is later needed.

\section{The exact coefficient-dominant residual}

The complement of (4.5) is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 T_{>}:={1\over2}\int_0^1(1-t)^2
 \sum_r\sum_{\substack{|m-n|\le L\\m>r+L}}
 \int\langle(\Delta_mK_t)(\Delta_nJ),Q_{\rm II}G_r\rangle
 \dd x\dd t .
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{5.1}
\]
Equations (4.5) and (5.1) are a disjoint exhaustive partition of the shifted
resonance at every finite cutoff.  Equality is assigned to $T_{\le}$.

Because $G_r$ is ball-supported, a fixed enlargement $L_*$ gives
\[
 T_{>}=\sum_r\int
 S_{r+L_*}\left[
  \sum_{\substack{|m-n|\le L\\m>r+L}}
   (\Delta_mK_t)(\Delta_nJ)\right]G_r,                  \tag{5.2}
\]
with the path weight and tensor contractions suppressed.  This is a genuine
high--high-to-low form.  Its two high factors may create arbitrarily low
output.  The elementary carrier
\[
 e^{iNx}e^{-iNx}=1                                     \tag{5.3}
\]
shows that no deterministic separation decay follows from $m-r\to\infty$.
The zero-mode fixture in R-076 is the corresponding production-frame
diagnostic.

The proper remaining object is not isolated $T_{>}$.  Let
$\mathfrak B_J^{>}$ denote the nonduplicating predictable block containing

\begin{enumerate}
\item $T_{>}$ and the base-frozen term not already paid by R-075/R-076;
\item both R-073 restored first variations;
\item the corresponding terminal-square polarization;
\item coefficient curvature and the complete R-063
$\mathrm P_3/\mathrm P_1$ and
$\mathrm P_4/\mathrm P_2/\Sigma Q/\mathrm P_0$ conversion;
\item R-066 trace transport and the finite-low boundary.
\end{enumerate}

The Doob projection of Section 2 may be applied only to complete fresh-root
packets inside this list.  Its conditional compensator, Wick contractions,
mixed ties, and coefficient-control-high pieces remain in
$\mathfrak B_J^{>}$.  The smallest honest successor is
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\mathfrak B_J^{>}
 \ge-\eta\E\sum_j\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}^2
     -\zeta\E\|Z_J\|_6^6-C_{\eta,\zeta,e,j_0}}
                                                               \tag{5.4}
\]
uniformly in $J$, for regular one-shot strict-past controls.  If (5.4) is
proved, R-075 graph recovery supplies the fixed-cutoff finite-energy
extension and R-066 completes controlled-shell one-use.  Only then may the
existing $q=10/9$ equivalence be used for Nelson synthesis.

\section{Correction of the old root map and failed routes}

The exact proof map after this note is sequential, not a raw three-coloring
of monomials:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Decompose every complete terminal shell increment by (2.9).  The Doob
part has zero signed expectation; retain the predictable baseline.

\item In the exact endpoint representation of that baseline, split only the
shifted resonance by (4.5) and (5.1).  Pay $T_{\le}$ once by (4.11).

\item Keep $T_{>}$ with every signed companion listed below (5.3), and prove
the single lower bound (5.4).
\end{enumerate}

This ordering prevents duplication: Section 2 acts on the complete endpoint
packet, while Section 4 subtracts one proved form from its predictable
baseline.

The investigated alternatives are now completely recorded.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Raw monomial largest-root ownership fails as a theorem.}
Coefficient/payload ties have no declared unique owner, and Wick contraction
can remove the nominal largest Gaussian pair.  This is recorded as
\texttt{AUDIT-2026-07-25-A13-R076-ROOT-TAXONOMY}.  The proposal in R-076 was
explicitly open; the correction changes no earlier proved identity.

\item \textbf{Termwise forest centering fails.}  Equations (3.3)--(3.4)
contain nonzero lower chaoses.  Only the complete packet has the martingale
cancellation (2.10).

\item \textbf{Absolute fresh-root summability is unnecessary and unproved.}
The required variational quantity is signed expectation.  Replacing (2.10)
by (2.11) discards the load-bearing cancellation.

\item \textbf{A standalone shifted resonance is not constructed.}
$L^\infty\times\cC^{-s}$ does not canonically define $K_t\circ J$.
Equation (4.5) defines and bounds the cutoff trilinear form directly.

\item \textbf{Raw sharp projectors are not used in (4.6).}  Their
$L^\infty$ operator norms are not uniformly controlled.  Smooth analysis
blocks supply the required uniform kernel bound without altering the
physical sharp regulator.

\item \textbf{Output support does not kill $T_{>}$.}  Equation (5.3) is an
exact counterexample.  The low-output insertion in (5.2) isolates rather
than removes the obstruction.

\item \textbf{The pair-high harvest is valid but dominated.}  It gives
(4.15), but the simpler geometric sum proves (4.12) for the whole
payload-comparable orientation with a smaller moment.

\item \textbf{Gaussian integration by parts is not a substitute.}  Applied
to a later adapted control it differentiates an uncontrolled Malliavin
direction.  The Doob packet uses only strict-past measurability and avoids
that derivative.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}

\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  The lower-chaos polynomials in (3.3)--(3.4) are

\item \textbf{``Doob cancellation proves an absolute Carleson estimate.''}
\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  It proves (2.10), not (2.11).  The signed
expectation is exactly the quantity needed at this stage.

\item \textbf{``Later adapted controls can be frozen before every earlier
root.''} \textbf{UPHELD as false.}  Partial controls are frozen only after
the causal shell telescope.  Formula (2.9) never freezes the terminal shift

\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  Only the oriented finite trilinear form (4.5) is
defined.  This avoids repeating the separated-multiplier error of R-076.

\item \textbf{``High--high-to-low inside the payload was discarded.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  The input-maximum $G_r$ blocks retain it.  No output
projection appears in (4.8)--(4.9).

\item \textbf{``High--high-to-low in the multiplier/current product is also

\item \textbf{``Every payload tie contains two maximal explicit factors.''}

\item \textbf{``The optional thirtieth-moment refinement is the main

\item \textbf{``Smooth analysis blocks change the sharp-cube model.''}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  They are used only for Bony analysis of an already

\item \textbf{``This closes controlled-shell one-use or Sector A.''}
\textbf{UPHELD as an overclaim.}  The complete coefficient-dominant bound
