\section{Exact Gibbs path and endpoint likelihood}

Fix a finite cutoff $J$ and write $q=10/9$.  Throughout this note the
R-093 stabilized potential and energy are
\[
 U_J(\phi):={3\over20}\|\phi\|_6^6,\qquad
 G_J(x):=U_J(K_Jx)+{1\over2}\{x^TT_J(x)x-\Tr T_J(x)\}. \tag{2.0}
\]
Let $T_J(x)\succeq0$ be the self-coupled coefficient operator and put
\[
 z_t=\sqrt{1-t}\,x+\sqrt t\,y,
 \qquad 0\le t\le1,                                  \tag{2.1}
\]
for independent standard Gaussian sources $x,y$.  The lifted Gibbs law is
\[
 \dd\widehat\nu_{J,t}(x,y)
 ={1\over Z_{J,t}}\exp\left[-qU_J(K_Jx)
 -{q\over2}\{z_t^TT_J(x)z_t-\Tr T_J(x)\}\right]
 \dd\gamma_J(x)\dd\gamma_J(y).                     \tag{2.2}
\]
Integrating $y$ gives A9's marginal
\[
 \dd\nu_{J,t}=e^{W_{J,t}-\Phi_{J,t}}\dd\gamma_J,
 \qquad Z_{J,t}=\E_{\gamma_J}e^{W_{J,t}},
 \qquad\Phi_{J,t}=\log Z_{J,t}.                     \tag{2.3}
\]
The endpoints are
\[
 W_{J,0}=-qU_J+{q\over2}\{\Tr T_J-x^TT_Jx\},       \tag{2.4}
\]
\[
 W_{J,1}=-qU_J-{1\over2}\log\det{}_2(\Id+qT_J)
 =-qU_J+{q\over2}\Tr T_J
  -{1\over2}\log\det(\Id+qT_J).                   \tag{2.5}
\]
Thus $t=0$ is the self-coupled endpoint and $t=1$ is the controlled A8
independent-carrier endpoint.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-2.1-gibbs-endpoint-likelihood
\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (exact endpoint likelihood and entropy identities)}

Define
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal L_J(x):=W_{J,0}(x)-W_{J,1}(x)
 ={1\over2}\log\det(\Id+qT_J(x))
  -{q\over2}x^TT_J(x)x.}                              \tag{2.6}
\]
The potential and the full linear trace cancel exactly.  Moreover
\[
 {\dd\nu_{J,0}\over\dd\nu_{J,1}}
 =\exp\{\mathcal L_J-(\Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1})\},      \tag{2.7}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1}
 =\E_{\nu_{J,0}}\mathcal L_J
   -H(\nu_{J,0}\mid\nu_{J,1})}                      \tag{2.8}
\]
as well as
\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1}
 =\E_{\nu_{J,1}}\mathcal L_J
   +H(\nu_{J,1}\mid\nu_{J,0}).}                     \tag{2.9}
\]
More generally the Gibbs variational formula gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_\nu\{\E_\nu\mathcal L_J-H(\nu\mid\nu_{J,1})\}
 =\log\E_{\nu_{J,1}}e^{\mathcal L_J}
 =\Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1},}                            \tag{2.10}
\]
and the optimiser is $\nu_{J,0}$.

\emph{Proof.}
Subtract (2.5) from (2.4) to obtain (2.6).  Dividing the two normalized
densities in (2.3) gives (2.7).  Integrating its logarithm first against
$\nu_{J,0}$ and then against $\nu_{J,1}$ proves (2.8)--(2.9).  Exponentiating
(2.7) gives $Z_{J,0}/Z_{J,1}=\E_{\nu_{J,1}}e^{\mathcal L_J}$, and the Gibbs
variational formula proves (2.10). \hfill$\square$

The signs in (2.8)--(2.9) are load-bearing.  Forward entropy has a minus sign
but is taken under the unknown self-coupled Gibbs law.  Reverse entropy is
available from the controlled endpoint only with a plus sign and hence does
not upper-bound the desired free energy.

\section{Why total time integration is not a new estimate}

Let $\mathfrak B_{J,t}$ denote the complete A9 bracket from A9-(3.8).  At
every finite cutoff ordinary differentiation gives
\[
 \Phi'_{J,t}={q\over2}\E_{\nu_{J,t}}\mathfrak B_{J,t}. \tag{3.1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 {q\over2}\int_0^1\E_{\nu_{J,t}}\mathfrak B_{J,t}\dd t
 =\Phi_{J,1}-\Phi_{J,0}.                              \tag{3.2}
\]
Equation (3.2) is exact thermodynamic integration.  It does not supply a
lower bound for its right side.

The independent endpoint is bounded on both sides uniformly in the cutoff.
A8 gives the upper bound.  Since $T_J\succeq0$ implies
$-\frac12\log\det_2(\Id+qT_J)\ge0$, Jensen gives
\[
 Z_{J,1}\ge\E e^{-qU_J}\ge e^{-q\E U_J},             \tag{3.3}
\]
and the regulated point covariance makes $\sup_J\E U_J<\infty$.  Therefore a
uniform lower bound for the integral in (3.2) is equivalent, up to this
already controlled endpoint, to the missing upper bound for $\Phi_{J,0}$.

The same boundary appears in R-093 source coordinates.  There
$\nu_{J,0}=\nu_J^*$ and, for a temporally faithful source chart $h$ with
fibre entropy $\Phi_h$,
\[
 q\mathcal A_J(h)
 =-\Phi_{J,1}
  +H(\nu_h\mid\nu_{J,1})
  -\E_{\nu_h}\mathcal L_J+\Phi_h.                    \tag{3.4}
\]
Taking the directed-union infimum of the last three terms gives exactly
$\Phi_{J,1}-\Phi_{J,0}$.  R-093 near minimisers make the excess
$H(\nu_h\mid\nu_{J,0})+\Phi_h$ tend to zero.  This is the only nonnegative
excess over $-\Phi_{J,0}$.  The term $H(\nu_h\mid\nu_{J,1})$ is not an
independent reserve because (3.4) pairs it exactly with
$-\E_{\nu_h}\mathcal L_J$.

This fires the narrow route verdict
\texttt{NG-2026-07-28-A13-TOTAL-A9-TIME-INTEGRATION-IDENTITY}: reporting
(3.2), (2.10), or (3.4) as an estimate without a separately proved root-local
production lower bound is circular.  A Gibbs-only \emph{local} differential
estimate or a root-summed signed estimate remains open.

\section{Pointwise endpoint-likelihood coercivity fails}

Let $Q_N=\{n\in\mathbb Z^3:|n|_\infty\le N\}$ be an admitted common
sharp-cube cutoff.  The letter $N$ here is the physical Fourier radius; in a
dyadic convention $N=2^J$.  Let $u$ be a real active-doublet unit vector and
choose the constant physical configuration
\[
 K_Nx_A=Au.                                            \tag{4.1}
\]
Then $DK_Nx_A=0$, hence
\[
 x_A^TT_N(x_A)x_A=0.                                  \tag{4.2}
\]
The accepted two-sided production covariance ellipticity gives, on every
outer-shell mode $N/2\le|n|_\infty\le N$,
\[
 \widehat C_N(k_n)\succeq c_C\langle k_n\rangle^{-4}\Id. \tag{4.3}
\]
The exact production radial formula proved again in Section 5 gives an
active eigenvalue at least $4aA^2$.  Since $T_N$ is Fourier block diagonal at
the constant field, every outer block has an eigenvalue at least
$c_1A^2/N^2$.  There are at least $c_{\rm sh}N^3$ retained real degrees of
freedom for a fixed $c_{\rm sh}>0$, and for
$A^2/N^2$ sufficiently small, $\log(1+s)\ge s/2$ gives
\[
 \log\det(\Id+qT_N(x_A))\ge c_2A^2N.                 \tag{4.4}
\]
Equations (2.6), (4.2), and (4.4) imply
\[
 \mathcal L_N(x_A)\ge c_3A^2N.                       \tag{4.5}
\]

The configuration $x_A$ uses only the zero mode.  Since the admitted
covariance has $\widehat C(0)>0$, its Cameron--Martin norm obeys
$\|x_A\|^2=c_{\rm CM}A^2$ with $c_{\rm CM}$ independent of $N$.
Take $A=\sigma N^{1/4}$.  For any fixed $\zeta,\eta\ge0$,
\[
 \mathcal L_N(x_A)-\zeta\|Au\|_6^6-\eta\|x_A\|^2
 \ge N^{3/2}\{c_3\sigma^2-\zeta L^3\sigma^6\}
      -O_\eta(N^{1/2}).                               \tag{4.6}
\]
Choosing $\sigma>0$ sufficiently small makes (4.6) tend to $+\infty$.
Therefore no cutoff-uniform pointwise upper bound of the likelihood by any
fixed sextic plus Cameron--Martin payment exists.

This fires
\texttt{NG-2026-07-28-A13-POINTWISE-ENDPOINT-LIKELIHOOD-COERCIVITY}.  The
constant field is a Gaussian-null configuration.  Suppressing all nonconstant
modes has an entropic cost invisible in a pointwise inequality, so (4.6) is
not a Gibbs or Nelson counterexample.

\section{Exact production radial coefficient}

\[
 P=4+10^{-12},\qquad
 a={9\over500P},\quad b={3\over400P},\quad
 c={3\over320P},\quad d=a+2b+c.                       \tag{5.1}
\]
Let $E_Z$ be the active four-real doublet projector, let $J$ be its phase

 B(fu)=yB_\infty(u)+\delta_\epsilon(f)u\otimes u,      \tag{5.2}

 B_\infty(u)=4d(E_Z-Ju\otimes Ju)-(8b+4c)u\otimes u, \tag{5.3}

\[
 \delta_\epsilon(f)
 ={8b\epsilon y\over y+\epsilon}
  +{4c\epsilon^2y\over(y+\epsilon)^2}.               \tag{5.4}

In particular
\[
 B_\infty(u)u=4au,\qquad
 0\le\delta_\epsilon(f)\le K_\epsilon,\qquad
 K_\epsilon:=\epsilon(8b+c).                          \tag{5.5}

\emph{Proof.}
The radial Fierz identities are
\[

Since $4d-(8b+4c)=4a$, (5.5)'s eigenvalue follows.  Finally
$y/(y+\epsilon)\le1$ and

\section{The production $1{:}2$ merge obstruction}

Let $k$ and $2k$ both lie inside one fixed cutoff, put
$\theta=k\cdot x$, and use normalized torus average $\langle\cdot\rangle$.
For fixed positive-semidefinite derivative covariance matrices $\Gamma_i$,
define
\[
 V_\Gamma(X)={1\over2}\left\langle
 \sum_i(\partial_iX)^TB(X)\partial_iX
 -\sum_iB(X):\Gamma_i\right\rangle,                  \tag{6.1}
\]
\[
 g_{\Gamma,u}:=\sum_i u^T\Gamma_i u<\infty.           \tag{6.2}
\]
For fixed $r\ne0$ set
\[
 F=1+r\cos\theta-\cos2\theta,\quad
 F_1=1+r\cos\theta,\quad
 F_2=1-\cos2\theta,\quad F_0=1.                     \tag{6.3}
\]
The inclusion--exclusion merge defect is
\[
 \Delta_\lambda
 :=V_\Gamma(\lambda Fu)-V_\Gamma(\lambda F_1u)
   -V_\Gamma(\lambda F_2u)+V_\Gamma(\lambda F_0u).   \tag{6.4}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-6.1-production-radial-merge-nogo
\subsection*{Theorem 6.1 (exact production radial merge boundary)}

For every $\lambda>0$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \Delta_\lambda\le
 -{a|k|^2r^2\over2}\lambda^4
 +{K_\epsilon|k|^2(r^2+4)\over2}\lambda^2
 +K_\epsilon g_{\Gamma,u}.}                           \tag{6.5}
\]
In particular $\Delta_\lambda\to-\infty$ as
$\lambda\to\infty$.  More explicitly, if
\[
 A={a|k|^2r^2\over2},\quad
 B={K_\epsilon|k|^2(r^2+4)\over2},\quad
 C=K_\epsilon g_{\Gamma,u},                           \tag{6.6}
\]
then $\Delta_\lambda<-M$ whenever
\[
 \lambda^2>{B+\sqrt{B^2+4A(C+M)}\over2A}.             \tag{6.7}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Exact trigonometric integration gives
\[
 \left\langle F^2|F'|^2-F_1^2|F_1'|^2-F_2^2|F_2'|^2\right\rangle
 =-{r^2\over4},                                      \tag{6.8}
\]
\[
 \langle F^2-F_1^2-F_2^2+F_0^2\rangle=0,             \tag{6.9}
\]
and
\[
 \langle|F'|^2+|F_1'|^2+|F_2'|^2\rangle=r^2+4.       \tag{6.10}
\]
By (5.5), the zero-floor raw defect is exactly
$-(a|k|^2r^2/2)\lambda^4$, including the physical outer factor $1/2$.
Its covariance trace cancels by (6.9).  Bounding every rational raw term in
absolute value by $K_\epsilon$ and using (6.10) gives the quadratic term in
(6.5).  Four numbers in $[0,K_\epsilon]$ have the signed
inclusion--exclusion magnitude at most $2K_\epsilon$; the outer $1/2$ leaves
the last term in (6.5).  Solving the quadratic inequality in $\lambda^2$
proves (6.7). \hfill$\square$

Thus there is no finite fixed-cutoff constant $K_J$ for which
\[
 V_\Gamma(\lambda Fu)\ge
 V_\Gamma(\lambda F_1u)+V_\Gamma(\lambda F_2u)
 -V_\Gamma(\lambda F_0u)-K_J                         \tag{6.11}
\]
holds at every amplitude.  This fires
\texttt{NG-2026-07-28-A13-PRODUCTION-INPUT-MODE-}\allowbreak
\texttt{MERGE-TENSORIZATION}.
It strengthens R-105's scalar sign diagnostic to the exact production
coefficient, same-root covariance-normal coordinate.

The stabilising sextic cannot be split among these leaves either.  Exact
integration gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \langle F^6-F_1^6-F_2^6+F_0^6\rangle
 =-{15r^2(9r^2+2)\over32}<0.}                         \tag{6.12}
\]
This does not say that the physical sextic or complete action is negative.
It says only that a leafwise inclusion--exclusion duplicates the sextic with
the wrong superadditivity sign.  Keeping the sextic once at the parent/root
remains viable and is mandatory for this candidate route.
