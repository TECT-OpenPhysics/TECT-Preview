\section{Purpose and scope}

R-093 reduced the remaining regular one-shot problem to a coefficient-
unconditioned root-local estimate for the complete R-079 future packet.  It
suggested the scale $2^{j-4k}$ for a probability root $j$ and a later physical
control shell $k>j$.  This note determines exactly where that scale is valid,
proves the resulting centered Gram-secant estimate without assuming selector
independence, and audits whether it closes the complete packet.

There are four positive conclusions.  First, the literal positive quadratic
Gram-curvature atom does have the scale $2^{j-4k}$.  Second, the full centered
Gram secant contains a mixed term of the weaker scale $2^{j-2k}$, but its
weighted Hardy sum is still form-subcritical: its mixed budget is
$X^{1/2}Y^{1/6}$ and has Young slack $1/3$.  The proof uses product-space
$L^2$--$L^6$ interpolation, not a pathwise Bernstein estimate that would ask
for an unavailable higher selector moment.  Third, the isolated fresh
derivative channel has budget $X^{1/2}Y^{1/3}$ and slack $1/6$.  Fourth, the
combined value-innovation plus heat coefficient acting on the accumulated
control derivative costs only $O(2^{-3j_0}X)$ when completed against a fixed
fraction of the already retained derivative-feedback square.

The hostile nonduplication audit is decisive.  These estimates do not yet
embed themselves into the complete R-079 packet.  After the legitimate
partial-square payment, the coefficient/derivative cross, the Gaussian value
innovation and heat residual, the R-086 $T_G^>$ coefficient-dominant branch,
and the R-093 conditional mean debts remain.  The square, covariance trace,
backward heat, R-063 forest, low term, and paid subtraction must each occur
once in the missing embedding identity.  Thus R-094 is a genuine analytic
advance and a sharper boundary, not a proof of $H_N$, REG,
$\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$, Nelson, a measure, or Sector A.  Tier stays T4.

The result is for the regular mutually orthogonal strict-past one-shot class.
A two-step cancelling revisit shows why its per-shell sixth-moment step cannot
be summed absolutely for arbitrary temporal revisits.  The complete signed
endpoint remains the required object there.

\section{Regular hypotheses and budgets}

Work on the normalized product space
$S=\Omega\times\mathbb T^3$ and write $\|\cdot\|_p$ for $L^p(S)$.
The probability filtration is $(\mathcal F_j)_{j\ge j_0-1}$.  The fresh
Gaussian value root is $g_j$, its spatial derivative is $d_j=Dg_j$, and
\[
 \mathcal Q_j=d_j\otimes d_j-\Delta\Gamma_j.             \tag{2.1}
\]
The accepted fourth-order covariance scaling gives constants independent of
the terminal cutoff such that
\[
 \|d_j\|_6\le \kappa_1 2^{j/2},\qquad
 \|\mathcal Q_j\|_3\le\kappa_Q2^j,\qquad
 \operatorname{tr}\Delta\Gamma_j\le\kappa_\Gamma2^j,   \tag{2.2}
\]
and the value covariance satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{tr}\Sigma_j^{\rm val}\le\kappa_0 2^{-j}.
                                                                    \tag{2.3}
\]

Let $M$ be the complete production frame.  Its linear rows are linear and its
normalized rational row has bounded first derivative by R-092.  Consequently,
after the common target-value heat if present,
\[
 \|M(z+a)-M(z)\|\le L_M|a|,\qquad
 |M(z)|\le L_M(1+|z|).                                  \tag{2.4}
\]
For $B(z)=M(z)Q_{\rm II}M(z)^T$, Gram expansion gives
\[
 |B(z+a)-B(z)|
 \le L_B\{(1+|z|)|a|+|a|^2\}.                          \tag{2.5}
\]

The regular controls are
\[
 a_k=K_kh_k,\qquad a_k\in\mathcal F_{k-1},              \tag{2.6}
\]
with mutually orthogonal physical shells and
\[
 \|a_k\|_2\le\kappa_K2^{-2k}q_k^{1/2},\qquad
 \|Da_k\|_2\le\kappa_D2^{-k}q_k^{1/2},\qquad
 q_k=\mathbb E\|h_k\|_{\mathcal H_k}^2.               \tag{2.7}
\]
Put
\[
 X=\sum_kq_k,\qquad v_k=\|a_k\|_6,\qquad
 A_{>j}=\sum_{k>j}a_k.                                  \tag{2.8}
\]
The regular Littlewood--Paley square function and the accepted base sixth
moment give
\[
 \sum_kv_k^6\le C_{\rm LP}\{1+Y\},\qquad
 Y=\mathbb E\|Z_*\|_6^6.                               \tag{2.9}
\]
This is the only sixth-moment input below.  No $L^p$ source moment with
$p>2$ is assumed.

\section{The factor audit: quadratic and mixed scales differ}

% evidence-anchor: theorem-3.1-quadratic-gram-curvature
\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (quadratic Gram-curvature atom)}

Let $E_k$ be a frame increment with $\|E_k\|\le L_M|a_k|$ and define
\[
 R^{(2)}_{j,k}={1\over2}\mathbb E\int
 \left\{|Q_{\rm II}^{1/2}E_k^Td_j|^2
 -(E_kQ_{\rm II}E_k^T):\Delta\Gamma_j\right\},
 \qquad j<k.                                             \tag{3.1}
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{R^{(2)}_{j,k}\ge-C_{\rm curv}2^{j-4k}q_k.}     \tag{3.2}
\]
Indeed, retain the first square and bound only the trace by (2.2), (2.4),
and (2.7).  No independence between $E_k$ and root $j$ is used.  Summing gives
\[
 \sum_{j\ge j_0}\sum_{k>j}R^{(2)}_{j,k}
 \ge-C2^{-3j_0}X.                                      \tag{3.3}
\]
The dimensionless triangular kernel has the exact diagnostic
\[
 \sum_{j\ge j_0}\sum_{k>j}2^{j-4k}
 ={8\over105}2^{-3j_0}.                                \tag{3.4}
\]

The conclusion cannot be promoted to the mixed Gram term.  For
$g=\sqrt\gamma\,\xi$, $H_2=\xi^2-1$,
$m_0=1$, and $m_1=1-\varepsilon tH_2$, Gaussian moments give
\[
 {1\over2}\mathbb E[(m_1^2-m_0^2)(g^2-\gamma)]
 =-2\gamma\varepsilon t+4\gamma\varepsilon^2t^2.       \tag{3.5}
\]
After adding $\rho t^2$, its minimum is
\[
 -{\gamma^2\varepsilon^2\over
       \rho+4\gamma\varepsilon^2}<0.                  \tag{3.6}
\]
The linear secant therefore scales as $2^{j-2k}$; only its post-Young square
has scale $2^{2j-4k}$.  The latter remains summable, with
\[
 \sum_{j\ge j_0}\sum_{k>j}2^{2j-4k}
 ={4\over45}2^{-2j_0}.                                 \tag{3.7}
\]
Equations (3.2) and (3.5) are the factor correction to the R-093 prototype.

\section{The complete centered Gram secant is form-subcritical}

% evidence-anchor: theorem-4.1-centered-gram-secant
\subsection*{Theorem 4.1 (regular centered Gram-secant bound)}

Let $Z_j=Z_*-A_{>j}$ and define
\[
 \mathcal S_{\rm sec}=\sum_{j=j_0}^{J}
 \left|\mathbb E\int
  [B(Z_*)-B(Z_j)]:\mathcal Q_j\right|.                  \tag{4.1}
\]
Then, uniformly in $J$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal S_{\rm sec}
 \le C_{\rm sec}2^{-j_0}X^{1/2}(1+Y)^{1/6}.}           \tag{4.2}
\]
Consequently, for all $\eta,\zeta\in(0,1]$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal S_{\rm sec}\le\eta X+\zeta Y
 +C_{\eta,\zeta},}                                    \tag{4.3}
\]
where one may take
\[
 C_{\eta,\zeta}\le C\{1+\eta^{-1}
       +\eta^{-3/2}\zeta^{-1/2}\}.                    \tag{4.4}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
By (2.2), (2.5), and Holder on the product space,
\[
 \mathcal S_{\rm sec}\le C\sum_j2^j
 \left\{(1+\|Z_*\|_6)\|A_{>j}\|_2
             +\|A_{>j}\|_3^2\right\}.                \tag{4.5}
\]
For the linear part, Tonelli and (2.7) give
\[
 \sum_j2^j\|A_{>j}\|_2
 \le\sum_k\|a_k\|_2\sum_{j<k}2^j
 \le C\sum_k2^{-k}q_k^{1/2}
 \le C2^{-j_0}X^{1/2}.                                 \tag{4.6}
\]

For the quadratic and cross-shell part, let $b_k=\|a_k\|_3$.  Under the
change $c_k=2^{k/2}b_k$, the tail map is convolution by
$2^{-n/2}\mathbf1_{n\ge1}$.  Its $\ell^1$ norm is $1+\sqrt2$.  Therefore
\[
 \sum_j2^j\left(\sum_{k>j}b_k\right)^2
 \le(3+2\sqrt2)\sum_k2^kb_k^2.                         \tag{4.7}
\]
The load-bearing interpolation is on $S$, not pathwise:
\[
 b_k^2=\|a_k\|_3^2\le\|a_k\|_2\|a_k\|_6.             \tag{4.8}
\]
Using (2.7), Holder in the shell index, and (2.9),
\[
 \begin{split}
 \sum_k2^kb_k^2
 &\le\kappa_K\sum_k2^{-k}q_k^{1/2}v_k\\
 &\le\kappa_KX^{1/2}
       \left(\sum_k2^{-2k}v_k^2\right)^{1/2}\\
 &\le C2^{-j_0}X^{1/2}
       \left(\sum_kv_k^6\right)^{1/6}\\
 &\le C2^{-j_0}X^{1/2}(1+Y)^{1/6}.
 \end{split}                                             \tag{4.9}
\]
This proves (4.2).  The mixed power $X^{1/2}Y^{1/6}$ has slack
$1-1/2-1/6=1/3$, and multifactor Young proves (4.3)--(4.4).
\hfill$\square$

The theorem is expectation-inside and tolerates arbitrary strict-past
selectors in the declared regular class.  A spatial Bernstein estimate for
each selected shell would incorrectly demand a higher probability moment.

\subsection*{Theorem 5.2 (combined value--heat control prefix)}

Let $\overline M_j=P_{\Sigma_{j+1:J}}M$ and let $W_{j-1}$ be the predictable
translated background.  Define
\[
 \delta_jM=\overline M_j(W_{j-1}+g_j)
             -\overline M_{j-1}(W_{j-1}).               \tag{5.4}
\]
This is exactly the value innovation plus heat compensator in R-079 (6.8).
It is conditionally centered.  Gaussian Poincare, the common-coupling
Lipschitz bound, and (2.3) give
\[
 \mathbb E_{j-1}|\delta_jM|^2\le L_M^2\kappa_0 2^{-j}.
                                                                    \tag{5.5}
\]
Let
\[
 H_j=D(A^{(j)}-A^0)=\sum_{k\le j}Da_k,\qquad
 x_j=Q_{\rm II}^{1/2}(\delta_jM)^TH_j.                  \tag{5.6}
\]
Then
\[
 \sum_j\mathbb E\|x_j\|^2
 \le C\sum_j2^{-j}\mathbb E\|H_j\|_2^2.              \tag{5.7}
\]
Without physical orthogonality, the prefix matrix
$T_{jk}=2^{-j/2}2^{-k}\mathbf1_{k\le j}$ has
\[
 \|T\|_{\rm HS}^2
 =\sum_{j\ge j_0}\sum_{k=j_0}^j2^{-j-2k}
 ={16\over7}2^{-3j_0}.                                 \tag{5.8}
\]
Hence
\[
 \boxed{\sum_j\mathbb E\|x_j\|^2
 \le C{16\over7}2^{-3j_0}X.}                          \tag{5.9}
\]
Mutually orthogonal shells improve the dimensionless constant to $2$.

For any derivative-feedback vector $y_j$ and $0<\theta<1$,
\[
 \mathbb E\langle x_j,y_j\rangle
 +{\theta\over2}\mathbb E\|y_j\|^2
 \ge-{1\over2\theta}\mathbb E\|x_j\|^2.             \tag{5.10}
\]
Thus this prefix uses only a declared fraction $\theta$ of the retained
feedback square and costs $C\theta^{-1}2^{-3j_0}X$.  The fraction
$1-\theta$ remains visible for the successor packet.

 \tau_j={1\over2}\mathbb E\int
   (\mathcal B_* -\mathcal B_j):\Delta\Gamma_j.          \tag{6.3}

For every $0<\theta<1$, the future block has the exact identity

\begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak I_j={}&

 &+\mathbb E\langle p_j+u_j,y_j\rangle

       +{\theta\over2}\mathbb E\|y_j\|^2\right].
\end{aligned}}                                           \tag{6.4}

It is merely the expansion of R-079 (3.4), but it is the necessary

so the leftover has no standalone sign.

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: The centered secant theorem already proves $H_N$.}
\textbf{UPHELD against that conclusion.}  The theorem estimates (4.1), but
the rootwise Doob-to-terminal embedding with backward heat, low, paid, trace,
and forest companions is not proved.  Equation (6.4) displays the remaining
atoms.

\item \textbf{Objection: The quadratic $2^{j-4k}$ factor was assigned to the
whole Gram increment.}  \textbf{UPHELD and repaired.}  It belongs only to
(3.1).  The mixed scalar fixture (3.5) proves that the full secant begins at
$2^{j-2k}$; Theorem 4.1 pays that weaker factor directly.

\item \textbf{Objection: The $a_k^2$ estimate silently uses a higher moment of
an adapted selector.}  \textbf{DISMISSED.}  Equation (4.8) is interpolation
on $\Omega\times\mathbb T^3$, and (4.9) uses only source $L^2$ and the accepted
terminal sixth moment.  No pathwise Bernstein step appears.

\item \textbf{Objection: Partial completion spends the endpoint square twice.}
\textbf{DISMISSED for Theorem 5.2, upheld for the naive full assembly.}
Equation (6.4) labels the fraction $\theta$ used by (5.10) and leaves exactly
$1-\theta$ in (6.6).  Any later proof must retain that reduced coefficient.

\item \textbf{Objection: The fresh derivative bound controls $T_G^>$.}
\textbf{UPHELD against that identification.}  Lemma 5.1 is an isolated
unshifted form.  The shifted adapted coefficient in (6.7) requires a genuine
expectation-inside multiplier or cancellation theorem.

\item \textbf{Objection: Correcting the zero-slack rows proves Nelson.}
\textbf{UPHELD against that conclusion.}  Positive arithmetic slack is only
a budget check.  R-093 requires the full uniform shifted inequality before
R-087 CORE can be invoked.

\item \textbf{Objection: The revisit example falsifies the full action.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  It falsifies absolute per-increment sixth-moment
summation.  The terminal shift and the signed outgoing/return contribution
cancel, so it supplies no negative divergence of the complete action.
\end{enumerate}
