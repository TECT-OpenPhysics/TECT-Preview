\section{Purpose and scope}

R-106 left the complete coherent output-frequency current as a legal
coordinate for the remaining finite-cutoff, fixed-positive-floor
$q=10/9$ production problem.  The present note determines the smallest
coordinate on which that suggestion can be correct and audits the two
equivalent Gibbs and direct-source formulations before another owner split is
introduced.

The result has ten exact parts.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The endpoint likelihood tilt has a positive entropy-production identity
      and an exact root-martingale variance decomposition.  These are
      identities, not a new free-energy bound.
\item A jointly frozen family of coherent-output Gaussian rows has an exact
      backward-resolvent density martingale.  Its conditional entropy
      inequality is saturable and therefore has no spare reserve for revealing
      a same-root nonlinear coefficient.
\item One fresh-root packet with a past-measurable output map and mixed
      baseline closes by one $\det_2$ formula without output factorisation.
\item A bounded predictable future row has a strictly positive normalization
      defect, so rowwise predictability does not license the global frozen
      backward-resolvent formula.  Stepwise conditional normalizers remain
      legal.
\item The coefficient trace has a canonical positive output allocation, but
      an exact one-Fourier-pair fixture proves that a single output frequency
      is not a signed atom.  Cancellation occurs only across the
      contraction-connected cluster $\{0,2,-2\}$.
\item Independent output determinant normalisers lose positive slack which
      grows linearly under repeated rank-one rows.  Sequential Schur updates
      are mandatory.
\item The direct source action reduces exactly to the sum of complete
      predictable baselines plus one terminal sextic and one source-energy
      payment.  Covariance mass against one deterministic predictable PSD
      weight is subdivision invariant.
\item An adapted sine fixture proves that separating the second-jet/forest
      companions creates two $M^2$ costs although their signed combination
      tends to zero.
\item A pure carrier-information bridge costs $-(d/2)\log t$ and is therefore
      unusable at the diagonal and across growing root dimension.
\item A useful Gaussian-divergence convexification identity is exact, but its
      natural inward-flow remainder has the unfavourable sign.  That route is
      parked, not declared impossible.
\end{enumerate}

The remaining theorem is one subdivision-invariant adapted complete-cluster
or matrix-Carleson estimate after all visits, all input convolutions, the
entire R-063 forest, the mixed baseline, and future feedback have been
recombined.  No such estimate is proved here.  Hence
$\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$, Nelson, removals, the interacting measure, and
Sector A remain open.  Tier stays T4.

\section{Endpoint likelihood tilt and entropy production}

Use the R-106 convention: $\nu_{J,0}$ is the self-coupled endpoint,
$\nu_{J,1}$ is the controlled independent-carrier endpoint, and
\[
 {\dd\nu_{J,0}\over\dd\nu_{J,1}}
 =\exp\{\mathcal L_J-(\Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1})\}.       \tag{2.1}
\]
To avoid confusing the new exponential parameter with the A9 interpolation
time, define for $0\le s\le1$
\[
 \psi_J(s):=\log\E_{\nu_{J,1}}e^{s\mathcal L_J},
 \qquad
 {\dd\rho_{J,s}\over\dd\nu_{J,1}}
 :=e^{s\mathcal L_J-\psi_J(s)}.                      \tag{2.2}
\]
Then $\rho_{J,0}=\nu_{J,1}$, $\rho_{J,1}=\nu_{J,0}$, and
$\psi_J(1)=\Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1}$.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-2.1-endpoint-entropy-production
\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (exact endpoint entropy production)}

At every finite cutoff,
\[
 \psi_J'(s)=\E_{\rho_{J,s}}\mathcal L_J,
 \qquad
 \psi_J''(s)=\Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)\ge0.   \tag{2.3}
\]
Moreover
\[
 H(\nu_{J,0}\mid\nu_{J,1})
 =\int_0^1s\,\Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)\dd s,  \tag{2.4}
\]
\[
 H(\nu_{J,1}\mid\nu_{J,0})
 =\int_0^1(1-s)\,\Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)\dd s,\tag{2.5}
\]
and hence
\[
 \Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1}
 =\E_{\nu_{J,0}}\mathcal L_J
  -\int_0^1s\,\Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)\dd s, \tag{2.6}
\]
\[
 \Phi_{J,0}-\Phi_{J,1}
 =\E_{\nu_{J,1}}\mathcal L_J
  +\int_0^1(1-s)\,\Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)\dd s.\tag{2.7}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Differentiate (2.2).  The two intermediate entropies are
\[
 H(\rho_{J,s}\mid\nu_{J,1})=s\psi_J'(s)-\psi_J(s), \tag{2.8}
\]
\[
 H(\rho_{J,s}\mid\nu_{J,0})
 =(s-1)\psi_J'(s)-\psi_J(s)+\psi_J(1).              \tag{2.9}
\]
Their derivatives are respectively $s\psi_J''(s)$ and
$(s-1)\psi_J''(s)$.  Integrating from zero to one proves
(2.4)--(2.7). \hfill$\square$

Let $(\mathcal F_j)$ be any finite root-reveal filtration exhausting the
cutoff source, and define the $\rho_{J,s}$ martingale differences
\[
 d_j^{(s)}F
 :=\E_{\rho_{J,s}}[F\mid\mathcal F_j]
  -\E_{\rho_{J,s}}[F\mid\mathcal F_{j-1}].           \tag{2.10}
\]
Orthogonality gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \Var_{\rho_{J,s}}(\mathcal L_J)
 =\sum_j\E_{\rho_{J,s}}|d_j^{(s)}\mathcal L_J|^2.}  \tag{2.11}
\]
Thus entropy production has a positive root decomposition.  It does not
bound the endpoint difference: the conditional laws in (2.10) depend on the
unknown tilt, and (2.6)--(2.7) are exact rewritings of R-106's endpoint
identity.

\section{Jointly frozen coherent-output likelihood martingale}

Let independent standard Gaussian roots be $\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_N$, and let
$A_r:E_r\to\mathcal H_{\rm out}$ be jointly frozen maps into the complete
coherent heat/output Hilbert space: conditionally on an initial sigma-field
$\mathcal F_0$, every $A_r$ is fixed and independent of all roots.  Put
\[
 K:=\sum_{r=1}^N A_rA_r^*,\quad
 K_{>j}:=\sum_{r>j}A_rA_r^*,\quad
 R_{>j}:=(\Id+qK_{>j})^{-1},                         \tag{3.1}
\]
\[
 s_j:=\sum_{r\le j}A_r\xi_r.                         \tag{3.2}
\]
The normalized Gaussian likelihood is
\[
 \mathcal L_A
 ={1\over2}\log\det(\Id+qK)-{q\over2}\|s_N\|^2.  \tag{3.3}
\]
If
\[
 Q_A={1\over2}(\|s_N\|^2-\Tr K),\qquad
 D_q(K)={1\over2}\{q\Tr K-\log\det(\Id+qK)\},      \tag{3.4}
\]
then
\[
 \mathcal L_A=-qQ_A-D_q(K),\qquad
 \E[e^{\mathcal L_A}\mid\mathcal F_0]=1.            \tag{3.5}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-3.1-backward-resolvent-likelihood-martingale
\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (backward-resolvent density martingale)}

Let $\mathcal F_j=\mathcal F_0\vee\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_j)$.  For
$0\le j\le N$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \log M_j
 ={1\over2}\log\det(\Id+qK)
  -{1\over2}\log\det(\Id+qK_{>j})
  -{q\over2}\langle s_j,R_{>j}s_j\rangle .}         \tag{3.6}
\]
Then
\[
 M_j=\E[e^{\mathcal L_A}\mid\mathcal F_j],\qquad
 M_0=1,\qquad M_N=e^{\mathcal L_A}.                  \tag{3.7}
\]
Writing $m_j=\log(M_j/M_{j-1})$ gives
\[
 \E[e^{m_j}\mid\mathcal F_{j-1}]=1.                \tag{3.8}
\]
For every conditional law $\lambda_j$ of the fresh root with finite entropy
relative to its standard Gaussian law $\gamma_j$,
\[
 \E_{\lambda_j}m_j-H(\lambda_j\mid\gamma_j)\le0,    \tag{3.9}
\]
and equality is attained by the exact conditional exponential tilt.

\emph{Proof.}
Integrate the unrevealed Gaussian sum in (3.3).  This gives (3.6).
One more Gaussian integration, or the determinant lemma together with the
Woodbury identity, proves (3.7)--(3.8).  Equation (3.9) is the conditional
Gibbs variational formula; its normalizing logarithm is zero by (3.8).
\hfill$\square$

The saturation in (3.9) is load-bearing.  Frozen determinant entropy cannot
be spent again on the difference between a fixed $A_r$ and the true
same-root state-dependent production coefficient.

% evidence-anchor: proposition-3.2-progressive-future-row-normalization-defect
\subsection*{Proposition 3.2 (a predictable future row breaks the frozen normaliser)}

The jointly frozen hypothesis cannot be replaced by the usual progressive
condition $A_r\in\mathcal F_{r-1}$.  Let $\xi_1,\xi_2$ be independent standard
Gaussians and take the bounded scalar rows
\[
 A_1=1,\qquad A_2=c(\xi_1),\qquad
 c(x)={\bf1}_{\{|x|>1\}}.                                           \tag{3.10}
\]
Inserting the random total and tail covariances into the frozen candidate
(3.6) gives, at the first reveal,
\[
 \widetilde M_1(x)
 =\left({1+q(1+c(x))\over1+qc(x)}\right)^{1/2}
   \exp\left\{-{qx^2\over2(1+qc(x))}\right\}.                      \tag{3.11}
\]
After multiplication by the standard Gaussian density, the inner region has
precision $\beta_0=1+q=19/9$ and the outer region has precision
$\beta_1=(1+2q)/(1+q)=29/19$.  Hence
\[
 \E\widetilde M_1
 =\operatorname{erf}\sqrt{19\over18}+\operatorname{erfc}\sqrt{29\over38}
 =1+\operatorname{erf}\sqrt{19\over18}-\operatorname{erf}\sqrt{29\over38}>1, \tag{3.12}
\]
because $19/18>29/38$ and $\operatorname{erf}$ is strictly increasing.  The independent
executable obtains
\[
 \E\widetilde M_1=1.070433115292664\ldots                            \tag{3.13}
\]
from both the closed error-function formula and direct piecewise quadrature.
The discontinuity is inessential.  For the bounded smooth choice
$A_2=\epsilon\tanh\xi_1$, with $v=(1+q)^{-1}$ and
$\delta=\epsilon^2$, the same candidate satisfies
\[
 {d\over d\delta}\E\widetilde M_1\big|_{\delta=0}
 ={q^2\over2}\Cov_{N(0,v)}(X^2,\tanh^2X)>0.                          \tag{3.14}
\]
Indeed $y\mapsto\tanh^2\sqrt y$ is strictly increasing and
$2\Cov(Y,g(Y))=\E[(Y-Y')(g(Y)-g(Y'))]>0$.  Thus the global
backward-resolvent expression is not a density martingale for general smooth
predictable future rows either.  This is a method no-go, not a negative
complete-action fixture.  A genuinely progressive construction must multiply
the actual one-fresh-root conditional normalisers rather than substitute
random future rows into one frozen global determinant.

On the normalized circle take

 \tau_0=\sigma^2|z|^2,\qquad

 n=0:-{\sigma^4\over4},\qquad

\emph{Proof.}

\hfill$\square$

The fixture embeds, up to a positive constant, in a linear Pauli--Fierz row.

\section{Why independent output normalisers fail}

For a positive semidefinite covariance $T$, let
\[
 D_q(T)={1\over2}\{q\Tr T-\log\det(\Id+qT)\}.        \tag{7.1}
\]
For positive semidefinite blocks $T_a$,
\[
 \log\det(\Id+q\sum_aT_a)
 \le\sum_a\log\det(\Id+qT_a),                       \tag{7.2}
\]
and therefore
\[
 D_q(\sum_aT_a)\ge\sum_aD_q(T_a).                   \tag{7.3}
\]
The lost determinant slack is not harmless.  For $m$ copies of one rank-one
block $\lambda P$,
\[
 S_m={1\over2}\{m\log(1+q\lambda)
                  -\log(1+qm\lambda)\},             \tag{7.4}
\]
\[
 {S_m\over m}\longrightarrow {1\over2}\log(1+q\lambda)>0.\tag{7.5}
\]

This fires the scoped output-normalisation no-go: multiplying independently
normalised row or output likelihoods can lose a linear amount.  The exact
replacement is the sequential Schur identity.  With
$K_m=\sum_{a\le m}T_a$ and $R_{m-1}=(\Id+qK_{m-1})^{-1}$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \log\det(\Id+qK_m)-\log\det(\Id+qK_{m-1})
 =\log\det\{\Id+qR_{m-1}^{1/2}T_mR_{m-1}^{1/2}\}.} \tag{7.6}
\]
The increments depend on the reveal order, but their sum is the invariant
whole-output determinant.

\section{Termwise adapted second-jet estimates fail}

Let $G\sim N(0,1)$ and
\[
 h_M(G)=a\sin(MG),\qquad r_M=e^{-2M^2}.              \tag{10.1}
\]
Direct Gaussian calculation gives
\[
 \E h_M^2={a^2\over2}(1-r_M),                       \tag{10.2}
\]
\[
 \E(h_M')^2={a^2M^2\over2}(1+r_M),\qquad
 \E(h_Mh_M'')=-{a^2M^2\over2}(1-r_M),              \tag{10.3}

 \E\{(h_M')^2+h_Mh_M''\}=a^2M^2r_M,               \tag{10.4}

 \E[h_M^2(G)(G^2-1)]

Thus the source cost (10.2) stays bounded, while the two terms in (10.3)
separately approach $\pm a^2M^2/2$.  Their signed sum (10.4) tends to zero.
Termwise absolute or derivative-square control of the adapted forest is
therefore impossible from the source cost alone.  This is a method no-go;
the complete signed second jet remains live.

\section{Pure carrier information is not a diagonal bridge}

\section{A parked convexified Gaussian-divergence route}

 \operatorname{div}_\gamma F=\operatorname{div}F-x\cdot F. \tag{12.1}

 V+U=-{1\over2}\operatorname{div}_\gamma F

To audit the most tempting flow shortcut, let
$A=-\operatorname{div}_\gamma F$ and follow the inward flow

diffeomorphism hypotheses, the change-of-variables remainder is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_t={}&\int_0^t A(\Psi_sX)\dd s\\
      &-tA(\Psi_tX).
\end{aligned}
\tag{12.4}
\]
Already for $F(x)=x$, $U=0$, and $A=x^2-1$,
\[
 \Psi_sx=e^{-s}x,\qquad
 D_t=c_tx^2,\qquad
 c_t={1-(1+2t)e^{-2t}\over2}>0.                     \tag{12.5}
\]
Indeed
\[
 \E e^{D_t}={1\over\sqrt{1-2c_t}}
 ={e^t\over\sqrt{1+2t}}
 =\E e^{-t(X^2-1)}.                                 \tag{12.6}
\]
The natural contractive flow therefore produces a positive remainder which
cannot be discarded for an upper bound.  The negative
$-(q/6)\Delta U(\Psi_tX)$ term may still help in the production problem, but
no accepted theorem currently dominates (12.4) with it.  We record the route
as inconclusive and parked, not as a general flow no-go.

\section{Minimal remaining adapted cluster theorem}

Join two output frequencies when they share a root input, an R-063
contraction, or an off-diagonal covariance block.  A legal output atom is a
union of connected components of this graph.  Proposition 6.1 proves that
arbitrary singleton outputs are illegal; in the worst case the legal cluster
is the whole root output.

\subsection*{Conditional target 13.1 (complete adapted cluster bound)}

A sufficient new theorem would assign to each root its complete
contraction-closed packet $\mathfrak P_j$, nonnegative allocations
$\eta_j,\zeta_j$, and deterministic loss $c_j$, such that
\[
 \E\left[
 \mathfrak P_j+\eta_j\mathcal E_j
 +\zeta_j\mathcal Y_{\operatorname{parent}(j)}
 \right]\ge-c_j,                                    \tag{13.1}
\]
\[
 \sum_j\eta_j\le{9\over20},\qquad
 \sum_j\zeta_j\le{3\over20},\qquad
 \sum_jc_j<\infty.                                  \tag{13.2}
\]
The sextic in (13.1) is one unfragmented parent/root payment.

Equivalently, in the direct source-action form, one may prove constants
$\eta<9/20$, $\zeta<3/20$, and $C<\infty$, uniform in cutoff, chart, and
representation-preserving subdivision, for which
\[
 \sum_k\E P_k^{\rm comp}
 \ge-\eta\E\sum_k\|h_k\|^2
     -\zeta\E\|Z_K\|_6^6-C.                         \tag{13.3}
\]
Here $P_k^{\rm comp}$ contains the complete heat, covariance trace, mixed
baseline, future feedback, rational current, all visits, and the full R-063
forest exactly once.

A determinant-compatible stronger form would prove for every
contraction-closed cluster $\mathcal C$
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\log\E\left[
 e^{-q(\mathfrak P_{j,\mathcal C}
       +\eta_j\mathcal E_j
       +\zeta_j\mathcal Y_{\operatorname{parent}(j)})}
 \mid\mathcal F_{\rm past}\right]\\
 &\qquad\le {q^2\over4}\|S_{j,\mathcal C}\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2
      +r_{j,\mathcal C}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13.4}
\]
with a summable matrix-Carleson ledger
\[
 \sum_{j,\mathcal C}
 \{\|S_{j,\mathcal C}\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2+r_{j,\mathcal C}\}
 <\infty.                                            \tag{13.5}
\]

Equations (13.1), (13.3), and (13.4) are targets, not theorems of this note.
The operations must occur in this order: sum all visits, assemble every input
convolution coherently at its output, restore the complete forest, and retain
the terminal sextic once.  Only then may one estimate.

\section{Devil's-advocate audit}

\begin{enumerate}

jointly frozen multi-row formulas and one-fresh-root past-measurable

orientations of the same endpoint difference.  The frozen conditional
entropy inequality (3.9) is saturable.

realization of the trace/coefficient-curvature part already present in

\item \textbf{Objection: independent output normalisers are conservative and

matrix-Carleson sum.  Available absolute dyadic majorants do not establish it.

\textbf{UPHELD AS UNSUPPORTED.}  The exact linear fixture (12.5) gives the
natural inward-flow remainder the opposite sign from the desired discard.
