\section{Purpose and scope}

This note continues R-069 from its displayed finite-cutoff target (6.7).
The setting is the hash-pinned six-real production model on the torus of side
$L=16$, at fixed positive density floor, with common entrywise real-even
regulation and mutually orthogonal one-shot strict-past controls.  The purpose
is to decide whether the remaining Gaussian-rooted current can be closed by a
conditional Gaussian, paraproduct, or retained-resolvent argument.

The main positive result is an exact Wick--Doob product identity.  It combines
the $GG$, $CC$, and mixed-current channels into the terminal covariance-normal
current form while retaining the complete finite-low boundary.  The
transported covariance trace is already an R-066 summable channel, and the
R-069 endpoint-curvature defect is already an R-068 one-use centered form.
Thus the covariance and endpoint-curvature side-channel bookkeeping is
closed; the full linear-frame model attribution is not.

The result does \emph{not} prove the remaining lower bound.  Terminalization
lands exactly on the adapted translated-current stochastic coercivity problem
which A13 was designed to prove.  A second exact identity retains the terminal
Schur resolvent and pays its Hilbert--Schmidt trace, but leaves one adapted
centered-resolvent chaos.  A scalar diagnostic proves that covariance normal
ordering does not center this term when its coefficient is adapted.  The
smallest non-circular successor consequently has two stages: first the
balanced non-$pp$ linear-frame model attribution, then a coupled nonlinear
rational-frame/cross-square estimate.  A7 existence or the desired Nelson
moment cannot be cited as either proof.

All identities below are finite-cutoff statements.  They use only pointwise
conditional independence of a fresh derivative shell from the same-point
value variables.  They do not identify the target-space heat dummy with a
future spatial field and never spatially differentiate it.

\section{Strict-past fields and normal current}

Let
\[
 \mathcal F_j=\sigma(\omega_k:k\le j),\qquad
 (a_j,b_{j,i})\ \hbox{be }\mathcal F_{j-1}\hbox{-measurable}.       \tag{2.1}
\]
For one terminal shell $J$, use the coherent value coupling
\[
 U_J(x)=\sum_{k\le J}\xi_k(x),                                  \tag{2.2}
\]
and define
\[
 G_{j,i}=\sum_{k<j}d_{k,i},\qquad
 \Gamma_{j-1,i}=\operatorname{Cov}G_{j,i},                       \tag{2.3}
\]
\[
 A_j=A_{<j},\qquad C_{j,i}=D_iA_j,                               \tag{2.4}
\]
\[
 A_{j+1}=A_j+a_j,\qquad C_{j+1,i}=C_{j,i}+b_{j,i}.                \tag{2.5}
\]
The future heat variable in (2.2) is frozen under $D_i$; therefore $G_{j,i}$
contains only exposed spatial derivative shells.

Write the production coefficient in its current frame as
\[
 B(z)=\sum_{A=1}^3M_A(z)Q_{\rm II}M_A(z)^T,\qquad B_j=B(U_J+A_j), \tag{2.6}
\]
and put
\[
 Q_{j,i}=G_{j,i}^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j-1,i}.                      \tag{2.7}
\]
The covariance-normal current energy at the pre-control endpoint is
\[
 \mathcal W_j={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 B_j:\left[(G_{j,i}+C_{j,i})^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j-1,i}\right]
 \dd x.                                                         \tag{2.8}
\]
At the post-control, pre-noise endpoint define
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal W}_j={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 B_{j+1}:\left[(G_{j,i}+C_{j+1,i})^{\otimes2}
                    -\Gamma_{j-1,i}\right]\dd x.               \tag{2.9}
\]

\section{Exact Wick--Doob transport}

Fix $x$ and enlarge the strict-past sigma field only by the same-point future
values:
\[
 \mathcal G_{j,x}=\mathcal F_{j-1}\vee
             \sigma(\xi_k(x):k\ge j).                           \tag{3.1}
\]
Common real-even covariance and shell independence give
\[
 \E[d_{j,i}(x)\mid\mathcal G_{j,x}]=0,\qquad
 \E[d_{j,i}(x)^{\otimes2}\mid\mathcal G_{j,x}]
       =\Delta\Gamma_{j,i}.                                     \tag{3.2}
\]
The fields $B_{j+1}(x)$, $G_{j,i}(x)$, and $C_{j+1,i}(x)$ are
$\mathcal G_{j,x}$-measurable.  Since
\[
 G_{j+1,i}=G_{j,i}+d_{j,i},\qquad
 \Gamma_{j,i}=\Gamma_{j-1,i}+\Delta\Gamma_{j,i},                \tag{3.3}
\]
equation (3.2), followed by spatial Fubini, proves
\[
 \boxed{\ \E\mathcal W_{j+1}=\E\widehat{\mathcal W}_j.\ }     \tag{3.4}
\]
This is a pointwise conditional statement.  It does not assert independence
of the complete value and derivative fields.

Polarizing (2.8)--(2.9) gives exactly
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal W}_j-\mathcal W_j
 =\mathcal R_j^C+\mathcal R_j^\times
  +\mathcal R_j^{G,{\rm Wick}},                                 \tag{3.5}
\]
where $\mathcal R_j^C$ and $\mathcal R_j^\times$ are R-069
(5.7) and (5.9), and
\[
 \mathcal R_j^{G,{\rm Wick}}
 ={1\over2}\sum_i\int(B_{j+1}-B_j):Q_{j,i}\dd x.               \tag{3.6}
\]
Summing (3.4)--(3.6) proves the first main identity:
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\sum_{j=j_0}^J
 (\mathcal R_j^C+\mathcal R_j^\times
                 +\mathcal R_j^{G,{\rm Wick}})
 =\E\mathcal W_{J+1}-\E\mathcal W_{j_0}.}                       \tag{3.7}
\]
In particular, the $GG$ coefficient alone obeys the Wick--Doob product rule
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\sum_{j=j_0}^J{1\over2}\sum_i\int
 (B_{j+1}-B_j):Q_{j,i}\dd x
 ={1\over2}\E\sum_i\int
 (B_{J+1}:Q_{J+1,i}-B_{j_0}:Q_{j_0,i})\dd x.}                    \tag{3.8}
\]
Unlike the R-069 scalar-envelope transfer, (3.8) transports the full
coefficient increment.  Its price is that the result is the intact terminal
rough form, not a smooth deterministic multiplier.

At the terminal endpoint $U_J=X_J$, $G_{J+1}=DX_J$,
$A_{J+1}=A$, and $C_{J+1}=DA$.  Therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal W_{J+1}={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 B(Z_J):\left[(D_iZ_J)^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{J,i}\right]\dd x,}    \tag{3.9}
\]
which is exactly the finite-cutoff A7 covariance-normal current form.  The
initial term in (3.7) is the \emph{full} finite-low boundary and is retained
until the R-066 low-block estimate is applied.

\section{Raw terminal-minus-injection form and trace restoration}

Let
\[
 \mathcal E_j^{\rm raw}={1\over2}\sum_i\int
 (G_{j,i}+C_{j,i})^TB_j(G_{j,i}+C_{j,i})\dd x.                   \tag{4.1}
\]
Fresh-noise centering also gives the equivalent raw identity
\[
 \boxed{
 \E\sum_{j=j_0}^J
 (\mathcal R_j^C+\mathcal R_j^\times+\mathcal R_j^{G,{\rm raw}})
 =\E\mathcal E_{J+1}^{\rm raw}-\E\mathcal E_{j_0}^{\rm raw}
 -{1\over2}\E\sum_{j,i}\int
 \operatorname{Tr}(B_{j+1}\Delta\Gamma_{j,i})\dd x.}           \tag{4.2}
\]
Thus terminal raw energy and injection may not be estimated separately: at
zero control they cancel exactly shell by shell after expectation.

The discrete integration-by-parts identity
\[
 \begin{split}
 B_{J+1}\Gamma_J-B_{j_0}\Gamma_{j_0-1}
 =\sum_{j=j_0}^J\{&(B_{j+1}-B_j)\Gamma_{j-1}\\
                  &+B_{j+1}\Delta\Gamma_j\}
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{4.3}
\]
converts (4.2) into (3.7).  Consequently the raw R-069 target (6.7) must not
be silently called the Wick terminal form: the previously separated trace
channel
\[
 {1\over2}\sum_{j,i}\int
 \operatorname{Tr}[(B_{j+1}-B_j)\Gamma_{j-1,i}]\dd x            \tag{4.4}
\]
must first be restored or charged to its existing budget.

R-066 already proves that (4.4) is a one-use summable channel.  For clarity,
write $N_j=2^j$, $a_j=K_jh_j$, and use
\[
 \|\Gamma_{j-1}\|_{\rm op}\le C_\Gamma N_j,\qquad
 \|K_jh_j\|_2\le C_KN_j^{-2}\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}.           \tag{4.5}
\]
The frame increment gives a mixed term
$CN_j^{-1}\|U_J+A_j\|_2\|h_j\|$ and a quadratic term
$CN_j^{-3}\|h_j\|^2$.  Orthogonality and dyadic Cauchy--Schwarz then yield,
for a sufficiently large fixed $j_0$ and every $\eta>0$,
\[
 \E |(4.4)|\le
 \eta\E\sum_j\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}^2+C_{\eta,j_0}.           \tag{4.6}
\]

There is an equivalent transported-covariance tail.  If $A=A_{J+1}$, put
\[
 \mathcal P_J={1\over2}\sum_{j,i}\int
 \operatorname{Tr}\{[B(U_J+A)-B(U_J+A_{j+1})]
                      \Delta\Gamma_{j,i}\}\dd x.               \tag{4.7}
\]
Discrete Abel summation gives exactly
\[
 \mathcal P_J={1\over2}\sum_{k,i}\int
 \operatorname{Tr}[(B_{k+1}-B_k)\Gamma_{k-1,i}^{\rm hi}]\dd x,
 \quad
 \Gamma_{k-1}^{\rm hi}=\sum_{j_0\le j<k}\Delta\Gamma_j.        \tag{4.8}
\]
The same bounds prove
\[
 |\mathcal P_J|\le C\sum_k\left[
 N_k^{-1}\|U_J+A_k\|_2\|h_k\|_{\mathcal H_k}
 +N_k^{-3}\|h_k\|_{\mathcal H_k}^2\right].                    \tag{4.9}
\]
If $X=\sum_k\|h_k\|^2$ and
$W=\sum_kN_k^{-2}\|U_J+A_k\|_2^2$, the dyadic Hardy estimate is
\[
 W\le {8\over3}N_{j_0}^{-2}\|U_J\|_2^2
       +{2C_K^2\over3}N_{j_0}^{-6}X.                            \tag{4.10}
\]
Hence (4.9) has the same arbitrary control allocation as (4.6), without a
sextic allocation.  No paraproduct theorem is needed for the covariance tail.

Use the R-069 endpoint lift on the Gaussian derivative only and define

 \varepsilon_j=\sum_{A,i}\int
 \langle g_{j,A,i},Q_{\rm II}E^G_{j,A,i}\rangle\dd x.           \tag{5.1}

Then, exactly,
\[
 \mathcal R_j^{G,{\rm raw}}
 =\mathcal T_j^G+\mathcal J_j^G+\varepsilon_j.                  \tag{5.2}
\]
The absolute form of the global R-069 estimate is
\[
 |\varepsilon_j|\le C_*\int|a_j|^2|G_j|^2\dd x,\qquad
 C_*=24\lambda_Q(\sqrt{34}+4\sqrt2).                            \tag{5.3}
\]
R-068/R-069 transfer the centered scalar part of (5.3) once to the terminal
$H^{-1-\kappa}$ model and pay its covariance trace at order $N_j^{-3}$.

Combining (3.7), (4.4), and (5.2) shows that the correctly normal-ordered
R-069 target is
\[
 \boxed{
 \E(\mathcal W_{J+1}-\mathcal W_{j_0})
 -\E\sum_{j=j_0}^J\varepsilon_j.}                               \tag{5.4}
\]
Equivalently, the literal raw target (6.7) is (5.4) plus the trace channel
(4.4).  Since (4.4) and (5.3) already have arbitrary one-use allocations,

 \E\mathcal W_{J+1}
 \ge-\eta\E\sum_j\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}^2
      -\zeta\E\|Z_J\|_6^6-C_{\eta,\zeta,j_0}.                  \tag{5.5}
\]
Equation (5.5) is the terminal translated-current stochastic coercivity
problem itself.  Thus Wick--Doob transport closes the shell bookkeeping but
does not provide a new lower-bound mechanism.

\section{Exact retained terminal resolvent}

There is a second useful exact reduction.  Whiten the terminal Gaussian as
$\xi$, write its Cameron--Martin displacement as $\widehat h$, and, at the
actual endpoint $z=K_J(\xi+\widehat h)$, define
\[
 L_z=M_{B(z)}^{1/2}DK_J,\qquad T_z=L_z^*L_z\succeq0.            \tag{6.1}
\]
For $p>0$ put
\[
 R_z=(I+pT_z)^{-1},\qquad S_z=T_zR_z.                            \tag{6.2}
\]
Although $T_z$ is random, the following is a pointwise Hilbert-space
completion and uses no independence:
\[
 \begin{split}
 V_J^{\rm ren}(z)+{1\over2p}\|\widehat h\|^2
 ={}&{1\over2}\{\langle\xi,S_z\xi\rangle-\operatorname{Tr}S_z\}
 -{p\over2}\operatorname{Tr}(T_z^2R_z)\\
 &+{1\over2}\|(T_z+p^{-1}I)^{1/2}
       (\widehat h+pR_zT_z\xi)\|^2.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{6.3}
\]
To verify the trace term, use
$S_z=T_z-pT_z^2R_z$.  To verify the square, expand it and use
$(T_z+p^{-1}I)R_z=p^{-1}I$.

The deterministic trace loss closes:
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(T_z^2R_z)\le\|T_z\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2
 \le C\|B(z)\|_2^2\le C\|z\|_4^4.                             \tag{6.4}
\]
Consequently, for every $\zeta>0$,
\[
 {p\over2}\operatorname{Tr}(T_z^2R_z)
 \le\zeta\|z\|_6^6+C_{p,\zeta}.                               \tag{6.5}
\]
The sole unresolved term in (6.3) is therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 {1\over2}\E\{\langle\xi,S_{Z_J}\xi\rangle
                         -\operatorname{Tr}S_{Z_J}\}.}          \tag{6.6}
\]
If $S$ were deterministic or independent of $\xi$, (6.6) would vanish and
the A9 frozen-shell theorem would close the form.  At the adapted terminal
endpoint it is neither.

\section{Adapted centering and Stein no-go diagnostics}

The failure of automatic centering is already visible in one Gaussian
coordinate.  Let $\xi\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ and choose the bounded adapted
coefficient
\[
 z(\xi)=A\mathbf1_{\{|\xi|\le a\}}.                             \tag{7.1}
\]
Smooth approximations give the same limiting conclusion.  Since
\[
 \int_{-a}^a(x^2-1)\varphi(x)\dd x=-2a\varphi(a),               \tag{7.2}
\]
one has
\[
 \E\,{1\over2}z(\xi)^2(\xi^2-1)=-a\varphi(a)A^2<0.             \tag{7.3}
\]
For the scalar resolvent $S=z^2/(1+pz^2)$ the same calculation gives
\[
 \E\,{1\over2}S(\xi)(\xi^2-1)
 =-{a\varphi(a)A^2\over1+pA^2}<0.                              \tag{7.4}
\]
This is a route diagnostic, not a production counterexample: realizing
(7.1) as a spatial Cameron--Martin control has a cost which the diagnostic
does not model.  It proves only that covariance normal ordering and a terminal
resolvent do not themselves center an adapted coefficient.

Direct Gaussian integration by parts also leaves an unauthorized norm.  If
$G=L\omega$, $U=K\omega$, and the same-point evenness gives $LK^*=0$, then for
an adapted shift $A(\omega)$,
\[
 \E[:G^{\otimes2}:F(U+A)]
 =\E\operatorname{Tr}\{L^*D_\omega^2[F(U+A)]L\}.                \tag{7.5}
\]
The right side contains
\[
 D^2F[DA,DA]+DF[D^2A],                                         \tag{7.6}
\]
and the mixed current similarly contains $DA$ and $DC$.  Strict-past
adaptation kills only the same-shell diagonal derivative; a later control may
still differentiate an earlier innovation.  For example
\[
 h_2^{(n)}(\omega_1)=\sin(n\omega_1)                            \tag{7.7}
\]
has uniformly bounded $L^2$ control energy but Malliavin derivative norm of
order $n$.  The declared Boue--Dupuis cost controls no such derivative.
Therefore a pure Stein proof would impose an additional hypothesis and would
not extend to the stated finite-energy drift class.

Finally, a global entropy shortcut is circular.  Applying the Gibbs
variational formula directly to (5.5) bounds it by
$-\log\E\exp(-pV_J^{\rm ren}(X_J))$, which is precisely the open Nelson
moment.  A7 supplies existence and convergence of the composite, not this
negative exponential bound.

\section{The exact non-circular successor}

Write $U=X_J$, $A=Z_J-X_J$, $G_i=D_iU$, $C_i=D_iA$, and
\[
 M_{r,0}=M_r(U),\quad M_{r,1}=M_r(U+A),\quad
 \Delta M_r=M_{r,1}-M_{r,0},\qquad r=1,2,3.                    \tag{8.1}
\]
The terminal translated form is exactly
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Delta V_J^{\rm ren}(U;A)={}&{1\over2}
   \langle B(U+A)-B(U),Q_J\rangle\\
 &+\sum_{r,i}\langle M_{r,1}^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}M_{r,1}^TC_i\rangle
 +{1\over2}\sum_{r,i}|M_{r,1}^TC_i|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2,
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{8.2}
\]
where $Q_J=\sum_i(G_i^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{J,i})$.

Subtract the uncontrolled conservative first variation
\[
 \mathcal S_0(U;A)={1\over2}\langle DB(U)[A],Q_J\rangle
 +\sum_{r,i}\langle M_{r,0}^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}M_{r,0}^TC_i\rangle. \tag{8.3}
\]
Then ordinary algebra gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \Delta V_J^{\rm ren}-\mathcal S_0={}&{1\over2}
 \langle B(U+A)-B(U)-DB(U)[A],Q_J\rangle\\
 &+\sum_{r,i}\{\langle M_{r,0}^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}(\Delta M_r)^TC_i\rangle\\
 &\quad+\langle(\Delta M_r)^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}M_{r,1}^TC_i\rangle
       +{1\over2}|M_{r,1}^TC_i|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2\}.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{8.4}
\]
The exact surviving transported-current paraproduct is
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal C_J=\sum_{r,i}\langle M_{r,0}^TG_i,
 Q_{\rm II}(\Delta M_r)^TC_i\rangle.}                         \tag{8.5}
\]
The fundamental theorem of calculus must retain its linear part:
\[
 \Delta M_r=DM_r(U)[A]+E_r^M,\qquad
 E_r^M=\int_0^1[DM_r(U+tA)-DM_r(U)][A]\dd t,
\]
and hence $\mathcal C_J=\mathcal L_J+\mathcal N_J$, where
\[
 \mathcal L_J=\sum_{r,i}\langle M_{r,0}^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}
 DM_r(U)[A]^TC_i\rangle,\quad
 \mathcal N_J=\sum_{r,i}\langle M_{r,0}^TG_i,Q_{\rm II}
 (E_r^M)^TC_i\rangle.                                         \tag{8.6}
\]
Write $Q_{\rm II}=(q_{mn})_{m,n=1}^2$ and $M_r=[p_r,v_r]$.  Put
\[
 P_{r,i}=p_r(U)\cdot G_i,\qquad V_{r,i}=v_r(U)\cdot G_i,
\]
and, with $\rho=|U|^2$, $m_r=U^TS_rU$,
\[
 K_{r,p}=2S_r,\qquad
 K_{r,v}=2(S_r-q_rI)-{v_rU^T+Uv_r^T\over\rho+e},\qquad
 d\omega_r={d\rho\wedge dm_r\over\rho+e}.
\]
Direct differentiation gives the exact symmetric--Cartan split
\[
 Dp_r(U)[A]\cdot C_i=A^TK_{r,p}C_i,\quad
 Dv_r(U)[A]\cdot C_i=A^TK_{r,v}C_i
       +{1\over2}d\omega_r(U)[A,C_i].
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal L_J=\mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}+\mathcal L_J^{\rm Car},
\]
where
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}=\sum_{r,i}\int\{&
 q_{11}P_{r,i}A^TK_{r,p}C_i\\
 &+q_{12}[P_{r,i}A^TK_{r,v}C_i
             +V_{r,i}A^TK_{r,p}C_i]\\
 &+q_{22}V_{r,i}A^TK_{r,v}C_i\}\dd x,
 \end{split}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal L_J^{\rm Car}={1\over2}\sum_{r,i}\int
 (q_{12}P_{r,i}+q_{22}V_{r,i})
 d\omega_r(U)[A,C_i]\dd x.
\]
Since $K_{r,p}$ and $K_{r,v}$ are symmetric, torus integration by parts gives
\[
 \mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}
 =\sum_r\langle\mathbb Q_r^{\rm lin}(U),A^{\otimes2}\rangle,
\]
\[
 \mathbb Q_r^{\rm lin}=-{1\over2}\sum_i\partial_i[
 q_{11}P_{r,i}K_{r,p}
 +q_{12}(P_{r,i}K_{r,v}+V_{r,i}K_{r,p})
 +q_{22}V_{r,i}K_{r,v}].
\]
The pure $p$--$p$ subchannel is therefore
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal L_J^{pp}
 &=q_{11}\sum_{r,i}\int
 \partial_i(U^TS_rU)\,\partial_i(A^TS_rA)\dd x\\
 &=-q_{11}\sum_r\langle\Delta(U^TS_rU),A^TS_rA\rangle.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{8.7}
\]
After covariance centering, R-068 pays (8.7) once.  The production weight
$q_{11}$ and the sum over all generators are essential.  The same payment for
all of $\mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}$ additionally requires the balanced
rational-current model lemma
$\mathbb Q_r^{\rm lin}\in L^p(\Omega;H^{-1-\kappa})$; R-070 has not yet
proved or pinned that lemma for the non-$pp$ terms.

The exact $\mathcal L_J^{\rm Car}$ is a Gaussian-current pairing with
$A\wedge DA$.  If
$X=\|A\|_{H^2}^2$, $Y=\|A\|_6^6$, and
$\theta=(1+2\kappa)/3$, interpolation gives
\[
 \|A\wedge DA\|_{H^\kappa}
 \le C X^{(1+\theta)/2}Y^{(1-\theta)/6}.
\]
At $\kappa=1/10$, the powers are $X^{7/10}Y^{1/10}$, and weighted Young gives
\[
 |\langle\mathbb J,A\wedge DA\rangle|
 \le\eta X+\zeta Y+C\eta^{-7/2}\zeta^{-1/2}
 \|\mathbb J\|_{H^{-1/10}}^5.                                \tag{8.8}
\]
The balanced Gaussian-current model has the required fifth moment, so (8.8)
pays $\mathcal L_J^{\rm Car}$.  This does not prove the missing
$H^{-1-\kappa}$ model attribution for the non-$pp$ part of
$\mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}$.

With $d_{r,i}=(\Delta M_r)^TG_i$ and $c_{r,i}=M_{r,1}^TC_i$, define
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal R_{\rm shift}={}&\mathcal N_J
 +\sum_{r,i}\{\langle d_{r,i},Q_{\rm II}c_{r,i}\rangle
                  +{1\over2}|c_{r,i}|_{Q_{\rm II}}^2\}\\
 &+{1\over2}\langle B(U+A)-B(U)-DB(U)[A],Q_J\rangle.
 \end{split}                                                    \tag{8.9}
\]
The exact identity is
\[
 \Delta V_J^{\rm ren}-\mathcal S_0
 =\mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}+\mathcal L_J^{\rm Car}
  +\mathcal R_{\rm shift}.
\]
The terms in (8.9) must remain coupled.  Separating them recreates the old
saturated rational-frame paraproduct, while completing the cross/square
globally creates $|A|^2|DU|^2$ with terminal covariance and destroys the
shellwise $N_j^{-3}$ trace gain.  The smallest honest successor is therefore:
\begin{quote}
\textbf{Successor (8.10).}  First prove the balanced model attribution for the
non-$pp$ part of $\mathbb Q_r^{\rm lin}$ and pay
$\mathcal L_J^{\rm sym}$ once through R-068.  Then prove a full
$Q_{\rm II}$-weighted predictable rational-frame/cross-square inequality for
(8.9), retaining $\mathcal N_J$, the coefficient remainder, and terminal
square jointly and using no Malliavin derivative of the control.
\end{quote}
Only after (8.10) is closed can localization and lower semicontinuity extend
the result to all declared finite-energy drifts, followed by controlled-shell
one-use and the $q=10/9$ Nelson theorem.

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{``The full coefficient Wick product may be transported to the
