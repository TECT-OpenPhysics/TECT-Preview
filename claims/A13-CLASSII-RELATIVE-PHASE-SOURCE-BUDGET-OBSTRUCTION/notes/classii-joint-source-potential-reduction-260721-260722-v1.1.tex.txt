\section*{2. Exact conditional joint formula}

At shell $j$, set $x=\phi_{j-1}$, $z=x+K_j\xi_j$, and let $\xi_j$ be the
standard real Gaussian innovation.  With
\[
 A_j=M_{B(x)}^{1/2}G_j,\quad T_j=A_j^*A_j,\quad
 r_j=M_{B(x)}^{1/2}Dx,\quad \ell_j=A_j^*r_j,            \tag{2.1}
\]
the A11 true increment is
\[
 I_j={1\over2}\{\langle\xi_j,T_j\xi_j\rangle-
                  \operatorname{Tr}T_j\}
       +\langle\ell_j,\xi_j\rangle.                    \tag{2.2}
\]
The coefficient increment is
\[
 \cC_j=q_{B(z)-B(x)}(Dz)
       -t_{\Gamma_{\le j}}(B(z)-B(x)),                  \tag{2.3}
\]
and $I_j+\cC_j=V_j-V_{j-1}$ exactly.

For $q=p\theta$, put
\[
 R_q=(\Id+qT_j)^{-1},\qquad \mu_q=-qR_q\ell_j.          \tag{2.4}
\]
Let $\kappa=0$ mean that the full local potential is retained and
$\kappa=1$ mean that only its increment is retained.  Completing the
Gaussian square, without estimating $\cC_j$ or $U$, gives
\[
\begin{split}
&\log\E_j\exp\{-p[\theta I_j+\cC_j+U(z)-\kappa U(x)]\}\\
={}&p\kappa U(x)-{1\over2}\log\det{}_2(\Id+qT_j)
 +{q^2\over2}\langle\ell_j,R_q\ell_j\rangle\\
&+\log\E_{\eta\sim N(\mu_q,R_q)}
 \exp\{-p[\cC_j(x,\eta)+U(x+K_j\eta)]\}.
\end{split}                                             \tag{2.5}
\]
Equation (2.5) fixes all signs.  Both the regularised-determinant term and
the noncentral source term are nonnegative.  The sextic supplies the negative
large-field exponent.  The sign-indefinite $\cC_j$ cannot be dropped.

The primary executable checks (2.5) pointwise at random matrix inputs.  The
non-importing route checks the integrated identity by independent

\section*{3. Why the complete local source has a factor four}

For the symmetric opposite-corner carrier write, after removal of the fast

\[

\[

\[

Let $y=(\eta,\bar\eta,0)$ be a same-corner shell amplitude variation.
Differentiating only the derivative slots of
$q_B=\frac12\sum_i(D_ix)^TB(x)D_ix$ produces the A11 frozen source.
Differentiating the explicit expression (3.4) produces twice that result.

\[
 {1\over2}\sum_i(D_ix)^TDB(x)[y]D_ix
 =\sum_i(D_ix)^TB(x)D_iy,                              \tag{3.5}
\]

\[
 \boxed{\ell_{\rm joint}=2\ell_{\rm frozen},\qquad

the factor four is exact for the principal symbol and asymptotic for the

\section*{6. A mixed Hardy/Riesz lemma}

The relative-phase carrier nevertheless admits a useful analytic enclosure.
Use normalised Haar measure on $\T$.  Let $g=P_{<0}f$ and let
$Q=P_{\le0}$ be used only on products.  Since $f-g$ has nonnegative modes
and $\bar g$ has strictly positive modes,
\[
 Q(f\bar g)=Q(|g|^2).                                   \tag{6.1}
\]
For the real function $u=|g|^2$, Fourier symmetry gives
\[
 \|Qu\|_2^2={1\over2}\{\|u\|_2^2+|\widehat u(0)|^2\}
 \ge {1\over2}\|u\|_2^2.                              \tag{6.2}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \|g\|_4^4
 \le2\|Q(f\bar g)\|_2^2
 \le2\|f\|_6^2\|g\|_3^2.                             \tag{6.3}
\]
The sharp Riesz norm $\|P_{<0}\|_{3\to3}=\csc(\pi/3)=2/\sqrt3$ and
$\|f\|_3\le\|f\|_2^{1/2}\|f\|_6^{1/2}$ imply
\[
 \boxed{
 {\|P_{<0}f\|_4^8\over\|f\|_2^2\|f\|_6^6}
 \le {64\over9}.}                                     \tag{6.4}
\]

For the three-dimensional tensor frozen carrier this yields the coherent-ray
