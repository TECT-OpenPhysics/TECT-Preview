\section*{Purpose and scope}

This note proves the finite-dimensional strict-past identity that prevents a
predictable shell source from charging the Gaussian past energy at every
scale.  It closes the scoped child
\begin{verbatim}
A13-CLASSII-STRICT-PAST-RESOLVENT-SIGNED-CHARGE-REDUCTION.
\end{verbatim}
The result converts the controlled-shell lower bound into one signed charge:
the positive predictor square is kept together with the divergence of the
innovation.  There is one control-energy payment, not one payment per shell.

The theorem is exact for a finite ordered Gaussian filtration.  It does not
bound the resulting production charge uniformly in the cutoff.  In
particular, it does not by itself control the centered derivative-square
tensor $Q$, the rational current, the translated-frame square, or the Cartan
curvature term.  The current selected gate
\begin{verbatim}
A13-CLASSII-STRICT-PAST-SIGNED-Q-CURRENT-FORM-BOUND
\end{verbatim}
and its parent one-use/Nelson gate remain open.

\section*{1. Ordered Gaussian convention}

Let $\xi=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_N)$ be standard Gaussian and let
$\mathcal F_j=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_j)$.  A map $a:\mathbb R^N\to
\mathbb R^N$ is strictly past-adapted when $a_j$ is measurable with respect
to $\mathcal F_{j-1}$.  For smooth $a$ define the Gaussian divergence
\[
 \delta a(z)=\sum_{j=1}^N\{z_j a_j(z)-\partial_j a_j(z)\}.       \tag{1.1}
\]
Let $h$ be strictly past-adapted and put $Z=\xi+h(\xi)$.  Let
$b=b^{<}+r$, where $b^{<}$ is strictly past-adapted in its argument and $r$
is the unstructured remainder.  The ordering may encode all coordinates in
one sharp shell before moving to the next shell; strict past then means that
the present shell never enters its own predictor.

Strict triangularity implies
\[
 \partial_j b^{<}_j=0,
 \qquad \operatorname{Tr}\{Db^{<}(Z)Dh(\xi)\}=0.               \tag{1.2}
\]
The second identity records the absence of same-shell feedback.  It is not a
smallness estimate.

\section*{2. Exact causalization lemma}

\textbf{Lemma 2.1 (strict-past Gaussian causalization).}
Assume that all expressions below are integrable.  Then
\[
 \mathbb E\,\delta b^{<}(Z)
 =\mathbb E\,\langle h(\xi),b^{<}(Z)\rangle.                  \tag{2.1}
\]

\begin{proof}
For each $j$, both $h_j(\xi)$ and $b^{<}_j(Z)$ are
$\mathcal F_{j-1}$-measurable.  Indeed, the triangular recursion shows that
$Z_1,\ldots,Z_{j-1}$ depend only on
$\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_{j-1}$.  Hence $\xi_j$ is independent of
$b^{<}_j(Z)$ and is centered.  By (1.2),
\begin{align}
 \mathbb E\,\delta b^{<}(Z)
 &=\sum_j\mathbb E[(\xi_j+h_j(\xi))b^{<}_j(Z)]\\
 &=\sum_j\mathbb E[h_j(\xi)b^{<}_j(Z)],
\end{align}
which is (2.1).
\end{proof}

The cancellation in (2.1) is conditional shell by shell, but the equality is
an expectation identity.  The integrand $\delta b^{<}(Z)$ cannot in general
be replaced pointwise by $h\cdot b^{<}(Z)$.

\section*{3. One-use signed-charge reduction}

Suppose the finite controlled interaction has the divergence form
\[
 2V(Z)=\delta b(Z)=\delta b^{<}(Z)+\delta r(Z).                \tag{3.1}
\]
For $\varepsilon>0$ define the coupled signed charge
\[
 \mathcal S_\varepsilon(Z)
 =\frac{|b^{<}(Z)|^2}{16\varepsilon}-\frac12\delta r(Z).       \tag{3.2}
\]

\textbf{Theorem 3.1 (one-use reduction).}
Under the hypotheses of Lemma 2.1,
\[
 \mathbb E V(Z)
 =-\varepsilon\mathbb E|h|^2
  -\mathbb E\mathcal S_\varepsilon(Z)
  +\varepsilon\mathbb E\left|h+\frac{b^{<}(Z)}{4\varepsilon}\right|^2,
                                                                    \tag{3.3}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \mathbb E V(Z)\ge
 -\varepsilon\mathbb E|h|^2-\mathbb E\mathcal S_\varepsilon(Z).    \tag{3.4}
\]

\begin{proof}
Equations (3.1) and (2.1) give
\[
 \mathbb E V(Z)=\frac12\mathbb E\langle h,b^{<}(Z)\rangle
                  +\frac12\mathbb E\delta r(Z).              \tag{3.5}
\]
Complete exactly one square:
\[
 \frac12\langle h,b^{<}\rangle
 =-\varepsilon|h|^2-\frac{|b^{<}|^2}{16\varepsilon}
   +\varepsilon\left|h+\frac{b^{<}}{4\varepsilon}\right|^2.  \tag{3.6}
\]
Substitution yields (3.3), and deletion of only the final nonnegative
expectation yields (3.4).
\end{proof}

The source-square and the innovation divergence in (3.2) remain signed and
coupled.  Applying absolute values or Young inequalities to their individual
shell constituents would undo the theorem: it would reintroduce repeated
past-energy costs and discard the cancellation still needed from the
$Q$/current/Cartan structure.

\section*{4. Positive affine block and resolvent completion}

The production filtration contains more structure than Theorem 3.1 uses.
Split the $j$th coordinate into a shell block and let
$T_j=T_j(Z_{<j})$ be symmetric positive semidefinite and
$\ell_j=\ell_j(Z_{<j})$ be strict-past measurable.  Define
\[
 a_j(Z)=T_jZ_j+2\ell_j.                                  \tag{4.1}
\]
Because $T_j$ and $\ell_j$ do not depend on $Z_j$,
\[
 {1\over2}\delta_ja_j(Z)
 ={1\over2}\{Z_j^TT_jZ_j-\operatorname{Tr}T_j\}
   +\langle\ell_j,Z_j\rangle.                          \tag{4.2}
\]
Conditioning on the strict past gives
\[
 \mathbb E_j{1\over2}\delta_ja_j(\xi+h)
 ={1\over2}h_j^TT_jh_j+\langle\ell_j,h_j\rangle.      \tag{4.3}
\]

Put $q=(2\varepsilon)^{-1}$ and $R_{q,j}=(I+qT_j)^{-1}$.
The exact metric completion is
\begin{align}
 {1\over2}h_j^TT_jh_j+\langle\ell_j,h_j\rangle
 ={}&-\varepsilon|h_j|^2
 -{q\over2}\langle\ell_j,R_{q,j}\ell_j\rangle\\
 &+{1\over2}\left|
 (T_j+q^{-1}I)^{1/2}(h_j+qR_{q,j}\ell_j)
 \right|^2.                                           \tag{4.4}
\end{align}
Thus the positive present-shell quadratic form is not discarded into the
remainder.  It rebates the raw source square by the resolvent $R_{q,j}$.
For the production control allocation $\varepsilon=0.45$,
\[
 q={1\over0.9}={10\over9},                            \tag{4.5}
\]
which is exactly the Nelson exponent isolated by the earlier
Boue--Dupuis equivalence, not a new fitted constant.

\section*{5. Exact A11 production signed charge}

Use the A11 notation
\[
 T_j=A_j^*A_j,\qquad \ell_j=A_j^*r_j,
\qquad
 I_j={1\over2}\{\langle\xi_j,T_j\xi_j\rangle-
 \operatorname{Tr}T_j\}+\langle\ell_j,\xi_j\rangle.  \tag{5.1}
\]
Equation (4.2) says $I_j=\delta_ja_j/2$.  A11 also proves the exact
finite-cutoff telescope
\[
 V_J^{\rm ren}=\sum_{j\le J}(I_j+\mathcal C_j),
 \qquad V_0=0.                                        \tag{5.2}
\]
Let $b_J$ be the A13 whitened production divergence field, so that
$2V_J^{\rm ren}=\delta b_J$, and set $r=b_J-a$.  Equations (5.1)--(5.2)
then give the pointwise algebraic identity
\[
 {1\over2}\delta r=\sum_{j\le J}\mathcal C_j.         \tag{5.3}
\]
Combining (4.4) over the shells with (5.3) yields the exact one-use identity
\begin{align}
 \mathbb EV_J^{\rm ren}(X_J+h)
 ={}&-\varepsilon\mathbb E\sum_j|h_j|^2
      -\mathbb E\,\mathfrak S_{J,q}(X_J+h)\\
 &+{1\over2}\mathbb E\sum_j\left|
 (T_j+q^{-1}I)^{1/2}(h_j+qR_{q,j}\ell_j)
 \right|^2,                                           \tag{5.4}
\end{align}
where
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak S_{J,q}(z)=
 \sum_{j\le J}\left\{
 {q\over2}\langle\ell_j,R_{q,j}\ell_j\rangle
 -\mathcal C_j(z)\right\}.}                           \tag{5.5}
\]
Consequently the current child gate is reduced, without repeated past-energy
payment, to
\[
 \mathbb E\,\mathfrak S_{J,10/9}(X_J+h)
 \le \varepsilon_6\mathbb E\|X_J+h\|_6^6+C           \tag{5.6}
\]
uniformly for strict-past controls, with $\varepsilon_6<\gamma/6$.
The endpoint Cameron--Martin norm is bounded by the Boue--Dupuis control
energy, so (5.6) implies the umbrella one-use estimate.

Formula (5.5) is the promised coupling.  The first term is the A11 positive
source-square with its exact resolvent rebate; the second is the complete
coefficient increment.  Its balanced A7 reconstruction contains the
centered $Q$, rational current, translated-frame square, and Cartan curvature.
The relative-phase factor-four effect has not vanished: it is retained in
the dependence of $\mathcal C_j$ on the shifted current shell.  Therefore a
termwise or one-shell bound on the two summands is still forbidden.  The
remaining theorem is the global signed estimate (5.6), not a separated
source bound and not a restatement of the Nelson moment.

\section*{6. Executable adversarial audit}

The primary fixture uses tensor Gauss--Hermite quadrature on a nonlinear
three-coordinate triangular shift, predictor, and unrestricted innovation.
It checks (2.1), the trace cancellation, (3.3), the PSD affine identity (4.3), the resolvent completion (4.4), retention of the innovation
divergence, and a point at which the corresponding pointwise completion gap
is negative.  The independent fixture does not import the primary program.
It uses a different Hermite order and finite-difference Jacobians.  It also
breaks strict-past adaptation in two ways: a present-coordinate shift and a
future-dependent predictor produce nonzero residuals.  These are negative
controls for the precise filtration hypothesis, not evidence for the
infinite-dimensional production estimate.

\section*{Devil's-advocate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DISMISSED: the predictor cancellation is merely a fitted
numerical pattern.}  Lemma 2.1 is an exact conditional-expectation proof; two
quadrature implementations are regression tests for conventions and signs.

\item \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION: (3.3) is a pointwise square identity.}
Only (3.6) is pointwise after causalization.  Replacing
$\delta b^{<}(Z)$ by $h\cdot b^{<}(Z)$ is legal only after expectation.  The
primary negative control exhibits a negative pointwise gap.

\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST NONCAUSAL USE: an adapted shift may depend on
the present or future shell.}  Either dependence destroys the conditional
centering step.  The independent negative controls fail by macroscopic
residuals.

\item \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION: the positive predictor square may be
estimated separately from $\delta r_J$.}  Formula (4.2) is deliberately a
signed coupled charge.  Separate absolute-value estimates can recreate the
repeated energy loss that T-050 forbids.

\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST PROMOTION: causalization proves the production
one-use bound.}  It does not.  The cutoff-uniform bound on (4.2), with the
full $Q$/current/frame/Cartan algebra, is still open, so A13 remains T4.
\end{enumerate}
