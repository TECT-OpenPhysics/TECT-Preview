\begin{abstract}
R-161 closes a cardinality-uniform neighbourhood only for one-shot raw-source
affine controls.  This note instantiates an actual predictable shifted-state
law.  For every finite acyclic chain
\(
 \eta_0=\xi_0,
 \eta_{j+1}=\xi_{j+1}+A_j\eta_j
\)
on the retained pure-dyadic modes \(p,2p,\ldots,2^Jp\), it proves one positive
analytic coefficient radius, uniform in the finite chain length, nonzero
starting mode, finite cutoff, and admitted common-even contraction regulator.
Inside that radius the complete expected \emph{controller-pullback} Hessian of
the globally summed terminal action is larger than \(13/100\) in coefficient
norm and larger than \(1/10\) in the induced physical-source tangent metric.
The proof includes the projected-force connection and the full sixth-power
connection; neither may be discarded after nonlinear pullback.  The theorem
does not cover unrelated multi-chain forests, random or nonlinear coefficient
feedback, revisits, a pathwise fibrewise conditional Hessian, or T-050.
\end{abstract}

\section{Purpose and scope: result and exact boundary}
\hypertarget{section-1-result}{}

Work on the fixed side-16 three-torus, in physical dimension three, with the
hash-pinned A1 symbol, the fixed positive A7 floor, exact nonaliased continuum
integration, and one common real-even covariance-matched scalar contraction
multiplier.  Let \(p\ne0\) be retained and let
\[
 p_j=2^jp,\qquad j=0,\ldots,J.                              \tag{1.1}
\]
The independent whitened both-sign real Gaussian blocks are denoted by
\(\xi_j\).  For deterministic matrices \(A_j\) of the registered block size,
define the temporally ordered recursion
\[
 \eta_0=\xi_0,\qquad
 h_{j+1}=A_j\eta_j,\qquad
 \eta_{j+1}=\xi_{j+1}+h_{j+1}.                             \tag{1.2}
\]
Thus the later controller reads the actual shifted state \(\eta_j\), not the
raw innovation \(\xi_j\).  Put
\[
 \|A\|_{\mathfrak E}^2=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\|A_j\|_{\HS}^2.   \tag{1.3}
\]

\paragraph{R-162.}
There exists an explicit analytic number \(\delta_*>0\), independent of
\(J\), \(p\), the finite retained cutoff, and the admitted multiplier, such
that \(\|A\|_{\mathfrak E}<\delta_*\) implies, for every \(H\ne0\),
\[
 \boxed{
 D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_A[H,H]
   >{13\over100}\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}^2
   >{1\over10}\cG_A(H,H).}                               \tag{1.4}
\]
Here \(\chi(A)=h(A)\), \(\cG_A(H,H)=\E\|D\chi_A[H]\|^2\),
and \(\cA\) is the one globally summed R-141 production scalar: source cost,
complete global endpoint/current owner, and terminal sixth-power term, each
included exactly once.  Equation (1.4) is a controller-pullback theorem.  No
intrinsic affine physical-source Hessian is promoted in R-162.

The radius is analytic and deliberately nonnumerical.  Its compact-data
derivative inputs are proved finite but are not evaluated numerically.  No
finite-dimensional family-by-family continuity argument is used.

\section{The recursive transfer and its exact jets}
\hypertarget{section-2-resolvent}{}

Let \(\mathfrak E_J=\bigoplus_{j=0}^J E_j\) and define the one-step block shift
\[
 (N_Ax)_{j+1}=A_jx_j,\qquad (N_Ax)_0=0.                  \tag{2.1}
\]
It is nilpotent.  Set
\[
 T_A=(\Id-N_A)^{-1},\qquad B_A=T_A-\Id=N_AT_A.            \tag{2.2}
\]
Then \(\eta=T_A\xi\) and \(h=B_A\xi\).  If
\(r=\|A\|_{\mathfrak E}<1\) and \(\tau=(1-r)^{-1}\), then
\[
 \|N_A\|_{\op}\le\|N_A\|_{\HS}=r,\qquad
 \|T_A\|_{\op}\le\tau,\qquad
 \|B_A\|_{\HS}\le r\tau .                              \tag{2.3}
\]
These estimates contain no factor depending on the path length.

For coefficient directions \(H_1,\ldots,H_m\), exact resolvent
differentiation gives
\[
 D^mT_A[H_1,\ldots,H_m]
 =\sum_{\sigma\in S_m}
 T_AN_{H_{\sigma(1)}}T_A\cdots
 N_{H_{\sigma(m)}}T_A .                                  \tag{2.4}
\]
Consequently, for \(m=1,2,3\),
\[
 \|D^mT_A[H_1,\ldots,H_m]\|_{\HS}
 \le m!\tau^{m+1}\prod_{a=1}^m\|H_a\|_{\mathfrak E}.  \tag{2.5}
\]
The same derivatives apply to \(B_A\).  In particular,
\[
 \|D\chi_A[H]\|_{L^2_\xi}\le\tau^2\|H\|_{\mathfrak E},
 \quad
 \|D^2\chi_A[H,K]\|_{L^2_\xi}\le
 2\tau^3\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}\|K\|_{\mathfrak E}.       \tag{2.6}
\]
Since \(N_H=T_A^{-1}(D B_A[H])T_A^{-1}\), one also has
\[
 (1+r)^{-4}\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}^2
 \le\cG_A(H,H)\le\tau^4\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}^2.         \tag{2.7}
\]

The complete source cost is not the affine surrogate.  It is
\[
 \cA_{\rm CM}(A)={9\over20}\|B_A\|_{\HS}^2.             \tag{2.8}
\]
For \(r\le1/2\), the exact product rule and (2.5) yield
\[
 \|D^3\cA_{\rm CM}(A)\|_{\op}
 \le {27\over5}(1+r)\tau^5\le {1296\over5}.             \tag{2.9}
\]
Thus the source projected-force connection is paid inside one scalar
third-derivative bound.

\section{Origin transfer: the acceleration must cancel}
\hypertarget{section-3-origin}{}

At \(A=0\), the tangent is the R-160 one-shot family:
\[
 D\chi_0[H]=N_H\xi .                                     \tag{3.1}
\]
The recursive chart nevertheless has nonzero acceleration,
\[
 D^2\chi_0[H,K]=(N_HN_K+N_KN_H)\xi .                    \tag{3.2}
\]
Only adjacent coefficient blocks survive.  In the output block \(j+1\),
\[
 (D^2\chi_0[H,K])_{j+1}
 =(H_jK_{j-1}+K_jH_{j-1})\xi_{j-1}.                     \tag{3.3}
\]
It follows directly that
\[
 \|D^2\chi_0[H,K]\|_{L^2_\xi}
 \le2\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}\|K\|_{\mathfrak E},         \tag{3.4}
\]
uniformly in \(J\).

Every term in (3.3) is a \(p_j\to4p_j\) connection.  Against the original
carrier it has only Fourier support \(\{\pm3p_j,\pm5p_j\}\).  The R-155
centered stationary common-even exact-torus cancellation therefore kills its
pairing with the complete global first variation, term by term.  The source
connection vanishes because \(B_0=0\).  This is a global-action statement; it
does not identify a local \(E_{\rm CN}\) atom with \(P_{\rm comp}\).

Equivalently, if \(P_A=T_AT_A^*\), then
\[
 D^2P_0[H,H]
 =2N_HN_H^*+2N_H^2+2(N_H^*)^2.                           \tag{3.5}
\]
The first term is the R-160 affine covariance Hessian.  The last two terms are
exactly the cancelled acceleration directions.  Hence the recursive pullback
at the origin equals the R-160 affine Hessian and obeys
\[
 \boxed{D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_0[H,H]
       >{4\over25}\|H\|_{\mathfrak E}^2.}               \tag{3.6}
\]
Aliasing, a zero mode, a nonzero mean, mismatched regulators, or a local-owner
substitution would invalidate this transfer and are excluded.

\section{One global scalar, including the sixth power}
\hypertarget{section-5-scalar}{}

Let
\[
 \overline{\mathcal B}(C)=\E_{N(0,C)}\mathcal B(W),
 \qquad
 \overline{\mathcal T}(C)=\E_{N(0,C)}D^2\mathcal B(W). \tag{5.1}
\]
The globally summed R-107/R-141 endpoint identity and the centered Gaussian
sixth moment combine into the single pointwise scalar
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Phi(C,U,K)={1\over2}\bigg\{&
 \Tr[U\overline{\mathcal B}(C)]
 +\sum_iK_i\otimes K_i:\overline{\mathcal T}(C)\bigg\}\\
 &+{3\over20}\left[(\Tr C)^3
 +6\Tr C\,\Tr(C^2)+8\Tr(C^3)\right].
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{5.2}
\]
Up to an irrelevant constant, the full expected action is exactly
\[
 F(A)={9\over20}\|B_A\|_{\HS}^2
      +\int_{\mathbb T_{16}^3}\Phi(Y_A(x))\,dx.          \tag{5.3}
\]

This placement of the sixth power is load-bearing.  Its intrinsic affine
physical-source Hessian is positive semidefinite, but its nonlinear
controller pullback contains the indefinite projected-force/acceleration
term.  For the scalar test
\[
 f(z)=z^6,\qquad z(a,b)=ab,                              \tag{5.4}

the Hessian of \(a^6b^6\) at \((1,1)\) is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}30&36\\36&30\end{pmatrix},              \tag{5.5}

with eigenvalues \(66\) and \(-6\).  Therefore R-161's affine step of
dropping the global positive sextic Hessian cannot be reused here.

For \(r\le1/2\), \(\|P_A-\Id\|_{\op}\le\tau^2-1\le3\).
R-161's background bound and (4.7) place every \(Y_A(x)\) in one compact
finite-dimensional covariance-data box, independent of the chain.  The

\[
 M_j=\sup_{Y\ \mathrm{in\ the\ box}}\|D^j\Phi(Y)\|_{\op}<\infty,
 \qquad j=1,2,3,                                         \tag{5.6}

\section{Uniform Hessian modulus and retained gap}
\hypertarget{section-6-modulus}{}

At \(r_0=1/2\), one has \(\tau_0=2\), and (4.10) gives the three constants

 J_1=16c_Z,\qquad J_2=96c_Z,\qquad J_3=768c_Z.          \tag{6.1}

 \|D^3(\Phi\circ Y)\|
 \le M_3J_1^3+3M_2J_1J_2+M_1J_3.                        \tag{6.2}

The \(M_1J_3\) term is mandatory: recursive covariance has a nonzero third
jet.  With torus volume \(16^3=4096\), define
\[
 L_*={1296\over5}+4096\bigl(4096M_3c_Z^3
 +4608M_2c_Z^2+768M_1c_Z\bigr).                          \tag{6.3}

Equations (2.9), (4.10), and (6.2) prove
\[
 \|D^2F(A)-D^2F(0)\|_{\op}\le L_*\|A\|_{\mathfrak E},
 \qquad \|A\|_{\mathfrak E}\le{1\over2}.              \tag{6.4}

Every forward block and its legal reverse are adjoints inside this one
self-adjoint Hessian.  Their balanced term is the symmetric cross, not a
third payment.  The derived states \(\eta_j\) are not independent low or

\[
 \boxed{\delta_*=

Using (2.7), (6.6) therefore implies
\[
 D^2F(A)[H,H]>{1\over10}\cG_A(H,H),                     \tag{6.8}
\]
which completes (1.4).

The pullback identity represented by (5.3)--(6.8) is precisely

 D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_A[H,K]
 =\langle D\chi_AH,H_{\rm prod}(h_A)D\chi_AK\rangle

\section{What failed and what remains open}
\hypertarget{section-7-boundaries}{}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Dropping the sextic as positive failed.}  The exact fixture
(5.4)--(5.5) has a negative pullback eigenvalue.  Repair: include the full
Gaussian sextic polynomial in \(\Phi\) and retain all three terms in (6.2).

\item \textbf{Bare finite-dimensional continuity failed as a uniform proof.}
The registered coherent fixture
\[
 \mathcal F_N(h)={2\over25}\|h\|_2^2
 -{(\sum_jh_j)^4\over4N}+{(\sum_jh_j)^6\over6N^2}       \tag{7.1}
\]
has origin Hessian \(4/25\), but along
\(h=N^{-1}(1,\ldots,1)\) its coherent Hessian is
\(4/25-3+5/N<0\) for \(N\ge3\).  Repair: use the explicit
chain-length-free resolvent and dyadic synthesis bounds.

\item \textbf{A stationarity-free abstract resolvent transfer failed.}
A positive intrinsic quadratic endpoint can still have a negative recursive
pullback if its origin gradient pairs with the \(p\to4p\) acceleration.
R-162 survives only because Section 3 proves the exact full-action Fourier
cancellation.  The abstract counterexample is not an A1 counterexample.

\item \textbf{The single-chain raw-current bound does not union over an
arbitrary forest.}  Across all unrelated three-dimensional roots the crude
sum of \(d_p^2\sim|p|^{-2}\) grows with the cutoff.  A multi-chain result needs
a new full-lattice weighted recursive estimate, not repetition of (4.3).

\item \textbf{Random nonlinear coefficients leave the Gaussian chart.}
Already \(A(\xi)=\varepsilon\operatorname{sgn}\xi\) sends a centered Gaussian
to a component containing \(\varepsilon|\xi|\), which is noncentered and
non-Gaussian.  The covariance scalar (5.2) no longer determines the law.
\end{enumerate}

R-162 therefore advances from the fixed two-stage R-159 chart to every finite
acyclic pure-dyadic deterministic-matrix shifted-state chain, uniformly in
chain length.  It does not prove an intrinsic physical-source Hessian,
multiple unrelated chains, random or nonlinear past-dependent coefficients,
revisits or cycles, a pathwise fibrewise conditional inequality, cutoff or
floor removal, T-050, A13, Nelson, an interacting measure, any phase or PDE,
or Sector-A closure.
