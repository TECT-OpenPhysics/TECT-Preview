\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (changing-current telescope)}

For every $j_0\leq j\leq J$,
\[
 \boxed{Y_j-Y_{j-1}=b_j+c_j.}
 \tag{3.1}
\]
Consequently, for $n\geq j_0$,
\[

 =Y_n-Y_\ell-\sum_{j=j_0}^{n}c_j.}
 \tag{3.2}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Insert and subtract $P_{j-1}\mathcal J_j$:
\begin{align*}
Y_j-Y_{j-1}
&=(P_j-P_{j-1})\mathcal J_j
  +P_{j-1}(\mathcal J_j-\mathcal J_{j-1})\\
&=b_j+c_j.
\end{align*}
Summing gives (3.2).  No limiting operation is involved. \hfill$\square$

Because $b_j$ is a martingale difference, the vectors $\Pi_m b_j$ are
pairwise orthogonal in $L^2(\Omega;H)$ for fixed $m$.  Define the deterministic

\subsection*{Lemma 6.1 (orthogonal causal Carleson summation)}

Suppose
\[
 f_j=\sum_{k\leq j}u_k,
\]
and, for every fixed output shell $m$, the vectors $\Pi_mu_k$ are pairwise
orthogonal in $L^2(\Omega;H)$.  If
\[

 \leq2^{-2s(m-k)}q_k,
 \qquad m\geq k+C_0,\qquad s>\frac12,
 \tag{6.3}
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_m\sum_{j\leq m-C}2^j
 \mathbb E\|\Pi_mf_j\|^2
 \leq C_s2^{-2sC}\sum_k2^kq_k,}
 \tag{6.4}
\]
where one may take
\[
 C_s=\frac{1}{
 (1-2^{1-2s})(1-2^{-2s})}.
 \tag{6.5}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Orthogonality removes every $k\neq k'$ cross term.  All terms are
nonnegative, so the summations may be rearranged:
\begin{align*}
\sum_m\sum_{j\leq m-C}2^j\|\Pi_mf_j\|_2^2
&\leq\sum_kq_k\sum_{j\geq k}2^j
       \sum_{m\geq j+C}2^{-2s(m-k)}\\
&=\frac{2^{-2sC}}{1-2^{-2s}}
  \sum_k2^kq_k\sum_{r\geq0}2^{(1-2s)r},
\end{align*}
which is (6.4). \hfill$\square$

 \lesssim2^{-3j_0}\mathbb EX,

\section{Heat lifting and the exact covariance criterion}

Target heat must act on the Gram matrix, not on $C$ before squaring.  The
correct enlarged Hilbert coordinate is
\[

 \in L^2(\mu_\Sigma;\mathbb R^4),
 \tag{8.1}
\]

\[

 \tag{8.2}
\]

\[
 P_\Sigma B-(P_\Sigma C)^T(P_\Sigma C)

\]

$P_\Sigma C=0$ but $P_\Sigma B=B(te_1)\neq0$.  This also shows why changing
heat covariance across roots requires the compensator in Section 4.

\subsection*{Theorem 8.1 (conditional square--trace identity)}

Let $C(z)$ and a derivative control $c$ be measurable with respect to a
sigma-algebra $\mathcal A$.  For a fresh derivative vector $G$, put
\[
 \mu=\mathbb E[G\mid\mathcal A],
 \qquad
 \Sigma_c=\operatorname{Cov}(G\mid\mathcal A),
 \qquad B=C^TC.
\]
Then
\begin{align*}
&\mathbb E\left[
 \frac12|C(z)(G+c)|^2-\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(B(z)\Gamma)
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal A\right]\\
&\qquad=
 \boxed{
 \frac12|C(z)(c+\mu)|^2
 +\frac12\operatorname{Tr}
 [B(z)(\Sigma_c-\Gamma)].}
\tag{8.4}
\end{align*}

\emph{Proof.}
Write $G+c=(G-\mu)+(c+\mu)$.  The conditional mixed term vanishes.  The
conditional expectation of the centered square is
$\operatorname{Tr}(B\Sigma_c)$, which gives (8.4). \hfill$\square$

If $\mu=0$ and $\Sigma_c=\Gamma$, (8.4) is the nonnegative square
$\frac12|C(z)c|^2$.  This closes the predictable coefficient/fresh derivative
subbranch.  General later feedback changes $\mu$ or $\Sigma_c$; the remaining
trace defect is signed.  The two conditional laws $G=0$ and
$G=\pm\sqrt2$ relative to $\Gamma=1$ give defects $-1/2$ and $+1/2$.
Accordingly, covariance preservation is a hypothesis to prove, not a
consequence of adaptedness.

\section{Moving shell edges are a signed repartition}

Let $L_j\leq U_j$ be nested exact orthogonal spatial projectors commuting
with the target quadratic form.  At a fixed complete current $c$, divide
space into low $L_j$, near $U_j-L_j$, and high $I-U_j$.  In the forward

\begin{align*}
 \Phi_{\rm low}
 &=+\frac12\|\Delta L_jc\|_Q^2,\\
 \Phi_{\rm near}
 &=+\frac12\|\Delta U_jc\|_Q^2
   -\frac12\|\Delta L_jc\|_Q^2,\\
 \Phi_{\rm high}
 &=-\frac12\|\Delta U_jc\|_Q^2.
 \tag{9.1}
\end{align*}
Therefore
\[
 \Phi_{\rm low}+\Phi_{\rm near}+\Phi_{\rm high}=0.
 \tag{9.2}
\]

sharp-shell repartition of the R-069 pure-control telescope.  It does not
create a positive absolute pair-high term and does not control smooth
