\section*{1. Purpose and scope}

R-125 identifies the exact fixed-root total symbol
\[
 \cT_k=\mathcal V_{{\rm fut},k}
       -\mathcal F^{\rm ad}_{063,k}                         \tag{1.1}
\]
after all same-root visits have been recombined and the covariance owner
$\Delta\Gamma_k$ is used once.  It does not display the directional force of
that symbol.  It also leaves two apparently separate tasks: estimate the
controlled secant and bound the root-only stationary baseline.

This note performs four proof-first audits of that boundary.
\begin{enumerate}
\item It differentiates (1.1) as one complete signed endpoint and gives its
      periodic Euler force.  The derivative contains the covariance trace,
      current variation, derivative injection, and the surviving Cartan skew
      exactly once.
\item It distinguishes this Euler force from the predictable Cameron--Martin
      Riesz representative actually available to a legal control.
\item It transports the R-079 low, injected, and future blocks with every
      predictable-mean reserve and the generally nonzero finite-low reference
      $\E\cW_J(A^0)$ retained.
\item It replaces a scalar off-diagonal norm slogan by the exact block-Loewner
      acceptance condition and computes the forward mixed-shell sums.  It also
      records why neither the strong quadratic gap nor an unrestricted reverse
      extension is available.
\end{enumerate}

The result is a finite-cutoff structural theorem, not the production one-use
bound.  It does not prove a forward, reverse, balanced, or low multiplier
estimate for the actual projected force.  It does not prove the production
common-terminal identification used by the low/root diagnostic.  It proves no

interacting measure, Sector-A closure, or tier promotion.  The host claim
remains T4.

Throughout Sections 2--5 the cutoff and positive floor are fixed.  The heat
law, output projection, covariance owner, and conditioning are fixed while an
endpoint is varied.  Coefficients are smooth bounded cylindrical objects, the
covariance matrices are deterministic symmetric positive semidefinite owners,

\section*{2. The once-owned total trace-excess symbol}

Fix a probability root $k$ and suppress its already recombined visit index.

\[
 B(W)=C_6(W)^TC_6(W)=\sum_{a=1}^{6}c_a(W)c_a(W)^T,             \tag{2.1}
\]
where $c_a$ denotes one real row vector.  For the spatial directions put
\[
 V_i=\partial_iW,
 \qquad

\]
Here $\E_f$ is exactly the R-125 future/heat conditioning: it may retain the
current root.  Define
\[
 \boxed{
 \cT_k(W)=
 \sum_i\E_f\int B(W):\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}\dd x
 -\sum_{a,i}\int |\Phi_{a,i}(W)|^2\dd x.}                    \tag{2.3}

Under the R-125 exhaustive orthogonal cluster resolution, retained heat
coordinate, and once-owned covariance hypotheses, (2.3) is the cluster sum

\[

                                                                    \tag{2.4}

The R-125 variance/forest bridge then gives

\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (exact directional derivative)}

Let $W_t=W+tH$ and $V_{i,t}=V_i+t\partial_iH$.  The direction $H$ may still
belong to the retained finite-dimensional law; no force factorization is
claimed at this stage.  Then
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 D\cT_k(W)[H]
={}&\sum_i\E_f\int DB(W)[H]:\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}\dd x\\
 &-2\sum_{a,i}\Rea\int \Phi_{a,i}(W)\,
 \E_f\bigl[(Dc_a(W)H)^TV_i+c_a(W)^T\partial_iH\bigr]\dd x.
\end{aligned}}                                                \tag{2.6}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The covariance owner is fixed, so only $B(W)$ varies in the first term of
(2.3).  The product rule gives
\[
 {\dd\over\dd t}\Phi_{a,i}(W_t)\big|_{t=0}
 =\E_f[(Dc_a(W)H)^TV_i+c_a(W)^T\partial_iH].                  \tag{2.7}
\]
Differentiate the Hilbert norm square and take its real polarization.  This
gives (2.6).  No forest atom, coefficient derivative, or covariance trace has
been estimated separately. \hfill$\square$

For a legal moving visit $W_{k,v,t}=W_{k,v-1}+t a_{k,v}$, summing (2.6)
gives the exact endpoint telescope
\[
 \cT_k(W_{k,V})-\cT_k(W_{k,0})
 =\sum_v\int_0^1D\cT_k(W_{k,v,t})[a_{k,v}]\dd t.              \tag{2.8}
\]
A terminal straight homotopy is equally valid.  A visit-square majorant is
not invariant: two visits $a,-a$ have zero terminal displacement but positive
sum of visit squares.  Thus (2.8), not an arbitrary visitwise square, is the
quotient-safe coordinate.

\section*{3. Periodic Euler force and the causal Riesz correction}

Assume now that $H$ is measurable with respect to the sigma-field retained
outside $\E_f$, so it can be pulled through that conditional expectation.
Using $DB[H]=\sum_a\{(Dc_aH)c_a^T+c_a(Dc_aH)^T\}$ and periodic integration
by parts in the derivative-injection term yields the following.

\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (Euler-force factorization)}

Under the hypotheses above,
\[
 D\cT_k(W)[H]=\int H\cdot G_k(W)\dd x,                       \tag{3.1}
\]
where
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
G_k={}&2\sum_{a,i}\Bigl\{
 \E_f[(Dc_a)^T\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}c_a]
 +\partial_i(\Phi_{a,i}\bar c_a)
 -\Phi_{a,i}\E_f[(Dc_a)^TV_i]
 \Bigr\}\\
={}&2\sum_{a,i}\Bigl\{
 \E_f[(Dc_a)^T\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}c_a]
 +\bar c_a\,\partial_i\Phi_{a,i}
 +\Phi_{a,i}\E_f[(Dc_a-Dc_a^T)V_i]
 \Bigr\}.
\end{aligned}}                                               \tag{3.2--3.3}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
The trace variation is
\[
 DB[H]:\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}
 =2\sum_a H^T(Dc_a)^T\Delta\Gamma_{k,i}c_a.                 \tag{3.4}
\]
The current-coefficient variation gives the last term of (3.2).  Periodic
integration by parts sends
\[
 -2\Phi_{a,i}\bar c_a^T\partial_iH
 \quad\hbox{to}\quad
 2H^T\partial_i(\Phi_{a,i}\bar c_a).                        \tag{3.5}
\]
Finally $\partial_i\bar c_a=\E_f[Dc_aV_i]$, which converts the first line of
(3.2) into its second line. \hfill$\square$

\subsection*{3.1 Curl convention}

For the coefficient one-form $\vartheta_a=c_a(W)^T\dd W$, the repository

 -2\Phi_{a,i}\E_f[\Omega_a(\,\cdot\,,V_i)],                 \tag{3.7}

\subsection*{Lemma 4.1 (forward mixed geometric sum)}

Suppose, after the total symbol and all matrix channels have been assembled,
one has the declared block estimate
\[
 \|A^{\rm mix}_{r,m}\|
 \le C_{\rm mix}2^{r-2m},
 \qquad m\ge r+C.                                           \tag{4.3}

Then its scalar-kernel square sum is
\[
 \sum_{r\ge j_0}\sum_{m\ge r+C}2^{2r-4m}
 ={64\over45}2^{-4C}2^{-2j_0}.                               \tag{4.4}
\]
Its Hilbert--Schmidt majorant is therefore
\[
 K_{\rm mix}
 \le {8C_{\rm mix}\over\sqrt{45}}2^{-2C}2^{-j_0}.             \tag{4.5}
\]
At $C=1$, $K_{\rm mix}\le2C_{\rm mix}2^{-j_0}/\sqrt{45}$.

For comparison, the positive prototype has the exact $\ell^1$ scalar sum
\[
 \sum_{r\ge j_0}\sum_{m\ge r+C}2^{r-4m}
 ={128\over105}2^{-4C}2^{-3j_0}.                             \tag{4.6}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Sum first in $m$.  The ratios are $2^{-4}$ for both inner sums and
$2^{-2}$, respectively $2^{-3}$, for the outer sums.  Their products give
$64/45$ and $128/105$. \hfill$\square$

\section*{5. Exact block-Loewner acceptance condition}

Let $R=R^*$ and $S=S^*$ collect signed diagonal reserves that remain in the
same two-channel block as a mixed operator $A$.  The desired upper payment is
\[
 \ip{x}{Rx}+\Rea\ip{x}{Ay}+\ip{y}{Sy}
 \le2\eta\|x\|^2+2\zeta\|y\|^2.                            \tag{5.1}
\]

\subsection*{Theorem 5.1 (block-Loewner criterion)}

Equation (5.1) holds for all $x,y$ if and only if
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{pmatrix}
 2\eta\Id-R&-A/2\\
 -A^*/2&2\zeta\Id-S
 \end{pmatrix}\succeq0.}                                   \tag{5.2}
\]
For $R=S=0$, this is equivalent to
\[
                         \boxed{\|A\|\le4\sqrt{\eta\zeta}.} \tag{5.3}
\]
If the adverse form is $2\Rea\ip{x}{Ay}$, the threshold is instead
$2\sqrt{\eta\zeta}$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Subtract the left side of (5.1) from the right side and write the resulting
quadratic form on $x\oplus y$.  This is exactly the matrix in (5.2).  With
$R=S=0$, its Schur complement is nonnegative exactly when
$A^*A\preceq16\eta\zeta\Id$. \hfill$\square$

Weighted or singular reserves require the corresponding generalized Schur
complement, including kernel/range compatibility.  One may not replace them
by scalar $\eta,\zeta$ before the production matrix has been assembled.

For the disjoint R-103 debts and the R-124 legal-row source allocation, the
trace-scale scalar budget is
\[
 K_{\rm bud}=4\sqrt{
 \left({197\over440}-{3\over125P}\right){3\over25}}
 =0.92093233\ldots,
 \qquad P=4+10^{-12}.                                      \tag{5.4}
\]
Combining (4.5) with (5.4) at $C=1$ would be sufficient if
\[
 C_{\rm mix}2^{-j_0}
 <{\sqrt{45}\over2}K_{\rm bud}
 =3.08890\ldots.                                           \tag{5.5}

Again, (5.5) is not asserted for production.

\section*{8. Low-injected stationary-baseline transport}

The root-only baseline of R-124 is not automatically signed.  Nevertheless,
R-079 gives an exact way to transport it without introducing an independent
symbol, provided the production root family is first identified with the
R-079 common-terminal Doob family and the low/root trace partition matches.

Let $e\in\{0,j,*\}$ denote the common reference, middle, and terminal
endpoints.  For each root/output owner put
\[
 \Phi^e_{j,\cC}=b^e_{j,\cC}+Y^e_{j,\cC},
 \qquad b^e_{j,\cC}=P_{j-1}P_\cC\mathcal J_e,
 \qquad Y^e_{j,\cC}=d_jP_\cC\mathcal J_e,                  \tag{8.1}
\]
\[
 D^e_{j,\cC}=\Theta^e_{j,\cC}-\|Y^e_{j,\cC}\|^2,
 \qquad
 S^e_{j,\cC}=D^e_{j,\cC}-\|b^e_{j,\cC}\|^2.              \tag{8.2}
\]
The low owner has $Y^e_{\ell,\cC}=P_\ell P_\cC\mathcal J_e$ and
$b^e_{\ell,\cC}=0$.

\subsection*{Theorem 8.1 (R-079 sign ledger)}

Under the common-terminal, exhaustive-output, and matching once-owned trace
hypotheses,
\[
 \sum_\cC\E(D^j_{j,\cC}-D^0_{j,\cC})=-2\mathfrak F_j,      \tag{8.3}
\]
\[
 \sum_\cC\E(D^*_{j,\cC}-D^j_{j,\cC})=-2\mathfrak I_j,     \tag{8.4}
\]
and
\[
 \sum_\cC\E(D^*_{\ell,\cC}-D^0_{\ell,\cC})
 =-2\mathfrak L_\ell.                                      \tag{8.5}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{
 D_*^{\rm full}-D_0^{\rm full}
 =-2\left\{\mathfrak L_\ell+
       \sum_j(\mathfrak F_j+\mathfrak I_j)\right\},}       \tag{8.6}
\]
and, with $M_e=\sum_{j,\cC}\E\|b^e_{j,\cC}\|^2$,
\[
 \boxed{
 S_*^{\rm full}-S_0^{\rm full}
 =-2\left\{\mathfrak L_\ell+
       \sum_j(\mathfrak F_j+\mathfrak I_j)\right\}
 -(M_*-M_0).}                                               \tag{8.7}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
R-079 defines $f_j=d_j(\mathcal J_j-\mathcal J_0)$ and
$i_j=d_j(\mathcal J_*-\mathcal J_j)$.  Expanding the two Hilbert squares in
its definitions of $\mathfrak F_j$ and $\mathfrak I_j$ gives (8.3) and
(8.4), with the minus sign because $D$ is trace minus energy.  The low
polarization gives (8.5).  Summation gives (8.6); subtracting predictable
mean squares gives (8.7). \hfill$\square$

The same hypotheses imply
\[
 D_e^{\rm full}
 =\E\Theta_e^{\rm tot}-\E\|\mathcal J_e\|^2
 =-2\E\cW_J(A^e).                                          \tag{8.8}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \boxed{S_0^{\rm full}=-2\E\cW_J(A^0)-M_0.}                \tag{8.9}
\]
The finite-low reference $A^0=A_{<j_0}$ is generally controlled.
R-120 proves $\E\cW_J(0)=0$ only in its raw uncontrolled stationary scope;
it does not permit deleting $\E\cW_J(A^0)$ from (8.9).

For the middle/root baseline define
\[
 B_{\rm mid}=\sum_{j,\cC}\E S^j_{j,\cC},qquad
 B_\ell^0=\sum_\cC\E S^0_{\ell,\cC}.                      \tag{8.10}

Equations (8.3) and (8.9) cancel the reference root predictable means and

\[

  -2\sum_j\mathfrak F_j
  -\sum_{j,\cC}\E\|b^j_{j,\cC}\|^2.}                     \tag{8.11}

\[

is equivalent, under the stated bridge hypotheses, to
\[
 \boxed{
 B_\ell^0+2\E\cW_J(A^0)+2\sum_j\mathfrak F_j
 +\sum_{j,\cC}\E\|b^j_{j,\cC}\|^2\ge-2C_0.}              \tag{8.13}

Equation (8.13) removes a separately named baseline symbol only by coupling
it to the injected block, finite-low endpoint, and favorable middle mean
