\section{Purpose and scope}

\[

further facts about (1.1).

normal form.  If $L$ is the second-order Taylor remainder of the heat-lifted

\[
 \boxed{

  +{1\over2}c^TB_Tc,}                                   \tag{1.2}

not a positivity statement.

Second, all coefficient-nonresonant and payload-comparable dyadic pieces of

estimates.  The $Q$ branch retains the R-085 quadratic-payload total $7/10$;
the $G$ branch retains the R-078 $a^2Da$ total $23/30$ and moment $30/7$.
An independent base-frozen cubic coordinate has total $13/15$, slack $2/15$,
and moment $15/2$.  Thus the whole rational question is reduced to one
coefficient-dominant high--high-to-low shifted resonance, with the endpoint

\section{A sharp cubic Sobolev payment}

Let $a$ be a finite-dimensional vector field on the fixed three-torus, with

\subsection*{Theorem 4.1}

For $0\le s\le2$,

\[
 \boxed{
 \|a^{\otimes3}\|_{H^s}
 \le C_s\|a\|_{H^2}^{s}\|a\|_6^{3-s}.}                \tag{4.1}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
At the low endpoint,

\[
 \|a^{\otimes3}\|_2\le C\|a\|_6^3.                  \tag{4.2}
\]

At the high endpoint, the three-dimensional Gagliardo--Nirenberg inequality
gives

\[
 \|a\|_\infty^2\le C\|a\|_{H^2}\|a\|_6.             \tag{4.3}
\]

The zeroth and first derivatives are bounded by the right side below using
$H^2\hookrightarrow W^{1,6}\cap L^6$.  For the second derivative, the only
types are $a^2D^2a$ and $a(Da)^2$, and hence

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|a^{\otimes3}\|_{H^2}
 &\le C\{\|a\|_\infty^2\|a\|_{H^2}
          +\|a\|_6\|Da\|_6^2\}\\
 &\le C\|a\|_{H^2}^2\|a\|_6.
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{4.4}
\]

Sobolev interpolation of the fixed tensor $a^{\otimes3}$ between (4.2) and
(4.4) proves (4.1). \hfill$\square$

Put

\[
 X=\|a\|_{H^2}^2,\qquad Y=\|a\|_6^6.                 \tag{4.5}
\]

If $M_s$ is a control-independent $H^{-s}$ model norm, (4.1) yields

\[
 |\langle\mathbb Q,a^{\otimes3}\rangle|
 \le C M_sX^{s/2}Y^{(3-s)/6}.                           \tag{4.6}
\]

At $s=11/10$ the exponent ledger is

\[
 {s\over2}={11\over20},\qquad
 {3-s\over6}={19\over60},\qquad
 {11\over20}+{19\over60}={13\over15},                \tag{4.7}
\]

with slack $2/15$.  One three-factor weighted Young inequality therefore
gives

\[
 \boxed{
 M_{11/10}X^{11/20}Y^{19/60}
 \le\eta X+\zeta Y
 +C\eta^{-33/8}\zeta^{-19/8}M_{11/10}^{15/2}.}         \tag{4.8}
\]

The R-063 balanced coefficient-jet theorem supplies every finite moment of
the base-frozen decorated model
$D^3\overline B(U)\diamond Q$.  Thus the base-frozen part of (3.3) closes
with moment $15/2$.  Formula (4.8) is sharper than multiplying the old
single-control ledger three times, which would give the false impression of
the supercritical total $21/20$.

The qualification ``base-frozen'' is load-bearing.  R-063's translation is
for deterministic $H^2$ shifts.  It does not turn
$D^3\overline B(U+ta)-D^3\overline B(U)$ under an adapted random shift into
