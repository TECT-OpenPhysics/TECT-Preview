\section{A sharpened cubic Besov theorem}

\subsection{Statement and proof}

Let $0<s<1$ and $A\in H^2(\mathbb T_L^3)\cap L^6$.  Then (1.2) holds.
The estimate is tensor-entrywise, so finite component contractions change
only its constant.

Let $\Delta_j$ be inhomogeneous Littlewood--Paley blocks.  Expand the four
inputs and assign each monomial to its largest \emph{input} dyadic index
$j$, resolving ties by a fixed ordering.  Write the resulting sum as
\[
 A^3DA=\sum_{j\ge-1}G_j.                               \tag{3.1}
\]
Every $G_j$ has Fourier support in a ball of radius $C2^j$, even when two
high inputs produce a low output.  Hence
$\|G_j\|_{B^s_{1,1}}\lesssim2^{sj}\|G_j\|_1$.  If the
differentiated input is maximal, put $\Delta_jDA$ in $L^2$ and the other
three partial sums in $L^6$.  If an undifferentiated input is maximal, put
that $\Delta_jA$ in $L^2$ and use
$\|DS_jA\|_6\lesssim2^j\|A\|_6$ for the differentiated partial sum.
The finitely many tie patterns obey the same estimate, so
\[
 \|G_j\|_{B^s_{1,1}}
 \lesssim 2^{(1+s)j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2\|A\|_6^3.          \tag{3.2}
\]
This input-maximum decomposition, rather than an output projection, retains
all high--high-to-low interactions.  Summing (3.2) gives the multilinear
paraproduct bound
\[
 \|A^3DA\|_{B^s_{1,1}}
 \lesssim \|A\|_{B^{1+s}_{2,1}}\|A\|_6^3               \tag{3.3}
\]
with no frequency-separation hypothesis.

It remains to interpolate the single-input Besov norm.  Split its dyadic
sum, now legitimately by the input index, at $K$.  Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \sum_{j\le K}2^{(1+s)j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2
 \lesssim2^{(1+s)K}\|A\|_2,
\]
whereas
\[
 \sum_{j>K}2^{(1+s)j}\|\Delta_jA\|_2
 \lesssim2^{-(1-s)K}\|A\|_{H^2}.
\]
Balancing $2^{2K}\simeq\|A\|_{H^2}/\|A\|_2$ proves
\[
 \|A\|_{B^{1+s}_{2,1}}
 \lesssim
 \|A\|_{H^2}^{(1+s)/2}\|A\|_2^{(1-s)/2}               \tag{3.4}
\]
and (1.2) follows from $\|A\|_2\lesssim\|A\|_6$ on the fixed torus.
The inhomogeneous bottom block and the zero cases are absorbed into the
fixed-volume constant.

\subsection{The exact Young ledger}

Let $R\ge0$ be the $C^{-s}$ norm of a reconstructed current.  From (1.2),
\[
 R\|A^3DA\|_{B^s_{1,1}}
 \lesssim R X^aY^b,\qquad
 a={1+s\over4},\quad b={7-s\over12}.                    \tag{3.5}
\]
The unused exponent is
\[
 c=1-a-b={1-s\over6}>0.                                  \tag{3.6}
\]
Weighted AM--GM therefore yields, for every $\eta,\zeta>0$,
\[
 \boxed{
 RX^aY^b
 \le \eta X+\zeta Y
 +C_s\eta^{-a/c}\zeta^{-b/c}R^{1/c}.}                  \tag{3.7}
\]
The required random moment is $6/(1-s)$.  At $\kappa=1/10$,
\[
 s={3\over5},\quad a={2\over5},\quad b={8\over15},
 \quad c={1\over15},                                    \tag{3.8}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{
 RX^{2/5}Y^{8/15}
 \le\eta X+\zeta Y+C\eta^{-6}\zeta^{-8}R^{15}.}       \tag{3.9}
\]
R-050 and R-063 supply every finite moment for their unshifted enhanced
currents, so the fifteenth moment in (3.9) is available.

\section{Why the shifted resonance cannot be separated}

Boundedness of $D^3M$ does not make multiplication by $H_t$ continuous on
$C^{-s}$.  The obstruction occurs in the actual production frame.  On the
active-real $\sigma_3$ radial line, its nonlinear column is
\[
 v(r)={2er\over r^2+e},\qquad
 v^{(4)}(\sqrt e)=-{6\over e^{3/2}}\ne0.                 \tag{5.1}
\]
Take on a one-dimensional spatial carrier
\[
 U=\sqrt e,\qquad A_N=\delta\cos(Nx),\qquad
 J_N=N^s\cos(Nx),                                       \tag{5.2}
\]
with $0<\delta\ll\sqrt e$, and fix $t\in(0,1]$.  Then
$\|J_N\|_{C^{-s}}\asymp1$, whereas
\[
 J_N\{v'''(U+tA_N)-v'''(U)\}                            \tag{5.3}
\]
has zero Fourier mode
$\tfrac12t v^{(4)}(\sqrt e)\delta N^s+O(t^2\delta^2N^s)$.
For each fixed $t>0$ this is comparable to $\delta N^s$ when $\delta$ is
small.  At $t=0$ the difference is identically zero, as it must be.  Hence no
$A$-uniform separated estimate for a nontrivial path time
\[
 \|K_tJ\|_{C^{-s}}\le C(\omega)                         \tag{5.4}
\]
can follow from $\|K_t\|_\infty$ and
$\|J\|_{C^{-s}}$.

Moreover $\|A_N\|_{H^2}\asymp\delta N^2$, so the separated multiplier
loss behaves like $\|A_N\|_{H^2}^{s/2}$.  Appending this loss to (3.5)
changes the total deterministic exponent from $(5+s)/6$ to
\[
 {5+s\over6}+{s\over4}={10+5s\over12}.                  \tag{5.5}
\]
At $s=3/5$, (5.5) is $13/12>1$.  The separated-multiplier architecture is
therefore supercritical even though the corrected bare payload is not.
This is a method counterexample, not a counterexample to the complete paired
endpoint (2.8).

Taylor iteration does not repair the problem.  Exactly,
\[
 E_3={1\over6}D^3M(U)[A,A,A]+E_4,                        \tag{5.6}
\]
\[

 D^4M(U+rA)[A,A,A,A],dr.                               \tag{5.7}
\]
The $C$-branch of (5.7) has the payload $A^4DA$; assigning one factor to
$H^2$ and four to $L^6$ already gives $1/2+4/6=7/6>1$ in spatial Holder.
Thus an infinite base-point Taylor expansion worsens the deterministic
budget.

The correct object to retain is the exact paired remainder
\[
 \mathcal E_3(U,A)=M(U+A)-M(U)-DM(U)[A]
                   -{1\over2}D^2M(U)[A,A].               \tag{5.8}

At fixed floor,
\[
 |\mathcal E_3(U,A)|\lesssim_e|A|^3,\qquad
 |D_A\mathcal E_3(U,A)|\lesssim_e|A|^2,                 \tag{5.9}

and, schematically,
\[
 \begin{aligned}

 \lesssim_e{}&(|A|^2+|A'|^2)|\delta A|\\
 &+(|A|^3+|A'|^3)|\delta U|.
 \end{aligned}                                           \tag{5.10}
\]
The explicit control-high part remains degree four.  The second line of
(5.10), in which rough $U$ enters an adapted shifted coefficient, is exactly
the missing paired enhancement rather than an extra deterministic power.

\section{Affine Bregman and path positivity are false shortcuts}

\section{The exact causal frontier}

The terminal shift cannot simply be frozen at every Gaussian root.  If
$g_1,g_2$ are successive fresh shells and the strict-past second control is
$a_2=f(g_1)$, then $a_2$ is admissible at shell two but the terminal shift
$A=a_2$ is not measurable before root one.  R-075's smooth infinite-Hermite
example makes this failure explicit.  A valid proof must assign every
monomial to its largest causal root rather than condition on the terminal
coefficient wholesale.

The largest-root audit suggests the following three candidate classes.  It
is a causal decomposition proposal; this note does not prove the uniform
root expansion, conditional moment, or shell summability for the first two
classes.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If a fresh Gaussian root is largest, all earlier and equal-shell
strict-past controls may be conditionally frozen at that root.  A successor
must still prove that the exact R-063
$\mathrm{P}_3/\mathrm{P}_1$ and
$\mathrm{P}_4/\mathrm{P}_2/\Sigma Q/\mathrm{P}_0$ compensators reassemble
and sum uniformly after this conditioning.

\item If a largest control pair lies among the explicit $A^3DA$ factors,
the candidate move is to charge that pair first to its own shell
Cameron--Martin energy and use the remaining positive slack.  The no-reuse
shell charge and the residual dyadic summation are not proved here.

\item The hard branch has its largest control roots only inside the shifted
coefficient $K_t$, while the explicit $A^3DA$ payload is lower.  Absolute
fractional multiplier estimates are incompatible with both shell summability
and Young slack for $s>1/2$.  This coefficient-only balanced-high branch must
remain signed and cancel against the terminal square, coefficient curvature,
R-063 lower chaoses, and trace transport.
\end{enumerate}

The smallest honest successor is therefore a three-class largest-root
proposition.  Its apparently hardest component is a
\emph{coefficient-only balanced-root signed Carleson lemma}: after the R-073
endpoint is reassembled exactly, the complete block containing (4.6), the
corresponding terminal-square polarization, the coefficient Wick forest,
R-066 trace, and finite-low term would have to obey
\[
 \mathbb E\mathcal B_J(A)
 \ge-\eta\,\mathbb EX-\zeta\,\mathbb EY-C_{\eta,\zeta}
                                                               \tag{7.1}
\]
uniformly in $J$.  The notation $\mathcal B_J$ refers to that complete
nonduplicating block, not to the isolated resonance.  Only after the
fresh-root, explicit-payload-high, and coefficient-only estimates are all
proved for regular strict-past controls may one invoke R-075 graph recovery.
The remaining R-066 reductions would then give controlled-shell one-use,
after which the already established $q=10/9$ equivalence returns the proof
to the Nelson synthesis.
