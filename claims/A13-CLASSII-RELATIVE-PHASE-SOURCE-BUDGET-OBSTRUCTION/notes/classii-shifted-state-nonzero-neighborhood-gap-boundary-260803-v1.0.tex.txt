\section*{1. Purpose and scope: the first nonzero shifted-state neighbourhood}
\hypertarget{section-1-result}{ }

R-155 proves, at the zero-control origin of a pure dyadic fixed-law affine
family and with $v=(H,G)$, the exact lower bound
\[
 D^2\cA(0)[v,v]>{147\over1000}
       (\|H\|_{\HS}^2+\|G\|_{\HS}^2).                    \tag{1.1}
\]
R-156 instantiates the smallest genuinely recursive predictable chart and
extends (1.1), by finite-dimensional continuity, to some nonzero coefficient
neighbourhood.  Let $\xi_p$ and $\xi_{2p}$ be independent whitened both-sign
raw roots and define
\[
 \chi(A,B)=\bigl(A\xi_p,\ B(\xi_{2p}+A\xi_p)\bigr).       \tag{1.2}
\]
The first component is revealed at $2p$ and the second at $4p$; hence
\[
 h_{2p}\in\mathcal F_p\quad\hbox{and}\quad
 h_{4p}\in\mathcal F_{2p}.                              \tag{1.3}
\]

At the registered fixed finite cutoff, positive floor, covariance-matched
common-even background and exact nonaliased torus integration, there exists
some $\delta>0$ such that
\[
 \|A\|_{\HS}+\|B\|_{\HS}<\delta                         \tag{1.4}
\]
implies, for every nonzero coefficient direction $v=(H,G)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_{A,B}[v,v]>
 {1\over10}(\|H\|_{\HS}^2+\|G\|_{\HS}^2).}             \tag{1.5}
\]
The corresponding intrinsic physical-source Hessian, restricted to the
tangent image of $D\chi_{A,B}$, also remains above $1/10$ in its induced
source Gram metric after possibly decreasing $\delta$.

The radius in (1.4) is existential.  R-156 supplies no numerical value and
no radius uniform in the cutoff, floor, refinement, chart, or realised
Gaussian past.  It does not prove global finite-amplitude convexity, arbitrary
predictable nonlinear/revisit feedback, T-050, A13, Nelson, an interacting
measure, any phase, lattice, vacuum, BCC, or PDE verdict, or Sector-A closure.

\section*{2. Exact shifted-state differential and source geometry}
\hypertarget{section-2-chart}{ }

For directions $v=(H,G)$ and $w=(K,L)$, direct differentiation of (1.2)
gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 k_v=D\chi_{A,B}v
 &=\bigl(H\xi_p,\ G\xi_{2p}+(GA+BH)\xi_p\bigr),\\
 D^2\chi_{A,B}[v,w]
 &=\bigl(0,(GK+LH)\xi_p\bigr).
\end{aligned}                                           \tag{2.1}
\]
The physical composition order is fixed.  Exchanging the two Hessian
arguments makes $GK+LH$ reappear; it does not replace either product by an
illegitimate label-swapped composition.

Whitened independence gives the intrinsic tangent Gram
\[
\boxed{
 \cG_{A,B}(v,w)=
 \ip{H}{K}_{\HS}+\ip{G}{L}_{\HS}
 +\ip{GA+BH}{LA+BK}_{\HS}.}                            \tag{2.2}
\]
In particular,
\[
 \cG_{A,B}(v,v)=
 \|H\|_{\HS}^2+\|G\|_{\HS}^2+\|GA+BH\|_{\HS}^2>0      \tag{2.3}
\]
for $v\ne0$.  There is no extra low variable in (2.1); all three summands
are coordinates of the same predictable source tangent.

The Cameron--Martin part of the scalar action is
\[
 \cC(A,B)={9\over20}
  \left(\|A\|_{\HS}^2+\|B\|_{\HS}^2+\|BA\|_{\HS}^2\right).           \tag{2.4}
\]
Its controller-coordinate Hessian is
\[
\boxed{
 D^2\cC(A,B)[v,w]={9\over10}\left[
 \cG_{A,B}(v,w)+\ip{BA}{GK+LH}_{\HS}\right].}          \tag{2.5}
\]
The second term in (2.5) is a gradient--chart-acceleration connection.  It
is absent from the intrinsic affine physical-source Hessian and vanishes at
$A=B=0$.  Thus the positive $9/10$ intrinsic source metric may not be copied
unchanged into a nonlinear controller-coordinate Hessian away from the
origin.

\section*{3. One production scalar, projected force, and all owners once}
\hypertarget{section-3-hessian}{ }

On a fixed predictable chart $\pi$, R-141 writes the complete production
action as one scalar
\[
 \cA_\pi(h)=\sum_\alpha\E\,\mathcal P_{{\rm comp},\alpha}(Z_h)
 +{9\over20}\|h\|_{\mathcal S_\pi}^2
 +{3\over20}\E\|Z_{K,h}\|_6^6.                         \tag{3.1}
\]
Its projected force and intrinsic source Hessian are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 g_\pi^{\rm prod}
 &= {9\over10}h+P_{\rm pred}L_\pi^*G_{\rm end},\\
 H_\pi(h)
 &= {9\over10}I+P_{\rm pred}L_\pi^*
       (Q_{\rm comp}+Q_6)L_\pi P_{\rm pred}.
\end{aligned}                                             \tag{3.2}
\]
Consequently the complete pullback Hessian is exactly
\[
\boxed{
 D^2(\cA_\pi\circ\chi)_{A,B}[v,w]
 =\ip{k_v}{H_\pi(h_{A,B})k_w}
 +\E\ip{(g_\pi^{\rm prod})_{4p}}{(GK+LH)\xi_p}.}         \tag{3.3}
\]
The second term is the unique controller-coordinate connection.  It already
contains the source, endpoint, sextic and trace first variations; adding
them again as separate reserves would double count the same scalar action.

For the intrinsic affine physical interpolation
\[
 h=h_{A,B}+t k_v+s k_w,                                  \tag{3.4}
\]
there is no chart acceleration, and (3.3) reduces to
\[
 D^2\cA_\pi(h_{A,B})[k_v,k_w]
 =\ip{k_v}{H_\pi(h_{A,B})k_w}.                           \tag{3.5}
\]
The production current still has its genuine internal second jet; it is not
controller curvature.  In the R-153 conditional notation $W=m+X$,
$n_i=\partial_i m$, $z=L_\pi k_v$, $w=L_\pi k_w$,
$u_{i,z}=\partial_i z$, the A7 block is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 K_E[z,w]=\int\sum_i\bigg[&
 u_{i,z}^{T}\overline B\,u_{i,w}
 +u_{i,z}^{T}\overline{DB[w]},n_i
 +u_{i,w}^{T}\overline{DB[z]},n_i\\
 &+{1\over2}\Tr\!\left(
  \overline{D^2B[z,w]}(n_in_i^T-\Gamma_{<,i})\right)
 \bigg]dx .
\end{aligned}}                                           \tag{3.6}
\]
The sixth-power contribution is the single global form
\[
 K_6[z,w]={9\over10}\int\E\left[
 |W|^4z\cdot w+4|W|^2(W\cdot z)(W\cdot w)\right]dx,       \tag{3.7}
\]
which is positive semidefinite on the diagonal.  Equations (3.2), (3.6),
and (3.7) are one source payment, one complete endpoint/current owner, and
one sextic owner.  Forward and reverse entries are adjoints of the same
bilinear Hessian.

\section*{4. Two-block acceptance and why no new low is allowed}
\hypertarget{section-4-schur}{ }

For the two affine source blocks, write the complete intrinsic Hessian and
source metric as $M$ and $\cG$.  The target is
\[
 N=M-{1\over10}\cG\succeq0\quad\hbox{and}\quad
 N=\begin{pmatrix}E&C\\C^*&F\end{pmatrix}.              \tag{4.1}
\]
The forward block is $C$; its sole legal reverse is $C^*$.  The balanced
term is the real symmetric cross $2\operatorname{Re}\ip{x}{Cy}$, not a
third reserve.

If $F>0$, exact block congruence gives
\[
 N\succeq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 F>0\quad\hbox{and}\quad E-CF^{-1}C^*\succeq0.        \tag{4.2}
\]
For semidefinite $F$, (4.2) requires the Douglas range condition and a
reduced solution of $F^{1/2}X=C^*$.  A free Schur minimisation is legitimate
only if the lower coordinate is an actual independent variable and its
stationary minimiser exists.  No such variable occurs in (1.2).  The
R-141 source-Doob low component is an isometric reparameterisation of $h$,
while historical mean, forest, future-variance and returned-low expressions
are owner decompositions already contained in $Q_{\rm comp}$.  R-156
therefore adds no independent low/Feshbach block.

\section*{5. Continuation proof and the exact global boundary}
\hypertarget{section-5-continuation}{ }

At fixed cutoff and positive floor, (3.1) is a smooth function on a finite
dimensional coefficient space, and (1.2) is polynomial.  Hence both
\[
 (A,B)\longmapsto D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_{A,B},\quad
 (A,B)\longmapsto\bigl(D\chi_{A,B}^*H_\pi D\chi_{A,B},
                         \cG_{A,B}\bigr)                    \tag{5.1}
\]
are continuous.  At $A=B=0$, the projected-force connection in (3.3)
vanishes by the R-155 stationary translation/Fourier cancellation, and
$\cG_{0,0}=I$.  The exact headroom over the desired target is
\[
 {147\over1000}-{1\over10}={47\over1000}.                 \tag{5.2}
\]
Continuity in operator norm therefore supplies a ball on which the change
is, for example, less than half of (5.2).  On that ball the retained lower
bound is at least
\[
 {147\over1000}-{47\over2000}={247\over2000}>{1\over10}.  \tag{5.3}
\]
This proves (1.5), after shrinking once more for the intrinsic generalised
eigenvalue statement.

The argument cannot be applied to the general factor-three R-155 family:
its registered origin gap is only
\[
 {7\over250}<{1\over10}.                                  \tag{5.4}
\]
Nor does compactness produce a usable uniform radius when the cutoff,
floor, chart, or dimension changes.  R-153 separately shows why a
deterministic fibrewise collar cannot be uniform over an unbounded
nondegenerate Gaussian past.

\section*{6. Nonlinear pullback curvature no-go}
\hypertarget{section-6-nogo}{ }

Take an aligned rank-one projection with
$\|H\|_{\HS}^2=\|G\|_{\HS}^2=\|GH\|_{\HS}^2=1$, and put
$A=tH$, $B=sG$.  Then
\[
 \cC(t,s)={9\over20}(t^2+s^2+t^2s^2),                    \tag{6.1}
\]
\[
 D^2\cC={9\over10}
 \begin{pmatrix}1+s^2&2ts\\2ts&1+t^2\end{pmatrix}.      \tag{6.2}
\]
On $t=s=R$, the tangent source Gram and controller Hessian have eigenvalues
\[
 \operatorname{spec}\cG_R=\{1,1+2R^2\},\quad
 \operatorname{spec}D^2\cC_R=
 \left\{{9\over10}(1-R^2),{9\over10}(1+3R^2)\right\}.   \tag{6.3}
\]
At $R=2$, these are respectively $\{1,9\}$ and
$\{-27/10,117/10\}$.  Thus even a convex Cameron--Martin norm becomes
indefinite in large nonlinear controller coordinates.  This is not a
counterexample to intrinsic production convexity or to T-050.  It falsifies
only the route that treats nonlinear pullback curvature as if it were the
intrinsic $9/10$ source reserve.
