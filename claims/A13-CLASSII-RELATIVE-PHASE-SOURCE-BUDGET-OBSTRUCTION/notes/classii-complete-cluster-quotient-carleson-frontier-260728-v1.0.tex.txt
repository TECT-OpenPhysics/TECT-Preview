% evidence-anchor: theorem-4.1-complete-endpoint-conditional-mean-covariance
\subsection*{Theorem 4.1 (complete endpoint conditional mean/covariance)}

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 \E[\Delta W_R\mid\mathcal H]
 ={}&{1\over2}(c+\mu)^TB_1(c+\mu)

\section{A nonvacuous statement of the remaining theorem}

\boxed{
 \sum_k\E P_k^{\rm comp}
 \ge-\eta\E\sum_k\|h_k\|^2

\]
Here $P_k^{\rm comp}$ contains complete heat, covariance trace, mixed

A determinant-compatible route must first form a legal contraction-closed

\begin{aligned}
 &\log\E\left[
 e^{-q(\mathfrak P_{j,\mathcal C}
       +\eta_{j,\mathcal C}\mathcal E_j
       +\zeta_{j,\mathcal C}\mathcal Y_{\operatorname{parent}(j)})}
 \mid\mathcal F_{j-1}\right]\\
 &\qquad\le {q^2\over4}
 \|S_{j,\mathcal C}^{\rm real}\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2
 +r_{j,\mathcal C}.
\end{aligned}                                        \tag{8.3}
\]
The data on the right must be legal at the conditioning step,
$r_{j,\mathcal C}\ge0$, and not chosen after observing the inequality's
deficit.  One unambiguously sufficient, though possibly stronger than
necessary, global specification is a pathwise or deterministic ledger

uniformly in the same four parameters, with

An equivalent conditional-supermartingale ledger is also admissible.  A
mere finite-cutoff assertion $\sum r<\infty$ is vacuous because $r$ could be
defined to equal the observed deficit.  Equations (8.3)--(8.5) are
\textbf{TARGETS, NOT THEOREMS}.

Proposition 6.1 fixes the order in (8.3): form the realized legal conditional
cluster covariance, square its HS norm, and only then take the outer
