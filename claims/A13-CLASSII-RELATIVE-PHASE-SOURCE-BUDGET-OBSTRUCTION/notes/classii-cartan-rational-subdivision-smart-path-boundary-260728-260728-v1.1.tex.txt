\section{Purpose and scope}

R-103 closed the complete regular $H_N$/REG packet, and R-104 proved that
the complete endpoint-owner algebra can be reconstructed without defect on
each temporally faithful fixed source chart.  The remaining analytic theorem
is nevertheless the uniform lower bound for the complete source action,
which R-093 identifies exactly with $\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$ and the
$q=10/9$ Nelson bound.  This note returns to the two historical R-085 atoms
and asks which parts survive representation-preserving subdivision.  It also
tests the most economical static continuation, the A9 smart path.

The outcome has four parts.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Complete common-root Cartan edges have an exact signed-first endpoint
      quotient.  Any two complete-heat subdivisions with the same extended
      endpoints give the same grouped square.  A closed extended loop gives
      zero grouped charge, even when the sum of edgewise squares is positive.
\item The historical rational form $F_{6.5}$ and the later fixed-chart
      coordinate $\mathcal K_R$ do not descend to the subdivision quotient.
      An exact production-fibre subdivision changes the sign of both.  The
      complete raw-Wick endpoint increment $\Delta W_R$ is unchanged because
      the labelled-owner defects cancel exactly.
\item Positive semidefiniteness, divergence freedom, quadratic coefficient
      growth, and sextic coercivity do not imply A9 smart-path monotonicity.
      A two-dimensional exact fixture has strictly negative first variation,
      and independent block copies amplify it linearly.
\item The natural all-finite-entropy pointwise relative-bracket theorem is a
      sufficient route to Nelson but is itself false.  One exact production
      top-shell ray and countably many translated Gaussian laws force its
      putative coefficient to satisfy $b(t)\ge3/t$.  Only a Gibbs-law-specific
      or time-integrated signed A9 estimate survives; invoking the complete
      source-action lower bound directly would simply assume Nelson.
\end{enumerate}

The first two results are finite-cutoff, fixed-positive-floor algebraic
statements in the A1 production frame.  The Cartan quotient uses one fixed
Malliavin root and the complete value-plus-heat edge.  The rational fixture
uses the exact active scalar production Gram and one common target heat.  The
A9 fixture is a structural method no-go, not a production counterexample.
No uniform $\mathrm{OVERLAP}_{\rm src}$ bound, Nelson estimate, cutoff or
floor removal, interacting measure, or Sector-A closure is claimed.  Tier
stays T4.

\section{Complete common-root Cartan edges}

Fix one Malliavin root $j$, one spatial output $m$, and one OU parameter
$t$.  Write
\[
 \mathcal L_{j,m,t}:=\Pi_mP_t^{(j)}\mathbb D_j.       \tag{2.1}
\]
The point is that $\mathcal L_{j,m,t}$ is linear and remains fixed during a
pure subdivision at that root.  Let
\[
  (\Sigma_0,z_0),(\Sigma_1,z_1),\ldots,(\Sigma_L,z_L) \tag{2.2}
\]
be a finite path in the extended value--heat state.  Its complete edge is
\[
\begin{split}
 E_\ell={}&J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_\ell)
             -J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_{\ell-1})\\
          &+J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_{\ell-1})
             -J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}(z_{\ell-1}).
\end{split}                                           \tag{2.3}
\]
The first line is the value edge.  The second is the heat compensator and is
part of the complete R-099 coordinate.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-2.1-complete-common-root-cartan-quotient
\subsection*{Theorem 2.1 (complete common-root endpoint quotient)}

Pathwise,
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\ell=1}^L\mathcal L_{j,m,t}E_\ell
 =\mathcal L_{j,m,t}
   \{J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0)\}.}         \tag{2.4}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{split}
 &\int_0^\infty e^{-2t}\,
   \mathbb E\left\|\sum_\ell\mathcal L_{j,m,t}E_\ell
              \right\|_{\rm HS}^2\,dt\\
 &\qquad=
 \int_0^\infty e^{-2t}\,
   \mathbb E\left\|\mathcal L_{j,m,t}
       [J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0)]
              \right\|_{\rm HS}^2\,dt.
\end{split}                                           \tag{2.5}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
The two lines of (2.3) cancel at their common middle value and give
\[
 E_\ell=J_{\Sigma_\ell}(z_\ell)
        -J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}(z_{\ell-1}).            \tag{2.6}
\]
Summation telescopes in the extended state.  Apply the fixed linear map
$\mathcal L_{j,m,t}$, then square only after summation.  Integration and
expectation preserve the exact identity. \hfill$\square$

Two consequences are immediate.  Every complete common-root partition with
the same extended endpoints has the same grouped energy.  If the endpoints
agree, its grouped charge and grouped energy are exactly zero.  In contrast,
\[
 \sum_\ell\|\mathcal L E_\ell\|^2                  \tag{2.7}
\]
can be strictly positive on a closed loop.  Thus (2.7) is a representation
artefact, not an invariant energy coordinate.  The executable uses a
nonlinear two-component current and an exact closed loop for which the
grouped square is zero and the edgewise square is $62132$.

\subsection*{Why the heat compensator is mandatory}

Keeping only the first line of (2.3) gives
\[
\begin{split}
 S_{\rm src}
  ={}&J_{\Sigma_L}(z_L)-J_{\Sigma_0}(z_0)\\
    &-\sum_\ell
      [J_{\Sigma_\ell}-J_{\Sigma_{\ell-1}}](z_{\ell-1}).
\end{split}                                           \tag{2.8}
\]
The last sum is a genuine defect.  The quotient theorem is therefore a
complete-heat theorem, not a value-edge theorem.

\section{Deterministic extraction boundaries}

The translation authority already rules out a cutoff-uniform pathwise
remainder.  Write $N$ for the physical sharp-cube Fourier radius (so

 \kappa=0.001248334393361145\ldots .                 \tag{7.1}
\]
Even using the entire R-104 source and sextic budgets

Its minimizer satisfies $t^4=d_N/(3\zeta)$ and

 =-{2\over3\sqrt{3\zeta}}L^3d_N^{3/2}
 =-{4\sqrt5\over9}L^3d_N^{3/2}\longrightarrow-\infty. \tag{7.3}

Since $X=0$ is Gaussian-null, this is a method no-go rather than a Nelson

\[
 CR E^{3/4}Y^{1/4}\le\eta E+\zeta Y+F(R)             \tag{7.4}

With the full budgets the threshold is exactly $3/5$.  A Gaussian current
norm is unbounded, so extracting it before the signed expectation cannot

\subsection*{Theorem 8.1 (uniform predictable-source bound for one pair)}

For every $q>0$ and every strict-past value of $h$,
\[
 \log\mathbb E_{\xi,\eta}e^{-qV}
 \le4q\mu+t-\log(1+t)
 \le4q\mu+{t^2\over2},\qquad t=2q\alpha.             \tag{8.5}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Since $S_h^2-4S_h=(S_h-2)^2-4\ge-4$,
\[
 -qV\le4q\mu+2q\alpha-q\alpha S_h.                 \tag{8.6}
\]
For $a\ge0$, completing the square in two independent Gaussians gives
\[
 \mathbb E e^{-a|\zeta+h|^2}
 ={1\over1+2a}
 \exp\left\{-{a|h|^2\over1+2a}\right\}
 \le{1\over1+2a}.                                   \tag{8.7}
\]
Take $a=q\alpha$.  Finally,
$t-\log(1+t)\le t^2/2$ because the difference is zero at $t=0$ and has
derivative $t^2/(1+t)\ge0$. \hfill$\square$

For production covariance $\sigma_k^2\lesssim\langle k\rangle^{-4}$ in

\[
 \sum_k\mu_k\lesssim\sum_k\langle k\rangle^{-6}<\infty,
