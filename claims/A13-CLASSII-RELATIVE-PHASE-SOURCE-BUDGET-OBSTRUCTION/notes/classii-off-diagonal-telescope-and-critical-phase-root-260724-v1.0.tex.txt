The reconstruction yields a second exact fact.  The apparent terminal

result is a projector-free terminal current square.  This is an algebraic

same adapted terminal coercivity problem isolated by R-069 and R-070.

The other possible route is audited as well.  Within the declared termwise
absolute largest-shell architecture at the raw R-050 root order, the two
value-high branches are exactly critical.  One branch requires
$\theta>1/4$ for shell decay and $\theta<1/4$ for probability slack; the
other requires simultaneously $\theta>1/2$ and $\theta<1/2$.  The endpoint
has neither decay nor a finite random Young exponent.  This is a no-go only
for that narrowly specified proof architecture.  It is not a counterexample
to A13 and not a no-go for signed cancellation, multilinear null structure,
or an adapted root with positive causal gain.

All statements use the hash-pinned A1 production coefficients, the R-066

\section{Projector-free completion and the phase kernel}

It is useful to see precisely why the terminal square appeared blind in
R-071.  Put
\[
 \mathfrak P_{\rm mix}(C)
 =\langle w_0,QE_{\rm tot}^TC\rangle
  +\langle\Delta w,Qc_T\rangle+{1\over2}|c_T|_Q^2.       \tag{5.1}
\]
If $B_x=\sum_rM_r(x)QM_r(x)^T$, then the same tensor is
\[
 \mathfrak P_{\rm mix}(C)
 =C^T\{[B_T-B_0]G-DM_0[A]QM_0^TG\}
  +{1\over2}C^TB_TC.                                    \tag{5.2}
\]
The terms $M_TQ\Delta w$ and $B_T$ in (5.2) are visible to the terminal
analysis map $T_TC=(M_{r,T}^TC)_r$.  After $S_C$ and $L$ have been split
away, the square-invisible quotient is
\[
 (P_{\ker T_T}C)\cdot
 \sum_rE_{r,{\rm tot}}Qw_{r,0}.                          \tag{5.3}
\]
It is irreducible to the terminal square alone, but it is not irreducible in
the full signed energy.

Indeed, ordinary algebra gives on every rank stratum
\[
 \boxed{
 S_C+L+\mathfrak P_{\rm mix}(C)
 =\langle w_T,Qc_T\rangle+{1\over2}|c_T|_Q^2.}           \tag{5.4}
\]
Therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 {1\over2}|w_T|_Q^2+S_C+L+\mathfrak P_{\rm mix}(C)
 ={1\over2}|w_T+c_T|_Q^2.}                              \tag{5.5}
\]
No projector or pseudoinverse occurs.  In particular, if $C\in\ker T_T$,
then
\[
 S_C+L+\langle w_0,QE_{\rm tot}^TC\rangle=0.            \tag{5.6}
\]
The apparent kernel obstruction is created by separating terms which the
full Taylor identity $M_0+DM_0[A]+E_{\rm tot}=M_T$ couples exactly.

For completeness, write a terminal value as $x=z+A=(u,s)$ and set
\[
 n_D=J_Dx,\qquad n_S=J_Sx.                               \tag{5.7}
\]
The exact Euclidean kernel projector is
\[
 P_{\ker T_x}=
 \begin{cases}
 {n_Dn_D^T\over|u|^2}+{n_Sn_S^T\over|s|^2},
      &u\ne0,\ s\ne0,\\[3pt]
 {n_Dn_D^T\over|u|^2}+P_S,&u\ne0,\ s=0,\\[3pt]

 \end{cases}                                             \tag{5.8}
\]
Its ranks are $2$, $3$, and $6$, so a smooth fixed-rank inverse cannot
replace (5.5).

There is also an explicit formula for the nonlinear vector in (5.3).  Write
\[
 M_r=[p_r,v_r],\quad
 \ell_r=Dq_r(z)[A],\quad
 \epsilon_r=q_r(x)-q_r(z)-\ell_r,                        \tag{5.9}

\[
 \beta_r=e_2^TQw_{r,0},\qquad

Since $p_r$ is linear, the following identities are exact:
\[
 E_r^p=0,\qquad E_r^v=-2(\epsilon_rx+\ell_rA),\qquad
 k:=\sum_rE_rQw_{r,0}=-2(\mathcal E_\beta x+\mathcal L_\beta A).         \tag{5.11}

On the generic stratum this gives
\[
 (P_{\ker T_x}C)\cdot k
 =-2\mathcal L_\beta\left\{
 { (C\cdot n_D)(A\cdot n_D)\over|u|^2}
 +{ (C\cdot n_S)(A\cdot n_S)\over|s|^2}\right\}.       \tag{5.12}
\]
At $s=0$, the second summand in braces is replaced by
$(P_SC)\cdot(P_SA)$.  At $u=0$, the whole target is the kernel and one uses

show that no division by a small field is needed in the complete energy.

For either genuine phase line, R-072 differentiation gives
\[

\section{Scoped largest-shell absolute-route audit}

Let
\[
 x_j=\|h_j\|_{\mathcal H_j}^2,\qquad
 y_j=\|a_j\|_6^6,\qquad N_j=2^j.                          \tag{6.1}
\]
The declared raw estimates are
\[
 \|b_j\|_2\lesssim N_j^{-1}x_j^{1/2},                   \tag{6.2}
\]
\[
 \|a_j\|_6\lesssim
 N_j^{-\theta}x_j^{\theta/2}y_j^{(1-\theta)/6},
 \qquad0\le\theta\le1.                                 \tag{6.3}
\]
After expanding a term and choosing its largest control index $r$, exact
strict-past causalization gives a normalized Gaussian factor
\[
 \|G_r\|_6\lesssim N_r^{1/2}\mathfrak g_r,               \tag{6.4}
\]
with every fixed finite moment.  The base-current difference family has an
extra $N_j^{-1/2}$ and closes with budgets $X^{1/2}Y^{1/3}$ and a sixth
moment.  Comparable-index and derivative-high parts of the other two
families also retain negative dyadic powers.  Two value-high orientations
are different.

For $\mathcal O_2$ with $j\gg\ell$, (6.2)--(6.4) give the dyadic weight
\[
 N_j^{1/2-2\theta}N_\ell^{-1}.                            \tag{6.5}
\]
Decay in the largest shell requires $\theta>1/4$.  The total deterministic
budget exponent is
\[
 \alpha_2=\theta+{1\over2}+{1-\theta\over3}
 ={5\over6}+{2\theta\over3}.                             \tag{6.6}
\]
A finite random Young exponent requires $\alpha_2<1$, or
$\theta<1/4$.  At $\theta=1/4$, (6.5) has no separation decay and
$\alpha_2=1$.

For $\mathcal O_3$ with $j\gg k,\ell$, the weight is
\[
 N_j^{1/2-\theta}N_\ell^{-1},                             \tag{6.7}
\]
which requires $\theta>1/2$.  Its deterministic exponent is
\[
 \alpha_3={1\over2}+{\theta\over2}
           +{1-\theta\over6}+{1\over6}
 ={5\over6}+{\theta\over3},                              \tag{6.8}
\]
so random slack requires $\theta<1/2$.  Again the endpoint has zero decay
and $\alpha_3=1$.

This is a formal obstruction to the stated termwise absolute two-budget
Young architecture.  At $\alpha=1$, scaling both abstract positive budgets
by a common factor leaves the unbounded random multiplier in the leading
coefficient, while every additive random remainder is lower order in that
scale.  Moving $\theta$ to either side fixes one condition and violates the
other.  This argument does not exclude cancellations unavailable after
absolute values, and it is not an integrated production counterexample.

A separated controlled finite-low factor produces the same endpoint warning:
the schematic budget is $X^{1/2}Y^{1/2}$.  It must remain in the signed
finite-low telescope rather than be assigned a second absolute payment.
