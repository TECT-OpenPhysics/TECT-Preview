\subsection*{Theorem 3.1 (exact affine-Gaussian raw owner)}

With indices $a,b$ in field space and $m,n$ in current space,
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 2\E\mathcal R_{\rm raw}
 &=\E(\|J\|^2-\Theta)\\
 &=\E\{v_mB_{mn}v_n
   +2v_mK_{an}\partial_aB_{mn}
   +K_{am}K_{bn}\partial_{ab}B_{mn}\}(W).
\end{aligned}}                                         \tag{3.6}

\[
 \boxed{

 =\E[K_{am}K_{bn}\partial_{ab}B_{mn}(W)].}             \tag{3.7}
\]

\textit{Proof.}
Write $A_i(W)=\mathrm C(W)Ye_i$.  Then
\[
 \|J\|^2-\Theta
 =\|\mathrm C(W)v\|^2
 +2\sum_i g_i\ip{\mathrm C(W)v}{A_i(W)}
 +\sum_{i,j}(g_ig_j-\delta_{ij})\ip{A_i(W)}{A_j(W)}.
                                                                  \tag{3.8}
\]
One Gaussian integration by parts in the middle term and two in the last
term give (3.6).  The contractions are
$\sum_iX_{ai}Y_{ni}=K_{an}$. \hfill$\square$

For covariance scale $\varepsilon$, replace $X,Y$ by
$\sqrt\varepsilon X,\sqrt\varepsilon Y$.  With $v=0$,
\[
