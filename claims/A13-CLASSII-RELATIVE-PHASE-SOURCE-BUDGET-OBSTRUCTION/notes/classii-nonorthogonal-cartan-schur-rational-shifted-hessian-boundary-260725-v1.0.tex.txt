\section{Nonorthogonal weighted causal Schur lemma}

\subsection*{Theorem 4.1}

Let $u_{j,k}$ be Hilbert-valued atoms for $k\le j$ and set

\[
 f_j=\sum_{k\le j}u_{j,k}.
 \tag{4.1}
\]

Assume that for output shells $m\ge j+C$,

\[
 \mathbb E\|\Pi_m u_{j,k}\|^2
 \le 2^{-2s(m-k)}q_k,\qquad s>{1\over2}.
 \tag{4.2}
\]

No orthogonality between the $u_{j,k}$ is assumed.  For every
$0<\eta<2s-1$,

\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_m\sum_{j\le m-C}2^j\mathbb E\|\Pi_mf_j\|^2
 \le C_{s,\eta}2^{-2sC}\sum_k2^kq_k,}
 \tag{4.3}
\]

where

\[
 \boxed{
 C_{s,\eta}=
 {1\over
 (1-2^{-\eta})(1-2^{-2s})(1-2^{1+\eta-2s})}.}
 \tag{4.4}
\]

\emph{Proof.}
Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
 \left\|\sum_{k\le j}\Pi_mu_{j,k}\right\|^2
 \le {1\over1-2^{-\eta}}
 \sum_{k\le j}2^{\eta(j-k)}\|\Pi_mu_{j,k}\|^2.
 \tag{4.5}
\]

Insert (4.2), use nonnegativity to exchange the three sums, and write
$j=k+r$:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sum_m\sum_{j\le m-C}2^j\mathbb E\|\Pi_mf_j\|^2\\
 &\quad\le {1\over1-2^{-\eta}}
 \sum_kq_k\sum_{r\ge0}2^{k+r}2^{\eta r}
 \sum_{m\ge k+r+C}2^{-2s(m-k)}\\
 &\quad={2^{-2sC}\over
 (1-2^{-\eta})(1-2^{-2s})}
 \sum_k2^kq_k\sum_{r\ge0}2^{(1+\eta-2s)r}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{4.6}
\]

The last series converges exactly when $\eta<2s-1$, proving (4.3).
\hfill$\square$

For fixed $s$, the two factors containing $\eta$ are balanced at

\[
 \eta={2s-1\over2}.
 \tag{4.7}
\]

Taking (1.1) gives

\[
 C_{7/12,1/12}=572.4472106721531\ldots,\qquad
 2^{-2sC}=2^{-7C/6}.
 \tag{4.8}
\]

The executable checks four aligned worst-direction fixtures and four
independent changing-sign vector fixtures against (4.3).

\[

\emph{Proof.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DISMISSED: Theorem 4.1 secretly assumes orthogonality.}
Equation (4.5) is weighted Cauchy--Schwarz and retains every cross term
through a deterministic majorant.  No probability or output orthogonality is

\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST FIVE-TERM CLOSURE: bounded $D^2B_R$ permits

and depends on the shifted random field.  Equations (7.2)--(7.3) show the

\item \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION: the rational fixture disproves every

preserves cost but not the disjoint physical ranges used by regular estimates.

\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST ONE-USE/NELSON CLOSURE.}  Neither (8.1) nor its
admissible-core extension is proved.  Boue--Dupuis cannot provide the missing
lower bound without circularity.
\end{enumerate}
