\section{The current frame and its exact jets}

$P_D$ denotes the active-doublet projector, then $S_A^2=P_D$.  For a
positive floor $e$ put
\[
 d_e(z)=|z|^2+e,\qquad
 q_A(z)={z^TS_Az\over d_e(z)},\qquad

\]
and introduce the two-column current frame
\[
 M_A(z)=\bigl[\,2S_Az,\ 2r_A(z)\,\bigr].                \tag{1.2}
\]
For a derivative vector $y\in\R^6$, the corresponding two-current is
$M_A(z)^Ty$.  Since
\[

one has the floor-uniform frame bound
\[

Direct differentiation gives
\[
 Dq_A(z)[h]={2r_A(z)\cdot h\over d_e(z)},                \tag{1.5}

\[
 D^2q_A(z)[h,k]
 ={2h\cdot(S_A-q_A\Id)k\over d_e}
 -{4(r_A\cdot k)(z\cdot h)\over d_e^2}

\]
Writing $s_e(z)=\sqrt{d_e(z)}$, the elementary bounds
$|q_A|\le1$, $|r_A|\le|z|\le s_e$, and
$\|S_A-q_A\Id\|_{\rm op}\le2$ yield

 \boxed{\ \|DM_A(z)[h]\|_F\le\sqrt{68}|h|,\qquad

\section{Why the affine full-score tangent fails}

\]

\[
 {1\over2}b^TB(z+a)b+b^T[B(z+a)-B(z)]y.                  \tag{2.2}

Because $B(z+a)$ is merely positive semidefinite, (2.2) is bounded below in

\[
 [B(z+a)-B(z)]y\in\Ran B(z+a).                           \tag{2.3}

directions which rotate with $z$.

\[

The crucial point is that $E_A$ is independent of $b$.  With
$\langle u,Qv\rangle_A=\sum_Au_A^TQv_A$ and

\[

\[
 \cT_{\rm end}=\langle w,QL\rangle_A,

The object $L$ is a hybrid endpoint lift, not the Fr\'echet derivative at the
old endpoint: its $a$ column is differentiated at $z$, while its $b$ column
