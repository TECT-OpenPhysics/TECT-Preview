\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
\hypertarget{section-1-result}{ }

production principal fibre.  This note closes that annulus analytically.  On
the fixed side-16 torus retain one strict-past antipodal pair at $p$, one at
$2p$, and one fresh final pair at $4p$, with unit retained regulator
multipliers.  Let
\[
 g=(g_p,g_{2p})\in\mathbb R^{24},\quad G=|g|,
 \qquad \alpha\in\mathbb R^{12}.                    \tag{1.1}

Hessian, the new bound is

 \geq \widetilde P(G)|\alpha|^2},                  \tag{1.2}

\section*{2. Exact radial-Gram decomposition}
\hypertarget{section-2-decomposition}{ }

Let $P$ be the real orthogonal projector onto the complex doublet.  For a
six-real field $w$, set
\[
 d=|w|^2+e,\qquad \lambda={|Pw|^2\over d},
 \qquad b=2Pw,\qquad a=2(Pw-\alpha_{\rm rad}\lambda w),
 \qquad \alpha_{\rm rad}={5\over9}.                \tag{2.1}
\]
If $B_J$ is the three-row Pauli-current Gram and $B_L$ the conormal Gram, the
R-130 Fierz identity gives the exact matrix equality
\[
 B_L=B_J+aa^T-bb^T.                                 \tag{2.2}
\]
The A1 coefficients are
\[
 c_0={3\over250P_X},\qquad
 c_1={243\over8000P_X},\qquad P_X=4+10^{-12},       \tag{2.3}
\]
and hence
\[
 \boxed{B=(c_0+c_1)B_J+c_1C,\qquad C=aa^T-bb^T.}   \tag{2.4}
\]
This isolates the only rational radial correction instead of applying the
R-130 absolute derivative envelope to all six rows.

\section*{3. Exact Pauli resonance cancellation}
\hypertarget{section-3-pauli}{ }

Condition on the strict past and write
\[
 W=m+X,\qquad n=\partial m,\qquad z=S_{4p}\alpha,
 \qquad u=\partial z.                               \tag{3.1}
\]
For every real symmetric embedded Pauli generator $S_A$, put
\[
 J_A(t)=2(W+tz)^TS_A(n+tu).                         \tag{3.2}
\]
Then
\[
 J_A''(0)=4z^TS_Au=2\partial(z^TS_Az).              \tag{3.3}
\]
After the centered fresh expectation, $\E J_A(0)=2m^TS_An$ has phase
frequencies of magnitude at most four.  Equation (3.3) has only frequencies
$\pm8$: its zero coefficient vanishes because it is a periodic derivative.
Consequently
\[
 \int\E[J_A(0)J_A''(0)]\dd x=0.                    \tag{3.4}
\]
The remaining current contribution is $\int\E|J_A'(0)|^2\dd x\geq0$.
The unaveraged $Xn$ term also cannot resonate: its frequencies have magnitude
two, three, five, or six.  The strict-past trace debit is not discarded; it
is paid separately in Section 6.  Thus (3.4) is a current cancellation, not
a claim that the complete Pauli owner is positive.

\section*{4. Floor-uniform radial correction bounds}
\hypertarget{section-4-radial}{ }

Put $R=(P-\lambda\Id)w$.  Direct differentiation gives
\[
 \nabla\lambda={2R\over d},\qquad
 D^2\lambda={2(P-\lambda\Id)\over d}
 -{4(wR^T+Rw^T)\over d^2}.                          \tag{4.1}
\]
Writing $x=|Pw|^2/d$ and $\delta=e/d$ shows
\[
 {|R|^2\over d}=x(1-x)-x^2\delta\leq x(1-x)\leq{1\over4},
 \qquad {|w|^2\over d}\leq1.                      \tag{4.2}
\]
Therefore
\[
 |w||\nabla\lambda|\leq1,\qquad
 |w|^2\|D^2\lambda\|_{\op}\leq6.                 \tag{4.3}
\]
The apparent division by $|w|$ below is removable at $w=0$.  Equations
(2.1) and (4.3) imply
\[
 |a|,|b|\leq2|w|,\quad
 \|Da\|\leq{38\over9},\quad \|Db\|\leq2,\quad
 |D^2a[z,z]|\leq{80\over9}{|z|^2\over|w|}.         \tag{4.4}
\]
For every real symmetric $Q$ this yields
\[
 |C:Q|\leq8\|Q\|_{\op}|w|^2,                      \tag{4.5}
\]
\[
 |D(C:Q)[z]|\leq{224\over9}\|Q\|_{\op}|w||z|,    \tag{4.6}
\]
\[
 \left|{1\over2}D^2(C:Q)[z,z]\right|
 \leq{3208\over81}\|Q\|_{\op}|z|^2.             \tag{4.7}
\]
Indeed the three terms in (4.7) are
$1444/81$, $1440/81$, and $324/81$.  Since
$c_0<3/1000$ and $c_1<243/32000$, the outward rational constants are
\[

\section*{5. Direct harmonic coercivity}
\hypertarget{section-5-harmonic}{ }

The R-165 factor $5/6$ is sharp relative to
$\langle|m|^4\rangle\langle|z|^2\rangle$, but that product is not the
quantity needed for the source-coordinate lower bound.  A stronger direct
coefficient estimate is available.  Put $r=|m|^2$, $f=r^2$, and $q=|z|^2$.
Then $q$ has only modes $0,\pm8$, $f_8=r_4^2$, and positivity gives
$q_0\geq2|q_8|$.  Parseval gives
\[
 f_0-|f_8|
 =r_0^2+2\sum_{k=1}^3|r_k|^2+|r_4|^2\geq r_0^2.   \tag{5.1}
\]
Hence
\[
 \boxed{\langle|m|^4|z|^2\rangle
 \geq\langle|m|^2\rangle^2\langle|z|^2\rangle.}  \tag{5.2}
\]
This does not contradict the sharp $5/6$ result, because
$\langle|m|^4\rangle\geq\langle|m|^2\rangle^2$ can have strict slack.
Centered Gaussian fourth-moment expansion only increases the retained term,
so the sixth Hessian supplies
\[
 A={9\over10}{(100/63)^2(20/91)\over4096^2}
 ={625\over21040201728}.                            \tag{5.3}
\]
The other sixth-Hessian square is left unused.

\section*{7. Exact global minimum certificate}
\hypertarget{section-7-minimum}{ }

For $\widetilde P$ in (1.3), exact arithmetic gives
\[
 \widetilde P'(70)=-{1360786403\over1277632512000}<0,
 \qquad
 \widetilde P'(71)={97738714417\over876455903232000}>0,\tag{7.1}
\]
and
\[
 \widetilde P''(70)={6865745\over5962285056}>0.     \tag{7.2}
\]
Since $\widetilde P''$ has exactly one positive zero and is increasing
after it, $\widetilde P'$ has exactly one nonnegative zero $G_*$, with
$70<G_*<71$.  Convexity on that interval gives the rational lower bound
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widetilde P(G_*)
 &\geq \widetilde P(70)+\widetilde P'(70)\\
 &=-{332863942666997\over439505584128000}.
\end{aligned}                                       \tag{7.3}
\]
Its margin above the target is
\[
 {9\over10}+\widetilde P(G_*)
 \geq {62691083048203\over439505584128000}>0.       \tag{7.4}
\]
This proves (1.4) on the full radial half-line and closes the R-165 annulus
without numerical quadrature, interval subdivision, or an exact Gaussian
matrix evaluation.

\section*{8. Route decision and Devil's-advocate review}
\hypertarget{section-8-review}{ }

The useful lesson is methodological.  A direct high-dimensional interval
attack on the rational Gaussian $12\times12$ matrix was a valid fallback,
but it hid the exact Pauli resonance cancellation and the one-row radial
defect.  That brute-force route is now parked for this fibre.  It remains a
possible falsifier for future fibres lacking the same support algebra.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: the new harmonic coefficient contradicts the sharp
$5/6$ fixture. DISMISSED.}  The two inequalities have different right-hand
sides.  Equation (5.2) is proved coefficient by coefficient and is weaker
than replacing its right side by
$\langle|m|^4\rangle\langle|z|^2\rangle$.

\item \textbf{Objection: the Pauli trace was silently discarded. DISMISSED.}
Only the current resonance vanishes.  The full strict-past trace is included
in $H_{\rm tr}$ and $D_{\rm trace}$.

\item \textbf{Objection: the positive floor makes (4.3) singular at the
origin. DISMISSED.}  The bounds follow with $e/d\geq0$, and $C$ and its
displayed products extend continuously at $w=0$.

\item \textbf{Objection: a finite floating search proves the result.
UPHELD against that inference.}  No sampled value enters (1.3) or (7.4).
The certificate is rational and analytic.

\item \textbf{Objection: this closes T-050. UPHELD against that inference.}
Only one sparse principal fibre is closed.  Other past harmonics, final roots,
cross-root blocks, low variables, random/nonlinear/revisit feedback, and
removal remain open.

\item \textbf{Objection: success selects BCC or validates the candidate PDE.
UPHELD against that inference.}  No morphology is assumed.  This is a scoped
mathematical test of one candidate action, not a physical-vacuum result.
\end{enumerate}
