\paragraph{Proof.}

\[

 =\operatorname{Re}\langle\Phi_h+\Phi_0,
                          \Phi_h-\Phi_0\rangle
 =2\operatorname{Re}\langle\Phi_0,\Delta\Phi\rangle
  +\|\Delta\Phi\|^2.                                \tag{2.7}
\]
Subtract (2.3) at $u=0$ from (2.3) at $u=h$ and use (2.7).
No derivative of $h$ is taken. \hfill$\square$

The symmetric quantity
\[

       \langle\Phi_h+\Phi_0,\Delta\Phi\rangle        \tag{2.8}
