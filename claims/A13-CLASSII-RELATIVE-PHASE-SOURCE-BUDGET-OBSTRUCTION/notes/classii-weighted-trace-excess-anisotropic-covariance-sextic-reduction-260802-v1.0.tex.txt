\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
\hypertarget{section-1-result}{ }

R-143 writes the owner-complete fixed-chart augmented action as
\[
 \cY_\pi(h)=\Phi_\pi(h)
 \oplus\sqrt{9\over10}\,h
 \oplus\sqrt{3\over10}\,|Z_{K,h}|^2Z_{K,h},
 \qquad
 \cA_\pi(h)=a_\pi+{1\over2}
 \{\|\cY_\pi(h)\|^2-\|\cU_\pi(h)\|^2\}.            \tag{1.1}
\]
R-144 then gives two sufficient routes: a global common-output contraction
with a bounded residual, or a positive reduced/source-low Hessian gap with
an origin-force bound and an absolute anchor.  Neither production input is
registered.

This note takes a weaker, pointwise route.  Define the complete trace excess
\[
 \cT_\pi(h)={1\over2}
 \{\|\cU_\pi(h)\|^2-\|\Phi_\pi(h)\|^2\}.             \tag{1.2}
\]
The first result is an exact equivalence at the level needed by T-050: it is
enough to bound \(\cT_\pi\) by the source and sextic budgets.  No common
linear map on a family of endpoints is required.

The second result is deliberately narrower.  The terminal A1 total
covariance has an exact scalar-principal plus positive anisotropic split.
The complete spatial derivative trace of the anisotropic terminal remainder
is trace class, has an explicit cutoff-independent lattice bound, and obeys
a terminal trace-owner sextic estimate with coefficient \(1/100\) and
certified constant at most 24 when the regulator bound is one.

The terminal lemma is not promoted through an arbitrary temporally faithful
future-increment partition.  R-129 proves that a total Fourier multiplier
does not by itself intertwine each temporal covariance increment, and R-143
records the still missing output, reveal, collar, and low propagation.  Thus
the production temporal anisotropic owner, scalar principal sea, complete
low/anchor owner, T-050, A13, Nelson estimate, removal, interacting measure,
and Sector-A closure all remain open.

\section*{2. Exact direct trace-excess criterion}
\hypertarget{section-2-weighted-trace}{ }

Put
\[
 X(h)=\E\sum_k\|h_k\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 Y_6(h)=\E\|Z_{K,h}\|_6^6.                            \tag{2.1}
\]
The norm convention in (1.1) gives
\[
 {1\over2}\|\cY_\pi(h)\|^2
 ={1\over2}\|\Phi_\pi(h)\|^2
 +{9\over20}X(h)+{3\over20}Y_6(h).                   \tag{2.2}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \cA_\pi(h)=a_\pi+{9\over20}X(h)+{3\over20}Y_6(h)
 -\cT_\pi(h).                                        \tag{2.3}
\]
The controlled renormalised potential is obtained by subtracting the two
one-use stabilisers already inserted in the augmented action:
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \E V_J^{\rm ren}(Z_h)=a_\pi-\cT_\pi(h).
 \quad}                                               \tag{2.4}
\]
This cancellation is why the \(9/20\) and \(3/20\) coordinates do not shift
the direct T-050 trace-excess thresholds.

% evidence-anchor: theorem-2.1-weighted-trace-excess
\textbf{Theorem 2.1 (direct weighted trace-excess criterion).}
Assume, uniformly in cutoff, admissible temporally faithful chart, floor,
and control, that
\[
 a_\pi\geq-C_0,
 \qquad
 \cT_\pi(h)\leq\eta X(h)+\zeta Y_6(h)+B              \tag{2.5}
\]
for finite \(B,C_0\), with
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \eta<{5\over11}={1\over2p},
 \qquad
 \zeta<{27\over100}={\gamma\over6},
 \qquad p={11\over10}.
 \qquad}                                              \tag{2.6}
\]
Then T-050 holds with \(\epsilon_v=\eta\),
\(\epsilon_6=\zeta\), and \(C=B+C_0\).

\textit{Proof.}
Equations (2.4)--(2.5) give directly
\[
 \E V_J^{\rm ren}(Z_h)
 \geq-\eta X(h)-\zeta Y_6(h)-(B+C_0).                 \tag{2.7}
\]
The strict inequalities (2.6) are exactly the two canonical T-050
acceptance conditions. \hfill\(\square\)

This theorem is weaker than demanding one common contraction
\(\cU=C\cY\) on a whole endpoint family.  For example, take two paid
endpoint vectors with Gram \(G_{\rm paid}=\Id_2\) and an adverse Gram
\[
 G_U={9\over16}
 \begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.               \tag{2.8}
\]
Each diagonal endpoint has adverse squared norm \(9/16<1\), but
\(G_U\) has largest eigenvalue \(9/8>1\), and
\(G_{\rm paid}-G_U\) has eigenvalue \(-1/8\).  Thus endpointwise weighted
control may hold although the stronger mixed-Gram contraction fails.
The fixture does not prove the production inequality (2.5); it proves only
that failure of a common contraction is not a logical obstruction to it.

\section*{4. Complete spatial lattice trace bound}

On the \(L=16\) torus write \(k=(\pi/8)n\) and
\(m=\|n\|_\infty\).  Direct summation gives
\[
 \sum_{\|n\|_\infty\leq r}|n|^2
 =r(r+1)(2r+1)^3,                                    \tag{4.1}

hence the exact shell identity
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \sum_{\|n\|_\infty=m}|n|^2=40m^4+14m^2.
 \quad}                                               \tag{4.2}

The radius-two cube has squared-radius sum 750.

Use the rational enclosure \(3<\pi<22/7\).  On the low cube,
\(a(k)>1/4\), so (3.3), (3.6), and (4.1) give the unnormalised complex
Fourier-sum bound
\[
 G_{R,{\rm low}}^{\mathbb C}
 \leq M_\chi^2{221\over1000}\,16\,750
 \left({11\over28}\right)^2
 =M_\chi^2{80223\over196}.                           \tag{4.3}

For \(m\geq3\), the exact A1 value satisfies \(q_0/(\pi/8)<2\).  Therefore
\[
 a(k)\geq c^2|k|^4,
 \qquad c=1-{4\over3^2}={5\over9}.                  \tag{4.4}

The integral test gives
\[
 \sum_{m\geq3}m^{-4}\leq{2\over81},
 \qquad
 \sum_{m\geq3}m^{-6}\leq{8\over3645}.              \tag{4.5}

Combining (3.3), (3.6), and (4.2)--(4.5) yields
\[

Thus
\[

 {7807039549231\over6201562500}.
 \quad}                                               \tag{4.7}

Equation (4.7) is an unnormalised complex Fourier sum, not yet the physical

\[

\section*{5. Terminal anisotropic trace-owner lemma}
\hypertarget{section-5-terminal-young}{ }

This section states exactly what (4.8) proves and no more.  Let
\(\Gamma_R\) be a translation-invariant terminal derivative covariance with
pointwise six-real trace at most \(g_R^{(6)}\).  Let \(P\) be an orthogonal
output projection and let the registered Pauli--Fierz coordinate obey
\[
 0\preceq C_6(z)^*P C_6(z)
 \preceq\beta_{\rm op}|z|^2\Id.                      \tag{5.1}
\]
From the A1 coefficients,
\[
 \beta_{\rm op}
 ={169500000000\over4000000000001}
 <{339\over8000}.                                    \tag{5.2}
\]
The coefficient Gram determinant is positive, and (5.1) follows from the
Pauli operator norms and the triangle bound on the three coefficient rows.

For a full terminal random field \(Z_h\), define the abstract terminal
anisotropic trace owner
\[
 T_{\rm an}(Z_h)
 ={1\over2}\E\int
 \Tr\{P C_6(Z_h)\Gamma_R C_6(Z_h)^*P\}\,dx.          \tag{5.3}
\]

% evidence-anchor: theorem-5.1-terminal-anisotropic-sextic
\textbf{Theorem 5.1 (terminal anisotropic sextic payment).}
Under (4.8) and (5.1),
\[
 T_{\rm an}(Z_h)
 \leq A_R\,Y_6(h)^{1/3},
 \qquad
 A_R={\beta_{\rm op}\over2}g_R^{(6)}V^{2/3}.         \tag{5.4}
\]
At \(M_\chi=1\), the safe coefficient is
\[
 A_R\leq{882195469063103\over264600000000000}
 =3.334072\ldots.                                    \tag{5.5}
\]
For every \(\zeta_{\rm an}>0\),
\[
 T_{\rm an}(Z_h)
 \leq\zeta_{\rm an}Y_6(h)
 +{2A_R^{3/2}\over3\sqrt{3\zeta_{\rm an}}}.         \tag{5.6}
\]
With \(\zeta_{\rm an}=1/100\), the last constant is less than 24.

\textit{Proof.}
The trace cyclicity, positivity, and (5.1) give
\[
 T_{\rm an}(Z_h)
 \leq{\beta_{\rm op}\over2}g_R^{(6)}
       \E\|Z_h\|_2^2.                                \tag{5.7}
\]
For every sample, spatial Holder gives
\[
 \|Z_h\|_2^2\leq V^{2/3}\|Z_h\|_6^2
 =V^{2/3}\left(\int|Z_h|^6\right)^{1/3}.             \tag{5.8}
\]
Probability Jensen for the concave function \(s^{1/3}\) then gives
\[
 \E\|Z_h\|_2^2\leq V^{2/3}Y_6(h)^{1/3}.             \tag{5.9}
\]
This proves (5.4).  Maximizing
\(A_Rx-\zeta_{\rm an}x^3\) over \(x\geq0\) proves (5.6).
The executable does not treat a rounded decimal as an upper certificate:
it verifies exactly that the square of the constant is
\[
 {686585249332581678649212904712608513290193727
  \over
  1250470044180000000000000000000000000000000}
 <24^2.                                               \tag{5.10}
\]
This proves the stated integer ceiling. \hfill\(\square\)

For general regulator bound, (4.8) scales as \(M_\chi^2\) and the Young
constant in (5.6) scales as \(M_\chi^3\).  In particular, for
\(M_\chi\leq1\),
\[
 T_{\rm an}(Z_h)\leq{1\over100}Y_6(h)+24.            \tag{5.11}
\]

The field in Theorem 5.1 is the full terminal field.  No centered-future
heat constant is needed.  Substituting an individual future increment would
be a different theorem and would require the missing temporal owner
intertwiner; (5.11) does not make that substitution.

\section*{6. What remains ultraviolet and why}
\hypertarget{section-6-frontier}{ }

The terminal anisotropic covariance is summable because the mass difference
adds four inverse spatial powers.  The scalar principal covariance does not
share that gain.  Before signed owner cancellation, its model shell is
\[
 (24m^2+2){m^2\over m^4}=24+{2\over m^2}\longrightarrow24.              \tag{6.1}
\]
The shell series therefore diverges.  Absolute trace estimates cannot close
the scalar principal sea.

The remaining production target is still (2.5), with all conditional means,
common scalar noise, cross reveals, returned low, and anchors recombined
exactly once.  The terminal split (3.4) can be useful only after either:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a temporally faithful owner transfer is proved, including output
projections, heat, reveal, collar, and low blocks; or
\item the anisotropic term is retained inside the complete signed production
trace excess and controlled there without a separate transfer.
\end{enumerate}
The scalar principal common-noise sea must remain signed in either route.

\section*{7. Independent Branch-B audit}
\hypertarget{section-7-branch-b}{ }

R-141 supplies the exact finite-chart action and projected force.  A
conditional Branch-B theorem would follow from a uniform positive Hessian
gap on one declared reduced source/low action, a uniform origin-force bound,
and a uniform absolute anchor.  None is currently a production input.

The low coordinate cannot simply be minimized as a free stationary variable.
The current conditional low and returned low are prescribed, distinct owner
functionals.  If low is a nonlinear graph \(\Lambda(h)\), the pullback
Hessian contains the connection term
\[
 D\cA\,D^2\Lambda,                                    \tag{7.1}
\]
not only the Schur complement of an ambient Hessian.  A stationary reduction
is legal only after an attained \(C^2\) low minimizer with the same action,
force, and anchor is constructed.  Branch B therefore remains data-gated;
it is not an escape from the scalar signed trace-excess problem.

\section*{9. Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: The trace-excess thresholds should be shifted by
\(9/20\) and \(3/20\).  DISMISSED.}
Those terms belong to the augmented action and are subtracted in (2.4).
T-050 concerns \(V^{\rm ren}=a_\pi-\cT_\pi\), so the direct thresholds are
\(5/11\) and \(27/100\).

\item \textbf{Objection: R-129 was wrong.  DISMISSED.}

action positive.  This note corrects only its use as the purported full
T-050 acceptance range.

\item \textbf{Objection: The raw lattice sum is already the physical trace.
UPHELD against the shortcut.}
The six-real pointwise trace is \((2/V)G_R^{\mathbb C}\).  Equations
(4.7)--(4.8) keep realification and Fourier normalization explicit.

\item \textbf{Objection: The terminal covariance split propagates through
every temporal reveal.  UPHELD.}
R-129 gives a finite-dimensional noncommuting increment fixture, and R-143
lists the missing owner data.  The production-temporal transfer is false as
an inference and remains open as a theorem.

\item \textbf{Objection: The rounded decimal \(23.432\ldots\) is a certified
upper bound.  UPHELD against that presentation.}
Decimal square roots round.  The certificate instead proves the rational
squared inequality (5.10) and reports the integer ceiling 24.

\item \textbf{Objection: The kinetic square bound follows merely from
\(Y>0\).  UPHELD.}
The displayed comparison with coefficient one uses the pinned \(Y=1\).
Both executable paths assert that exact value before using (3.9).

\item \textbf{Objection: The anisotropic estimate closes T-050.  DISMISSED.}
It is terminal-only.  The scalar principal sea, production temporal transfer,

\item \textbf{Objection: A positive reduced Hessian removes the need for an
anchor.  DISMISSED.}
Convexity controls variation, not absolute normalization.  Branch B still

\end{enumerate}
