\section*{1. Purpose and scope: a cylinder-free uniform local radius}
\hypertarget{section-1-result}{ }

R-156 proved a nonzero shifted-state neighbourhood only after fixing one
finite cutoff.  R-159 removes that auxiliary-cylinder dependence for the
single centered pure-dyadic chart.  It does so without choosing a preferred
cutoff or multiplier and without turning a qualitative radius into a
numerical one.

Fix the side-$16$ torus, the hash-pinned A1 symbol, the A7 floor
$\varepsilon_\rho=10^{-12}$, and exact continuum torus integration.  For
each cutoff use the same real-even scalar multiplier on every real component,
with
\[
             |m_\Lambda(k)|\le 1,\qquad
             m_\Lambda(-k)=m_\Lambda(k),                 \tag{1.1}
\]
and subtract that regulator's exact primitive derivative covariance.  Let
$p\ne0$ be a dual-lattice momentum for which $p,2p,4p$ are retained.  The raw
both-sign roots $\xi_p,\xi_{2p},\xi_{4p}$ are centered, whitened and mutually
independent.  There is no deterministic past mean.  Define
\[
 \chi(A,B)
 =\bigl(A\xi_p,
        B(\xi_{2p}+A\xi_p)\bigr),
 \qquad A,B\in\mathbb R^{12\times12}.    \tag{1.2}
\]

\textbf{Theorem 1.1 (regulator-uniform local gap).}
There exists $\delta_*>0$, depending only on the fixed torus, A1 symbol,
positive floor and multiplier bound in (1.1), but independent of the cutoff,
the particular admitted regulator and the retained $p$, such that
\[
        \|A\|_{\HS}+\|B\|_{\HS}<\delta_* \tag{1.3}
\]
implies, for every nonzero coefficient direction $v=(H,G)$,
\[
 D^2(\cA\circ\chi)_{A,B}[v,v]
 >{1\over10}\bigl(\|H\|_{\HS}^2+\|G\|_{\HS}^2\bigr).   \tag{1.4}
\]
The intrinsic physical-source Hessian on the tangent image also obeys
\[
 D^2\cA(h_{A,B})[D\chi v,D\chi v]
 >{1\over10}\cG_{A,B}(v,v),             \tag{1.5}
\]
where
\[
 \cG_{A,B}(v,v)
 =\|H\|_{\HS}^2+\|G\|_{\HS}^2
  +\|GA+B H\|_{\HS}^2.                 \tag{1.6}
\]

The radius is existential.  No numerical value, floor-uniform statement,
finite-grid alias theorem, realised-past fibrewise result, growing multiroot
union, arbitrary predictable nonlinear or revisit estimate, T-050 closure,
or Sector-A closure is claimed.

\section*{2. The nonlinear controller still has a centered Gaussian endpoint}
\hypertarget{section-2-gaussian}{ }

Write $S_r(x)$ for the regulator-weighted whitened synthesis at the antipodal
pair $\{rp,-rp\}$ and $D_{r,i}=\partial_iS_r$, for $r=1,2,4$.  The endpoint
columns after (1.2) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 R_1&=S_1+S_2A+S_4BA,
 &R_2&=S_2+S_4B,&R_4&=S_4,\\
 U_{1,i}&=D_{1,i}+D_{2,i}A+D_{4,i}BA,
 &U_{2,i}&=D_{2,i}+D_{4,i}B,&U_{4,i}&=D_{4,i}.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{2.1}
\]
Thus the chart is nonlinear in $(A,B)$ but the endpoint is
linear in the raw Gaussian vector.  In particular the field $W$ and currents
$V_i$ remain jointly centered Gaussian for every deterministic pair
$(A,B)$.

Let $C_{\rm bg}$ and $Q_{\rm bg}$ be the background value and derivative
covariances.  Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
 C&=C_{\rm bg}+\sum_{r=1,2,4}R_rR_r^*,\\
 Q&=Q_{\rm bg}+\sum_i\sum_{r=1,2,4}U_{r,i}U_{r,i}^*,\\
 K_i&=\sum_{r=1,2,4}R_rU_{r,i}^*.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{2.2}
\]
Common evenness gives $K_i(0,0)=0$.  Put
\[
                    Q_0=Q(0,0).                           \tag{2.3}
\]
This $Q_0$ is exactly the primitive covariance used by the A7 subtraction.

\subsection*{2.1 All-state covariance-normal Gaussian identity}

Let $\mathcal B(w)=C_6(w)^*C_6(w)$ be the fixed six-row coefficient matrix.
For any
centered jointly Gaussian $(W,V_i)$, Gaussian integration by parts gives
\[
 \E\bigl[(V_i)_m(V_i)_n \mathcal B_{mn}(W)\bigr]
 =Q_{i,mn}\E \mathcal B_{mn}(W)
 +(K_i)_{am}(K_i)_{bn}\E\partial_{ab}\mathcal B_{mn}(W).\tag{2.4}
\]
Consequently the complete covariance-normal current endpoint satisfies, for
every $(A,B)$,
\[
\boxed{
 2\cR(A,B)=\int\!\left\{
 \Tr\bigl[(Q-Q_0)\overline{\mathcal B}(C)\bigr]
 +\sum_i(K_i)_{am}(K_i)_{bn}\overline T_{abmn}(C)
 \right\}dx,}                                             \tag{2.5}
\]
where
\[
 \overline{\mathcal B}(C)=\E_{N(0,C)}\mathcal B(W),\qquad
 \overline T(C)=\E_{N(0,C)}D^2\mathcal B(W).              \tag{2.6}
\]

Equation (2.5), rather than the origin-only R-151 Hessian formula, is the new
load-bearing lemma.  Differentiating the complete scalar in (2.5) includes
all first and second $C,Q,K$ jets and exactly the projected-force pairing with
the chart acceleration.  Source, trace, current, forward, legal reverse and
balanced descriptions are not added again.

\section*{3. Exact cancellation before compactness}
\hypertarget{section-3-cancellation}{ }

The raw derivative-covariance family is not uniformly bounded.  For the
positive-density sharp-cube member, the A1 fourth-order symbol gives
\[
 \Tr Q_0^{\rm sharp}\asymp\sum_{|k|\le\Lambda}
 { |k|^2\over 1+|k|^4}=\Theta(\Lambda).                  \tag{3.1}
\]
Other admitted multipliers can have smaller or scheme-dependent growth; the
class as a whole is nevertheless not precompact in raw $Q$.  Thus any proof
which first puts the whole raw-$Q$ family in a compact parameter set is false.
Equation (2.5) instead cancels $Q_0$ algebraically.  The difference
$\Delta Q=Q-Q_0$ contains only the finitely many controlled $p,2p,4p$
columns.

R-151's exact lower symbol polynomial is
\[
 f(x)=x^2-{4626377063\over5000000000}x
          +{5020336473\over10000000000}.                 \tag{3.2}
\]
Its continuous minimum is
\[
 \nu={28800000000947494031\over10^{20}}>0.               \tag{3.3}
\]
For $r\in\{1,2,4\}$ and $V=16^3$,
\[
 \|S_r\|_{\rm op}^2\le {2\over Vf(r^2|p|^2)}
                  \le {2\over V\nu},                    \tag{3.4}
\]
and
\[
 \|D_r\|_{\rm op}^2\le {2r^2|p|^2\over Vf(r^2|p|^2)}
 \le {2\over V}\sup_{y\ge0}{y\over f(y)}<\infty.       \tag{3.5}
\]
The multiplier bound in (1.1) can only decrease these norms.  The value and
derivative syntheses vanish respectively as $|p|^{-2}$ and $|p|^{-1}$ at
large momentum.  On any fixed controller ball, (2.1) therefore gives uniform
polynomial bounds for $C$, $\Delta Q$, $K_i$ and all controller jets needed
by a Hessian.

The background value covariance is harmless:
\[
 0\preceq C_{\rm bg}\preceq C_{\rm val}\Id,
 \qquad C_{\rm val}\lesssim {1\over V}\sum_k\langle k\rangle^{-4}<\infty.
                                                               \tag{3.6}
\]
Its possible values have compact closure in the finite-dimensional positive
semidefinite cone.

\section*{4. Finite invariants and the uniform modulus}
\hypertarget{section-4-compactness}{ }

The controller degrees are finite and fixed:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccc}
\hbox{object}&\chi&R,U&C,\Delta Q,K&K\otimes K&\cC_{\rm CM}&\E|W|^6\\ \hline
\hbox{maximum degree}&2&2&4&8&4&12.
\end{array}                                                \tag{4.1}
\]
The Cameron--Martin term is exactly
\[
 \cC_{\rm CM}={9\over20}\left(
 \|A\|_{\HS}^2+\|B\|_{\HS}^2
 +\|BA\|_{\HS}^2\right),              \tag{4.2}
\]
and for a centered six-real Gaussian
\[
 \E|W|^6=(\Tr C)^3+6\Tr C\,\Tr(C^2)+8\Tr(C^3).          \tag{4.3}
\]

At the fixed positive floor every derivative of the rational matrix
$\mathcal B(W)$
has polynomial growth.  The maps
\[
 C\longmapsto\E_{N(0,C)}D^j\mathcal B(W)                 \tag{4.4}
\]
are therefore continuous on bounded positive semidefinite covariance sets,
including singular covariances.  This derivative-expectation representation
is essential: the alternative score formula containing $C^{-1}$ is not
uniform when a regulator makes a covariance eigenvalue tend to zero.

The remaining parameters have compact closure after the following exact
reductions: $C_{\rm bg}$ lies in (3.6); the three controlled multiplier values
lie in $[-1,1]$; the momentum direction lies in $S^2$; and the radial variable
$|p|^2$ is compactified by adjoining its infinite limit, where every
controlled synthesis vanishes.  All carriers are the collinear multiples
$p,2p,4p$.  For every nonzero dual-lattice $p$, Haar measure under
$x\mapsto p\cdot x$ is uniform on the circle, so exact spatial integration is
one common compact phase average.  We do not assert that the oscillatory
functions themselves form a compact family in a strong function norm.

Let $J_{A,B}=D\chi_{A,B}$.  R-156 gives
\[
 \cG=J^*J=\Id+T^*T\succeq\Id,                            \tag{4.5}
\]
where $T(H,G)=GA+B H$.  On a bounded
controller ball, both $\cG$ and $\cG^{-1/2}$ are uniformly continuous.  No
physical covariance inverse is used.

Write $\mathcal H_{\rm ctl}$ for the complete pullback Hessian and
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal H}_{\rm int}
 =\cG^{-1/2}J^*H_\pi(h)J\cG^{-1/2}                       \tag{4.6}
\]
for the normalized intrinsic Hessian.  Define
\[
\begin{split}
 \omega(r)=\sup\max\{&\|\mathcal H_{\rm ctl}(A,B)
                -\mathcal H_{\rm ctl}(0,0)\|_{\rm op},\\
 &\|\widehat{\mathcal H}_{\rm int}(A,B)
                -\widehat{\mathcal H}_{\rm int}(0,0)\|_{\rm op}\},
\end{split}                                                   \tag{4.7}
\]
where the supremum is over (1.1), all admitted cutoffs and retained $p$, and
$\|A\|_{\HS}+\|B\|_{\HS}\le r$.  The repaired compact
finite-invariant reduction proves
\[
                         \omega(r)\longrightarrow0
                         \quad(r\downarrow0).             \tag{4.8}
\]

\section*{5. Uniform gap and owner-complete two-block meaning}
\hypertarget{section-5-gap}{ }

R-155 supplies, uniformly over the pure-dyadic origin family,
\[
 \mathcal H_{\rm ctl}(0,0)
 =\widehat{\mathcal H}_{\rm int}(0,0)
 \succeq {147\over1000}\Id.                              \tag{5.1}
\]
The exact headroom over the T-050 comparison target is
\[
 {147\over1000}-{1\over10}={47\over1000}.                \tag{5.2}
\]
Choose $\delta_*>0$ so that
\[
                 \omega(\delta_*)<{47\over2000}.         \tag{5.3}
\]
Then both complete Hessians retain the comparison value
\[
 {147\over1000}-{47\over2000}
 ={247\over2000}>{1\over10},                              \tag{5.4}
\]
which proves Theorem 1.1.

In the actual two-source decomposition, subtracting the target metric gives
one self-adjoint block operator
\[
 N=M-{1\over10}\cG
  =\begin{pmatrix}E&C\\C^*&F\end{pmatrix}.               \tag{5.5}
\]
The forward entry is $C$, its sole legal reverse is $C^*$, and the balanced
cross is $2\operatorname{Re}\ip{x}{Cy}$.  These are three descriptions of
one Hessian block, not three payments.  The source, covariance-normal current,
trace and sextic terms occur once in the scalar differentiated above.  The
projected-force/controller connection also occurs once through the second
derivative of (2.5).  No independent low coordinate exists in (1.2); an
invented low/Feshbach reserve remains prohibited.  Thus R-159 supplies the
requested unified forward/legal-reverse/balanced/no-independent-low estimate
on this local chart, but not on the full arbitrary predictable source class.

\section*{6. Failure modes and scope firewall}
\hypertarget{section-6-boundary}{ }

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Raw-$Q$ compactness is false.}  Equation (3.1) diverges.  The
proof is valid only after the exact $Q-Q_0$ cancellation in (2.5).

\item \textbf{A covariance-inverse score bound is not uniform.}  Admitted
multiplier sequences can make a value covariance singular.  The proof uses
$\E_C D^jB$, never $C^{-1}$.

\item \textbf{Bounded oscillatory functions are not a compact family.}
The theorem uses the exact Haar phase reduction for the collinear
$p,2p,4p$ carriers, not a false strong-function compactness argument.

\item \textbf{Generic finite-grid de-aliasing is not covered.}  A grid on
which, for example, $3p$ aliases to zero can destroy the origin Fourier
cancellation.  The theorem assumes exact continuum torus integration; a
numerical grid needs a separate certified alias error.

\item \textbf{The entire unnormalised A7 multiplier union is not covered.}
The single radius is proved for the contraction subclass $|m|\le1$.  A class
with a separately fixed envelope $M$ would give a radius depending on $M$.

\item \textbf{The radius is not effective.}  An explicit value requires
registered bounds for higher coefficient derivatives, Gaussian moments,
all covariance jets, the sextic third variation, projected force and graph
normalization.  R-130 and A7 prove finiteness but do not pin all those
constants.

\item \textbf{The chart is not the full predictable class.}  A nonzero
deterministic mean, arbitrary nonlinear function of a realised past, a
growing simultaneous root union or a general revisit graph is outside the
compact parameter family.

\item \textbf{No phase or equation is selected.}  No BCC ansatz, vacuum
comparison, candidate PDE or morphology discriminator enters this theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{7. Devil's-advocate review}
\hypertarget{section-7-review}{ }

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: R-151 only proved an origin Hessian.}
\textbf{VALID WITH REPAIR.}  R-159 proves the all-state identity (2.5) before
differentiation.  It does not extend the origin formula by assertion.

\item \textbf{Objection: the projected-force connection was omitted.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  It is exactly the chain-rule part of the second
controller derivative of the one scalar (2.5).

\item \textbf{Objection: the derivative covariance destroys uniformity.}
\textbf{VALID FOR RAW $Q$, DISMISSED AFTER NORMAL ORDERING.}  $Q_0$ diverges,
but cancels identically.  Every controlled $\Delta Q$ jet is uniformly
bounded by (3.4)--(3.5).

\item \textbf{Objection: singular value covariance invalidates the proof.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  Fixed-floor coefficient derivatives have polynomial
growth and their Gaussian expectations extend continuously to singular
positive semidefinite covariance.  No score inverse is used.

\item \textbf{Objection: compactness silently chooses a production cylinder.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  The theorem is universally quantified over the declared
contraction regulator class.  Compactness is applied to the closure of finite
invariants after exact cancellation and phase reduction.

\item \textbf{Objection: a uniform local radius closes T-050.}
\textbf{UPHELD AGAINST THAT INFERENCE.}  General predictable functions,
realised past, growing source unions and arbitrary revisit structures remain
outside R-159.
\end{enumerate}
