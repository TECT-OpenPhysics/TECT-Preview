\section*{Purpose and scope}

This note continues the strict-past resolvent reduction for the finite ordered Gaussian sharp-cube filtration.  It keeps the positive completed square that the preceding sufficient signed-charge formulation discarded, differentiates the production coefficient increment at the conditional origin, and computes the exact conditional heat-current identity.  All identities are finite-dimensional and cutoff-wise.  No cutoff-uniform form bound, Nelson estimate, or measure construction is asserted.

Write \(H_j=T_j+q^{-1}I\), \(R_j=(I+qT_j)^{-1}\), so \(H_j^{-1}=qR_j\), and let
\[
C_j(u)={1\over2}\int [B(x+G_ju)-B(x)]:
 ((D(x+G_ju))^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_j).
\]
The strict-past coefficient score and exact nonlinear remainder are
\[
 \langle m_j,v\rangle=DC_j(0)[v]
 ={1\over2}\int DB(x)[G_jv]:(Dx^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_j),
 \qquad N_j(u)=C_j(u)-\langle m_j,u\rangle .
\]
Thus \(N_j(0)=DN_j(0)=0\).

\section*{Joint-score metric completion}

For every strict-past realization and shell displacement \(h_j\), the retained affine block plus coefficient increment satisfies the exact identity
\[
 \mathbb E_j(I_j+C_j)(\xi_j+h_j)
 =-\epsilon_v|h_j|^2-{q\over2}\langle \ell_j+m_j,
 R_j(\ell_j+m_j)\rangle+\mathbb E_jN_j(\xi_j+h_j)
 +{1\over2}|H_j^{1/2}(h_j+qR_j(\ell_j+m_j))|^2.
\]
Indeed, substitute \(C_j(u)=\langle m_j,u\rangle+N_j(u)\) before completing the \(H_j\)-square.  This is algebraic and loses no sign.  On the fast relative-phase principal symbol, where \(m_j=\ell_j\), the negative source rebate is four times its coefficient-one value.  Hence the earlier factor-four obstruction is recovered automatically rather than inserted as a separate convention.

The preceding charge involving only \(\ell_j\) remains a sufficient reduction, but it discards a potentially useful positive square too early.  The sharper remaining object must retain the joint score, nonlinear remainder, and square together.

\section*{Conditional heat-current identity}

Let \(g=G_j\xi_j\), condition on the strict past, and use independence of the same-shell Gaussian value and derivative variables under the pinned entrywise-even covariance.  With value covariance \(\Sigma_j\) and past derivative covariance \(\Gamma_{j-1}\),
\[
 \mathbb E_j C_j(\xi_j+h_j)
 ={1\over2}\int [P_{\Sigma_j}B(x+G_jh_j)-B(x)]:
 (D(x+G_jh_j)^{\otimes2}-\Gamma_{j-1}).
\]
The proof expands the derivative square.  The centered same-shell derivative covariance cancels \(\Gamma_j-\Gamma_{j-1}\), while all value--derivative cross terms vanish.  The remaining value average is precisely the Gaussian heat semigroup \(P_{\Sigma_j}\).

Moreover,
\[
 P_{\Sigma}B(u)-B(u)
 ={1\over2}\int_0^1P_{t\Sigma}(\Sigma:D^2B)(u)\,dt.
\]
This separates a translated current/Cartan contraction from Gaussian heat curvature without assigning either term an unsupported sign.

Conditional positivity of \(C_j\) is false.  At \(x=0\), \(Dx=0\), and \(h_j=0\), production \(B(0)=0\) and \(P_{\Sigma_j}B(0)\succeq0\), whence
\[
 \mathbb E_jC_j=-{1\over2}\operatorname{Tr}
 (P_{\Sigma_j}B(0)\Gamma_{j-1})<0
\]
for a nondegenerate past covariance.  Therefore the proof may not discard the current term by a shellwise positivity claim.

\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST A POSITIVITY READING:} the conditional coefficient increment is nonnegative.  The origin calculation gives a strict negative production example, so no such sign is used.
\item \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION:} the heat-semigroup formula already bounds the global sum.  It only identifies the curvature exactly; its cutoff-uniform Cartan/form estimate is the named next gate.
\item \textbf{UPHELD AGAINST PROMOTION:} this reduction proves the controlled-shell one-use or Nelson theorem.  It proves neither and leaves A13 at T4.
