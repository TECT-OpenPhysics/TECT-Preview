\section*{3. Sharp harmonic coercivity}
\hypertarget{section-3-harmonic}{ }

Let \(\theta=p\mathbin{\cdot}x\).  The real six-component fields have

\[

For normalized phase average \(\langle\cdot\rangle\), put

\subsection*{Lemma 3.1 (sharp (5/6) separation)}

For every pair satisfying (3.1),
\[
 \boxed{
 \langle|m|^4|z|^2\rangle
 \geq {5\over6}\langle|m|^4\rangle
                    \langle|z|^2\rangle.}           \tag{3.2}
\]
The constant is sharp.

\paragraph{Proof.}
The top coefficient of $r$ is $r_4$.  Reality and Cauchy--Schwarz on the
$2p$ Fourier coefficient give $r_0\geq2|r_4|$.  Since
\(f_8=r_4^2\), Parseval gives
\[
 f_0\geq r_0^2+2|r_4|^2\geq6|f_8|.                  \tag{3.3}
\]
The function $q$ has only frequencies $0,\pm8$, and positivity gives
\(q_0\geq2|q_8|\).  Therefore
\[
 \langle fq\rangle
 =f_0q_0+2\Re(f_8\overline{q_8})
 \geq f_0q_0-2|f_8||q_8|
 \geq{5\over6}f_0q_0.                               \tag{3.4}
\]
For scalar $m=\cos(2\theta)$ and $z=\sin(4\theta)$, the three averages
are $3/8,1/2,5/32$, so equality holds. \hfill\(\square\)

For centered Gaussian (X), direct moment expansion gives
\[
 \E|m+X|^4=|m|^4+2|m|^2\Tr C+4m^TCm
 +(\Tr C)^2+2\Tr(C^2)\geq|m|^4.                     \tag{3.5}
\]
Combining (3.2), (3.5), and spatial Jensen supplies the positive $G^4$
term in (1.4).
