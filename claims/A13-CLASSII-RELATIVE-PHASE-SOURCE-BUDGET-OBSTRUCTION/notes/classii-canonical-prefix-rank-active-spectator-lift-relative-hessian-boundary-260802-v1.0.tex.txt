\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
\hypertarget{section-1-executive}{ }

R-147 found an exact active--spectator direction on which the retained
production $P+L$ coefficient energy is not convex.  The next proposed step
was to place that direction in an R-146 canonical two-root chart and append
the shared trace, Doob bracket, R-063 forest, future variance, balanced rows,
returned low, and terminal sextic exactly once.  R-148 proves that this
proposal was not yet a well-defined computation.

There are four conclusions.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The fresh final scalar innovation of the exact R-147 line cannot be a
nonzero final increment of the R-146 proportional-covariance canonical chart.
On the active--spectator plane every nonzero canonical increment has rank two,
while that fresh line innovation has rank one.  A rank-one past block is only
the no-correction line fixture, not a necessity theorem for an adapted past.

\item This is not a no-go for a canonical Brownian chart.  It means that a
new canonical chart necessarily leaves the exact line and must recompute the
full-rank coefficient, forest, low, and spatial owners.  Missing companions
may not be set to zero or imported from the old chart.

\item A separately declared noncanonical last-root Gaussian coefficient
completion is exactly computable.  At each fixed point strictly inside the
R-147 adverse region, its predictable trace--bracket mismatch is positive
for all sufficiently small noise.  Thus even the toy lift fails automatic
trace--bracket matching.

\item The R-147 negative second derivative is an absolute-owner diagnostic,
not a physical deterministic-control Hessian.  In the declared coefficient-
background parameter family the parameter gradient uses $f'''$ and its
Hessian uses $f^{(4)}$.  At each fixed adverse point and sufficiently small
noise the owner and terminal-sextic curvature are positive and the source
curvature is nonnegative.  In the orientation $R>0$ the first derivative is
negative; invariantly it is nonzero.  The origin is therefore not stationary
in this diagnostic, but no physical control Hessian, local minimum, or phase
is identified.
\end{enumerate}

The result is phase-neutral.  BCC, a different modulated state, a uniform
state, or inadequacy of the present effective action are all downstream
possibilities.  None is preferred or excluded here.

\section*{5. Absolute owner versus coefficient-background parameter}
\hypertarget{section-5-relative-control}{ }

The sign in (4.9) was previously at risk of being read as a negative
physical deterministic-control Hessian.  That inference is false: the
following family moves only the coefficient background while retaining the
declared current prefactor $\sigma g$.

Introduce a coefficient-background parameter $m$ and put
\[
                  q=m+\sigma g.                       \tag{5.1}
\]
The expected complete coefficient owner is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \cP_{\rm comp}(m)
 &={\sigma^2\over2}\E[f(q)(g^2-1)]\\
 &={\sigma^4\over2}\E f''(m+\sigma g).
\end{aligned}
                                                               \tag{5.2}
\]
Hence, for this declared parameter family only,
\[
 \cP_{\rm comp}'(0)
 ={\sigma^4\over2}\E f'''(\sigma g),\qquad
 \cP_{\rm comp}''(0)
 ={\sigma^4\over2}\E f^{(4)}(\sigma g).             \tag{5.3}
\]

Direct exact differentiation gives
\[
 f'''(0)=
 -{9R(16R^2+27e)\over50P(2R^2+e)^2}<0,               \tag{5.4}
\]
and
\[
 f^{(4)}(0)=
 {18(112R^4+48R^2e-9e^2)
  \over25P(2R^2+e)^3}.                                \tag{5.5}
\]
With $r=R^2/e$, the positive root in (5.5) is
\[
 r_4=-{3\over14}+{3\sqrt{11}\over28}
     =0.14106694\ldots .                              \tag{5.6}
\]
The exact comparisons
\[
 r_2>{1\over3},\qquad r_4<{1\over7}                  \tag{5.7}
\]
follow from $\sqrt{154}>11$ and $\sqrt{11}<10/3$.
Therefore every R-147 adverse point has $f^{(4)}(0)>0$.

For each fixed adverse $(R,e,P)$, there is a
$\sigma_0(R,e,P)>0$ such that for $0<\sigma<\sigma_0$,
\[
 \cP_{\rm comp}'(0)<0,
 \qquad \cP_{\rm comp}''(0)>0.                       \tag{5.8}
\]
The first inequality is essential.  Positive curvature does not make
$m=0$ stationary and does not prove a local minimum.

This is not yet the Hessian of a physical source lift.  For example, if the
same parameter also changed the current prefactor to
$\Phi=(m+\sigma g)A(m+\sigma g)$, the uncentred signed owner would acquire
the additional terms
\[

\]
which change both derivatives at the origin.  A production control Hessian
therefore requires the missing physical synthesis and source lift.

\[

to a physical instability, and deleting the adverse sign merely because a
minimal toy Hessian is positive.
