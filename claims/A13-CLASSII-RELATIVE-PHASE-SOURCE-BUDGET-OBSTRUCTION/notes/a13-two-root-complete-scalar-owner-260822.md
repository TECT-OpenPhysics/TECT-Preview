# R-191 proof-evidence note

R-191 freezes the smallest complete finite scalar endpoint owner currently
available after R-176, R-177, and R-178. It retains the actual root labels and
the common-heat/root feedback order, and it differentiates the endpoint once
with the feedback coefficient present.

The exact stage telescope is useful because the registered fixture has a
positive total increment but two negative intermediate increments. Therefore a
future production proof must keep the signed recombined owner until the
source/forest/returned-mean terms have been inserted; a rootwise absolute
payment is not licensed by the endpoint identity.

This note is deliberately finite and claim-nonbearing. It supplies no
cutoff-uniform `q_k` ledger, progressive/revisit theorem, physical comparison,
or A13/T-050 closure.
