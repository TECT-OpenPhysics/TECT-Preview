\section{Exact global-current cross-Doob decomposition}

\subsection*{Theorem 7.1 (future-feedback residual identity)}

For every fixed low level $\ell$,
\[
\boxed{

 =\E\langle G_\ell,F_\ell\rangle
 +\sum_{j>\ell}\E\langle d_jG_J,d_jF_j\rangle.}         \tag{7.2}
\]
For partial controls, put
\[
 \Psi^{(j)}=L_{\Sigma_{\rm tar}}(U_J,A^{(j)})c^{(j)},
 \qquad \Lambda_j=P_j\Psi^{(j)}.                        \tag{7.3}
\]
Under the R-101 locality, real-even value--gradient independence, and
strict-past hypotheses,
\[
 \E\langle d_jG_J,\Lambda_j\rangle=0.                   \tag{7.4}
\]
Consequently the exact survivor is
\[
\boxed{
 \cR_{\rm cur}
 =\sum_{j>\ell}\E\left\langle d_jG_J,
 P_j(\Psi_*-\Psi^{(j)})\right\rangle.}                  \tag{7.5}
\]
If $\Delta_k\Psi^{(k)}=\Psi^{(k)}-\Psi^{(k-1)}$, then,
with that sign convention,
\[
 \boxed{
 \cR_{\rm cur}
 =\sum_{\ell<j<k\le J}
 \E\langle d_jG_J,\Delta_k\Psi^{(k)}\rangle.}          \tag{7.6}
\]

\paragraph{Proof.}
Both $G_j$ and $F_j$ are Hilbert-valued martingales, so their cross-Doob
increments prove (7.2).  The local partial coefficient in (7.3) depends on
the fresh root only through its value coordinate; $c^{(j)}$ is predictable.
The fresh derivative coordinate is independent at the same point, proving
(7.4).  Since $d_jG_J$ centres every $\cF_{j-1}$ quantity, subtracting
$\Lambda_j$ from the $j$th bracket gives (7.5).  Telescope
$\Psi_*-\Psi^{(j)}$ over $k>j$ and use conditional Fubini to obtain (7.6).
\qed

The product structure cannot be suppressed.  For a bilinear multiplication

\end{aligned}                                            \tag{7.7}
\]
Here $\operatorname{Cov}^{m}_j(H,c)=P_jm(H,c)-m(P_jH,P_jc)$.
R-101 centres a coefficient against a fresh raw-Wick derivative increment;
