\section{Exact scalar-ray reduction}

Take a real field in the first internal component, $\Psi=fe_1$.  Only
generator $T_3$ contributes, and
\[
 J_3=2f\nabla f,\qquad
 K_3={\varepsilon_\rho\over f^2+\varepsilon_\rho}J_3.
 \tag{2.1}
\]
Consequently the coefficient in the $e_1$ derivative direction is
\[
 B_{11}(fe_1)
 =4f^2\left[
 a+2b{\varepsilon_\rho\over f^2+\varepsilon_\rho}
 +c\left({\varepsilon_\rho\over f^2+\varepsilon_\rho}\right)^2
 \right].
 \tag{2.2}
\]
For fields uniformly separated from zero this is $4af^2+O(1)$, and the
$b,c$ and floor terms do not contribute to the leading high-frequency
coefficient.

\section{Same-shell resonant triad}

Let
\[
 g_K(x,y)=\cos(Kx)+\cos(Ky)-\cos(K(x+y)).
 \tag{3.1}
\]
Its frequencies have lengths $K,K,\sqrt2K$, so they lie in the annulus
$[K,2K)$.  Exact Fourier convolution gives
\[
 \langle g_K|\nabla g_K|^2\rangle=-K^2,\qquad
 \langle g_K^2|\nabla g_K|^2\rangle={5\over2}K^2,
 \tag{3.2}
\]
and
\[
\left\langle g^m\right\rangle_{m=0}^6
=\left(1,0,{3\over2},-{3\over2},{45\over8},
-{45\over4},{255\over8}\right).
 \tag{3.3}
\]

Choose the previous shell to be $\phi_{j-1}=Ae_1$ and the full shell to be
\[
 \phi_j=A(1+\epsilon g_K)e_1,\qquad 0<\epsilon<{1\over3}.
 \tag{3.4}
\]
This restriction keeps the field uniformly away from zero.  Substitution of
(3.2) into the raw coefficient increment gives
\[
 {1\over L^3}\cC_j^{\rm raw}
 =-aA^4K^2\epsilon^3(4-5\epsilon)+o(A^4K^2).
 \tag{3.5}
\]
The covariance counterterm grows only as
$\Gamma_{\le j}O(A^2)=O(K^3)$ when $A=tK$.  Thus it cannot cancel the
$K^6$ term in (3.5).  Fixed mass and vacuum terms are still lower order.

\section{Covariance-contracted Gaussian contradiction}

Let $C_J$ be the cutoff covariance, let $d_J=O(K^3)$ be its real dimension,
and put
\[
 h_K=tK(1+\epsilon g_K)e_1,\qquad
 \sigma_K=K^{-2},\qquad
 \nu_K=N(h_K,\sigma_K^2C_J).
 \tag{4.1}
\]
This law is absolutely continuous with respect to
$\gamma_J=N(0,C_J)$ at every finite cutoff.  Its exact relative entropy is
\[
 H(\nu_K\mid\gamma_J)
 ={1\over2}\|h_K\|_{\mathcal H_J}^2
 +{d_J\over2}(\sigma_K^2-1-\log\sigma_K^2).
 \tag{4.2}
\]
The lattice-shell count in dimension three and the production $q^4$ symbol
give, uniformly up to the cutoff,
\[
 \sum_{|q|\le K}{1\over 1+|q|^4}=O(1),\qquad
 \sum_{|q|\le K}{|q|^2\over 1+|q|^4}=O(K),\qquad
 d_J=O(K^3).
 \tag{4.2a}
\]
These follow by grouping lattice points into unit radial shells, whose
cardinality is $O(r^2)$, and comparing the resulting sums with integrals.
The second term in (4.2) is therefore
$O(K^3\log K)=o(K^6)$.  The production symbol and (3.3) give
\[
 {H(\nu_K\mid\gamma_J)\over L^3K^6}
 \longrightarrow c_Ht^2,\qquad c_H={3\over2}Y\epsilon^2,
 \tag{4.3}
\]
\[
 {\E_{\nu_K}\|\phi_J\|_6^6\over L^3K^6}
 \longrightarrow c_6t^6,\qquad
 c_6=M_6(\epsilon)=\langle(1+\epsilon g)^6\rangle,
 \tag{4.4}
\]
\[
 {\E_{\nu_K}\sum_{\ell\le J}\cC_\ell\over L^3K^6}
 \longrightarrow-c_Ct^4,\qquad
 c_C=a\epsilon^3(4-5\epsilon)>0.
 \tag{4.5}
\]
Here is the fluctuation estimate explicitly.  At fixed floor, $B$ is smooth
with quadratic growth and its first two derivatives have polynomial growth.
Before contraction the point-value variance is $O(1)$ and the point-derivative
variance is $O(K)$; after multiplication by $\sigma_K^2=K^{-4}$ they are
$O(K^{-4})$ and $O(K^{-3})$.  Hence the value and derivative standard
deviations are $O(K^{-2})$ and $O(K^{-3/2})$.  Against
$|h_K|=O(K)$ and $|\nabla h_K|=O(K^2)$, the largest Taylor/Wick corrections
to the raw term are respectively
\[
 O(K^{-1})O(K^4)=O(K^3),\quad
 O(K^2)O(K^2)O(K^{-3/2})=O(K^{5/2}),\quad
 O(K^2)O(K^{-3})=O(K^{-1}).
 \tag{4.5a}
\]
Second-order coefficient corrections are $O(1)$ after multiplication by
$|\nabla h_K|^2$.  The same moment accounting bounds every lower-shell term
by $O(K^3)$, so the $O(\log K)$ shell sum is still $o(K^6)$.  The covariance
trace subtraction is $O(K^3)$, and all sextic fluctuation corrections are at
most $O(K^3)$.  Fixed-floor Gaussian tail bounds justify the Taylor remainder.
This proves (4.4)--(4.5), rather than assuming that a same-covariance
translation is already deterministic.

The falsified estimate would therefore require, for all $t>0$,
\[
 c_Ct^4\le\eta(c_Ht^2+c_6t^6).
 \tag{4.6}
\]
Optimising in $t$ yields the necessary condition
\[
 \eta\ge\eta_{\min}
 ={c_C\over2\sqrt{c_Hc_6}}>0,\qquad t^4={c_H\over c_6}.
 \tag{4.7}
\]
This contradicts the quantifier \emph{for every $\eta>0$}.  More generally,
separate entropy and sextic budgets $\alpha,\epsilon_6$ must obey
\[
 \alpha\epsilon_6\ge {c_C^2\over4c_Hc_6}.
 \tag{4.8}
\]

At the production point choose $\epsilon=3/10$.  Then
\[
 M_6={4412239\over1600000}=2.757649375,\qquad
 \eta_{\min}=2.4891432\,10^{-4}.
 \tag{4.9}
\]
Hence $\eta=10^{-4}$ gives a strict $K^6$ violation; no fixed
$C_\eta$ can repair it as $K\to\infty$.  The fixed quartic term is only
$O(K^4)$ and likewise cannot change the contradiction.

\section{Why the positive A9 theorem survives}

The covariance-normal frozen shell term contains a positive source energy
whose leading coefficient on the same ray is
\[
 {Q_{\rm fr}^{\rm source}\over L^3K^6}
 \longrightarrow c_Ft^4,\qquad c_F=4a\epsilon^2.
 \tag{5.1}
\]
The ratio forced by the witness is
\[
 \theta_{\rm ray}={c_C\over c_F}
 ={\epsilon(4-5\epsilon)\over4}.
 \tag{5.2}
\]
For $\epsilon=3/10$, $\theta_{\rm ray}=3/16=0.1875$.  Thus the commutator
is compensated by a fixed part of the frozen energy.  A9 proved the exact
determinant and entropy control for the complete covariance-normal frozen
term; it never proved the now-falsified commutator-alone estimate.

\section{Corrected open gate}

Let $Q_j^{\rm fr}$ denote the complete covariance-normal frozen shell energy,
including its trace subtraction.  The corrected gate is
\[
\boxed{\texttt{A7-CLASSII-FROZEN-ENERGY-RELATIVE-COMMUTATOR-BOUND}.}
\]
For some fixed $\theta\in(0,1)$ set
\[
 R_j^\theta=\theta Q_j^{\rm fr}+\cC_j.
 \tag{6.1}
\]
The target is to prove explicit production-budget constants
$\alpha_c,\epsilon_6,K_\theta,C_\theta$ such that, uniformly in cutoff and
all $\nu\ll\gamma_J$,
\[
 \E_\nu\sum_{j\le J}R_j^\theta
 \ge-\alpha_cH(\nu\mid\gamma_J)
 -\epsilon_6\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_6^6
 -K_\theta\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_4^4-C_\theta.
 \tag{6.2}
\]
The untouched $(1-\theta)Q_j^{\rm fr}$ remains under A9's
$\det_2$/Schatten theorem.  A closure must use the full covariance-normal
quantity, not only its raw positive square; otherwise an uncancelled
Schatten-one trace is reintroduced.  On the registered ray the exact
necessary tradeoff is
\[
 \alpha_c\epsilon_6\ge
 {[(c_C-\theta c_F)_+]^2\over4c_Hc_6}.
 \tag{6.3}
\]
Thus $\theta=3/16$ neutralises this ray without entropy or sextic expenditure;
it is not an absolute lower bound on $\theta$ when positive budgets are
allowed.  This package proves the necessary resonant tradeoff and records a
candidate closure target; it does not prove (6.2) for all fields.

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: a single Fourier mode has no negative term.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  That mode misses the exact triad resonance (3.2).

\item \textbf{Objection: the floor or $K_A$ term repairs the sign.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  The witness stays uniformly away from zero, and (2.2)
shows that $b,c,\varepsilon_\rho$ are lower order.

\item \textbf{Objection: the covariance counterterm cancels the defect.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  It is $O(K^3)$ while the defect is $O(K^6)$.

\item \textbf{Objection: the tilted law is not admissible.}
\textbf{DISMISSED.}  It is a finite-cutoff nondegenerate Gaussian with a
Cameron--Martin mean and contracted positive covariance.  It is absolutely
continuous with respect to the reference Gaussian and has the exact finite
entropy (4.2).

\item \textbf{Objection: this disproves the full Nelson estimate.}
\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  Equation (5.1) supplies a positive term discarded
by the failed decomposition.

\item \textbf{Objection: a raw-square split is sufficient.}
\textbf{UPHELD as invalid.}  The fraction must be applied to the complete
covariance-normal frozen energy or the trace divergence returns.

\item \textbf{Objection: the corrected relative bound is now proved.}
\textbf{UPHELD as an overclaim.}  Only its necessary resonance budget and a
candidate closure target are recorded; the all-field estimate is open.

\item \textbf{Objection: every corrected proof requires
$\theta\ge3/16$.} \textbf{UPHELD as false.}  That value makes the registered
ray cost-free; smaller values remain possible if the explicit budget
tradeoff (6.3) is satisfied.
\end{enumerate}
