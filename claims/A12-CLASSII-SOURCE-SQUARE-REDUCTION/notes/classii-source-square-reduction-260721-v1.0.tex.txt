\section{Purpose and scope}

This note advances the first open gate left by the A11 true-increment
determinant.  On the three-torus of side length $L=16$, write
\[
 S_j=P_{\le j},\qquad u_j=S_{j-1}\phi,
 \qquad \ell_j=G_j^*B(u_j)Du_j,
 \qquad G_j=DP_jR_\Lambda C^{1/2}.
\]
The filtration is the strict sharp rectangular-cube filtration
\[
 Q_j=\{n\in\mathbb Z^3:\ |n|_\infty\le N_j\},
 \qquad N_j=2^j,
\]
with disjoint shell projector $P_j=S_j-S_{j-1}$.  The A11 first shell has
$u_1=\phi_0=0$; changing the harmless starting index only adds a finite low
block.

The result proved here is the cutoff-uniform analytic estimate
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \sum_j\|G_j^*B(S_{j-1}\phi)DS_{j-1}\phi\|_2^2
 \le C_{\rm src}\|\phi\|_6^6.\quad}                 \tag{1.1}
\]
The constant is explicit in two precisely defined sharp-cube $L^6$ operator
norms.  This note does not supply a certified decimal enclosure of those
norms.  Consequently it does not complete T-047, prove a positive production
sextic reserve, or prove the A7 Nelson bound.  The surviving quantitative gate
is
\texttt{A12-CLASSII-SHARP-CUBE-L6-VECTOR-NORM-ENCLOSURE}.

\section{The sharp Pauli/Fierz operator constant}

Write $\psi=(z,w)\in\mathbb C^2\oplus\mathbb C$, $s=|z|^2$,
$\rho=s+|w|^2$, and $d=\rho+\varepsilon$.  For a variation
$h=(\eta,\omega)$ and the three Pauli matrices $\sigma_A$, define
\[
 m_A=z^*\sigma_Az,\qquad
 J_A=2\mathop{\rm Re}\langle\sigma_Az,\eta\rangle,
 \qquad q_A={m_A\over d},
 \qquad K_A=J_A-q_A\,d\rho .                         \tag{3.1}
\]
Here $d\rho=ds+dt$, with
$ds=2\mathop{\rm Re}\langle z,\eta\rangle$ and
$dt=2\mathop{\rm Re}(\bar w\omega)$.  Pauli completeness gives
\[
 |m|^2=s^2,\qquad m\cdot J=s\,ds,
 \qquad |J|^2\le4s|\eta|^2.                          \tag{3.2}
\]
An exact expansion, including the fixed floor, is
\[
 |K|^2=|J|^2-ds^2+\left(ds-{s\over d}d\rho\right)^2. \tag{3.3}
\]
Put $a_0=s/d$.  Since
\[
 (1-a_0)^2+a_0^2{|w|^2\over s}
 ={(|w|^2+\varepsilon)^2+s|w|^2\over d^2}\le1,       \tag{3.4}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz and $|dt|\le2|w||\omega|$ show that the last square in
(3.3) is at most $ds^2+4s|\omega|^2$.  Thus
\[
 |J|^2\le4s|h|^2,\qquad |K|^2\le4s|h|^2,
 \qquad |J\cdot K|\le4s|h|^2.                        \tag{3.5}
\]
The production quadratic form is
\[
 h^TB(\psi)h=a|J|^2+2bJ\cdot K+c|K|^2.
\]
Because $b>0$, (3.5) yields the global operator inequality
\[
 \boxed{
 0\le B(\psi)\le\beta_{\rm op}|\psi|^2I_6,
 \qquad
 \beta_{\rm op}=4(a+2b+c)=0.0423749999999894.}       \tag{3.6}
\]
It is sharp: take $w=0$ and a doublet tangent direction with $d\rho=0$
which saturates Pauli completeness.  Then $K=J$ and equality holds.  This is
substantially sharper than the older Frobenius envelope
$\beta_B=0.2564999999999359$, while remaining fully compatible with it.

\section{Exact sharp-shell decay}

Shell $j$ lies strictly outside the previous cube.  Therefore every nonzero
shell mode satisfies
\[
 |k_n|\ge\kappa_j:=\alpha_L(N_{j-1}+1).               \tag{4.1}
\]
The $N_{j-1}+1$ is load-bearing: replacing it by an ambiguous continuous
boundary loses the exact low-shell convention.  From (2.1),
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|G_j^*F\|_2^2
 &\le M_R^2\sup_{n\in Q_j\setminus Q_{j-1}}
 { |k_n|^2\over c_{\rm sym}(1+|k_n|^2)^2}\,\|F\|_2^2\\
 &\le {M_R^2\over c_{\rm sym}(1+\kappa_j^2)}\|F\|_2^2.
 \end{split}                                                   \tag{4.2}
\]
If a starting shell contains $n=0$, $D^*$ annihilates it and the remaining
nonzero modes use $|n|\ge1$.  Equation (4.2) contains $M_R^2$: the source has
one $G_j$, subsequently squared.  A9's Hilbert--Schmidt term has two covariance
legs and hence $M_R^4$; transferring that fourth power here is an error.

\section{Sharp-cube vector-valued theorem}

Define the dimensionless Hilbert-valued operator norms
\[
 M_6:=\sup_{f\ne0}{\|\sup_j|S_jf|\|_6\over\|f\|_6},  \tag{5.1}
\]
and
\[
 Q_6:=\sup_{f\ne0}
 {\left\|\left(\sum_j{ |DS_{j-1}f|^2\over1+\kappa_j^2}
             \right)^{1/2}\right\|_6\over\|f\|_6}. \tag{5.2}
\]
The Hilbert space includes six real field components and all three derivative
directions.  Both constants are finite and independent of the terminal cutoff.

For completeness, choose a product de la Vall\'ee Poussin multiplier
$V_j$ which equals one on $Q_j$ and vanishes outside a twice enlarged cube.
The maximal $V_j$ operator is controlled by iterated one-dimensional Fej\'er
maximal operators.  The remainder $R_j=S_j-V_j$ has uniformly finite
cube-annular overlap.  For arbitrary Rademacher signs, the multiplier
\[
 \sum_j\epsilon_j(1_{Q_j}-V_j)                       \tag{5.3}
\]
has uniformly bounded mixed discrete variation on every signed dyadic
rectangle.  The periodic Hilbert-valued product Marcinkiewicz theorem and
Khintchine's inequality therefore give
\[
 \left\|\left(\sum_j|R_jf|^2\right)^{1/2}\right\|_6
 \le C\|f\|_6.                                       \tag{5.4}
\]
Since $\sup_j|R_jf|$ is bounded pointwise by the square function, (5.1)
follows.  This is a dyadic smooth-plus-annular argument; it does not assume a
multiparameter Carleson theorem.

For (5.2), let $\Delta_k=S_k-S_{k-1}$.  Random signed normalized shell
multipliers
\[
 \sum_k\epsilon_k2^{-k}\alpha_L^{-1}D\Delta_k       \tag{5.5}
\]
obey the same product Marcinkiewicz condition, including the cube faces.
Moreover, for $k<j$,
\[
 {\alpha_L2^k\over\sqrt{1+\kappa_j^2}}
 \le2^{k-j+1}.                                       \tag{5.6}
\]
The scale kernel on the right has finite $\ell^1$ norm.  Vector-valued
Littlewood--Paley plus scale Young proves (5.2).  Sharp radial balls are not
covered, and shell ratios tending to one would destroy this uniform scale
summability.

There is an independent six-linear interpretation.  After the pointwise
operator bound (3.6), polarization reduces the scalar envelope to a finite
sum of six-linear forms.  With normalized geometric weights their multiplier
is
\[
 m_J(n_1,\ldots,n_6)=
 \sum_{j\le J}2^{-2j}(n_5\cdot n_6)
 \prod_{r=1}^6 1_{\{|n_r|_\infty\le2^{j-1}\}}.       \tag{5.7}
\]
If $K=\max_r|n_r|_\infty$ and $j_0$ is the first admitted scale, the geometric
sum and $|n_5\cdot n_6|\le3K^2$ give $|m_J|\le1$.
Uniform dyadic face variation then invokes the multilinear product
Marcinkiewicz/paraproduct theorem.  This second route is useful as an
adversarial check, but the maximal/square route keeps the six-real tensor
structure transparent.  Neither route expands the rational $B$ in Fourier
series.

\section{Source-square conclusion}

Apply (4.2) and (3.6) with $u_j=S_{j-1}\phi$:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \sum_j\|\ell_j\|_2^2
 &\le {M_R^2\beta_{\rm op}^2\over c_{\rm sym}}
 \int\left(\sup_j|u_j|\right)^4
 \sum_j{|Du_j|^2\over1+\kappa_j^2}\\
 &\le {M_R^2\beta_{\rm op}^2\over c_{\rm sym}}
 M_6^4Q_6^2\|\phi\|_6^6.
 \end{split}                                           \tag{6.1}
\]
Thus (1.1) holds with
\[
 \boxed{
 C_{\rm src}={\beta_{\rm op}^2\over c_{\rm sym}}
 M_R^2M_6^4Q_6^2
 =0.016570372383568618\,M_R^2M_6^4Q_6^2.}            \tag{6.2}
\]
The rational fixed-floor coefficient is harmless here because only the exact
pointwise inequality (3.6) is used.  No polynomial band-limit shortcut occurs.

\section{Production budget target, not closure}

The A11 conditional determinant contributes at most
$p^2C_{\rm src}\|\phi\|_6^6/2$ from the source, while the production sextic
contributes $p\gamma\|\phi\|_6^6/6$.  Ignoring every other cost, source-only
absorption requires
\[
 C_{\rm src}< {\gamma\over3p}.                        \tag{7.1}
\]
At $p=1.1$, $M_R=1$, and $\gamma=1.62$, this becomes
\[
 M_6^4Q_6^2<29.62571266025876.                        \tag{7.2}
\]
This is a falsifiable numerical target, not an established inequality.  If
the obsolete A10 allocation $\epsilon_6=0.25$ were retained, the target would
be $2.194497234093243$.  That allocation belongs to the refuted historical
route and is not reused.  The full budget must be recomputed only after a
certified enclosure of $M_6^4Q_6^2$.

\section{Devil's-advocate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection:} a rational $B(S_{j-1}\phi)$ is not band-limited, so
a polynomial six-linear support proof is invalid.  \textbf{Verdict:
DISMISSED.}  The main proof uses the global Pauli/Fierz operator bound before
any Fourier argument.  Rational Fourier support is never truncated.

\item \textbf{Objection:} the previous Frobenius constant was silently replaced
by a smaller guessed number.  \textbf{Verdict: DISMISSED.}  Equations
(3.1)--(3.6) prove the operator constant, include the floor and third component,
and identify a saturating tangent direction.  Both executables reconstruct it.

\item \textbf{Objection:} the shell decay misses a factor two, three, or the
physical $2\pi/L$.  \textbf{Verdict: DISMISSED.}  The shell begins at the
integer $N_{j-1}+1$, $D^*$ has symbol norm $|k|$, and all derivative directions
are already in that norm.  The executable enumerates the production symbol.

\item \textbf{Objection:} A9 requires $M_R^4$, so (6.2) is undercounted.
\textbf{Verdict: DISMISSED.}  A9's Schatten square has two $G_j$ legs.  Here
$\ell_j$ has one $G_j^*$ and its Hilbert norm is squared, giving $M_R^2$.
The primary audit rejects the fourth-power negative control.

\item \textbf{Objection:} bounded individual sharp projections imply the
maximal theorem.  \textbf{Verdict: VALID WITH MITIGATION.}  That implication
alone is false.  Section 5 instead proves a strict-dyadic smooth-plus-annular
square-function estimate using product Marcinkiewicz variation.

\item \textbf{Objection:} sampled FFT ratios certify the infinite-cutoff
constant.  \textbf{Verdict: UPHELD.}  They do not.  This package records T4,
leaves the numerical enclosure gate open, and does not call (7.2) proved.

\item \textbf{Objection:} the theorem extends unchanged to radial balls or to
nearly continuous shell ratios.  \textbf{Verdict: UPHELD.}  The cube-face
Marcinkiewicz structure and strict dyadic scale kernel are explicit hypotheses.
No such extension is claimed.
\end{enumerate}
