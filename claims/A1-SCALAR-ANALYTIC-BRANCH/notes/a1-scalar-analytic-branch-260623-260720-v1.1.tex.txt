\section*{Purpose and scope}
The theorem and tier are unchanged.  Under $Y>0$, $Z<0$, and
$m_{\rm sh}^2>0$,
\[
 K(q)=m_{\rm sh}^2+Y(|q|^2-q_\star^2)^2
 \geq m_{\rm sh}^2>0,
\]
and every periodic-cell spectral minimum satisfies
$\lambda_0(L)\geq m_{\rm sh}^2$.

The current canonical N-001 convention stores mu2_shell as this shell mass.
Its original kinetic_coefficients routine reconstructs
$r_{\rm zero}=\text{mu2_shell}+Yq_0^4$.  Therefore mu2_shell and $r_{\rm zero}$ are distinct;
the alias ``mu2=r'' is forbidden.  The obsolete contrary sentence described the
failed legacy template, not the current canonical solver manifest.

The current verifier v1.7.0 passes 14/14 overall, including the two named
positivity assertions.  This card is the scalar A2/A3/A4 positivity input.  It
does not identify the scalar `0.005` shell mass with the full-production
`0.260000000009475` anchor.

\section*{Devil's-advocate}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The lower bound fails if the shell mass is nonpositive.  The theorem is
explicitly conditional on $m_{\rm sh}^2>0$.
\item A finite torus can miss the continuum minimizing shell, but that only
raises the spectral minimum; it cannot invalidate
$\lambda_0(L)\geq m_{\rm sh}^2$.
\item The scalar and full-production anchors are numerically different and
belong to different theorem branches.  This note forbids their identification
and makes no full-production measure claim.
\end{enumerate}
