\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
This supplement makes the first previously qualitative input to the P3
positive-time solution-ball route explicit.  It applies only to the canonical
P1/P2 three-component gradient flow on the fixed $16^3$ torus, with positive
regularisers and $\eta_{\rm shell}=0$.  For each declared $H^2$ radius $R$ it
computes an upper energy $E_+(R)$ and an all-time common bound $M_2(R)$:
\[
  \|u_0\|_{H^2}\leq R \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \sup_{t\geq0}\|u(t)\|_{H^2}\leq M_2(R).                 \tag{1}
\]
It does not yet compute the positive-time $H^6$ bound, the residual/aliasing
constant $C(R,\tau,T)$, or a continuum error bar for a Sector-B solver run.

\section{Fourier embedding bound}
Use the P2-compatible norm
\[
 \|u\|_{H^2}^2=|\mathbb T^3|\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^3}
 (1+|k_n|^4)|\widehat u_n|^2.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz,
\[
 \|u\|_\infty^2\leq {S\over|\mathbb T^3|}\|u\|_{H^2}^2,
 \qquad S=\sum_n(1+|k_n|^4)^{-1}.                         \tag{2}
\]
For a max-norm shell $m$, there are $24m^2+2$ points and
$|k_n|^4\geq (2\pi/L_{\max})^4m^4$.  Summing through $M$ and bounding the
tail by the integrals of $m^{-2}$ and $m^{-4}$ gives
\[
 S\leq1+\sum_{m=1}^{M}{24m^2+2\over1+a m^4}
       +{24\over aM}+{2\over3aM^3},\qquad a=(2\pi/L_{\max})^4. \tag{3}
\]
The audit uses $M=8192$ and obtains $S\leq445.8565811405$, with tail at most
$0.1231917871$.  Since $|\mathbb T^3|=4096$, (2) yields
$\|u\|_\infty^2\leq0.1088517044\|u\|_{H^2}^2$.

\section{Energy upper and lower bounds}
The hash-pinned embedded Pauli generators have operator norm one.  Therefore
$|J_A|\leq2|u||\nabla u|$ and $|K_A|\leq4|u||\nabla u|$.  With the P1
coefficients, the absolute Class-II contribution is bounded by
\[
 (2|a|+8|b|+8|c|)\int|u|^2|\nabla u|^2,
\]
whose computed coefficient is $0.04275$.  Combining this with (2), the
linear upper coefficient $2.1566713536$, and the quartic/sextic terms gives
\[
 F(u_0)\leq E_+(R)={L_+\over2}R^2+Q_+R^4+S_+R^6.          \tag{4}
\]
Here $Q_+=0.01402826340$ and $S_+=0.003199147258$ are calculated from pinned
coefficients, not fitted from trajectories.

P2 supplies energy dissipation and
\[
 F(u)\geq {c_{H^2}\over2}\|u\|_{H^2}^2-C_Y|\mathbb T^3|,
 \quad c_{H^2}=0.2048572626782363,\quad C_Y=0.010098435197886498. \tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) prove (1) with
\[
 M_2(R)^2={2\{E_+(R)+C_Y|\mathbb T^3|\}\over c_{H^2}}.   \tag{6}
\]

\section{Devil's-advocate and mandatory adversarial review}
\textbf{Tail direction.} The shell count and integral tail in (3) are upper
bounds, so truncation cannot make $S$ too small.  \textbf{Class-II factors.}
The $2,8,8$ coefficients follow from the hash-checked unit-norm Pauli
convention.  \textbf{Norm convention.} The fourth-power Fourier weight is
the P2 coercivity norm.  \textbf{Coarseness.} $M_2$ is deliberately large
because the global Young lower bound includes the whole torus volume; it is an
enclosure, not a sharp dynamical prediction.  \textbf{Limit cases.} This does
not cover a nonzero shell term, zero floors, infinite volume, or the historical
non-variational backend.  \textbf{No smoothing shortcut.} An $H^2$ envelope
does not prove a computable $H^6$ envelope.  External review of the Class-II
inequality and explicit semigroup/Moser constants remains invited.

\section{What remains open}
