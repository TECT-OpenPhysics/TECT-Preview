\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
This supplement closes the executable independent-reference part of P3 item 1.
It does not identify a same-backend fine-grid residual with the continuum
residual. Instead it prolongs a declared analytic field from $S_N$ to two
oversampling grids, differentiates an independently implemented portable
trapezoid-quadrature functional, and projects the result back to $S_N$.

\section{Independent quadrature proxy}
Let $I_M:S_N\to S_M$ be Fourier zero-padding and $P_N$ the real-$L^2$ Fourier
projector. The computed reference is
\[
 R_{N,M}^{\rm IQ}(\Psi_N)=P_N R_M^{P}(I_M\Psi_N),
\]
where $R_M^P$ is the real gradient of the portable functional implementation.
For the analytic, nonzero-density manufactured field, the density floor keeps
all rational Class-II coefficients smooth. Periodic trapezoid quadrature then
converges superalgebraically for smooth periodic integrands and exponentially
for this analytic test input. The M24/M32 difference is retained as observed
reference uncertainty, not hidden as exact continuum data.

\section{Executed result}
For N8, N12, and N16, exact Fourier prolongation to M24/M32 differs from direct
analytic sampling by at most $3.0848\,10^{-16}$. The portable and canonical
gradients agree exactly on every tested target and oversampling grid. The
projected M24/M32 uncertainty is at most $2.5180\,10^{-14}$. The canonical
collocation residual differs from the independent projected proxy by
\[
 5.3301196\,10^{-6},\qquad4.2482266\,10^{-10},\qquad6.6668345\,10^{-12},
\]
with observed orders 23.2750 and 14.4414. All 11 assertions pass.

\section{Devil's-advocate and mandatory adversarial review}
\textbf{Sign and factor.} The portable code differentiates its own real
functional in explicit real coordinates; it does not reuse the canonical
residual. The canonical comparison then detects a factor or pairing mismatch.
\textbf{Convention.} Fourier prolongation divides forward coefficients by the
source count and multiplies inverse reconstruction by the target count; the
same centered convention is used by $P_N$. \textbf{Units.} The fixed torus and
dimensionless P1 normalization are unchanged. \textbf{Convergence.} Two
independent oversampling resolutions measure the quadrature tail. \textbf{Hardcode
masking.} No measured residual, rate, or reference value is an input; grids and
thresholds are manifest inputs. \textbf{Limit cases.} This audit is analytic,
nonzero-density, eta-shell-zero, and CPU complex128 only. It does not bound
arbitrary H2 P2 data at time zero, floor removal, GPU behavior, or a historical
solver. External review of the periodic quadrature-to-continuum passage is
explicitly invited.

\section{Remaining analytic passage}
P2 gives $C^\infty$ regularity for every positive time, but the present result
does not yet supply a uniform Sobolev/analytic-norm bound on a P2 solution ball
that turns the observed quadrature tail into a theorem. That bound, plus a
dealiased/oversampled evolution comparison and the CUDA matrix, is required
before Sector-B solver outputs may be described as controlled continuum PDE
approximations.
