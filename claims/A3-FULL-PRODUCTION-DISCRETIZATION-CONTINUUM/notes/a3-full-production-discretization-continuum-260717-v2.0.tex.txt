\section{Continuum and discrete objects}
The field is $\Psi:\mathbb T^3\to\mathbb C^3$, treated as six real components,
with pairing
\[
 \langle u,v\rangle_{\mathbb R}=\operatorname{Re}
 \int_{\mathbb T^3}u^\dagger v.
\]
Let $F$ be the P1 functional, $R=\nabla_{\mathbb R}F$, and let the P2 flow be
$\partial_t\Psi=-R(\Psi)$.  The linear part has symbol
\[
 r+Z|k|^2+Y|k|^4+M_{\rm fam}+k_{\rm lock}(I-P_0),
\]
while the lower-order part contains the quartic/sextic potential and the
positive-definite Class-II $J$--$K$ quadratic form.

For the Fourier cube $S_N$, $P_N$ is the real-$L^2$ projector.  The exact
Galerkin residual is $P_NR(\Psi_N)$.  The canonical executable residual
$R_N^C$ is instead the real gradient of the periodic collocation energy.  This
package never identifies these two objects: their difference is the measured
aliasing error.

remains qualitative because P2 uses unnamed norm-equivalence, local Moser,
