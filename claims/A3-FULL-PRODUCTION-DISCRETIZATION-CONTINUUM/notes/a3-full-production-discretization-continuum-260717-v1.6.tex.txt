\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
The claim is promoted from T3 COMPUTABLE-SCAFFOLD to T4 STRONG-EVIDENCE under
TSv2.  The promotion is deliberately limited to the frozen P3 evidence scope:
the fixed $16^3$ torus, the hash-pinned P1/P2 canonical functional,
$\eta_{\rm shell}=0$, the declared grids and manufactured fields, and the one
recorded CUDA device/precision matrix.  It is not a continuum-error theorem
and does not close the gate \texttt{A3-FULL-DISCRETIZATION-CLOSURE}.

\section{Why T4 is justified}
TSv2 defines T4 as numerics plus analytic bounds plus a partial audit, while
explicitly stating that T4 is not a theorem.  P3 now has all three components.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The spatial residual, real energy-gradient identity, manufactured
orders, finite-time trajectories, and Hessian/Ritz tests are executed at their
frozen grids.
\item The independent portable functional supplies a second implementation of
the energy/residual path, and the CPU/CUDA complex128/complex64 matrix is closed
on the recorded RTX 5070 Laptop GPU environment.
\item P2 smoothing gives the positive-time solution-ball rate
$C(R,\tau,T)N^{-2}$, and Stage 7 makes its all-time $H^2$ energy-ball input
$M_2(R)$ explicit for the declared radii.
\item The hash-bound integrated verifier checks the immutable evidence matrix
at 79/79 PASS.
\end{enumerate}

These facts exceed a mere runnable scaffold.  They are strong convergent
evidence with analytic control in a pinned scope, which is exactly T4.

\section{Why T5 is not justified}
The constant $C(R,\tau,T)$ is still unevaluated because the P2 proof uses
unnamed norm-equivalence, Moser, Holder, and endpoint-Duhamel constants.  A
dealiased finite-time Galerkin evolution estimate has also not been propagated
from the residual bound.  Therefore the package cannot attach a controlled
continuum error bar to arbitrary P2 trajectories or historical N32/N64/N128
Sector-B runs.  The open gate and T5 blocker remain explicit.

\section{Devil's-advocate tier review}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Objection: the N8/N12/N16 manufactured-field convergence is a
continuum theorem.} \textbf{UPHELD as false.}  It is strong numerical evidence
only; the solution-ball constant is not enclosed.
\item \textbf{Objection: one CUDA device proves hardware-independent
equivalence.} \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION.}  The claim scope names the exact
Torch/CUDA environment and makes no all-device assertion.
\item \textbf{Objection: the Hessian/Ritz test proves the full Sector-B
spectrum.} \textbf{UPHELD as false.}  The evidence covers one homogeneous
invariant block and one declared nonuniform fixed Ritz subspace.
\item \textbf{Objection: the qualitative $N^{-2}$ rate already closes T5.}
\textbf{UPHELD as false.}  T5 requires the computable constant, the dealiased
finite-time passage, and a reproduction package in the final pinned scope.
\item \textbf{Objection: the large $M_2(R)$ values are fitted trajectory
outputs.} \textbf{DISMISSED.}  They are upper enclosures derived from the
Fourier embedding, P1 coefficients, and P2 coercivity; no trajectory fit is
used.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Next quantitative gate}

Moser, Holder, and semigroup constants needed to turn $M_2(R)$ into an explicit

valid enclosure; sharpness is secondary to correctness.  Afterward the
residual constant must be propagated through a dealiased finite-time evolution
estimate before a T5 review.
