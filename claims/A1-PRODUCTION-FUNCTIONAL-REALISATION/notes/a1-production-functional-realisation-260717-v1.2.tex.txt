\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
The manifest-defined three-component complex functional is now implemented in
a standalone, hash-pinned backend.  A separate verifier reproduces the discrete
variational identities on spectral torus grids $N=4,6,8$.  This closes the P1
technical implementation-and-verification task at T4 strong evidence.  It does

\section*{2. Frozen functional}
With $\rho=\Psi^\dagger\Psi$, $m_A=\Psi^\dagger T_A\Psi$,
$J_A=\nabla m_A$, and
$K_A=J_A-(m_A/(\rho+10^{-12}))\nabla\rho$, the standalone backend computes
\[
 F=F_{\rm Braz}+F_{\rm family}+F_{\rm lock}+F_{\rm shell}
 +\int\left(\frac\lambda4\rho^2+\frac\gamma6\rho^3\right)
 +\sum_A\int\left(\frac a2|J_A|^2
 +b\operatorname{Re}(\bar J_A\cdot K_A)+\frac c2|K_A|^2\right),
\]
where $a=c_{JJ}\alpha_X^2/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$,
$b=c_{JK}\alpha_X\beta_X/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$, and
$c=c_{KK}\beta_X^2/(M_X^2+10^{-12})$.  The implementation includes the
Brazovskii linear term, family masses, internal lock, shell bias, quartic and
sextic terms, all three Class-II coefficients, the density regularizer, and the
manifest projector conventions.
