\section*{1. Purpose and scope}
This record freezes and audits the current three-component complex working-branch
functional on a periodic three-torus.  It does not enlarge the pure-Brazovskii
scalar-slice T5 claim.  The authority is the external backend and configuration
pinned by SHA-256 in \texttt{production\_functional\_manifest.json}.  The audit
checks whether the declared energy, executable residual, and executable Hessian
are mutually compatible under the stated real inner product.

\section*{2. Frozen setting}
For $\Psi\in\mathbb C^3$ on a spectral grid on $\mathbb T^3$, use
\[
 \langle u,v\rangle_{\mathbb R}=dV\,\operatorname{Re}\sum_{a,x}\bar u_a(x)v_a(x).
\]
The frozen declared energy contains the Brazovskii quadratic term, family mass,
internal projector lock $P_0=z_0z_0^\dagger/(z_0^\dagger z_0)$, shell bias,
quartic and sextic density terms, and the Class-II $J,K$ terms.  The manifest
gives the complete formulas, all coefficients, $\rho+10^{-12}$ regularisation,
and the separate solver floor $\rho\geq\rho_\star$.

\section*{3. Audit procedure}
The standalone verifier independently reconstructs the declared energy with
Torch autodiff and compares it with finite differences.  It then imports the
hash-pinned external residual and Hessian and evaluates
\[
 DF[\Psi](v)=\langle R[\Psi],v\rangle_{\mathbb R},\qquad
 DR[\Psi]v=H[\Psi]v,\qquad
 \langle u,Hv\rangle_{\mathbb R}=\langle Hu,v\rangle_{\mathbb R}.
\]
It covers zero, homogeneous, random, commensurate $q_0$-shell, and
Class-II-active fields at three finite-difference steps on $N=4$.

\section*{4. Current result and named obstruction}
The audit is designed to pass an independent energy-derivative control and to
expose the current working-branch mismatch.  Already in the scalar core,
$(\lambda/2)\rho^2+(\gamma/3)\rho^3$ has real gradient
$2\lambda\rho\Psi+2\gamma\rho^2\Psi$, while the residual uses half those
nonlinear terms.  Independently, the declared Class-II energy uses $c_{JJ}$ and
$c_{KK}$, whereas the residual uses $c_{JJ}$ and $c_{JK}$; the pinned values
$c_{JK}=0.1$ and $c_{KK}=0.15$ are unequal.  Therefore the full production
configuration does not currently establish a single variational functional.
This is a diagnosis, not a rejection of the scalar-slice kernel result.

\section*{5. Devil's-advocate}
(alpha) \emph{``This is only a finite-difference artifact.''} DISMISSED for the
diagnosis only if the independent autodiff derivative and multi-step finite
differences agree while the imported residual disagrees; all are reported.

(beta) \emph{``Complex fields may use a different factor-of-two convention.''}
DISMISSED only relative to the explicitly frozen real pairing: both the
autodiff conversion and the finite-difference directional derivative use that
same convention.

(gamma) \emph{``The scalar T5 manifest has been invalidated.''} VALID only as a
scope warning, with mitigation: its pure scalar slice has all extra terms zero
and is tested as a separate control; no existing tier is changed here.
