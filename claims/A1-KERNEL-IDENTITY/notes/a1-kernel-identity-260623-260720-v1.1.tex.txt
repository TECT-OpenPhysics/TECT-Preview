\section*{Purpose and scope}
The algebraic theorem and scope are unchanged from v1.0.  This issue records
the already enacted operator approval and removes the obsolete sign-off action.
For $Y>0$ and $Z<0$,
\[
 r+Z|q|^2+Y|q|^4
 =m_{\rm sh}^2+Y(|q|^2-q_\star^2)^2,
\]
where $q_\star^2=-Z/(2Y)$,
$m_{\rm sh}^2=r-Z^2/(4Y)$, and
$r=m_{\rm sh}^2+Yq_\star^4$.  Thus the zero-momentum and shell masses are
distinct.

The current verifier v1.7.0 passes 14/14 overall.  Its named
kernel_identity_complete_square assertion has worst residual
$5.68\times10^{-14}$.  The remaining thirteen assertions belong to the shared
A1 production-manifest and scalar-positivity audit; they do not enlarge this
identity claim.

nonzero shell shift.  The displayed identity and the verifier test the two
values separately.
\item The identity alone does not imply positivity.  Positivity remains a
separate conditional statement in A1-SCALAR-ANALYTIC-BRANCH.
\item Updating an approval field could accidentally enlarge the theorem.
This issue changes administrative state and naming only; the algebraic scope
and T6 tier are unchanged.
\end{enumerate}
