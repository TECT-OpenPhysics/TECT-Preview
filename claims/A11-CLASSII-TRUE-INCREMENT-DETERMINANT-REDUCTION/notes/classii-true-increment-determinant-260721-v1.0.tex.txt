\section{The past-energy upper form is impossible}

Let $N_j=2^j$, let $\gamma_J$ be the terminal Gaussian, and write
\[
 E_J=\sum_{j\le J}
 q_{B(\phi_{j-1})}(D\phi_{j-1}).                     \tag{2.1}
\]
The candidate A10 upper form was
\[
 \E_\nu E_J\le \alpha_d\cH(\nu\mid\gamma_J)
 +\epsilon_d\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_6^6
 +K_d\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_4^4+C_d,                    \tag{2.2}
\]
with constants independent of $J$.

Set $\nu=\gamma_J$.  Then the entropy term vanishes.  Marginal consistency
identifies $\phi_{j-1}$ with the cutoff-$N_{j-1}$ Gaussian.  The Gaussian
integration-by-parts identity already fixed in A7 gives
\[
 \E_{\gamma_J}q_{B(\phi_{j-1})}(D\phi_{j-1})
 =\E_{\gamma_J}t_{\Gamma_{\le j-1}}
                  (B(\phi_{j-1})).                   \tag{2.3}
\]
If $e_N$ denotes this expectation per unit volume, the A6 sharp-cube theorem
states
\[
 {e_N\over N}\longrightarrow
 \kappa_{\rm II}=\delta_{\rm cube}\E W_\varepsilon(X_\infty)>0. \tag{2.4}
\]
Strict positivity follows from positivity of the production $Q_{\rm II}$
and from $W_\varepsilon$ being nonzero on an open set of the nondegenerate
Gaussian law.  Therefore
\[
 {\E_{\gamma_J}E_J\over L^3N_J}
 ={1\over N_J}\sum_{j\le J}e_{N_{j-1}}
 \longrightarrow \kappa_{\rm II}>0,                 \tag{2.5}
\]
because $\sum_{m=0}^{J-1}2^m=2^J-1$.

On the other hand $A(k)^{-1}=O(|k|^{-4})$ in dimension three, so the point
covariances $C_N$ converge in trace.  For a circular complex Gaussian vector
$X$ with eigenvalues $c_a$ of $C_N$,
\[
 \E|X|^4=(\Tr C_N)^2+\Tr C_N^2,\qquad
 \E|X|^6=(\Tr C_N)^3+3\Tr C_N\Tr C_N^2+2\Tr C_N^3. \tag{2.6}
\]
Thus the two endpoint moments on the right of (2.2) remain uniformly
bounded, while the left side grows like $L^3\kappa_{\rm II}N_J$.  This is a
contradiction for every fixed choice of
$\alpha_d,\epsilon_d,K_d,C_d$.  We register it as
\texttt{F-2026-07-21-A10-PAST-ENERGY-UPPER-FORM}.

\section{Exact true-increment telescope}

Put $x_j=\phi_{j-1}$ and $z_j=\phi_j$.  A10 proved
\[
 Q_j^{\fr}+\cC_j
 =V_j-V_{j-1}+q_{B(x_j)}(Dx_j).                       \tag{3.1}
\]
Define
\[
 \boxed{I_j:=Q_j^{\fr}-q_{B(x_j)}(Dx_j).}             \tag{3.2}
\]
Subtracting the last term of (3.1) is an exact identity, not an estimate:
\[
 \boxed{I_j+\cC_j=V_j-V_{j-1}.}                       \tag{3.3}
\]
The empty initial scale is fixed by
$\phi_0=0$, $\Gamma_{\le0}=0$, hence $V_0=0$.  Consequently
\[
 \boxed{V_J=\sum_{j\le J}(I_j+\cC_j).}               \tag{3.4}
\]
There is no residual $E_J$ in (3.4).

Condition on the past $x_j$.  In the real shell Hilbert space let
\[
 A_j=M_{B(x_j)}^{1/2}G_j,\quad
 r_j=M_{B(x_j)}^{1/2}Dx_j,\quad
 T_j=A_j^*A_j,\quad \ell_j=A_j^*r_j.                 \tag{3.5}
\]
For a standard real Gaussian shell coordinate $\xi_j$,
\[
 I_j={1\over2}
 \{\langle\xi_j,T_j\xi_j\rangle-\Tr T_j\}
 +\langle\ell_j,\xi_j\rangle.                       \tag{3.6}
\]

\section{Exact determinant and the positive source}

At finite cutoff $T_j\ge0$.  Diagonalising $T_j$ and completing the Gaussian
square gives, for $p>0$,
\[
\begin{split}
 \log\E_j e^{-pI_j}
 ={}&{1\over2}\{p\Tr T_j-\log\det(I+pT_j)\}\\
 &+{p^2\over2}
 \langle\ell_j,(I+pT_j)^{-1}\ell_j\rangle\\
 ={}&-{1\over2}\log\det{}_2(I+pT_j)
 +{p^2\over2}
 \langle\ell_j,(I+pT_j)^{-1}\ell_j\rangle.
\end{split}\tag{4.1}
\]
The sign of the second line is positive.  The scalar inequality
$u-\log(1+u)\le u^2/2$ for $u\ge0$ yields
\[
 \log\E_j e^{-pI_j}
 \le {p^2\over4}\|T_j\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2
     +{p^2\over2}\|\ell_j\|^2.                      \tag{4.2}
\]

The positive source is unavoidable.  In one dimension take $T=\tau>0$ and
$\ell=\sqrt\tau q$.  Its exact contribution is
\[
 {p^2\tau q^2\over2(1+p\tau)}.                       \tag{4.3}
\]
For fixed $\tau$, this diverges quadratically as $|q|\to\infty$, while
$\|T\|_{\mathfrak S_2}$ remains fixed.  Hence no bound depending only on the
Hilbert--Schmidt cost can replace (4.2).  The crude operator inequality that
bounds (4.3) by a multiple of the past energy merely recreates the already
refuted $E_J$ route.

Here
\[
 \ell_j=G_j^*B(x_j)Dx_j.                              \tag{4.4}
\]
A9 controls $\sum_j\|T_j\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2$ by a terminal quartic.  The
new load-bearing analytic statement is instead
\[
 \sum_j\|G_j^*B(P_{\le j-1}\phi)
 D P_{\le j-1}\phi\|_2^2
 \le C_{\rm src}\|\phi\|_6^6.                       \tag{4.5}
\]
Equation (4.5) is the gate
\texttt{A11-CLASSII-ADAPTED-SOURCE-SQUARE-BOUND}.  The rational coefficient
$B$ is not band-limited; a valid proof must use an actual sharp-cube
maximal/square-function or multilinear paraproduct theorem, not a polynomial
support shortcut.  This note does not prove the source-square bound.

\section{The relative variable must also change}

For $s=1-\theta$, define
\[
 \widetilde R_j^\theta:=\theta I_j+\cC_j.             \tag{5.1}
\]
Then (3.4) becomes
\[
 V_J=\sum_j\{sI_j+\widetilde R_j^\theta\}.            \tag{5.2}
\]
The A10 historical variable was
$R_j^\theta=\theta Q_j^{\fr}+\cC_j$, and
\[
 \widetilde R_j^\theta
 =R_j^\theta-\theta q_{B(x_j)}(Dx_j).                 \tag{5.3}
\]
Therefore the old relative log-Laplace gate cannot simply be renamed or
reused.  After (4.5), the next theorem must be stated for
$\theta I_j+\cC_j$ and is named
\texttt{A11-CLASSII-TRUE-INCREMENT-STABILISED-LOG-LAPLACE}.

If (4.5) holds and the A9 quartic constant is $C_{\rm fr}$, the determinant
part has the conditional entropy budget
\[
 \E_\nu s\sum_j I_j\ge-\alpha_f\cH(\nu\mid\gamma_J)
 -{s^2C_{\rm fr}\over4\alpha_f}\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_4^4
 -{s^2C_{\rm src}\over2\alpha_f}\E_\nu\|\phi_J\|_6^6. \tag{5.4}

This is only a conditional consequence of the open source-square gate.
The production sextic remainder must be recomputed with the explicit

\section{Devil's-advocate review}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DISMISSED -- perhaps positive $E_J$ helps a lower bound.}
The actual endpoint is the shell sum minus $E_J$.  Its positivity has the
wrong direction.  The base-Gaussian limit (2.5) makes this quantitative.

\item \textbf{DISMISSED -- the contradiction may confuse density and
integral.}  Equations (2.4)--(2.5) explicitly restore the volume $L^3$.
Both sides of (2.2) use torus integrals; the common volume does not alter the
linear cutoff contradiction.

\item \textbf{VALID WITH MITIGATION -- the initial telescope can hide a
boundary term.}  We fix $\phi_0=0$ and $\Gamma_{\le0}=0$, hence $V_0=0$.
For a different initial scale, (3.4) must include $V_0$.

\item \textbf{DISMISSED -- the determinant source should be negative.}
Completing the square gives the positive sign in (4.1).  Two independent
Gauss--Hermite routes verify it and a sign flip fails their assertions.

\item \textbf{UPHELD -- an HS-only estimate is insufficient.}  The scalar
family (4.3) is an exact counterexample.  The adapted source must be retained.

\item \textbf{UPHELD -- A10's individual $L^4$ projector bound closes
(4.5).}  It does not.  Equation (4.5) needs an $L^6$ vector-valued maximal or
multilinear estimate with a cutoff-uniform constant.

\item \textbf{UPHELD -- rational $B$ has finite polynomial Fourier support.}
It does not: the fixed positive denominator prevents a singularity but does
not make $B(P_{\le j}\phi)$ band-limited.  No support claim is used here.

\item \textbf{UPHELD -- the old relative gate can be reused unchanged.}
Equation (5.3) shows the missing past energy exactly.  A new relative theorem
is required.

\item \textbf{UPHELD as an overclaim -- this closes A7 or promotes T5/T6.}
It does neither.  The honest result is a scoped T4 structural reduction with
two explicit open gates.  Operator reproduction may later review T5 without
enlarging the statement; T6 and T7 are excluded.
\end{enumerate}
