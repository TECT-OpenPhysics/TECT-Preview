\section*{1. Purpose and scope}

This v1.1 note closes only the first analytic audit of the full-production P2
route.  It does not change the older scalar claim
\texttt{A2-PDE-WELLPOSED}, and it does not promote the full claim beyond T4.
The scope remains the real-$L^2$ gradient flow of the canonical P1 reference
functional on the fixed three-torus, with three complex components, positive
regularisers, and $\eta_{\rm shell}=0$.  The historical backend remains a
non-variational proxy and is excluded.

The purpose is precise: expand the Class-II Euler--Lagrange map in six real
coordinates, prove that it has spatial order at most two, and establish the
local $H^2\to L^2$ Lipschitz estimate required by the fourth-order semigroup
local-existence route.  The Galerkin energy passage and the positive-time
smoothing bootstrap are not addressed here; their two gates remain open.

\section*{2. Six-real-coordinate Class-II density}

Write
\[
 u=(\operatorname{Re}\Psi_1,\operatorname{Re}\Psi_2,
 \operatorname{Re}\Psi_3,\operatorname{Im}\Psi_1,
 \operatorname{Im}\Psi_2,\operatorname{Im}\Psi_3)\in\mathbb R^6.
\]
For each P1 Hermitian generator $T_A$, let $A_A$ denote its real symmetric
realification.  Then
\[
 \rho=u^Tu,\qquad m_A=u^TA_Au,\qquad
 q_A={m_A\over\rho+\epsilon_\rho}.
\]
With $w_j=\partial_j u$, define
\[
 p_A(u)=2A_Au,\qquad s_A(u)=2(A_A-q_A(u)I_6)u.
\]
The identities
\[
 \partial_jm_A=p_A(u)^Tw_j,\qquad
 K_{A,j}=s_A(u)^Tw_j
\]
turn the full Class-II density into
\[
 G_{II}(u,\nabla u)={1\over2}\sum_{j=1}^3w_j^TB(u)w_j,
\]
where
\[
 B(u)=\sum_A\left[a\,p_Ap_A^T+
 b(p_As_A^T+s_Ap_A^T)+c\,s_As_A^T\right].                 \tag{2.1}
\]
Here $a,b,c$ are exactly the production coefficients derived from the P1
manifest.  The matrix is symmetric.  Its definition is an identity, rather
than an approximation or an expansion around a nonzero background.

The regulariser is decisive: $\epsilon_\rho>0$ makes $q_A$, hence $B$, a
$C^\infty$ function on all of $\mathbb R^6$.  Moreover
\[
 |q_A(u)|\le \|A_A\|_{\rm op}{\rho\over\rho+\epsilon_\rho}
 \le\|A_A\|_{\rm op}.                                    \tag{2.2}
\]
For every finite $R$, the functions $B$, $DB$, and $D^2B$ have finite
supremum norms on $\{|u|\le R\}$.  No inverse power of $\rho$ is present
without the positive $\epsilon_\rho$ denominator.

\section*{3. Explicit Euler--Lagrange expansion}

For a density of the form (2.1), componentwise differentiation gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (N_{II}(u))_\gamma={}&-B_{\gamma\beta}(u)\Delta u_\beta\\
 &+\sum_{j=1}^3\left[{1\over2}\partial_\gamma B_{\alpha\beta}(u)
              -\partial_\alpha B_{\gamma\beta}(u)\right]
       (\partial_ju_\alpha)(\partial_ju_\beta),
 \end{aligned}                                               \tag{3.1}
\]
with repeated real-coordinate indices summed from $1$ to $6$.  Thus there are
only two types of terms:
\[
                 B(u)\nabla^2u,qquad
                 C(u)[\nabla u,\nabla u],                    \tag{3.2}
\]
where $C$ is a smooth tensor built from $DB$.  In particular, Class-II
contributes no spatial derivative of order three or four.  Since the pinned
Brazovskii coefficient is $Y=1>0$, the fourth-order linear operator remains
the unique principal order-four part of the PDE.

For auditability, the derivative used in (3.1) is completely explicit.  For
a direction $h\in\mathbb R^6$,
\[
 Dq_A(u)[h]={2(h^TA_Au)(\rho+\epsilon_\rho)-2m_A(h^Tu)
                 \over(\rho+\epsilon_\rho)^2},               \tag{3.3}
\]
\[
 Dp_A(u)[h]=2A_Ah,
 \qquad Ds_A(u)[h]=2(A_A-q_AI_6)h-2Dq_A(u)[h]u.               \tag{3.4}
\]
Applying the product rule to (2.1) gives $DB(u)[h]$ with no hidden
differential operator.  Equations (3.1)--(3.4) are the requested termwise
real-coordinate expansion.

\section*{4. Local H2-to-L2 Lipschitz estimate}

Fix an $H^2$ ball of radius $R$ on the fixed three-torus.  Sobolev embedding
gives
\[
 H^2\hookrightarrow L^\infty,\qquad H^2\hookrightarrow W^{1,6}.
\]
Equation (2.2) and the bounded-set smoothness of $B,DB,D^2B$ give constants
$C_R$ for all coefficient differences.  From (3.2),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|B(u)\Delta u-B(v)\Delta v\|_{L^2}
 &\le C_R\|u-v\|_{H^2},\\
 \|C(u)[\nabla u,\nabla u]-C(v)[\nabla v,\nabla v]\|_{L^2}
 &\le C_R\|u-v\|_{H^2}.
 \end{aligned}                                               \tag{4.1}
\]
For the second line, each product of two first derivatives is in $L^3$ by
$L^6\cdot L^6\subset L^3$, and $L^3\hookrightarrow L^2$ on the fixed
finite-volume torus.  The differences split into one derivative difference
and one bounded $W^{1,6}$ factor, plus a coefficient difference controlled by
$\|u-v\|_{L^\infty}$.  This proves
\[
 \|N_{II}(u)-N_{II}(v)\|_{L^2}
 \le C_R\|u-v\|_{H^2}.                                      \tag{4.2}
\]
The local potential has real gradient
\[
 N_{\rm pot}(u)=\lambda\rho u+\gamma\rho^2u,
\]
and obeys the same estimate because $H^2(\mathbb T^3)$ is an algebra.  The
family and lock terms are bounded linear maps.  Hence the full lower-order
map $N:H^2\to L^2$ is locally Lipschitz in the pinned canonical scope.

This closes the mapping input for the standard analytic-semigroup local
solution construction.  It does not itself establish the later Galerkin
chain rule, global continuation, continuous dependence, or smoothing claims.

\section*{6. Devil's-advocate}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{``$K_A$ divides by $\rho$, so the zero field is singular.''}
DISMISSED in the stated scope: the pinned functional has
$\rho+\epsilon_\rho$ with $\epsilon_\rho=10^{-12}>0$; (3.3) is defined at
$u=0$ and the executable includes that case.
\item \emph{``The $C(u)[\nabla u,\nabla u]$ term loses one derivative.''}
DISMISSED: $H^2\hookrightarrow W^{1,6}$ makes the product $L^3$, and on the
fixed torus $L^3\hookrightarrow L^2$.  The difference estimate needs only
$D^2B$ on an $L^\infty$-bounded ball.
\item \emph{``Realification may hide a factor of two or the complex
cross-term.''} DISMISSED by the independent complex-to-real density identity
and local Euler--Lagrange jet test, both of which pass with the recorded
errors above.
\item \emph{``This proves global well-posedness.''} UPHELD as an invalid
reading.  The Galerkin chain rule/energy passage and smoothing bootstrap are
still separate open gates.
\item \emph{``The conclusion transfers to the historical solver.''} UPHELD
as an invalid reading.  The audit is only for the canonical P1 variational
functional; the historical backend remains explicitly non-variational.
\end{itemize}
