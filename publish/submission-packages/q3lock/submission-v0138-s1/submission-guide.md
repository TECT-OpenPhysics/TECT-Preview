## Manuscript and contribution

Phase Coexistence in an Eight-Component Q3-Locked Quantum Anharmonic Crystal
studies a three-dimensional fixed-spacing lattice with eight real components
and a non-radial quartic locking interaction on the internal cube. Seven proof
blocks connect finite-volume heat traces, thermodynamic pressure, tempered DLR
states, continuous-loop association, spatial reflection positivity and
infrared bounds, a collective lower bound, and a strict source cusp with two
parity-related DLR states under an explicit sufficient regime.

The candidate model-specific contribution is the positive-locking collective
bound and its explicit loop/DLR composition. Standard inequalities, the
threshold's functional form and permission for non-radial finite FSS priors
are not claimed as new. No novelty or priority certificate is supplied.
