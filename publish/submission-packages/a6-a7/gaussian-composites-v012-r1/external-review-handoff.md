calculus or stochastic composites, and constructive-field stability bounds.
The reviewer should receive the final PDF, TeX, PLAN.md, dependency audit,
proof audit, frozen input snapshot, executable checks and their hash inventory.

certification. The core question is whether the random-coefficient centered

please examine the finite IBP identity, weighted kernel strong convergence,
uniform coefficient expectation limit, and four-cutoff polarization.

Other requested checks: the current's nonlinear reconstruction and localization;
the sharp slope extrema including the inactive-third-component escape;
the exact real/complex normalization; and the deterministic/background
operator domains and constants. Assess novelty relative to the named primary
comparators and identify any more direct earlier theorem.
In particular, compare Xu Theorem1.4 against the unrescaled mixed
value/derivative target, rather than treating nonpolynomiality as new.
