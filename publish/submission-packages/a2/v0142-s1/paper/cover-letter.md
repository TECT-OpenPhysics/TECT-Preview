# Cover letter - venue-neutral submission copy

Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Global Well-Posedness,
Neutral-State Rejection, and Ensemble-Induced Shell Coexistence in a
Regularized Multicomponent Brazovskii Functional", by Jusang Lee, for
editorial assessment as an original mathematical research article.

The manuscript studies one explicitly specified regularized functional for
three complex fields on a fixed periodic three-torus. It combines an
all-`H^2` gradient-flow theorem, an exact neutral-state rejection and decay
argument, and a distinct imposed-ensemble shell-coexistence result. The
contribution proposed for assessment is this model-specific conjunction,
not a claim that semigroup methods, constrained minimization or coexistence
methods are individually new.

The theorem concerns the displayed mathematical functional. It makes no
claim to derive a physical vacuum, a conserved physical charge, a quantum
or infinite-volume limit, BCC structure, or cosmology. Internal exact and
structural checks accompany the proof text; those checks are not a substitute
for mathematical peer review.

No external mathematical review or signed specialist novelty review has
been obtained. The enclosed manuscript has undergone AI-assisted internal
criticism and reproducibility checks. AI assistance also supported research
discussion, proof-text drafting, verification-code development and document
preparation, as disclosed in the manuscript. The named author remains
responsible for its content and final submission.

The package includes the manuscript PDF and source, a verification protocol,
input and artifact hashes, and supplementary scope and applicability audits.
The author welcomes referee criticism of proof steps and the precise relation
to prior work.

Sincerely,

Jusang Lee
