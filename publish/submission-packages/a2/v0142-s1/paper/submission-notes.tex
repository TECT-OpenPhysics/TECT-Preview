\section*{1. What is being submitted}
\textit{Global Well-Posedness, Neutral-State Rejection, and Ensemble-Induced
Shell Coexistence in a Regularized Multicomponent Brazovskii Functional.}

The paper concerns one explicitly printed regularized classical functional on
the fixed side-16 three-torus, with three complex (six real) field components.
The positive density floor, coefficients, pairing, Laplacian convention and
function spaces are part of the theorem, not optional implementation choices.

The proposed contribution is the conjunction of:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item Global well-posedness for all $H^2$ data, energy dissipation and
positive-time smoothness of the displayed gradient flow.
\item Exact neutral coercivity and radial rejection, unique zero critical
point/global minimizer, and exponential decay.
\item A separately imposed charge/chemical-potential ensemble with explicit
finite-shell saturation and zero/nonzero coexistence.
\end{enumerate}
Version 0.1.42 retains the v0.1.41 mathematical body and changes only
publication-status/disclosure prose and submission materials.
