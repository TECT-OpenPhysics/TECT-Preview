Five repairs improve what a referee can check directly:

1. **Fourier normalization (P-01/P-02/P-12): VALID, repaired.** The old
   Parseval equality omitted the coefficient convention. With ordinary
   Lebesgue measure, unnormalized Fourier coefficients would introduce a

   shows why a constant-density plane wave has `Q=V*rho/2`. Existing charge
   numbers were consistent with this convention; no number was retuned.
2. **Midpoint continuity (P-07): VALID proof-detail omission, repaired.**
   The previous reference to weak midpoint continuity was too compressed
   for the advertised self-contained route. A uniform-in-time estimate for
   differences of spectral truncations now proves continuity in `H^2`
   directly from `L^2_t H^4` and `H^1_t L^2`, including the initial endpoint.
3. **Energy/operator collision (P-08): VALID, repaired.** `mathcal F` named
   both the scalar energy and the vector-valued evolution map. The latter
   is now `mathcal G`; its structural checker was updated accordingly.
4. **Strict local minimality (P-14): VALID proof-detail omission, repaired.**
   A positive `L^2` spectral Hessian alone should not stand in for the
   requested `H^2` local assertion. The manuscript now bounds
   `L-mu_t >= (1-mu_t/lambda_0)L` and compares the positive quadratic graph
   norm with the negative quartic remainder. This closes the textual step
   without asserting a new branch classification or charge discontinuity.
5. **Spectral certificate accessibility (P-11): VALID, repaired.** The
   former text delegated the fine root and pi interval to executable audits.
   The new appendix exhibits three disjoint cubic root brackets, the
   alternating-series Machin enclosure, its angle branch, and the monotonic
