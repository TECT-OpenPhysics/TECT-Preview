# Author-controlled submission checklist

## Available in this review distribution

- Review-edition manuscript PDF and editable TeX.
- Reviewer guide PDF and complete A1-A23 review questions.
- Original source-applicability and bounded literature comparison documents.
- Editable, initially uncompleted review response and unsent invitation email.
- Journal-neutral cover-letter draft with explicit unconfirmed declarations.
- Snapshot integrity manifest, diagnostic replay and build/visual-review record.

## Not completed by producing the package

- Independent mathematical dispositions and resulting repairs.
- Specialist novelty/subsumption assessment and any revised contribution claim.
- Choice of journal and article type; current formatting and submission rules.
- Author confirmation of affiliation, ORCID, contact address and all coauthors.
- Originality/overlap and exclusive-submission declarations.
- Funding and competing-interest statements; applicable ethics declarations.
- Accurate disclosure of AI-assisted drafting, checking and typesetting under
  the selected journal's policy. AI output is not independent human review.
- Author approval of the exact final PDF, source, supplement and cover letter.
- Final scientific content freeze after review, release-gated integrated commit
  and remote backup confirmation. A review snapshot is not a final acceptance.
- Explicit permission for actual transmission, upload or journal submission.

Do not invent a journal policy or tick a declaration on the author's behalf.
The placeholders in the cover letter are intentional safeguards, not missing
mathematical text in the manuscript. Rebuild hashes and review the layout after
any modification to a deliverable. Keep every earlier distribution recoverable.
