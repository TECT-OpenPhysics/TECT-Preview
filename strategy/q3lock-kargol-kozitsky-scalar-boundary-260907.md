## Primary sources and exact statements

The primary record [Kargol--Kozitsky, arXiv:math-ph/0611017](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0611017)
states a scalar displacement model on `Z^d`. Its Hamiltonian has one real
coordinate per site, a positive nearest-neighbour dipole coupling, and a
one-site potential `V_0(x)-h x` with a continuous `V_0` satisfying a scalar
growth lower bound. Theorem 1.4 states that for `d >= 3`, sufficiently large
mass and interaction intensity produce a discontinuity of the thermodynamic
polarization at some field `h_*`, possibly nonzero. The proof uses the
Euclidean path representation, infrared estimates and a
Garsia--Rodemich--Rumsey regularity estimate. It does not state the
eight-component Q3LOCK model or a zero-field parity-pair theorem.

The primary 2008 survey/monograph by Kargol--Kondratiev--Kozitsky,
arXiv:0710.2303v1, separates the rotation-invariant vector theorem
(Theorem 3.21) from the scalar symmetric/asymmetric results (Theorems 3.22
and 3.25). The vector theorem assumes a rotation-invariant onsite potential;
the Q3LOCK onsite block is not rotation invariant. The scalar theorems do not
apply after flattening the eight components because the positive-lambda Q3
edge term is a non-bilinear quartic coupling between component coordinates.

## Q3LOCK comparison

Q3LOCK has eight real components at each spatial site, the non-radial Q3
locking polynomial with `lambda > 0`, a collective source along
`u=(1,...,1)/sqrt(8)`, and a claimed sufficient regime for a strict cusp at
the parity-symmetric source `h=0`. The scalar 2006 theorem has one component,
allows a general/asymmetric onsite potential, and locates a discontinuity at
some field. Its hypotheses and conclusion do not provide:

* the non-radial R^8 Q3 onsite/block interaction;
* the explicit positive-lambda collective lower bound `theta_Q`;
* the source-window tempered DLR tangent construction in the Q3LOCK metric;
* the continuous-loop FKG transfer for the selected vector laws; or
* two parity-related zero-source DLR states with the displayed bounded local
  witness.

Setting `lambda=0` or restricting every vector to the collective line would
change the Q3LOCK finite-volume law and is not a valid reduction. The
comparison therefore narrows a direct-import shortcut but does not establish
novelty, priority, or the absence of a stronger anisotropic vector theorem.
