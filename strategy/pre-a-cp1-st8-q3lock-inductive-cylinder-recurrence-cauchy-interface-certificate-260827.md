## Conditional implication

Assume, on the bounded cylinder algebra, a nonnegative local recurrence

`L_x(n+1) <= (1+C delta)L_x(n) + J delta sum_(y~x) L_y(n)`

with a volume-, cutoff- and source-uniform response estimate for the first and
double position commutators.  The base-weighted norm then has one-step factor
`1 + (C + J z base) delta`.  At a boundary distance `d`, the response is
bounded by the iterated factor times `base^(-d)` and the source mass.  A
two-orientation Duhamel integration over the declared horizon gives

`||D_Lambda - D_Lambda'|| + ||delta-D_Lambda - delta-D_Lambda'||`

at most the declared orientation/time factor times that response envelope,
subject to the separate first/double commutator hypotheses.  The package only
records the arithmetic interface; it does not assert that the actual Q3
observables satisfy the hypotheses.

## Adversarial review

1. **Recurrence substitution risk — UPHELD-OPEN.**  The scripts use the
   recurrence as an input hypothesis.  They never evaluate the actual Q3
   generator or promote the finite stress tests to a recurrence theorem.
2. **Weight/sign/factor risk — UPHELD.**  The degree contribution is `J*z*base`,
   the spatial loss is `base^(-d)`, and both declared orientations and the full
   time horizon are included.
3. **Finite-volume uniformity risk — UPHELD-OPEN.**  The coefficient is useful
   only if the response estimate is uniform in volume, cutoff, source and beta;
   that uniform estimate is not supplied here.
4. **Cylinder-domain risk — UPHELD.**  The interface is restricted to the
   bounded fixed-support C1 class from EXP-001150.  Unbounded polynomial
   products and exact CCR domains remain outside scope.
5. **QFT-promotion risk — UPHELD-OPEN.**  A conditional Cauchy coefficient is
   not finite-volume dynamics convergence, a common `alpha_t`, an exhaustion-
   independent state, OS/KMS/GNS reconstruction, a physical mass gap,
   continuum removal, C6, Sector A or Pre-A closure.
6. **Lean scope risk — UPHELD.**  R321 proves only the exact rational fixtures
   and the boolean firewall.  It contains no analytic assumptions, operator
   domains, commutator estimates, state, or limit theorem.
