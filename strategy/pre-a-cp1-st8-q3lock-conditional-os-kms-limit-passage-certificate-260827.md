# EXP-001194 certificate: conditional thermodynamic OS/KMS passage

## Exact interface

For a finite Q3 member `Lambda`, let

\[
T_\Lambda(s)=\exp(-sH_\Lambda/\hbar),\qquad
G^\Lambda_{ij}=Z_\Lambda^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}(F^\Lambda_i{}^*F^\Lambda_j),
\]

where the `F_i` are positive-half Euclidean transfer words. Assume that,
for every fixed finite word list and coefficient vector, the entries of
`G^Lambda` converge along an exhaustion to `G`, and that overlapping word
embeddings are compatible. For any finite coefficient vector `c`,

\[
 c^*Gc=\lim_\Lambda c^*G^\Lambda c\ge 0.
\]

Thus the limiting word form is positive semidefinite. A quotient by its null
space and Hilbert completion is legitimate only after the stated overlap and
compatibility conditions have been proved for the actual Q3 family.

For the finite thermal words

\[
W^\Lambda_{AB}(s)=Z_\Lambda^{-1}
\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(T_\Lambda(\beta\hbar-s)A
T_\Lambda(s)B\bigr),
\]
finite trace cyclicity gives
`W^Lambda_AB(s)=W^Lambda_BA(beta*hbar-s)`. If both sides converge for every
fixed local `A,B,s` with one common beta convention, taking the limit gives

\[
W_{AB}(s)=W_{BA}(\beta\hbar-s).
\]

This is the precise logical passage from finite OS/KMS data to a limiting
OS/KMS word functional.

## Reproducible fixture

The primary and independent programs use the rational Gram limit generated by
vectors `(1,0)`, `(1,1)`, `(0,1)`, with

\[
G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\1&2&1\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The two toy exhaustions use `G + I/n` and `G + 2I/n` for positive integer
`n`; both have the same entrywise limit. Their quadratic forms are sums of
squares plus a nonnegative diagonal term. KMS words use the common finite
value `2+(s+1)/n` on both cyclic sides, so their limit is `2`.

The integrated run also compiles Lean `R353`, which checks the exact
sum-of-squares Gram identity and the finite KMS fixture.

## Adversarial boundary

- **Positivity:** entrywise convergence is required for each finite word list;
  a diagonal-only or pointwise-in-one-vector check is insufficient for a
  whole word form.
- **Complex coefficients:** the fixture is real-rational for exact checking;
  the actual contract is sesquilinear and must be proved for complex words.
- **Exhaustion:** the two toy scales are not an actual Q3 volume theorem.
  Source-, volume-, cutoff- and beta-uniform Cauchy estimates remain open.
- **Dynamics:** OS positivity and KMS equality do not construct one common
  Hamiltonian-derived `alpha_t`; action intertwining, product/core density,
  group law and generator identification remain separate.
- **Physics:** no GNS coercivity, broken-sector gap, continuum limit, physical
  empty reference, C6, Sector A, Pre-A or Clay conclusion follows.

## Decision

`EXP-001194` is an advanced T0, claim-nonbearing conditional contract. It
closes the limit logic that the actual Q3 all-exhaustion word map must satisfy;
it does not claim that the Q3 hypotheses hold.
