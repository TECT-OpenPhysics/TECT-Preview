## 6. Exact nonlinear valley map

Let `s` be a valley, with retained neighbours `L,R`, and set


Changing `s` from the lower edge to the upper edge changes the Darboux

```text

P_s_prime=-P_s+ell*f(Q_s_prime),
```

## 7. Exact shear-control-parity unitary

Define the self-adjoint generators

```text
G_p(P_s)=-(1-kappa)*||P_s||^2/ell
         +beta*ell*W_Q3(P_s/ell)
        =-ell*v(P_s/ell),

G_q(Q_s)=-ell*(1-kappa)*||Q_s||^2
         +beta*ell*W_Q3(Q_s)
        =-ell*v(Q_s).                         (7.1)
```

Let

```text
U_p=exp[-i*G_p(P_s)/hbar],
U_kappa=exp[-i*kappa*(Q_L+Q_R) dot P_s/hbar],
U_q=exp[-i*G_q(Q_s)/hbar],                    (7.2)
```

and let `R_s` be parity on the central eight-coordinate factor.  With the
Heisenberg convention of Section 2, the correct Schrodinger product is

```text
U_flip=U_q U_kappa R_s U_p.                   (7.3)
```

The induced classical point map, equivalently the Schrodinger factor
composition, proceeds from right to left:

1. `U_p` sends `Q_s` to `Q_s-f(P_s/ell)`;
2. `R_s` changes the signs of `Q_s,P_s`;
3. `U_kappa` adds `kappa*(Q_L+Q_R)` to `Q_s` and adds
   `kappa*P_s(old)` to both retained momenta; and
4. `U_q` adds `ell*f(Q_s_prime)` to the central momentum.

The result is precisely (6.2).  All components entering `G_p` commute
strongly, as do those entering `G_q`.  The control generator is a linear
metaplectic shear between distinct factors.  Real quartic phases require no
semiboundedness.  Every factor and its inverse preserves Schwartz space.
Thus (7.3) is an exact unitary and defines a normal `B(H_C)->B(H_D)`
isomorphism.

This calculation fixes a serious order trap.  Writing parity first in the
Schrodinger product with the same generator signs reverses the retained-control
term.  The executable audits reject that mutant.
