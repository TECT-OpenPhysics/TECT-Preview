## Question

Does the primary Kargol--Kondratiev--Kozitsky survey/source make the
non-radial positive-lambda Q3LOCK phase route an immediate consequence of its
general vector or asymmetric-potential results?

## Primary source and locators

The source checked is A. Kargol, Y. Kondratiev and Y. Kozitsky, *Phase
Transitions and Quantum Stabilization in Quantum Anharmonic Crystals*,
arXiv:0710.2303v1, https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.2303 and its HTML rendering.

The relevant locators are Definition 3.1 and the surrounding discussion in
Section 3.1, which define phase transition by multiplicity without assuming
model symmetry; the vector infrared/phase route in Section 3.3, including
Corollary 3.14 and the rotation-invariant vector theorem; and Section 3.5,
which treats a scalar model with an asymmetric potential.  The source also
states that its scalar FKG route is restricted to scalar models in the quantum
stabilization discussion (Section 4.3).

## Hypothesis comparison

| Source statement | Q3LOCK comparison | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Definition 3.1 permits multiplicity without assuming symmetry | Q3LOCK also targets multiplicity of tempered Euclidean DLR states | A definition is not a phase theorem for the Q3 potential |
| Vector phase/infrared route is developed for rotation-invariant onsite structure | Q3LOCK onsite polynomial is Q3 endpoint-weighted and non-radial for `lambda > 0` | No direct vector import without a proved anisotropic replacement |
| Section 3.5 treats asymmetric onsite potentials in the scalar model | Q3LOCK has eight components and a collective source | Scalar asymmetric reasoning does not supply the non-radial `R^8` result |
| Scalar FKG is used in the source's scalar route | Q3LOCK writes a separate continuous-loop FKG argument for the Q3 cone | The source does not certify the Q3 loop association or selected-limit passage |

The source therefore sharpens, rather than removes, the existing scalar/vector
firewall.  The phrase “no symmetry is assumed” in the definition of phase
transition must not be promoted into a theorem for arbitrary anisotropic
vector potentials.  Conversely, the boundary is not an absence or novelty
theorem: a separate anisotropic continuous-oscillator result could still
cover the model.

## Finding and disposition

The primary source separates (i) a symmetry-free definition of multiplicity,
(ii) rotation-invariant vector phase arguments, and (iii) scalar asymmetric
arguments.  None is a direct import for the positive-lambda non-radial `R^8`
Q3LOCK cusp and parity-pair construction.  The exact source boundary should
be added to the specialist handoff and reviewed against any broader
anisotropic theorem before content freeze.

**Disposition: advanced internal literature boundary; direct import rejected
for the stated Q3LOCK model, specialist review still open.**
