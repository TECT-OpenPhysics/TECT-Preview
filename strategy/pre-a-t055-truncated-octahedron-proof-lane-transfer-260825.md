## Proof-lane use

Keep the cubic lattice as the reproducible baseline and add a parallel
truncated-octahedron candidate. For every comparison, freeze:

- metric-regular versus affine-combinatorial versus extracted-statistical
  geometry semantics;
- the center rule, periodic cell, boundary condition, equivalence class and
  realization parameters;
- one parent functional, physical-empty/reference constraint, units,
  counterterm finite parts, beta/ground convention and limit order;
- matched physical volume, density, degrees of freedom, cutoff and numerical
  error budget.

The minimum finite comparison contains the regular truncated-octahedron cell,
the cubic control, one declared affine `D_t` control, the preregistered empty
reference and one non-truncated negative control. Record free-energy sign,
full-space stationarity, symmetry-reduced transverse Hessian, finite spectral
gap, local propagation/causal cone and anisotropy separately.

## Promotion and stop rules

The truncated-octahedron route remains finite and claim-nonbearing until the
candidate/reference sign has one uniform margin, the stationarity and
transverse form are controlled, boundary and finite-size effects are bounded,
and the candidate survives regulator/volume tests. A result that appears only
for the truncated-octahedron geometry is geometry-dependent, not a universal
TECT result. Stop if the result depends on an unfixed finite counterterm,
post-selected center rule, dropped terms, or a non-uniform finite-size sign.

## Immediate tests

1. Reproduce the R-169 geometry and field-center fixtures as regression tests.
2. Run the same finite parent calculation on cubic and truncated-octahedral
   cells at matched volume and degrees of freedom.
3. Compare against the physical-empty/reference branch, not only against other
   ordered candidates.
4. Revalidate the legacy BZ/anisotropy method under the current parent before
   using it as evidence.
5. If a sign or gap survives, increase cell size and cutoff before any C6,
   Sector-A or Pre-A interpretation.

## Explicit non-claims

This transfer proves no BCC or truncated-octahedron vacuum, no Reading-H to
P1 map, no physical-empty sign, no continuum or thermodynamic limit, and no
C6, Sector A, Pre-A, QFT or Yang--Mills result.
