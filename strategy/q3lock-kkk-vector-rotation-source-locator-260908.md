## Question

Does the primary Kargol--Kondratiev--Kozitsky source supply a phase theorem
for a general non-radial vector onsite potential, or does its vector phase
route require internal rotation invariance?

## Source and locators

The checked primary source is A. Kargol, Y. Kondratiev, and Y. Kozitsky,
*Phase Transitions and Quantum Stabilization in Quantum Anharmonic Crystals*,
arXiv:0710.2303v1, [source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.2303v1).  Its Definition
3.1 defines phase-transition multiplicity for tempered Euclidean Gibbs
measures without imposing model symmetry.  The vector infrared and phase
route in Section 3.3 is stated for translation- and rotation-invariant models;
the scalar asymmetric route is treated separately in Section 3.5.  The source
also explains in Section 4.6 that the FKG-based uniqueness/stabilization
argument is available for scalar models, while the vector statement is weaker.

## Q3LOCK boundary

Q3LOCK has an eight-component, positive-lambda, endpoint-weighted Q3 onsite
polynomial.  It is even under global parity but is not invariant under the
full internal O(8) rotation group.  Therefore the source's symmetry-free
multiplicity definition is only a definition, while its rotation-invariant
vector phase theorem and scalar FKG route cannot be imported as the Q3LOCK
strict cusp or parity-related DLR-pair proof.  The manuscript's Q3-specific
continuous-loop FKG, collective lower bound, infrared subtraction, and
source-tangent construction remain separate obligations.

This locator sharpens the existing comparison boundary; it does not establish
that no other anisotropic theorem exists and makes no novelty or priority
claim.  EXP-001664 remains the earlier vector/scalar firewall, and this record
adds the exact source-role distinction for the current handoff.
