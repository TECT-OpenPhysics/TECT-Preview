# EXP-001083 / Finite Q3-cube local versus full-volume energy weight

## Finding

The finite model uses the exact Q3 cube graph on eight vertices and the
target edge `(0,1)`.  The two-site row is the target-edge subsystem; the
eight-site row contains all twelve Q3 cube bonds.  In both rows the Gibbs
state is generated by the full-volume Hamiltonian.  The comparison is between

`A_X = I + H_X - min(spec(H_X)) I`

for the target-edge support and

`A_Lambda = I + H_Lambda - min(spec(H_Lambda)) I`

for the complete finite cube.  Both are inserted into the corrected
two-sided estimate from EXP-001082, retaining the finite-CCR residual.

At oscillator dimension two and cutoff radius `L=1/2`, the primary lane
passes `22/22`, the independent lane passes `18/18`, and the integrated lane
passes `17/17` with Lean `R265` PASS.  The actual seminorm root is about
`0.70522682` in both volumes.  The support-local weight gives

```text

     corrected bound root=8.61609223
```

The full-volume weight gives


The support-local rows are stable across this two-volume finite stress test,
whereas the full-volume state factors grow and make the final bound larger.
This is evidence for choosing a support-local weight in a future graph proof,
not a volume-uniform theorem.
