## Primary records inspected

### Kargol--Kozitsky, scalar asymmetric crystal

The arXiv record for *A Phase Transition in a Quantum Crystal with Asymmetric
Potentials* (math-ph/0611017; DOI 10.1007/s11005-007-0140-8) describes a
translation-invariant system of interacting quantum anharmonic oscillators on
the simple cubic lattice Z^d. Its abstract states a discontinuity of the
global polarization at an external field when d >= 3 and the mass and
interaction are sufficiently large, using path measures, infrared estimates,
and the Garsia--Rodemich--Rumsey inequality.

This is a scalar comparator. It does not state a theorem for an R^8
non-radial onsite polynomial, a positive-lambda Q3 locking interaction, the
collective source normalization used here, or the parity-related DLR pair
constructed in the manuscript. In particular, "general type" in that record
does not convert a scalar oscillator into a multicomponent vector model.
No scalar reduction is inferred.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0611017

### Albeverio--Kozitsky--Kondratiev--Roeckner overview

The overview *Phase transitions and quantum effects in anharmonic crystals*
(arXiv:1204.6279; DOI 10.1142/S0217979212500634) describes the Euclidean
path-space formulation and discusses sufficient and necessary criteria for
phase transitions in the quantum-crystal setting. It is a framework and
literature overview, not a direct proof of the Q3LOCK source cusp or its
eight-component phase pair.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.6279

### Kozitsky equilibrium-state overview

Kozitsky's *Equilibrium States, Phase Transitions and Dynamics in Quantum
Anharmonic Crystals* (arXiv:1806.08264) presents local KMS/path descriptions,
global DLR Gibbs path measures, multiplicity as a phase-transition notion,
and sufficient criteria for phase transitions or quantum stabilization. The
record is useful for terminology and the Euclidean-DLR context. It does not
supply the non-radial R^8 positive-lambda Q3LOCK inequalities, the collective
Falk--Bruch lower bound, or the source-to-zero parity-state construction.
No statement from the overview is imported as a Q3LOCK theorem.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08264

### Faris--Minlos multidimensional crystal

Faris and Minlos, *A Quantum Crystal with Multidimensional Anharmonic
Oscillators*, J. Stat. Phys. 94 (1999), 365--387, DOI
10.1023/A:1004588002407, study multidimensional oscillators with a
nearest-neighbour quadratic coupling. Their main result is a small-coupling
cluster expansion for ground-state local expectations, with estimates
uniform in the finite crystal size. The accessible paper explicitly treats
the ground-state/small-coupling route; its remark that similar techniques may
be used at nonzero temperature is not a theorem used here.

This is the closest dimensionality comparator in the recheck, but it has a
different regime and conclusion: it is not a finite-temperature
thermodynamic-pressure, Euclidean-DLR, strict-cusp, or parity-pair theorem for
the positive-lambda non-radial Q3LOCK model.

Sources:
- https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004588002407
- https://math.arizona.edu/~faris/Crystal.pdf
