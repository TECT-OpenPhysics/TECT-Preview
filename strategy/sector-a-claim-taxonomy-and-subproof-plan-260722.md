## Decision

The appearance of `A13` does not mean that Sector A now contains thirteen
peer-level physical theorems. The live registry contains 21 Sector-A claim
cards because branch theorems, independently reproducible reference results,
and development obstructions were issued as durable verification packages.
That audit history is valuable, but the flat claim list obscures the smaller
theorem structure.

Sector A is henceforth reviewed as five theorem families:

1. shared kernel and convention pinning;
2. the scalar continuum branch;
3. the full-production classical branch;
4. the A5 branch-aware conditional synthesis; and
5. one full derivative Class-II constructive-measure programme.

The fifth family contains `A6` through `A13`. These cards are not eight new
Sector-A endpoint theorems. `A7-CLASSII-RENORMALISED-ENERGY-COMPOSITE` is the
long-term theorem anchor for the canonical interacting object, while
`A13-CLASSII-RELATIVE-PHASE-SOURCE-BUDGET-OBSTRUCTION` is the current subproof
host for the coefficient-jet and one-use work.

## Why the issued IDs remain

Claim IDs are immutable once issued. Renaming, deleting, or retroactively
folding `A8`--`A13` into `A7` would break evidence paths, negative-result
provenance, changelog history, and published references. No lifecycle or tier
is changed by this decision. The repair is an explicit hierarchy over the
existing cards, not a rewrite of history.

## Forward creation rule

The default next research unit is a subproof, gate, result-ledger entry, or
negative-result entry under an existing host. A new Sector-A claim above the
current numeric ceiling requires all five criteria in the theorem map and a
separate approved decision record. In particular, the selected
`A13-CLASSII-COEFFICIENT-JET-RENORMALISATION-CLASSIFICATION` work remains an
A13 subproof. It is not `A14`.

A genuinely new full Class-II constructive-measure theorem may eventually
justify a new claim only after the coefficient model, signed one-use estimate,
Nelson bound, measure construction, and its own scope/falsifier/reproduction
package exist. The present programme has not reached that point.
