## 1. Audit question

The physical target was decomposed before searching.  Because a null or
horizon boundary already presupposes causal/Lorentzian structure, the first
item is a compulsory fork rather than a hidden premise:

```text
C0  causal-origin fork:
    A. a causal-order or null-boundary primitive is declared fundamental; the
       imported primitive itself cannot later be counted as emergent, although
       continuum Lorentzian geometry not contained in it may still be derived;
       or
    B. the substrate is premetric, and an upstream theorem derives causal
       order and the null-boundary limit.
 -> N1 boundary degrees of freedom, observable algebra, and state consistent
       with the chosen C0 branch
 -> N2 reconstruction unique on a declared domain modulo declared gauge,
       diffeomorphism, or unitary equivalence, with constraint preservation
 -> N3 derived finite high-energy scale and cooling/control history
 -> N4 a phase transition of that same reconstructed dynamics, certified by a
       declared order parameter and nonanalyticity in a thermodynamic,
       continuum, or large-N limit
 -> N5 bulk geometry and matter/gauge excitations consistent with C0.

V1  independent validation and quantitative late holdouts not used to select
    the model.
```

`V1` is a verification gate, not a physical link.  Finite-system changes are
crossovers unless a controlled limiting nonanalyticity is proved.  Under
branch C0-A, a programme may still derive continuum Lorentzian geometry, but
it may not count its declared causal/null primitive itself as emergent.  A
claim that causal order or nullness itself emerges requires branch C0-B.

The audit distinguishes three questions:

1. Has any ingredient already been proved?  Yes.
2. Has a close multi-link programme already been studied?  Yes.
3. Was a research programme found whose primary-source sequence explicitly
   selects a `C0` branch and establishes the applicable `N1` through `N5`
   links under one compatible microscopic law and state history, deriving
   causal order/nullness as well under C0-B?  No, within this bounded audit.

The third answer is not a proof that no such work exists and is not a
world-first claim.  It is a falsifiable search result: a counterexample
programme and its primary-source sequence closing every physical link would
supersede it.

## 3. What is and is not novel

The following are **not** defensible novelty claims:

- using null or horizon data to reconstruct a bulk;
- requiring intersecting characteristic hypersurfaces and corner constraints;
- defining boundary observable algebras or transporting algebraic states;
- proposing a high-energy nongeometric phase and a later geometric phase;
- obtaining emergent gauge or matter excitations from collective structure;
- studying bounces, cycles, or boundary conditions for the universe.

The bounded audit supports only this narrower verdict:

> No research programme located in this bounded audit, considering its
> primary-source sequence, explicitly selects a C0 branch and establishes all
> applicable N1-N5 links under one compatible microscopic law and state
> history; under C0-B it would also have to derive causal order/nullness.  This
> is an absence-of-located-counterpart result, not a proof of global novelty.

Each coarse motif listed above has at least one scoped antecedent.  The
programme-level composition audited here remains open, while an individual
component theorem could be novel independently.  Validation gate `V1`
separately tests predictive and physical support; it is not a novelty
criterion.

## 4. Consequences for proof strategy

The literature changes the order of work:

1. Do not spend effort reproving generic null-IVP existence as if it were new.
   Use it as a benchmark and isolate what the TECT microscopic state adds.
2. Do not call the candidate surface an event horizon until global causal
   geometry exists.  If a causal primitive is inserted, identify exactly what
   was imported and do not recount it as emergent; otherwise begin with
   premetric data and derive causal order/nullness.
3. Do not infer a density matrix from an algebraic state without a type-I or
   finite-cutoff representation.
4. Do not combine an independent boundary toy and PA-M2 by narrative.  Prove a
   state/parameter/control map.
5. Make causal-structure circularity explicit: either causal order is a
   fundamental Pre-A input or its emergence is a separate upstream theorem.
6. Treat cyclicity as an optional return-map theorem after a state space and
   law exist, not as the explanation of their existence.
7. Keep independent computation and late holdouts as validation gate `V1`, not
   as a physical link used to manufacture an absence-of-prior-art verdict.

not "no one has done this."
