## Question and scope

This bounded audit asks whether anisotropic-spin, unbounded-classical-spin, or
quantum Pirogov--Sinai results directly cover the untruncated positive-lambda
Q3LOCK model: fixed-spacing Z^3, onsite Hilbert space L^2(R^8), non-radial
Q3 polynomial, source-pressure cusp, and two tempered Euclidean DLR states.
Only inspectable primary records or author-hosted/public copies are used. This
is not an exhaustive novelty survey or a theorem certificate.

## Fröhlich--Lieb

J. Froehlich and E. H. Lieb, *Phase transitions in anisotropic lattice spin
systems*, Commun. Math. Phys. 60 (1978), 233--267,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01612891), with the related
[1977 PRL](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.38.440) and public
[IHES copy](https://repo-archives.ihes.fr/FONDS_IHES/I_Prepublications/FROHLICH/1978-1980/P_78_199/P_78_199_web.pdf).
The accessible description concerns classical and quantum nearest-neighbour
lattice-spin models with finitely degenerate ground states and
Peierls/chessboard/reflection-positivity methods, including anisotropic
Heisenberg examples. The direct interface is different: finite-dimensional
spin examples (including the two-dimensional square lattice) versus an
unbounded continuous oscillator on Z^3, changing-mesh loops, source tangent,
and parity-related DLR states. Disposition: comparison-only,
DOES-NOT-APPLY-DIRECTLY; this is not a no-go theorem for a new extension.

## Lebowitz--Presutti

J. L. Lebowitz and E. Presutti, *Statistical Mechanics of Systems of
Unbounded Spins*, Commun. Math. Phys. 50 (1976), 195--218,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01609401) and
[public Rutgers copy](https://cmsr.rutgers.edu/images/people/lebowitz_joel/publications/jll.pub_142.pdf).
This classical result treats unbounded n-component spins with superstable and
strongly tempered potentials, proving broad thermodynamic/free-energy and
equilibrium-state results and a special one-component differentiability
uniqueness statement. It does not supply the quantum Feynman--Kac loop,
continuous-loop FKG, source cusp, or parity-state construction. Disposition:
CLASSICAL-COMPARATOR-ONLY; no quantum conclusion is imported.

## Datta--Fernandez--Frohlich

N. Datta, R. Fernandez and J. Frohlich, *Low-temperature phase diagrams of
quantum lattice systems. I*, [author-hosted text](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~ferna107/papers/pirogov.pdf).
The inspected formalism uses finite-dimensional onsite C^N, a classical
reference with finitely many periodic ground states and a Peierls condition,
and a small Duhamel quantum perturbation. The Q3 coordinate-space kinetic and
polynomial operators are unbounded and no matching cutoff-uniform
reference/Peierls package is established. Disposition:
DOES-NOT-APPLY-DIRECTLY; a relative-form or transformed-reference extension
would require a new hypothesis-level proof.

## Bricmont--Kuroda--Lebowitz

J. Bricmont, K. Kuroda and J. L. Lebowitz, *First order phase transitions in
lattice and continuous systems: extension of Pirogov--Sinai theory*, Commun.
Math. Phys. 101 (1985), 501--538,
[public record](https://hdl.handle.net/2078.5/44456).
The inspected record describes a classical restricted-ensemble extension and
Potts/coexistence examples. It is not a quantum oscillator
operator/Feynman--Kac theorem and does not provide the Q3 source cusp or
tempered DLR tangent. Disposition: COMPARISON-ONLY.

## Consolidated disposition

These checks narrow four direct-import shortcuts but do not establish
novelty. Finite-dimensional anisotropic spin results do not automatically
cover the unbounded R^8 loop limit; classical unbounded-spin thermodynamics
is not a quantum source-cusp theorem; finite-onsite quantum Pirogov--Sinai
requires a different reference and perturbation package; and classical
restricted-ensemble methods do not close the quantum oscillator interface.
Another anisotropic continuous-oscillator theorem may exist. A signed
specialist literature disposition remains required.
