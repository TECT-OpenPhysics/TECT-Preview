# Draft request: archival information on the 2015 solar clock analysis

Unsent draft. No recipient has been contacted and no reply has been received
for this request. Sending requires a separate operator instruction.

Dear analysis team,

We are documenting the analysis underlying the solar-potential modulation
coefficient for the 87Rb/133Cs frequency ratio discussed in Section 5 of
your 2015 review (DOI: 10.1016/j.crhy.2015.03.010). Could you point us to an
existing analysis note, configuration, supplementary file or small archival
packet describing the following?

1. The fitted quantity and potential template: ratio direction,
   logarithmic or fractional convention, sign and normalization,
   amplitude and phase, time standard, sampling or binning, and the
   ephemeris or orbital approximation actually used.
2. The estimator for that particular published coefficient: nuisance
   terms, phase variant, weights and correlations, masks or selection,
   and any choices estimated from the data. A reproducible recipe or
   influence weights aligned to observation epochs/bins would be useful;
   we are not requesting all raw measurements as a prerequisite.
3. The meaning and construction of the quoted uncertainty: statistical
   and systematic contributions, temporal correlations, propagation to
   the annual coefficient, and any distributional, coverage or posterior
   interpretation and its conditions.

Existing references, partial replies, and an indication that information
was not retained would all be helpful. Please indicate any restrictions
on review, reuse or redistribution. A private reply will not be treated
as permission to publish it.

We are not asking for a new analysis, endorsement of a theoretical model,
or certification of a new gravitational calibration. Translating these
archived analysis details into a different hypothetical response model,
and establishing its theoretical error bounds, would remain our own
research responsibility.

Thank you for considering this archival request.
