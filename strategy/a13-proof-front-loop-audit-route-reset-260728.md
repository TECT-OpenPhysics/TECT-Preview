## Secondary support: centered `det2` high-shell summability

R-117 proves an `O(N^{-3})` operator bound for the PSD canonical frozen
recession trace form at high dyadic shell `N`.  The shell input rank is
`O(N^3)`.  For any PSD operator `T` with `q ||T||_op<1`, the centered Gaussian
trace block obeys the exact estimate

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \log\mathbb E\exp\!\left[
  \frac q2\{\langle\xi,T\xi\rangle-\operatorname{Tr}T\}
 \right]
 &=-\frac12\log\det{}_2(I-qT)\\
 &\le \frac{q^2\|T\|_{\mathfrak S_2}^2}
 {4(1-q\|T\|_{\rm op})}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus `rank(T_N)=O(N^3)` and `||T_N||_op=O(N^{-3})` imply an
`O(N^{-3})` dyadic log-cost.  This explains the useful distinction between
R-117's coarse tail-existence bound and a centered `det2` ledger: first-order
trace cost may remain order one per shell, while the renormalized second-order
cost is summable.

### Conditional triangular Ramer--Schur quotient

This route first applies the R-116 same-root quotient and R-107 contraction
closure, then performs a genuine one-fresh-root conditional transport.  Its
potentially new invariant is the block-triangular Jacobian: strict-past
derivatives are off diagonal, while only current-root diagonal derivatives

It is not yet a primary route.  A quadratic residual can make

2. compatibility of the trace owner with `Tr(Du)`;
3. covariance-square decay of the remaining diagonal defect; and
