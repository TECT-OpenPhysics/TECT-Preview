## Question and exact scope

R-419 asks whether the finite R-417 core-tail corridor survives a bounded
increase of spatial volume while every model convention is held fixed.  The
test uses the same Q3 Hamiltonian, coordinate basis, log-domain Gibbs law,
momentum conductance, conditional-prefix rule, beta values, orientations,
Lyapunov parameters and tail thresholds as R-417.  It adds volumes three and
four at declared low oscillator cutoffs.

For a positive conditional law `pi` and symmetric conductance `c`, the tested
generator is

```text
L f(i) = pi_i^(-1) sum_j c_ij (f_j-f_i).
```

With `phi_i=log(pi_max)-log(pi_i)` and
`V_i=exp(alpha*phi_i)`, the recorded tail quantity is
`min_{phi_i>=theta}(-L V/V)`.  The complementary core is tested by the
projected positive gap of the induced graph after renormalizing by its core
mass.  Tail mass and the maximum core-to-tail boundary rate are retained as
separate diagnostics.

This is a finite growing-volume stress only.  It is not a global Poincare
theorem, a common-core result, a phase-selection result or a QFT statement.
