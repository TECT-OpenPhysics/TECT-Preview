## What the accessible record establishes

The abstract describes an n-component vector model for structural phase
transitions and studies how quantum effects alter critical properties, including
regimes in which quantum fluctuations can suppress a transition. This places the
article in the physical critical-property/vector-model comparison class. The
accessible record does not state a Euclidean Gibbs/DLR existence theorem, a
source-pressure cusp, a continuous-loop FKG passage, a Hilbert-valued
reflection-positivity bound, or a parity-related pair of tempered DLR states.
Those absences in an abstract are not evidence that the full article lacks such
results; they are the reason the direct applicability is left unresolved.
