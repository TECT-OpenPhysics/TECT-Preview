# Pre-A observation-first inverse lane

**Status:** T0 mainline strategy contract; no claim, result, tier, candidate
selection, or gate closure.

## Decision

The observation-first lane is registered beside, not instead of, the forward
proof lane.  Its question is not whether observations prove Pre-A.  It asks
which microscopic equivalence classes, if any, admit one coherent forward map
to established low-energy QFT, Yang--Mills, gravity, and unused observations.

The current answer is deliberately nonpositive: there are zero admitted
microscopic forward maps.  M1, M2, M5, the finite `F_ref` identity-mobility
Langevin comparison, the T-055 geometry candidate, and the reference-only
TECT-YM finite supplier all miss at least one earlier map or owner stage.
Consequently no fit, score, ranking, uniqueness, or empirical validation is
reported.

The machine-readable contract is
`strategy/pre-a-observation-first-inverse-lane-contract-manifest.json`.

## Four noninterchangeable layers

1. **Mathematical theorem:** a conclusion follows from pinned assumptions.
2. **Model consistency:** state, dynamics, projection, observables, regulator,
   volume, boundary, normalization, and limits form one model.
3. **Physical identity:** a controlled map identifies that model with known
   QFT/YM/GR structures.
4. **Empirical validation:** a frozen prediction passes an unused target with
   no post-disclosure tuning.

A pass at an earlier layer never supplies a later layer.  Existing TECT and
TECT-YM work remains primarily finite or conditional layer-1/layer-2 evidence.

## Forward and inverse maps

The forward lane keeps its existing order: functional and dynamics, finite
verification, common core and uniform estimates, ordered limits, then physical
sector.  The inverse lane uses

`observations -> effective target -> microscopic equivalence classes -> Pre-A boundary`.

The operational forward object is a predictive law

`P_M(D | theta, nuisance, regulator, volume, boundary, limit order)`,

not a deterministic slogan `O=F(M)`.  It is decomposed into finite regulated
model `F_reg`, uniform limit `F_lim`, effective-theory identification `F_eff`,
and detector/estimator map `F_obs`.  A missing stage makes every later stage
`NOT_ADMITTED`.

The physical owner and proof owner are separate.  The former carries the
generator or transfer, state, physical projection, temporal boundary, and
observable algebra.  The latter carries filtration, heat-root map,
raw-current intertwiner, and one-use source ledger.  Predictions must be
invariant under admitted proof realizations; otherwise those realizations are
different candidate versions.

candidate version, all map stages, nuisance ranges, covariance, theory

spectral positivity, and microcausality are effective-target constraints, not
detector observations.  Likewise the exact SI value of `c` is calibration,
not evidence for Lorentz invariance.

## Inverse adjudication

The tests are separate:

- **Existence:** a nonempty parameter set satisfies the joint calibration law.
- **Identifiability:** after quotienting gauge, diffeomorphism, RG, field
  redefinition, and prediction-preserving scheme changes, is the survivor a
  singleton or an equivalence class?
- **Stability:** do covariance, theory-map, regulator, boundary, volume, and
  limit-order perturbations preserve the survivor and prediction?
- **Predictivity:** does the frozen predictive distribution beat the mandatory
  effective baseline on a powered prospective target?

No compensatory scalar score is allowed.  Missing owners and maps are hard
failures.  Only admitted candidates can enter a Pareto or preregistered
lexicographic comparison.  Exact or practical twins are reported as a
surviving equivalence class, never resolved by arbitrary preference.
