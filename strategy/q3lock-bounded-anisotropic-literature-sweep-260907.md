## Crosswalk finding

The inspected sources split into three roles. The KP papers supply general
vector Euclidean-DLR infrastructure, not the Q3LOCK phase sign. The
Kargol--Kozitsky asymmetric theorem is a one-component scalar crystal; it does
not cover the Q3 endpoint-weighted R^8 interaction. The vector phase results in
the KKK source use rotation-invariant vector structure or scalar reductions,
whereas the Q3LOCK onsite block is non-radial and contains non-bilinear
component couplings for every lambda > 0. The inspected records therefore do
not provide a direct import of the full Q3LOCK cusp/state theorem.

This is a bounded search, not an absence theorem. It does not rule out a more
general anisotropic continuous-oscillator result, an unpublished theorem, or a
corollary whose hypotheses have not been checked. It also does not establish
novelty or priority. The manuscript's local FKG, FSS, collective lower-bound,
pressure, source-window, and parity-state interfaces remain independent proof
obligations.
