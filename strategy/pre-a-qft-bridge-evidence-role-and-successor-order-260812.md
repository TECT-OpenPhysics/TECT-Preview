## 1. Decision

Established QFT, Standard-Model effective field theory, and general relativity
are evidence baselines and target languages for low-energy comparison. They are
not axioms that a TECT candidate may insert or inherit as a microscopic answer.

Yang--Mills is a particularly important comparison theory if a candidate derives
a non-abelian gauge sector. It is not a required current branch of the Q3LOCK
proof, and no present TECT record authorizes importing its mass-gap problem,
gauge constraints, or continuum construction as if they had been derived by the
candidate.

## 2. Bridge order

The bridge is a sequence of separately falsifiable deliverables:

```text
declared microscopic candidate and common evidence baseline
  -> common infinite-volume dynamics on a declared observable algebra
  -> phase/KMS and zero-temperature state selection on that same dynamics
  -> regulator family with the full symmetry-allowed counterterm basis
  -> controlled continuum Schwinger functions
  -> OS reconstruction and identification of the low-energy QFT
  -> spectrum, causal cone, symmetry and massless/gapped-mode tests
  -> comparison with Standard-Model, Yang--Mills, gravity, or another
     predeclared effective-theory target
  -> prospective discriminating prediction
```

No arrow is automatic. In particular, a fixed-beta phasewise OS/KMS
reconstruction is not a beta-independent common dynamics; a local parity
doublet is not an infinite-volume GNS gap; and a fixed-lattice counterterm
classification is not a continuum limit.

## 3. Immediate relevance to the Q3LOCK route

The active Q3LOCK line supplies only the first bridge input under investigation:
the all-shape, bidirectional thermodynamic Cauchy estimate needed for a common
infinite-volume real-time automorphism group. The next independent QFT-bridge
input is an enlarged-counterterm regulator family and a controlled continuum
limit. These remain separate from the current fixed-lattice result.

The present Q3LOCK work must therefore be credited only for its declared
fixed-lattice, finite-temperature, or conditional common-dynamics children. It
may not be credited with Yang--Mills, a physical mass gap, light, time, gravity,
or a complete QFT until the relevant later bridge deliverable is registered.

## 4. Time-role separation and emergent-time successor

The time parameter used in the current Hamiltonian/OS/KMS and common-alpha
questions is a mathematical evolution parameter for the declared regulated
candidate.  Establishing a common automorphism group `alpha_t` answers the
strictly weaker consistency question: whether local observables admit one
exhaustion-independent, reversible composition law in the thermodynamic
limit.  It does not make `t` a fundamental external physical time, select a
clock, or derive a Lorentzian causal structure.

A candidate that treats time as relative or emergent must supply a later,
independent bridge with the following direction of explanation:

```text
microscopic relational states and changes
  -> declared internal clock or relational ordering observable
  -> stable coarse-grained temporal order and effective evolution parameter
  -> causal/kinetic structure, including tests of a common limiting cone
  -> comparison with the low-energy QFT and spacetime target
```

Each arrow needs a declared state/reference class, operational clock rule,
scaling or error control, and falsifier.  A common `alpha_t` may be a necessary
consistency input to this programme, but is neither proof of emergent time nor
evidence that the physical vacuum is a time-generating medium.  Conversely,
failure of the present regulated common-alpha route rules out that route; it
does not rule out relational time in another candidate.

### 4.1 Pre-A working hypothesis: time as structured action-change order

The working Pre-A hypothesis is that physical time is not an independently
fixed background coordinate.  It is the phenomenological order disclosed by
structured changes of the physical configuration: an internally comparable
sequence of action-relevant transitions, constraints, and persistent records.
On this hypothesis, physical structure and temporal order are not separable
ingredients; a stable temporal parameter is a coarse description of their
ordered change.

This wording requires a technical caution.  The ordinary variational action
`S` is a functional of an entire history, and on a classical stationary history
its first variation vanishes.  Therefore a future theorem may not use the
phrase "the action changes" as an unexamined clock.  It must declare a local
or relational action-relevant quantity, its state/reference class, a
composition/order relation, and the operational records by which distinct
transitions are compared.  A viable derivation must then show that this
relation yields a stable effective ordering parameter in the intended regime,
without inserting an external physical clock.

The hypothesis is narrowed or rejected for a candidate if no such quantity is
defined, if its ordering is non-transitive or representation-dependent, if it
fails to remain stable under the stated coarse graining, or if the resulting
causal/kinetic structure requires an independently imposed time coordinate.

### 4.2 Pre-A origin hypothesis: propagation, mass, and structural geometry

The TECT origin hypothesis treats light-like and matter-like excitations as
different modes of one microscopic structure.  A viable low-energy limit must
explain both their shared propagation structure and their difference: a
protected massless branch with a common limiting cone, and gapped or inertial
matter branches with a derived dispersion and rest-energy scale.  It is not
enough to insert independent values of a limiting speed and masses.

The stronger proposed mechanism is that matter-like localized structure changes
or deforms the effective propagation structure.  Light-like modes then follow
that effective causal/kinetic geometry, while relational clocks respond through
the same structure.  In this programme, the phrase "mass is gravity" is a
target explanatory relation, not an established identity: it must be replaced
by a derived universal coupling between the energy-momentum or stress response
of the candidate and the effective geometry seen by all relevant probes.

The required test chain is:

```text
one microscopic candidate
  -> massless and massive/gapped excitation branches with a common protected cone
  -> localized matter-like excitation changes the effective kinetic/causal tensor
  -> light-like propagation and relational-clock response follow that tensor
  -> universal low-energy response, equivalence-principle and gravity-limit tests
```

The mechanism is narrowed or rejected if common speeds require separate tuning,
mass terms are merely inserted, the deformation is probe-dependent without a
declared physical reason, light does not follow the derived cone, clock response
requires an independent rule, or the weak/long-distance response fails the
predeclared gravity and equivalence tests.

### 4.3 Atomic and chemical structure as a downstream recovery test

Atomic shell structure, discrete spectra, the periodic organization of the
elements, and stable chemistry are non-optional downstream recovery tests for
any candidate proposed as a microscopic physical theory.  They are not direct
evidence selecting one microscopic candidate: distinct microscopic theories may
share the same low-energy effective QED and fermionic content.  They therefore
cannot be inserted as microscopic premises or used to infer a unique TECT
structure.

The required recovery chain is:

```text
microscopic candidate
  -> controlled low-energy QFT with an electromagnetic sector and charged
     fermionic degrees of freedom
  -> spin/statistics and Pauli exclusion, gauge coupling, and bound-state
     spectral problem
  -> atomic energy levels, shell filling, and spectral selection rules
  -> periodic trends, stable bonds, and declared chemical/material tests
```

At the appropriate later stage, a candidate must reproduce predeclared
dimensionless spectral ratios, degeneracies, selection rules, and bound-state
stability without placing the atomic Hamiltonian or observed element data back
into its microscopic input.  Failure to derive the necessary fermionic,
gauge, or bound-state structure excludes the candidate from a microscopic
account of ordinary matter, even if it supplies another partial QFT bridge.

## 5. Candidate-neutral comparison rule

The M0 established low-energy baseline is retained as the comparison standard.
A microscopic candidate earns explanatory credit only by deriving a
predeclared low-energy structure without reinserting it as an input. A candidate
may lead to Yang--Mills-type QFT, another QFT, or fail the bridge; the outcome is
to be decided by the declared spectrum, symmetries, continuum correlators, and
prospective tests, not by preferred terminology.
