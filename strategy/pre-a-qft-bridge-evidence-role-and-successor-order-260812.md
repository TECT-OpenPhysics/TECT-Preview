# Pre-A QFT Bridge: Evidence Role and Successor Order

**Recorded:** 2026-08-12  
**Authority:** T0 route-selection record only; no theorem, candidate selection,
physical-vacuum statement, Yang--Mills claim, or tier change.  
**Task:** T-054  
**Bears on:** the M0 baseline, M1/Q3LOCK, M2 and M3 successor families;

## 1. Decision

Established QFT, Standard-Model effective field theory, and general relativity
are evidence baselines and target languages for low-energy comparison. They are
not axioms that a TECT candidate may insert or inherit as a microscopic answer.

Yang--Mills is a particularly important comparison theory if a candidate derives
a non-abelian gauge sector. It is not a required current branch of the Q3LOCK
proof, and no present TECT record authorizes importing its mass-gap problem,
gauge constraints, or continuum construction as if they had been derived by the
candidate.

## 2. Bridge order

The bridge is a sequence of separately falsifiable deliverables:

```text
declared microscopic candidate and common evidence baseline
  -> common infinite-volume dynamics on a declared observable algebra
  -> phase/KMS and zero-temperature state selection on that same dynamics
  -> regulator family with the full symmetry-allowed counterterm basis
  -> controlled continuum Schwinger functions
  -> OS reconstruction and identification of the low-energy QFT
  -> spectrum, causal cone, symmetry and massless/gapped-mode tests
  -> comparison with Standard-Model, Yang--Mills, gravity, or another
     predeclared effective-theory target
  -> prospective discriminating prediction
```

No arrow is automatic. In particular, a fixed-beta phasewise OS/KMS
reconstruction is not a beta-independent common dynamics; a local parity
doublet is not an infinite-volume GNS gap; and a fixed-lattice counterterm
classification is not a continuum limit.

## 3. Immediate relevance to the Q3LOCK route

The active Q3LOCK line supplies only the first bridge input under investigation:
the all-shape, bidirectional thermodynamic Cauchy estimate needed for a common
infinite-volume real-time automorphism group. The next independent QFT-bridge
input is an enlarged-counterterm regulator family and a controlled continuum
limit. These remain separate from the current fixed-lattice result.

The present Q3LOCK work must therefore be credited only for its declared
fixed-lattice, finite-temperature, or conditional common-dynamics children. It
may not be credited with Yang--Mills, a physical mass gap, light, time, gravity,
or a complete QFT until the relevant later bridge deliverable is registered.

## 4. Candidate-neutral comparison rule

The M0 established low-energy baseline is retained as the comparison standard.
A microscopic candidate earns explanatory credit only by deriving a
predeclared low-energy structure without reinserting it as an input. A candidate
may lead to Yang--Mills-type QFT, another QFT, or fail the bridge; the outcome is
to be decided by the declared spectrum, symmetries, continuum correlators, and
prospective tests, not by preferred terminology.
